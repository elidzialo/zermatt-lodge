Going to the Grand National from Ireland: Getting There and Where to Stay

Travelling from Ireland for the Grand National? Flights into Liverpool, the Belfast and Holyhead ferries, driving to Aintree, and a 4-bed house for groups.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Irish racegoers can fly direct into Liverpool from Dublin, Belfast and the west of Ireland, take the Belfast to Birkenhead ferry, or sail Dublin to Holyhead and drive. The 2027 festival runs Thursday 8 to Saturday 10 April, and Zermatt Lodge sleeps up to 7 with free parking for two cars.

Irish racegoers have more options than ever to reach the Grand National: short direct flights into Liverpool from Dublin, Belfast and the west of Ireland, the Belfast to Birkenhead ferry, or the Dublin to Holyhead crossing with a drive across North Wales. The 2027 festival runs from Thursday 8 to Saturday 10 April. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in north Liverpool, a few miles from Aintree Racecourse, sleeping up to 7 with free parking for two cars, so it suits groups who bring a car over on the ferry as well as those who fly in.

This guide compares the routes and explains how to get from each one to Aintree.

Option 1: Fly into Liverpool John Lennon Airport

Schedules change by season, and some routes run less often than others, so check before you plan. Festival week flights sell out, so book early.

From the airport to Aintree

The Jockey Club says the airport is about 20 minutes' drive from Aintree Racecourse. Options include:

  • Hire car from the airport, if your group wants to drive around Liverpool during the trip
  • Taxi straight to your accommodation
  • Bus and train: the Arriva 500 runs between the airport and the city centre, and from there Merseyrail serves Aintree

Our airport to Fazakerley guide has the step-by-step options for reaching north Liverpool.

Option 2: Belfast to Birkenhead by ferry

Stena Line runs the Belfast to Liverpool (Birkenhead) route:

  • Crossing time: about 8 hours
  • Sailings: typically two a day
  • Foot passengers: accepted, as well as cars

Birkenhead is on the Wirral, across the River Mersey from Liverpool. With a car, you drive through one of the Mersey road tunnels into Liverpool, which carries a toll. See our Mersey tunnel tolls guide. From the tunnel, it's a straightforward drive north to Aintree and Fazakerley. Check a live route for timings.

Compare the departure times: a sailing that arrives the evening before your race day is far less stressful than one that lands on the morning itself. Our Belfast ferry guide covers the terminal and arrival in more detail.

Option 3: Dublin via Holyhead

There used to be a direct Dublin to Liverpool ferry, but P&O Ferries ended its Liverpool to Dublin service at the end of 2023 after losing its Liverpool berth. For travellers from Dublin bringing a car, the main route is now:

  • Dublin to Holyhead with Stena Line (about 3 hours 15 minutes) or Irish Ferries (about 3 hours 30 minutes), each running around four sailings a day
  • Then drive from Anglesey along the North Wales coast to Liverpool. Check a live route, and allow extra time on busy weekends

Ferry routes change, so always check the operators' current timetables before booking.

Which option suits your group?

  • A couple or small group with no car needed: fly, and use taxis and trains
  • A group of 5 to 7 who want a car for the weekend: take the ferry with your own car, or fly and hire one
  • From Northern Ireland with a car: Belfast to Birkenhead avoids a drive across Wales
  • From Dublin with a car: Holyhead is the practical route

On race day

Whichever way you arrive, the advice for race day is the same:

  • Use the train if you can. Festival trains run every seven and a half minutes between Liverpool Central/Moorfields and Aintree, according to the Jockey Club
  • Driving? Car parking must be booked online in advance
  • Bring a card or phone. The racecourse is cashless for food and drink, and cash is only for on-course bookies. Pounds sterling are used throughout
  • Small bags only. No bigger than 30cm x 10cm x 20cm, and there's no left luggage

Our first-timer's guide covers enclosures, dress and the day itself.

Plan a long weekend

Many Irish groups arrive on the Thursday or Friday and leave on the Sunday or Monday. That gives you time for a day in the city, such as the waterfront, the museums or a match if there's a home fixture, and avoids travelling home tired straight after the big race.

Why Zermatt Lodge suits groups from Ireland

Zermatt Lodge is a whole house in north Liverpool, near Fazakerley station and a few miles from Aintree. It isn't in the city centre, but the train to Liverpool Central takes about 15 minutes.

  • 4 bedrooms, sleeps 7: king master with en-suite, second king, queen and single
  • 2 bathrooms for race-day mornings
  • Free parking for 2 cars, ideal if you've brought a car on the ferry
  • Big kitchen and dining room, handy when restaurants are packed during festival week

House rules: strictly no parties or events, no smoking and no pets. It's a comfortable base for racegoers, not a party house.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I fly direct from Ireland to Liverpool for the Grand National?

Yes. Flight data shows direct flights into Liverpool John Lennon Airport from Dublin (Aer Lingus and Ryanair), Cork, Knock and Shannon (Ryanair), and from Belfast International, Belfast City and Derry (easyJet). Routes and schedules change, so check with the airlines.

Is there a ferry from Dublin to Liverpool?

P&O Ferries ended its Liverpool to Dublin service at the end of 2023. From Dublin, most travellers with a car now sail to Holyhead with Stena Line or Irish Ferries, a crossing of about 3 hours 15 to 3 hours 30, then drive to Liverpool. Check current operators before you book.

Is there a ferry from Belfast to Liverpool?

Yes. Stena Line sails from Belfast to Liverpool (Birkenhead), about 8 hours, typically twice a day, and takes foot passengers as well as cars.

How far is Liverpool Airport from Aintree?

The Jockey Club says Liverpool John Lennon Airport is about a 20-minute drive from Aintree Racecourse. Traffic on festival days can make it longer.

When is the 2027 Grand National?

The Randox Grand National Festival runs from Thursday 8 to Saturday 10 April 2027, with the Grand National on the Saturday.
