Group Weekends in Liverpool This Winter: The Best Dates for January and February 2027

Planning a group weekend in Liverpool in January or February 2027? The best weekends for football, the derby, Strictly Live, Back to the Future, Lunar New Year and half term, with a house for 7.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

For a group weekend in Liverpool in early 2027, the big dates are the Merseyside derby at Anfield on Saturday 30 January (12.30pm), Strictly Come Dancing Live at M&S Bank Arena on 2 and 3 February, Back to the Future The Musical at the Empire from 12 January to 6 February, and Lunar New Year on Saturday 6 February, when Everton also host Newcastle. February half term is 15 to 19 February for Liverpool City Council schools. Weekends without a home match or a big show are usually easier to book for a big group.

January and February are a great time for a group trip to Liverpool. The city is usually calmer than in December, there's plenty on indoors, and the football is in full swing. The key is picking the right weekend: some are packed, some are quiet.

We checked these dates on 24 November 2026. Football fixtures can move for TV, so check again before you book.

Lunar New Year is Saturday 6 February 2027. Liverpool's Chinatown is home to the oldest Chinese community in Europe. The city's 2027 celebration dates hadn't been announced when we checked (in 2026 they were on 21 and 22 February). Our Chinese New Year guide has the latest on 2027 plans.

Tips for booking a group

  • Fix the date first. Agree it, then book the house before the tickets if the weekend is a busy one
  • Split the cost up front. One person books, everyone pays straight away
  • Check fixtures again in December and January, when TV picks are announced

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is the Merseyside derby in 2027?

Liverpool host Everton at Anfield on Saturday 30 January 2027 at 12.30pm, moved for TNT Sports. Expect the city to be very busy that weekend.

When is Strictly Live in Liverpool?

Strictly Come Dancing Live plays M&S Bank Arena on Tuesday 2 and Wednesday 3 February 2027, at 7.30pm.

Which home games are there in January and February 2027?

Anfield: Coventry (2 January), Crystal Palace (16 January), Everton (30 January) and Hull (20 February). Hill Dickinson Stadium: Brentford (Sunday 24 January, 2pm), Newcastle (6 February), Leeds (Wednesday 10 February, 8pm) and Nottingham Forest (27 February). All fixtures can move for TV.
