Liverpool and Everton Home Games Over Christmas 2026

Every Liverpool and Everton home game from December 2026 to early January 2027, with kick-off times, Boxing Day details and where to stay for a match.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Liverpool play at Anfield on 2, 9, 12 and 19 December 2026 and on Saturday 2 January 2027, but are away on Boxing Day at Hull. Everton's festive home games at Hill Dickinson Stadium are Sunderland on Boxing Day, Manchester City on Tuesday 29 December and Aston Villa on Tuesday 5 January 2027. All kick-off times can change for TV.

Liverpool are not at home on Boxing Day 2026: they're away at Hull City at 5.30pm. Their festive home games at Anfield are Sunderland (Wed 2 Dec), Porto (Wed 9 Dec, Champions League), Leeds (Sat 12 Dec), Tottenham (Sat 19 Dec) and Coventry (Sat 2 Jan 2027). Everton are at home on Boxing Day to Sunderland, then host Manchester City on Tuesday 29 December and Aston Villa on Tuesday 5 January.

If you searched for "Boxing Day at Anfield", this is the key point: the Boxing Day football in Liverpool this year is at Hill Dickinson Stadium, not Anfield.

All dates and times below were checked on 19 November 2026. Premier League games can be moved for TV, sometimes only a few weeks before, so confirm with the club before you book travel.

Busy dates to plan around

Some match days stack up with other big events, which affects trains, roads and accommodation:

  • Sat 5 Dec: Becher Chase Day at Aintree, plus Everton v Fulham if it stays on the Saturday. See our Becher Chase weekend guide
  • Sat 12 Dec: Liverpool v Leeds and Morrissey at M&S Bank Arena
  • Sat 19 Dec: Liverpool v Tottenham on the last Saturday before Christmas, with the Christmas shopping rush in town
  • Sat 26 Dec: Everton v Sunderland and Boxing Day racing at Aintree on the same day. See our Boxing Day in Liverpool guide

Tickets

Liverpool home tickets are sold through the club's membership schemes and are very hard to get for most games. Everton tickets are sometimes easier. Always buy through official club channels. Our LFC and Everton ticket guide explains the options.

Getting to the grounds

Hill Dickinson Stadium (Everton)

Sandhills is the usual station, on the Merseyrail Northern line. Our Everton stadium guide has the walking route.

Anfield (Liverpool)

There's no station right next to Anfield. Take the train to Sandhills and use the matchday Soccerbus, or walk in from Kirkdale. Our Anfield match day guide has the details. If you're driving, read our match day parking guide, as parking is restricted around the ground.

Festive transport

The 2026 festive timetables hadn't been published when we checked. In past years, Merseyrail hasn't run on Christmas Day and has run a reduced service on Boxing Day. Check merseyrail.org before Boxing Day travel.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are Liverpool at home on Boxing Day 2026?

No. Liverpool are away at Hull City on Saturday 26 December 2026, with a 5.30pm kick-off on Sky Sports. Everton are at home to Sunderland that day.

What are Liverpool's home games in December 2026?

Sunderland (Wed 2 Dec, 7.30pm), Porto in the Champions League (Wed 9 Dec, 8pm), Leeds (Sat 12 Dec, 3pm) and Tottenham (Sat 19 Dec, 5.30pm), as reported in October 2026.

When do Everton play at home over Christmas 2026?

Everton host Sunderland on Boxing Day, Manchester City on Tuesday 29 December at 8.15pm and Aston Villa on Tuesday 5 January 2027 at 7.30pm. Everton v Fulham is on the weekend of 5 and 6 December.

Can I just buy tickets for these games?

Liverpool home tickets are sold through the club's membership schemes and are very hard to get. Everton tickets can be easier for some games. Only buy from official club sales. See our LFC and Everton ticket guide.

How far is Zermatt Lodge from Anfield and Hill Dickinson Stadium?

It's a 4-bedroom house in Liverpool, about 15 minutes by train from the city centre. For both grounds, Sandhills is the station to use. Everton fans walk from there, and the matchday Soccerbus runs from Sandhills to Anfield.
