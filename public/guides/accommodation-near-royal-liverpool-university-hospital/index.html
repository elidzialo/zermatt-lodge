Visiting the Royal Liverpool University Hospital: A Practical Guide for Families

Visiting someone at the Royal Liverpool University Hospital? Car parks and charges, Blue Badge drop-off, the nearest stations and where families can stay.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

The Royal Liverpool University Hospital (satnav postcode L7 8YE) has limited short-stay parking on site, with the Q-Park on Prescot Street and Paddington Village car park both within a 10-minute walk. Liverpool Central and Lime Street stations are about a mile or less away on foot. For longer visits, a self-catering house on the Merseyrail network with free parking can be cheaper and calmer than a city hotel.

If someone you love is in the Royal Liverpool University Hospital, the practical side should be the easy part. The short version: use the Q-Park on Prescot Street or the Paddington Village car park, or come by train to Liverpool Central or Lime Street, both a mile or less on foot. If you're visiting for more than a day or two, think about where you'll sleep, eat and wash clothes. A self-catering house on the Merseyrail network can be calmer than a city-centre hotel, and free parking at the house cuts your costs.

Getting there

By car

Use the postcode L7 8YE for satnav. The hospital lists two main car parks:

Car park Postcode Hours Spaces
Q-Park multi-storey, Prescot Street L6 1LY 6am to 10pm daily 479, with 20 disabled bays and 10 EV charging points
Paddington Village, Elm Grove L7 3FA 24 hours 1,249, with 53 disabled bays and 13 EV charging points

Both are within a 10-minute walk of the hospital, and shuttle buses run from both for people who find walking difficult. Note that the Q-Park closes at 10pm, so for late visits or overnight stays Paddington Village is the 24-hour option.

Drop-off and Blue Badge

  • Drop-off is at the Mount Vernon Street and West Derby Street entrances.
  • Blue Badge holders park free at the Mount Vernon Street entrance. Spaces are limited, so allow extra time.

By train and bus

  • Liverpool Central (Northern and Wirral lines): less than a mile on foot
  • Liverpool Lime Street (main line): about a mile
  • Queen Square bus station: less than a mile on foot

Walking access is via Prescot Street and West Derby Street.

Parking charges

For the limited short-stay parking on site, the hospital lists:

Time Charge
Up to 30 minutes Free
30 minutes to 2 hours £4.50
2 to 24 hours £10.00

Liverpool University Hospitals (UHL), which runs the Royal, Aintree, Broadgreen and Liverpool Women's, also lists a 7-day visitor pass at £22.50 across its sites, and concessions for parents of babies on the Neonatal Unit, patients with cancer or long-term conditions, and people facing long clinic delays. Ask at the hospital which car parks the pass and concessions apply to.

Long visits: practical tips

  • Pace yourself. Visiting every day for weeks is tiring. Take turns if family can share.
  • Eat properly. A kitchen lets you cook real meals instead of living on hospital cafe food.
  • Wash clothes. A washer and dryer helps on long stays, for you and for the patient's things.
  • Register with a GP if you're staying for weeks. See our temporary resident GP guide.
  • Kosher meals: Merseyside Jewish Community Care says kosher meals are available on request at all Merseyside hospitals. If there's a problem, ask the ward or contact the local community directly.

More ideas in our long-stay hospital visiting tips.

Where to stay

Hotels near the Royal are in the city centre, where parking costs extra. Another option is a house: take the train into Liverpool Central and walk, and leave the car at the house for free. If you're also visiting The Clatterbridge Cancer Centre Liverpool, it's on the same city-centre campus area.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where do I park for the Royal Liverpool University Hospital?

The hospital lists the Q-Park multi-storey on Prescot Street (L6 1LY, open 6am to 10pm, 479 spaces) and Paddington Village car park on Elm Grove (L7 3FA, open 24 hours, 1,249 spaces). Both are within a 10-minute walk, and shuttle buses run from both for people who find walking difficult.

How much is parking at the Royal Liverpool Hospital?

For the limited short-stay parking on site the hospital lists: up to 30 minutes free, 30 minutes to 2 hours £4.50, and 2 to 24 hours £10. Liverpool University Hospitals also lists a 7-day visitor pass at £22.50 across its sites. Ask the hospital which car parks it covers.

Is there free Blue Badge parking?

Yes. The hospital says Blue Badge holders park free at the Mount Vernon Street entrance, but spaces are limited and you may be sent to the Q-Park if they're full.

Which train station is nearest?

Liverpool Central (Northern and Wirral lines) is less than a mile on foot, and Liverpool Lime Street is about a mile. From Zermatt Lodge, the train into Liverpool Central is straightforward.

Can patients get kosher meals?

Merseyside Jewish Community Care says kosher meals are available on request at all Merseyside hospitals. If there is a problem, ask the ward or contact the local community directly.
