Anfield Match Day Accommodation: A Stress-Free Base for Liverpool FC Games

Match day accommodation for Anfield: a 4-bed house in Fazakerley with free parking. How to reach Anfield via Sandhills and the Soccerbus, plus tips.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

For a Liverpool FC match, stay just outside the Anfield parking zone near a Merseyrail station that links to the matchday buses. Zermatt Lodge in Fazakerley is a short train ride from Sandhills, where the Soccerbus runs to Anfield, sleeps up to 7 and has free parking for two cars.

For a Liverpool FC match, the most practical place to stay is often just outside the Anfield parking zone, near a Merseyrail station that links to the official matchday buses. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Fazakerley, north Liverpool, near Fazakerley station. From there it's a short train ride to Sandhills, where the Soccerbus runs to Anfield. The house sleeps up to 7 and has free parking for two cars, so you can leave the car behind on match day.

Here's how to plan match day from a north Liverpool base, using Liverpool FC's own travel advice.

Why you shouldn't drive to Anfield

Liverpool FC is clear about this:

  • There's no paid parking at Anfield on match days.
  • Anfield is in a residents' parking zone. Enforcement is active and illegally parked cars will be fined.
  • Accessible drop-off points are on Gilman Street (near the Kop and Main Stand) and Oakfield Road.

Many visiting supporters waste an hour circling for a space and end up with a ticket. Leaving your car at your accommodation and using public transport is far less stressful. For more detail, see our Liverpool match day parking guide.

Getting to Anfield from Fazakerley

Option 1: Train to Sandhills plus the Soccerbus

This is usually the simplest route.

  1. Catch a Merseyrail Northern line train from Fazakerley towards Liverpool.
  2. Get off at Sandhills, a few stops down the line. The Kirkby branch runs Fazakerley, Rice Lane, Kirkdale, Sandhills.
  3. Take the Soccerbus from Sandhills to Anfield. Liverpool FC says it runs for Premier League and cup matches from two hours before kick-off, with the last bus about 15 minutes before kick-off.

After the match: return Soccerbuses run for about 90 minutes, with the last departure from Walton Lane about 75 minutes after the final whistle. Then take the Northern line back to Fazakerley.

Option 2: Walk from Kirkdale

Liverpool FC signposts walking routes to Anfield from Lime Street, Sandhills and Kirkdale. Kirkdale is also on the Kirkby line, one stop before Sandhills. It's a good option if your group likes to walk in and soak up the atmosphere.

Option 3: Via the city centre

If you're starting the day in town, the 917 express runs from Commutation Row (near Lime Street) about every 10 minutes on match days. Liverpool FC lists fares of £2.30 adult single and £4.60 adult return or day ticket. Other routes include the 26 from Liverpool ONE Bus Station and the 17 from Queen Square. After the match, the 501 Peoplesbus runs from Walton Lane to Lime Street for up to two hours.

Option 4: Taxi

There are designated ranks outside the stadium and on main routes. Liverpool FC quotes a typical £8 to £10 from Lime Street. The Taxi One taxibus runs from Commutation Row from £3 per person. On a busy match day, a taxi from Fazakerley may still get held up in traffic near the ground.

Planning your match day

Morning

Have a big breakfast at the house, then head off in good time. The Soccerbus starts two hours before kick-off, and the queue builds. Aim to arrive at Anfield at least an hour early. That gives you time to see the Shankly Gates and the statues, and get through security.

Pre-match

Stanley Park, between Anfield and Goodison Park, is a popular meeting point. Liverpool FC also runs a free attended Cycle Hub at the top of Stanley Park Car Park if anyone in your group cycles.

Evening kick-offs

Midweek European nights and evening kick-offs finish late. Check the last Northern line trains to Fazakerley before you go. Trains run less often in the evening, and much less on Sundays. See our guide to European nights at Anfield.

Weather

Anfield can be cold and windy, especially in the evening. Bring layers and a waterproof jacket.

Sample timeline for a 3pm kick-off

  • 11:30 Breakfast at the house
  • 12:30 Train from Fazakerley to Sandhills
  • 13:00 Soccerbus to Anfield (it starts two hours before kick-off)
  • 13:15 to 14:30 Walk round the ground, food, meet friends in Stanley Park
  • 14:30 Through the turnstiles
  • 17:00 Queue for the return Soccerbus or walk to Kirkdale
  • 18:00 Back at the house for dinner and the late game

Away fans and visiting supporters

If you're an away fan, check your club's travel advice and the away-end arrangements. Many travelling supporters stay over after evening kick-offs rather than tackling a long drive home late at night. See our away fans' guide to Anfield.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I park at Anfield on match days?

No. Liverpool FC says there's no paid parking at Anfield on match days. The stadium is in a residents' parking zone with active enforcement, and illegally parked cars will be fined.

What's the easiest way to get to Anfield from Fazakerley?

Take the Merseyrail Northern line from Fazakerley to Sandhills, a few stops towards the city, then use the matchday Soccerbus from Sandhills to Anfield. It runs from two hours before kick-off until about 15 minutes before.

How do I get back after the match?

The Soccerbus runs back to Sandhills for about 90 minutes after the game, with the last departure from Walton Lane about 75 minutes after the final whistle. From Sandhills, take the Northern line back to Fazakerley. Check the last trains for evening kick-offs.

How much is the 917 shuttle bus to Anfield?

Liverpool FC lists the 917 express from Commutation Row at £2.30 for an adult single and £4.60 for an adult return or day ticket. Child fares are £1.20 single and £2.30 return.

Is it better to stay in Anfield itself?

It depends. Staying right by the ground means you can walk in, but streets are packed on match days and parking is restricted. A base a short train ride away, with your own off-street parking, is calmer and often better for groups and families.
