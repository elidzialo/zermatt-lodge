Offshore Wind Crew Accommodation on Merseyside

Accommodation for offshore wind technicians and crews working from Merseyside. Burbo Bank's Birkenhead base, project news, what crews need and a 4-bed house.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Ørsted runs the Burbo Bank and Burbo Bank Extension offshore wind farms from an operations and maintenance base at Kings Wharf, Birkenhead, across the Mersey from Liverpool. Crews doing campaign work, surveys or turbine maintenance need short or medium stays with parking for vans, a washer and dryer, and quiet bedrooms for early starts. A whole house shared by the crew is often cheaper than hotel rooms.

The Irish Sea off Merseyside has working offshore wind farms, with more projects planned. Technicians, survey teams, vessel crews and specialist contractors come and go on campaigns that can last a week or several months. They all need somewhere practical to sleep, eat and wash their kit.

We checked the details below on 23 November 2026.

Offshore wind on the Mersey

  • Burbo Bank and Burbo Bank Extension are run by Ørsted from an operations and maintenance base at Kings Wharf, Birkenhead, overlooking the Mersey
  • The base was completed in August 2017, a £6.7m project with a warehouse plus offices, changing and welfare space for 45 full-time staff, according to Robertson, which built it
  • Mona (1.5 GW) is being taken into full ownership by JERA Nex bp after EnBW withdrew, offshorewind.biz reported in January 2026. Its sister project Morgan is being discontinued. Neither won a Contract for Difference in Allocation Round 7

Project timelines change, so check with your client for the latest on any campaign.

What offshore crews need from accommodation

  1. Parking for vans and cars. Kit and PPE go everywhere with you
  2. A washer and dryer. Wet weather gear and workwear need washing and drying every day
  3. Quiet bedrooms. Early mobilisations and weather-dependent shifts mean odd hours
  4. A proper kitchen. Long days on the water need a big breakfast and a hot meal at night. See our self-catering guide for work crews
  5. Flexible dates. Weather windows move, so ask how the host handles extensions and changes
  6. One invoice. Finance teams want a single invoice in the company's name. See our invoice and VAT guide

Liverpool or the Wirral?

The Birkenhead base is on the Wirral side of the Mersey. Staying in Liverpool gives your crew more choice of houses, shops and food, with the Mersey tunnels or Merseyrail to cross the river. Check tunnel tolls and journey times for your crew's start time before you book.

If your work is at the shipyard, see our Cammell Laird contractor guide.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is the Burbo Bank operations base?

Robertson, which built it, says Ørsted's O&M facility is at Kings Wharf, Birkenhead, overlooking the Mersey. It serves Burbo Bank and Burbo Bank Extension and was completed in August 2017.

What happened to the Mona and Morgan wind farms?

In January 2026 offshorewind.biz reported that JERA Nex bp was taking full ownership of the 1.5 GW Mona project after EnBW withdrew, and that the Morgan project was being discontinued. Neither had won a Contract for Difference in Allocation Round 7.

Can a crew book a whole house?

Yes. Zermatt Lodge sleeps 7 in 4 bedrooms, with 2 bathrooms, a washer and dryer and free parking for 2 vehicles. Stays run from 2 to 30 nights, with monthly rates.

Can we get an invoice in the company's name?

Is the house suitable for shift workers?

It's a quiet residential house with separate bedrooms. Agree quiet hours within the crew if shifts overlap.
