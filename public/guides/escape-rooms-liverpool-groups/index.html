Escape Rooms in Liverpool for Groups

Booking an escape room in Liverpool for a group? Which venues to look at, why groups of 6 or 7 split across two rooms, versus games, age rules for teens and booking tips.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Liverpool has several escape room venues, including Breakout Liverpool (14 themed rooms, 10am to 10pm daily), Tick Tock Unlock, Escape Hunt and Clue Finders. Rooms are built for small teams (Breakout takes 2 to 5 players per room), so a group of 7 usually books two rooms, or a 'versus' game where two copies of the same room race each other. Games last about 60 minutes. Under-16s normally need an adult with them.

An escape room is one of the easiest group activities to book in Liverpool. It's indoors, it's an hour long, nobody needs to be sporty and it works for mixed ages. The only catch for a bigger group is room size, so here's how to plan it.

Room sizes: why a group of 7 splits

Most rooms are built for small teams. Breakout Liverpool takes 2 to 5 players per room and says 3 or 4 is ideal. For a group of 6 or 7 you have two options:

  1. Two rooms at the same time. Split into a 4 and a 3, play different rooms, and compare times after
  2. A versus game. Two copies of the same room, two teams, one winner. Great for families (parents v kids) or friends who like a bit of competition

Book both rooms in one go so you start together.

Ages and rules

  • Breakout Liverpool: no set minimum age, but puzzles are mainly designed for adults. Under-16s need one adult per room. Under-7s play free but count towards the player total
  • Other venues: rules vary by venue and room, so check age limits when you book

Timing and price tips

  • Games last 60 minutes. Allow extra time for the briefing and photos
  • Off-peak is cheaper. At Breakout, Monday to Friday before 5pm costs less, and bigger groups pay less per person
  • Students get discounts on some weekdays at Breakout
  • Weekends book up, especially in school holidays, so book a week or more ahead

Accessibility

Ask before you book. Breakout Liverpool says its centre is in a basement with 13 steps and isn't wheelchair accessible.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can 7 people do an escape room together in Liverpool?

Usually not in one room. Breakout Liverpool, for example, takes 2 to 5 players per room. A group of 7 can split into a 4 and a 3 and book two rooms at the same time, or book a versus game.

What is a versus escape room?

A venue has two copies of the same room, and two teams race to escape first. Breakout Liverpool, Tick Tock Unlock, Escape Hunt and Clue Finders all have versus games.

How long does an escape room take?

The game itself is usually 60 minutes. Allow extra time for the briefing before and photos afterwards.

Can teenagers do escape rooms?

Yes, but rules vary by venue. At Breakout Liverpool, under-16s need one adult per room, and under-7s play free but count towards the player total.

Are Liverpool escape rooms accessible?

It varies. Breakout Liverpool says its centre is in a basement with 13 steps and isn't wheelchair accessible. Ask each venue before you book.
