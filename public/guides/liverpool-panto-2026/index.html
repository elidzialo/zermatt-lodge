Liverpool Panto 2026: Pantomimes and Christmas Shows Across the City

Liverpool panto 2026: Beauty and the Beast at the Everyman, Cinderella at the arena, Snow White at the Epstein, Mamma Mia, and dates, prices and travel.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Liverpool's main pantos for 2026 are the Rock 'n' Roll Panto Beauty and the Beast at the Everyman (14 November 2026 to 16 January 2027, £20 to £47), Cinderella at the Auditorium, M&S Bank Arena (12 to 27 December) and Snow White at the Epstein Theatre (5 December to 3 January). For grown-ups, the Royal Court has The Scouse Nativity, and the Empire has Mamma Mia from 8 December.

Liverpool's biggest pantos for Christmas 2026 are Beauty and the Beast (the Everyman's Rock 'n' Roll Panto), Cinderella at the Auditorium at M&S Bank Arena, and Snow White at the Epstein Theatre. For adults, the Royal Court has The Scouse Nativity, the Playhouse has Little Shop of Horrors, and the Empire hosts Mamma Mia over Christmas.

Here's every show, with dates and prices where they've been published. We checked on 19 November 2026. Some weekend performances are already sold out, so book soon.

The pantos

Beauty and the Beast: The Rock 'n' Roll Panto 2026 (Liverpool Everyman)

  • Dates: 14 November 2026 to 16 January 2027
  • Prices: £20 to £47
  • Running time: about 2 hours 20 minutes, including an interval
  • Good to know: "Contains haze, flashing lights, and loud noises"
  • Access performances: captioned on Sat 5 December at 7pm, audio described on Wed 9 December at 7pm, BSL on Fri 18 December at 7pm, and a relaxed performance on Thu 14 January 2027 at 1.30pm

The Everyman's rock 'n' roll panto has a live band of actor-musicians and a loyal following. When we checked, some evenings were already sold out, including Fri 4 December and Mon 21 December at 7pm.

Cinderella (The Auditorium, M&S Bank Arena)

  • Dates: 12 to 27 December 2026
  • Age: "Suitable for all ages." Strobe lighting and pyrotechnics are used
  • No shows: 14, 18 and 25 December
  • School matinees: 10am and 1.15pm on 15 to 17 December
  • Meet and greet packages: £60 (Silver) or £65 (Gold), with front-two-row seats, a signed poster and a post-show meet and greet
  • Cast includes: Bippo as Buttons, Rebecca Lake as Cinderella, Neil Jones as Prince Charming from 20 December, and The Queen of Scotty Road as Dandini

Standard ticket prices weren't listed on the venue page, so check Ticket Quarter. The page shows two different start times for the evening of Monday 21 December, so confirm yours before you book.

Snow White (The Epstein Theatre)

  • Dates: Saturday 5 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027
  • Cast includes: Ryan Mulvey
  • Prices and times: not published on the listing we checked. Use the official ticket link

The Epstein is a small, traditional theatre in the city centre, which suits younger children who might find a big arena overwhelming.

Christmas shows for grown-ups

The Scouse Nativity (Liverpool's Royal Court)

  • Dates: 6 November 2026 to 16 January 2027
  • Prices: from £19

The Royal Court's Christmas shows are comedies written for adult audiences, full of local humour. If you're bringing teenagers, check the age guidance with the box office first.

Little Shop of Horrors (Liverpool Playhouse)

  • Dates: 3 December 2026 to 9 January 2027
  • Prices: £17 to £62

Mamma Mia (Liverpool Empire)

  • Dates: 8 December 2026 to 3 January 2027
  • Prices: via ATG Tickets

Just before it, Elf The Musical is at the Empire from 19 to 22 November.

Choosing the right show

  • Under-5s: Cinderella (all ages, but note the pyrotechnics) or Snow White at the smaller Epstein
  • Families with older kids: the Everyman rock 'n' roll panto
  • Adults only: The Scouse Nativity at the Royal Court
  • Musical fans: Mamma Mia or Little Shop of Horrors
  • Needing an access performance: the Everyman has the clearest published list

Getting to the theatres

From Fazakerley, direct Merseyrail trains reach Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes. From there:

  • Liverpool Empire: opposite Lime Street station
  • Royal Court, Playhouse and Epstein: short walks from Central
  • Everyman: Hope Street, an uphill walk from Central
  • M&S Bank Arena: on the waterfront at Kings Dock. Walk from Central, or take the Wirral line one stop to James Street

Evening shows finish late, so check the last train before you go, or book a taxi back.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the Everyman panto in 2026?

The Rock 'n' Roll Panto 2026 is Beauty and the Beast, at Liverpool Everyman from 14 November 2026 to 16 January 2027. Tickets are £20 to £47 and it runs about 2 hours 20 minutes with an interval.

Is the Royal Court Christmas show suitable for children?

The Royal Court's Christmas shows are usually aimed at adults. The 2026 show, The Scouse Nativity, runs from 6 November 2026 to 16 January 2027 with tickets from £19. Check the age guidance with the theatre before booking for under-16s.

Which Liverpool panto is best for young children?

Cinderella at M&S Bank Arena says it's suitable for all ages, and Snow White at the Epstein Theatre is a traditional family panto. The Everyman's rock 'n' roll panto suits families too, but it uses haze, flashing lights and loud noise.

Are there relaxed or BSL panto performances in Liverpool?

Yes. The Everyman lists a BSL performance on Friday 18 December at 7pm, a captioned show on Saturday 5 December at 7pm, an audio-described show on Wednesday 9 December at 7pm and a relaxed performance on Thursday 14 January 2027 at 1.30pm.

How do I get to the Liverpool theatres by train?

The Empire is opposite Lime Street. The Playhouse, Royal Court and Epstein are a short walk from Liverpool Central or Lime Street. The Everyman is on Hope Street, an uphill walk from Central.
