Another Place on Crosby Beach: A Visitor Guide

Visiting Antony Gormley's Another Place on Crosby Beach? 100 iron men over 3km of sand, when to go for the tide, mud safety, the three car parks and the nearest Merseyrail stations.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Another Place is Antony Gormley's 100 cast-iron figures spread over more than 3km of Crosby Beach, north of Liverpool. It's free and open all the time, but check the tide first: at high tide the figures are covered and you can't get on the beach. Stay near the promenade, as Sefton Council warns of soft sand and mud near the furthest statues. Park at Hall Road, Crosby Leisure Centre or by Crosby Lakeside, or take Merseyrail to Hall Road, Blundellsands and Crosby or Waterloo.

Another Place is one of the best free things to see near Liverpool. Antony Gormley's iron men stand along Crosby Beach looking out to sea, some up to their knees in sand, some half covered by the tide. It's a short trip from the city and works in any season, as long as you check the tide and dress for the wind.

We checked the details below on 24 November 2026.

What it is

  • 100 cast-iron figures by Antony Gormley
  • Spread across more than 3km (about 2 miles) of sandy beach
  • Arrived in 2005 and is now permanent
  • Free, on a public beach

Check the tide first

This is the one thing to plan. At high tides of around 9 to 10 metres, the figures are submerged and you can't get onto the beach. Sefton Council links to tide times on its website. Aim to arrive around low tide for the most figures and the most beach.

Stay safe on the sand

Sefton Council's advice:

  • Walking near the promenade is generally safe
  • Don't walk out to the furthest statues. There's soft sand and mud
  • Obey warning signs and follow lifeguard, coastguard and ranger instructions
  • Don't feed the birds, as it can disturb them

With children, keep to the figures nearest the prom. The photos are just as good.

Parking

Sefton Council lists:

Car park Notes
Hall Road car park, Blundellsands, L23 8SY Closest to Another Place
Crosby Leisure Centre car park, Mariners Road, L23 6SX Also listed by the council
Two car parks by Crosby Lakeside Adventure Centre, off Cambridge Road, Waterloo, L22 1RR Also listed by the council

By train

Merseyrail's Northern Line runs about every 15 minutes:

  • Hall Road: about 5 minutes' walk
  • Blundellsands and Crosby: about 5 to 10 minutes
  • Waterloo: about 10 to 15 minutes

A nice walk is to get off at Waterloo, walk along the prom past the figures and catch the train back from Hall Road or Blundellsands and Crosby.

Tips for a winter visit

  • Sunset is early in winter, so plan an afternoon visit around the light as well as the tide
  • It's exposed. Bring a windproof coat, hat and gloves
  • Wellies or old boots if you want to walk on the sand
  • Combine it with a walk at Formby. See our Formby guide and winter walks near Liverpool

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many statues are on Crosby Beach?

There are 100 cast-iron figures, spread across more than 3km (about 2 miles) of the beach.

When is the best time to see Another Place?

Around low tide, when more of the figures are uncovered. At high tides of around 9 to 10 metres the figures are submerged and you can't get onto the beach. Check tide times before you go.

Is it safe to walk out to the statues?

Walking near the promenade is generally safe. Sefton Council advises against going out to the furthest statues because of soft sand and mud, and asks visitors to obey warning signs and follow lifeguard, coastguard and ranger instructions.

Where do I park for Another Place?

Sefton Council lists Hall Road car park in Blundellsands (closest to the statues), Crosby Leisure Centre car park on Mariners Road, and two car parks next to Crosby Lakeside Adventure Centre off Cambridge Road in Waterloo.

Can I get the train to Crosby Beach?

Yes. Hall Road station is about 5 minutes' walk, Blundellsands and Crosby about 5 to 10 minutes, and Waterloo about 10 to 15 minutes. Merseyrail Northern Line trains run about every 15 minutes.

How much does it cost to see Another Place?

Nothing. It's a free, permanent artwork on a public beach.
