Things to Do in Liverpool with Teenagers This Winter

Things to do in Liverpool with teenagers this winter: ice skating at ICEBOX and Skate St George's, the Cathedral Tower, stadium tours, escape rooms, karting and cheap train travel with Day Savers.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Teenagers usually love Liverpool's ice rinks (ICEBOX at BOXPARK runs to 10 January 2027, with junior tickets for 13 to 17 year olds from £10), the Liverpool Cathedral Tower (two lifts and 108 stairs, £8 or £22.50 for a family), Anfield and Everton stadium tours, escape rooms and go-karting. A Merseyrail Day Saver for 5 to 18 year olds is £2.95 for all areas, so they can get around cheaply.

Keeping teenagers happy on a family trip is mostly about giving them some choice and a bit of independence. Liverpool is good for that: it's compact, the trains are cheap for under-19s, and there's plenty to do that isn't a museum (though the museums are good too).

We checked prices and dates on 25 November 2026. Check again before you book.

On the ice

  • ICEBOX at BOXPARK Liverpool (Baltic Triangle): open daily 16 November 2026 to 10 January 2027. Adult and junior tickets (13 to 17) from £10, with skate hire and 45 minutes on the ice.
  • Skate St George's and the Pier Head rink are the other big winter options.

Our ice skating guide compares all three.

Up high

The Liverpool Cathedral Tower is a good one for teens who like a view. You go up two lifts and then 108 stairs. Tickets are £8 standard or £22.50 for a family (2 adults and up to 3 children). See our cathedrals guide for hours, which are shorter in late November and early December.

Football

Even non-fans tend to enjoy a stadium tour. See our Anfield stadium tour guide, or the Everton stadium guide for Hill Dickinson Stadium.

Games and challenges

  • Escape rooms: a good rainy-day activity for a family team. See our escape rooms guide.
  • Go-karting: check minimum ages and heights. See our go-karting guide.
  • Competitive socialising: crazy golf, darts, bowling and more. See our competitive socialising guide.
  • Curling at Liverpool ONE: Stone's Throw Social at Chavasse Park runs from 3 October 2026 to 3 January 2027, with eight ice-free curling lanes. Check age rules when you book.

Food and shopping

  • Street food at Baltic Market, BOXPARK or Duke Street Market. See our food markets guide.
  • Liverpool ONE for shops. See our Liverpool ONE Christmas shopping guide.

Getting around on their own

A Merseyrail Day Saver for 5 to 18 year olds is £2.95 for all areas. It isn't valid between 06.31 and 09.29 on weekdays. Our getting around guide explains Tap and Go contactless too.

Tips

  • Let them plan one day. Give them a budget and a train map.
  • Agree a meeting point in the city centre, like a station entrance.
  • Evening venues may be 18+, so check before you go.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What can teenagers do in Liverpool in winter?

Ice skating, the Liverpool Cathedral Tower, stadium tours at Anfield, escape rooms, go-karting, competitive socialising venues, street food in the Baltic Triangle, and shopping at Liverpool ONE.

How much is ice skating for teenagers at ICEBOX?

BOXPARK lists adult and junior (13 to 17) tickets from £10, with skate hire and 45 minutes on the ice included. ICEBOX is open daily until 10 January 2027.

How much is a train day ticket for a teenager?

A Merseyrail Day Saver for 5 to 18 year olds is £2.95 for all areas or £2.20 for one area. It isn't valid between 06.31 and 09.29 on weekdays.

Are venues in the Baltic Triangle OK for teenagers?

Many are fine in the daytime, but some bars and venues become 18+ in the evening. Check each venue's age policy before you go.
