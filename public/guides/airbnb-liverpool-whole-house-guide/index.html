Airbnb in Liverpool: A Guide to Booking a Whole House

Booking a whole-house Airbnb in Liverpool? Where to stay, what fees and AirCover mean, the party ban, parking and the questions to ask before you pay.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

For a whole-house Airbnb in Liverpool, decide first between the city centre (walkable, little parking) and the suburbs (more space and free parking, a short train ride in). Compare the total price including fees, check the bed layout and house rules, and know that Airbnb's party ban is permanent. Many hosts also take direct bookings, which can avoid platform fees.

A whole-house Airbnb is often the easiest way for a family or group to stay in Liverpool: everyone under one roof, a kitchen, and usually more space per pound than hotel rooms. The key decisions are where in the city to stay, what the total price really is, and whether the house rules fit your trip. Here's how to get those right.

Step 2: compare the total price

The nightly rate isn't the full story.

  • Airbnb fees: under Airbnb's single-fee model, used by many professional hosts, a 15.5% service fee is taken from the host's price, so hosts usually build it into their rates. Under the split-fee model you see a guest fee at checkout
  • Cleaning fees vary a lot between listings
  • Parking: in the city centre this can add a daily cost
  • Food: a kitchen saves money compared with eating out every meal

Many hosts also take direct bookings, which can avoid platform fees. Our book direct vs Airbnb guide covers how to do that safely.

Step 3: read the house rules

  • Parties: Airbnb made its global ban on disruptive parties and events permanent in June 2022. Guests who break it can be suspended or removed from the platform. Many hosts add their own strict rule
  • Occupancy: the number of guests is a limit, not a guide
  • Check-in and checkout times, especially for late arrivals or early flights
  • Smoking and pets

Step 4: check the beds and bathrooms

"Sleeps 8" can mean four doubles or two doubles and two sofa beds. Look for:

  • The actual bed list, room by room
  • Number of bathrooms: two makes a big difference for a group of 6 or more
  • Stairs and accessibility if anyone has mobility needs

Step 5: know what protection you have

Airbnb says AirCover for guests helps with rebooking or a full or partial refund if a host cancels before check-in, can't be reached, or the listing is significantly different from what was advertised, such as no heating in winter or fewer bedrooms than listed. Airbnb also says AirCover isn't an insurance policy and doesn't cover travel issues like delays. If you book direct, pay by card and check the host's terms.

Questions to message the host

  1. Where exactly can we park, and how many cars?
  2. How long is the walk to the nearest station?
  3. Is there a washing machine and dryer?
  4. Can we check in early or check out late?
  5. Is the house suitable for children or older guests?

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is the best area for an Airbnb in Liverpool?

It depends on the trip. The city centre suits short, walkable stays but parking is scarce and costly. North Liverpool suits football, Aintree and groups with cars. South Liverpool suits Sefton Park and the Jewish community in Childwall and Allerton.

Does Airbnb allow parties in Liverpool?

No. Airbnb made its global ban on disruptive parties and events permanent in June 2022. Guests who break it can face penalties up to removal from the platform, and many Liverpool hosts have their own no-parties rule as well.

What does AirCover cover for guests?

Airbnb says AirCover for guests helps with rebooking or a full or partial refund if a host cancels before check-in, can't be reached, or the listing is significantly different from what was advertised. It's not an insurance policy and doesn't cover travel problems like delays.

Is it cheaper to book a Liverpool house direct than on Airbnb?

Often, because no platform commission is built in. Compare the total price for the same dates, and only book direct with a card on a secure site.

Is there a limit on Airbnb nights in Liverpool?

No. The 90-night limit applies only in London. A national short-let registration scheme for England is expected from March 2027.
