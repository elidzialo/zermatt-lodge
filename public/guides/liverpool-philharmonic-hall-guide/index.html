Liverpool Philharmonic Hall: A Visitor Guide

Planning a night at Liverpool Philharmonic Hall? History, the Music Room, Spirit of Christmas 2026 dates, practical tips and a group house to stay in.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Liverpool Philharmonic Hall is on Hope Street and is home to the Royal Liverpool Philharmonic Orchestra. The current Art Deco hall opened in 1939, seats about 1,700, and has a smaller Music Room for 180 to 250 people. Its big Christmas concert, Spirit of Christmas, is listed for 18, 19, 22 and 23 December 2026.

Liverpool Philharmonic Hall is one of the best places in the city for a special night out. It's home to the Royal Liverpool Philharmonic Orchestra, but the programme goes well beyond classical music, with film concerts, folk, comedy and family shows. In December it's at its busiest, with a full Christmas season.

We checked the details below on 23 November 2026.

The hall at a glance

  • Where: Hope Street, in the Georgian Quarter, between the city's two cathedrals
  • Opened: the current hall opened on 19 June 1939. The first hall on the site opened in 1849 and burned down in July 1933
  • Architect: Herbert J. Rowse, in the Streamline Moderne (Art Deco) style
  • Listed: Grade II*
  • Capacity: about 1,700 in the main hall
  • The Music Room: a smaller venue for 180 to 250 people, opened in October 2015 after a renovation of about £14 million

Christmas 2026 at the Phil

It features the Royal Liverpool Philharmonic Orchestra, the Royal Liverpool Philharmonic Choir and the Liverpool Philharmonic Youth Choir. Check start times when you book.

In past years the Christmas season has also included family concerts, Messiah, film screenings and a New Year's Eve show. The 2026 line-up is on the Phil's official Christmas page, so check there for exact dates.

Tips for your visit

  1. Book early for December. Christmas concerts are the busiest of the year, and Saturday nights go first
  2. Eat on Hope Street. The street has plenty of restaurants. Book a table before the show
  3. Combine it with the cathedrals. Both cathedrals are at either end of Hope Street. See our Everyman and Playhouse guide for the theatre next door
  4. Getting there. Liverpool Central is the nearest Merseyrail station, a walk uphill. Taxis are easiest on a cold night
  5. Coming as a group? Book seats together early, especially for the family concerts

Make it a weekend

Pair a concert with the Christmas markets, skating at St George's Hall (see our St George's Hall guide) or a Christmas show. Our December 2026 events guide has the full month.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When did Liverpool Philharmonic Hall open?

The current hall opened on 19 June 1939. It replaced the original 1849 hall, which was destroyed by fire in July 1933.

How many people does the Philharmonic Hall hold?

The main hall holds about 1,700. The Music Room, opened in 2015, holds 180 to 250.

When is Spirit of Christmas 2026?

Bachtrack lists the Royal Liverpool Philharmonic Orchestra's Spirit of Christmas at Philharmonic Hall on 18, 19, 22 and 23 December 2026, with the Royal Liverpool Philharmonic Choir and Youth Choir. Check times with the Phil.

Is the building listed?

Yes. The hall, designed by Herbert J. Rowse, is Grade II* listed.

Are there other Christmas concerts?

The Phil runs a full Christmas season of concerts, family shows and film screenings. Check the official Christmas page for the 2026 programme, as dates change each year.
