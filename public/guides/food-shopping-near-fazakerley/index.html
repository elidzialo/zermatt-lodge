Food Shopping near Fazakerley: A Guide for Self-Catering Guests

Where to do the big shop for a self-catering stay in north Liverpool: Lidl Fazakerley, Asda Aintree's long hours, Sunday trading rules and Christmas Day closures.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

For a big shop near Fazakerley, Lidl on Longmoor Lane (listed 8am to 10pm Monday to Saturday, 10am to 4pm Sunday) and Asda Aintree on Ormskirk Road (open 24 hours Tuesday to Friday, with click and collect and a 24-hour petrol station) are the main options. Large shops in England can only open for six hours on Sundays, between 10am and 6pm, and must close on Christmas Day and Easter Sunday, so plan arrival-day shopping around that.

A self-catering stay works best when the first shop is sorted. Fazakerley and Aintree, in north Liverpool, have a discount supermarket and a large superstore close together, plus smaller shops for top-ups. This guide covers where to go, when they open, and the Sunday and bank holiday rules that catch visitors out.

Opening times change, especially around holidays. We checked these listings on 22 November 2026; confirm on the store's own site before you travel.

The big shop

Lidl Fazakerley

  • Where: Longmoor Lane, L9 9BY
  • Listed hours: Monday to Saturday 8am to 10pm, Sunday 10am to 4pm
  • Good for: a cheap first shop for a group, bakery items and basics

Asda Aintree

  • Where: Ormskirk Road, L10 3LN (a superstore)
  • Listed hours: Monday 6am to midnight, Tuesday to Friday open 24 hours, Saturday until 10pm, Sunday 10.30am to 4.30pm
  • Also on site: a 24-hour petrol station, pharmacy, drive-thru click and collect, a café and a cash machine
  • Good for: late arrivals, a full weekly shop, or collecting an online order on the way in

Top-up shops

Listing sites show smaller convenience stores on Longmoor Lane for milk, bread and snacks. Check they're still trading before you walk over. Smaller shops aren't bound by the Sunday six-hour rule, so they can be handy on a Sunday evening.

The rules that catch people out

Under the law for England and Wales, set out on GOV.UK:

  • Large shops are those over 280 square metres
  • On Sundays, they can open for 6 consecutive hours between 10am and 6pm
  • They must close on Christmas Day and Easter Sunday

So if you arrive on a Sunday afternoon, the big supermarkets may already be closed. Over Christmas, shop by Christmas Eve. If your stay starts on Easter Sunday, shop on the Saturday.

Planning a shop for a group

  1. Write a meal plan first. Our meal plan for a group of 7 has a ready-made list
  2. Book delivery or click and collect for arrival day, so you're not shopping tired
  3. Split the shop. One person does breakfasts and basics, another does dinners
  4. Check the kitchen. Ask the host what's provided (oil, salt, foil, bin bags) so you don't buy it twice
  5. Bring cool bags if you're driving a long way with fresh food

Special diets

If you need kosher food, supply in Liverpool is limited, so plan ahead. See our kosher food in Liverpool guide. Supermarkets may stock some certified products, but check the hechsher on each item.

Work crews

Crews on early shifts usually shop once a week and top up in between. A big fridge and freezer make a difference. See our self-catering guide for work crews.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What supermarkets are near Fazakerley?

Lidl Fazakerley on Longmoor Lane, and Asda Aintree, a superstore on Ormskirk Road. Listing sites also show smaller convenience stores on Longmoor Lane. Check opening times before you go.

Is there a 24-hour supermarket near Fazakerley?

Asda Aintree on Ormskirk Road lists 24-hour opening from Tuesday to Friday, 6am to midnight on Monday, until 10pm on Saturday and 10.30am to 4.30pm on Sunday. Its petrol station is listed as open 24 hours.

What time do supermarkets open on Sundays?

Large shops in England (over 280 square metres) can open for 6 consecutive hours between 10am and 6pm on Sundays. Smaller convenience stores can open longer.

Are supermarkets open on Christmas Day?

No. Large shops must close on Christmas Day and Easter Sunday in England. Shop the day before.

Can I get groceries delivered to a holiday rental?

Usually, yes. Book a supermarket delivery slot for your arrival time and make sure someone is in. Asda Aintree also offers drive-thru click and collect.
