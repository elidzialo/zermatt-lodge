Competitive Socialising in Liverpool: Where to Take a Group

Crazy golf, darts, bowling and shuffleboard for groups in Liverpool: Junkyard Golf, Golf Fang, One Under, Boom Battle Bar, Flight Club, Roxy Ball Room, Pins and Gravity MAX.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

For crazy golf in Liverpool, try Junkyard Golf Club at Liverpool ONE, Golf Fang (formerly Ghetto Golf) at the Cains Brewery in the Baltic Triangle, One Under near Bold Street or Boom Battle Bar near St Peter's Square. For darts there's Flight Club at Liverpool ONE. For bowling, Roxy Ball Room, Pins Social Club on Duke Street and Gravity MAX. Age rules vary by venue and time of day, so check before you book with under-18s.

"Competitive socialising" means bars and venues built around a game: crazy golf, darts, bowling, shuffleboard. They're ideal for groups because there's something to do besides drink, and they work for friends, families with older kids and work teams.

We checked these venues on 24 November 2026. Prices and opening times change, so check before you book.

Outdoor options

  • Otterspool Adventure Centre, by the river in Aigburth: a family-focused 12-hole course, plus a petting farm
  • Championship Adventure Golf, New Brighton: 18 holes across two courses, with river views. Adult tickets from £7.50 for one course

Darts

  • Flight Club, Liverpool ONE: social darts
  • Gravity MAX, Liverpool ONE: AR darts among many other games

Bowling

  • Roxy Ball Room: bowling, beer pong and arcade games, with several city-centre venues
  • Pins Social Club, Duke Street: bowling, karaoke, table tennis and live music
  • Gravity MAX: AR bowling

Everything under one roof: Gravity MAX

Gravity MAX at Liverpool ONE is a two-floor venue with e-karting, AR darts, AR bowling, street golf, VR, shuffleboard, batting cages and an arcade, plus food and bars. It's a good choice for a mixed group, as people can split up and meet again for food. For more on the karting, see our go-karting guide.

Tips for booking a group

  • Book ahead for Friday and Saturday nights
  • Check age rules. Bar venues set their own, and they can be stricter in the evening. Daytime slots are the safer bet with teenagers
  • Pick one venue with several games if your group has mixed interests
  • Combine with an escape room for a full day
  • Agree the budget before you go, as games and drinks add up

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where can you play crazy golf in Liverpool?

Junkyard Golf Club at Liverpool ONE (three themed courses), Golf Fang at Cains Brewery in the Baltic Triangle, One Under near Bold Street, Boom Battle Bar near St Peter's Square and Urban Street Golf at Gravity MAX. Outdoors, there's Otterspool Adventure Centre and Championship Adventure Golf in New Brighton.

Is Ghetto Golf still open in Liverpool?

The Baltic Triangle course at Cains Brewery is now called Golf Fang. It was previously Ghetto Golf.

Where can you play darts in Liverpool?

Flight Club at Liverpool ONE is the best-known social darts venue. Gravity MAX has AR darts, and Boom Battle Bar has other bar games such as axe throwing and shuffleboard.

Can children go to these venues?

Some, at certain times. Gravity MAX's street golf has smaller clubs for children aged 4 and over, and Otterspool Adventure Centre is family-focused. Bar-based venues set their own age rules, which can change in the evening, so check each venue's policy.

Which is best for a group of 7?

Somewhere with several games under one roof, such as Gravity MAX, Boom Battle Bar or Roxy Ball Room, so the group can split and swap. Book ahead for weekends.
