Family Holiday Homes in Manchester: Where to Stay with Children

Family holiday homes in Manchester and Prestwich: whole houses with cots, high chairs, gardens and parking, plus free and easy days out with children this winter.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

A whole house is the easiest way to stay in Manchester with children: separate bedrooms, a kitchen for fussy eaters, a washer and usually a garden and parking. Book Homes has family houses in Prestwich and Crumpsall in north Manchester, several listing a cot or high chair, close to Heaton Park and the Metrolink Bury line into the city. Manchester Museum on Oxford Road is free, and LEGOLAND Discovery Centre and SEA LIFE are side by side near the Trafford Centre.

Hotels and toddlers don't mix well. One room, one bathroom and a 7pm bedtime means the adults spend the evening whispering in the dark. A whole house fixes that: children in their own rooms, a kitchen for the meals they'll actually eat, a washer for the inevitable spills and, usually, a garden and a drive.

Book Homes has 8 whole homes in Greater Manchester, sleeping 4 to 12. See them on the Manchester hub.

Where to stay

  • Prestwich. Quiet streets of family houses next to Heaton Park, with Metrolink trams from Prestwich and Heaton Park stops into the city. Several of the homes on Book Homes here have gardens and free parking. See homes in Prestwich.
  • Crumpsall. A large 5-bedroom house with a pool table and free parking, good for families travelling together.
  • Salford. Smaller homes close to the city centre. See homes in Salford.

What to check for children

  1. Cot and high chair. Several of the Manchester listings on Book Homes include them. Confirm with the host, and ask if bedding for the cot is provided.
  2. Stairs and gates. Ask about stair gates if you have a crawler.
  3. Bath. Many listings have a bath, which matters for bedtime routines.
  4. Garden. Ask whether it's enclosed.
  5. Parking. Kids come with a lot of luggage. A drive at the door helps.
  6. Bed list. Check exactly who sleeps where before you book.

Easy days out with children

  • Heaton Park. Next to Prestwich. Manchester City Council says it is open from 8am to dusk every day. Our Heaton Park visitor guide covers the trams, buses and car parks.
  • Manchester Museum. On Oxford Road, and free to enter, with no booking for the general galleries. It's closed on Mondays. Over Christmas it says it is closed 24 to 28 December and 1 January.
  • LEGOLAND Discovery Centre and SEA LIFE Manchester. Next door to each other at the Trafford Palazzo, by the Trafford Centre. Adults can only enter LEGOLAND Discovery Centre with a child. See our LEGOLAND and SEA LIFE guide.
  • Christmas light trails. Heaton Park, Dunham Massey and RHS Garden Bridgewater all run trails this winter. See Christmas light trails 2026.
  • Christmas markets. Free to enter in the city centre. See Manchester Christmas Markets 2026.

School holiday dates

Manchester City Council lists the Christmas holiday from Friday 18 December 2026 (schools close) to Monday 4 January 2027 (schools reopen), and February half term from Friday 12 February to Monday 22 February 2027. Homes book up quickly around these dates.

House rules

Strictly no parties or events - bookings found to be for parties are cancelled. Family homes on quiet streets work best for families who want a quiet base.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are there family homes with cots in Manchester?

Yes. Several Book Homes listings in Greater Manchester list a cot and some a high chair. Check the amenities on each listing and confirm with the host.

What is free to do with kids in Manchester?

Manchester Museum on Oxford Road is free to enter and its general galleries need no booking. Heaton Park, next to Prestwich, is open from 8am to dusk every day, according to Manchester City Council.

When are the Manchester school holidays this winter?

Manchester City Council lists schools closing on Friday 18 December 2026 and reopening on Monday 4 January 2027, and February half term from Friday 12 February to Monday 22 February 2027. Some academies and church schools differ.

Is Manchester Museum open over Christmas?

The museum says it is closed 24 to 28 December and on 1 January, and open 10am to 5pm on 23 and 29 to 31 December. Check its website before you go.
