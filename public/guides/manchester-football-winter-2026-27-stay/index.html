Football in Manchester This Winter: Home Games, Trams and Where to Stay

Manchester City and Manchester United home games from December 2026 to February 2027, the trams to the Etihad and Old Trafford, and whole homes for groups of fans.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

From December 2026 to February 2027, the published fixture lists give Manchester City 7 home league games at the Etihad and Manchester United 8 at Old Trafford, including United v Liverpool on Saturday 23 January and City v Arsenal on Saturday 30 January. Dates and kick-offs often move for TV, so check the clubs' sites. The Etihad has its own Metrolink stop, Etihad Campus, and Wharfside on the Trafford Park line is opposite Old Trafford. A whole home with parking near a tram stop is an easy base for a group.

Manchester has two of the biggest football grounds in the country, and this winter both clubs have a busy run of home games. Here are the home league dates, how to get to each ground by tram, and where a group can stay.

Getting to the Etihad

  • The ground has its own Metrolink stop, Etihad Campus. Manchester City says it's especially useful on match days.
  • The club says trams run every six minutes Monday to Saturday and every seven and a half minutes on Sundays, and suggests touching in and out with a contactless card to skip the ticket machine queue.
  • The club also lists park and ride sites with direct trams, including Ashton Moss and Ashton West.

Getting to Old Trafford

  • Wharfside, on the Metrolink Trafford Park line, is opposite the stadium.
  • Old Trafford stop, on the Altrincham line, is next to the cricket ground and a short walk from the football ground.
