Dunham Massey: A Winter Visitor Guide

Visiting Dunham Massey near Altrincham this winter: park, garden and house opening times, 2026 prices, the Christmas after-dark trail, dogs, the 280 bus and family homes nearby.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Dunham Massey (Woodhouse Lane, Altrincham, WA14 4SJ) is a National Trust estate with over 300 acres of deer park. In late November 2026 the park opens 9am to 4pm and the garden 10am to 3.30pm, and parking is included in the ticket. Park and garden tickets are £11 for an adult (£12.10 with Gift Aid), children 5 to 17 pay half and under-5s are free. Christmas at Dunham Massey, an after-dark trail, runs from 25 November 2026 to 2 January 2027, 4.30pm to 8.30pm.

Dunham Massey is a big National Trust estate on the edge of Altrincham, in the Trafford borough of Greater Manchester. In winter it's all about the deer park: long, flat walks under old trees, with a herd of fallow deer. In December, the after-dark Christmas trail makes it an evening trip too. We checked these details on the National Trust website on 25 November 2026.

Where it is

Woodhouse Lane, Altrincham, WA14 4SJ.

Opening times

On a typical late November day in 2026 the National Trust lists:

Area Open
Park 9am to 4pm
Garden 10am to 3.30pm
House 10am to 3pm (see the note below)
Car park 9am to 4.15pm
  • Last admission is 45 minutes before closing.
  • No pre-booking is needed for a normal day visit.
  • The house: the National Trust page is inconsistent, and one part says the house is closed for cleaning and conservation until 15 February 2027. Check the opening times calendar if seeing inside matters to you.
  • Christmas: from 19 November to 2 January the garden closes at 3.30pm, last entry 2.45pm.

Prices (from 9 March 2026)

Ticket Adult
Park and Garden £11.00 (£12.10 with Gift Aid)
House, Park and Garden £20.00 (£22.00 with Gift Aid)

Children aged 5 to 17 pay half price and under-5s are free. Parking is included in both tickets. The main car park has 12 EV chargers.

The deer park

  • More than 300 acres of ancient parkland with a herd of fallow deer.
  • Please don't feed or touch the deer.
  • A mini park loop is about 1.8 miles, which suits young children and grandparents.

Christmas at Dunham Massey 2026

  • Christmas at Dunham Massey, the illuminated after-dark trail: 25 November 2026 to 2 January 2027, 4.30pm to 8.30pm. It's ticketed.
  • A Trail of Tails: Christmas in the House, a children's trail: 25 November to 24 December, 10am to 3pm.
  • Christmas at the Stables Restaurant: lunches from 25 November to 18 December.

See our Christmas light trails guide to compare it with Heaton Park and RHS Bridgewater.

Dogs

Dogs are welcome in the parkland and North Park during opening hours, and in the gardens from midday. Keep them on a short lead in the gardens and parkland. Well-behaved dogs can be off the lead in the North Park.

Getting there without a car

  • Altrincham and Hale stations are each about 3 miles away. Altrincham is at the end of the Metrolink Altrincham line.
  • The hourly 280 bus from Altrincham stops at the entrance gate, about 10 minutes away.
  • Show an on-the-day bus or train ticket for 10% off in the café and restaurant.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you have to pay to park at Dunham Massey?

Parking is included in the Park and Garden and the House, Park and Garden tickets. National Trust members should bring their card.

Can you take dogs to Dunham Massey?

Yes, in the parkland and North Park during opening hours, and in the gardens from midday. Keep them on a short lead in the garden and parkland; well-behaved dogs can go off lead in the North Park. Don't let them near the deer.

How do I get to Dunham Massey without a car?

Altrincham and Hale stations are each about 3 miles away. The National Trust says the hourly 280 bus from Altrincham stops at the entrance gate, about 10 minutes' ride.

Is the house at Dunham Massey open in winter?

The National Trust page gives mixed information, with one section saying the house is closed for cleaning and conservation until 15 February 2027. Check the opening times calendar before you go.
