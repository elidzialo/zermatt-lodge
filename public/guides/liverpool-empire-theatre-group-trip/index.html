Liverpool Empire Theatre: How to Plan a Group Trip

Planning a group trip to the Liverpool Empire? ATG group booking rules, what's on from December 2026 to spring 2027, seating tips and a house for up to 7.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

The Liverpool Empire on Lime Street seats about 2,350 and has the largest two-tier auditorium in the UK. Its operator ATG offers group booking for groups of 10 or more (sometimes 6 or more), with tickets held without a deposit until everyone has paid. Shows from December 2026 include Mamma Mia, Back to the Future, Matthew Bourne's Cinderella, Heathers and Mrs Doubtfire. A group of up to 7 can share one house in north Liverpool, about 15 minutes by train from the city centre.

A theatre trip is one of the easiest group weekends to organise in Liverpool. The Liverpool Empire is the city's biggest theatre, it takes the big touring musicals, and it sits right in the city centre. Here's how the group booking works, what's on over winter and spring 2027, and how to keep the rest of the weekend simple.

We checked show dates on 22 November 2026. Always confirm on the ATG site before you book travel.

The theatre at a glance

  • Where: on the corner of Lime Street and London Road, close to Lime Street station
  • Size: about 2,350 seats (Wikipedia gives 2,348), with the largest two-tier auditorium in the UK
  • Run by: ATG Entertainment, part of the Ambassador Theatre Group since 2011
  • History: the current building opened in 1925 and is Grade II listed. The Beatles played here, last on 5 December 1965, and it hosted the 2007 Royal Variety Performance

How ATG group booking works

ATG's groups service covers groups of 10 or more, and for some shows groups as small as 6. In summary:

  • Discounts vary by show. ATG gives examples of group prices on selected shows, so ask for the current offer on yours
  • No deposit to hold seats. ATG says you can hold tickets without commitment or deposit until all your group have confirmed and paid
  • How to book: groups of 10 or more submit a request through ATG's online form. A request isn't a booking, and ATG says to call if you haven't heard within 5 working days
  • Schools have separate rates, and some shows include free teacher places

If your group is under six, book standard tickets together as early as you can to get seats in one row.

Planning tips for groups

  1. Pick the show first, then the date. Short runs like Cinderella (five days) sell out fastest
  2. Choose matinee or evening early. A Saturday matinee leaves the evening free for dinner together
  3. Collect money before you book. ATG lets you hold seats while people pay, so set a deadline
  4. Check access needs when you book. ATG lists access performances and access information on the venue pages
  5. Eat before or after, not both. City centre restaurants near Lime Street get busy before curtain up

Getting there and back

Liverpool Central and Lime Street are both a short walk from the theatre. If you're staying in north Liverpool:

  • Fazakerley to Liverpool Central: about 15 minutes direct on Merseyrail
  • Last trains: on a sample day the last train from Central to Fazakerley left at 23.36. Check the timetable on the night, especially for a long show
  • Tap and go: Merseyrail accepts contactless cards and phones, so nobody needs a paper ticket

Driving into town for a show means paying for city centre parking. Leaving the cars at the house and taking the train is usually simpler.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the Liverpool Empire offer group discounts?

ATG, which runs the Empire, has a groups service for groups of 10 or more, and in some cases 6 or more. Discounts depend on the show. You can hold tickets without a deposit until everyone in the group has confirmed and paid.

How many seats does the Liverpool Empire have?

About 2,350. Wikipedia gives 2,348 and describes it as the largest two-tier auditorium in the United Kingdom.

What's on at the Liverpool Empire in early 2027?

ATG lists Mamma Mia to 3 January, Matthew Bourne's Cinderella (16 to 20 February), Heathers (23 to 27 February), Mrs Doubtfire (2 to 21 March) and Chicago (24 to 27 March), among others. Back to the Future's official site lists the Empire from 12 January to 6 February 2027.

Where is the Liverpool Empire?

On the corner of Lime Street and London Road in Liverpool city centre, close to Lime Street station.

How do I get back from a late show if I'm staying outside the city centre?

From the Empire, walk to Liverpool Central for Merseyrail. Trains from Central to Fazakerley take about 15 minutes, and the last train on a sample day left at 23.36. Check the timetable on the night.
