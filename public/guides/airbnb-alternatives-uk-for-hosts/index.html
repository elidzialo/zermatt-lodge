Airbnb Alternatives for UK Hosts: Where Else to List Your Home

Airbnb alternatives for UK hosts in 2026: Booking.com, Vrbo, your own site and niche marketplaces like Book Homes. Fees, calendar sync and who books.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Most UK hosts who move beyond Airbnb keep it and add one or two more channels: Booking.com, Vrbo, a niche marketplace such as Book Homes, or their own booking site. The key is calendar sync, so a booking on one site closes the dates on all the others.

You do not need to leave Airbnb to stop relying on it. Most UK hosts who list elsewhere keep Airbnb and add one or two more channels, then connect the calendars so a booking anywhere closes the dates everywhere. This guide covers the main options. We run Book Homes, so we are not neutral, but the facts below are sourced.

Why hosts add a second channel

  • Fees changed. Airbnb now charges most hosts a single 15.5% host-only fee. See Airbnb host fees UK 2026 explained.
  • One platform is a single point of failure. A paused listing, a search ranking change or a bad month leaves you with nothing else.
  • Different guests search in different places. Families, contractors and observant guests often look beyond the biggest sites.

How to choose

  1. Start with where your guests already are. If many of your guests are work crews or big families, a marketplace that filters for those helps more than another general site.
  2. Keep it manageable. Two or three channels with synced calendars is plenty for a single home.
  3. Compare the take-home, not the headline fee. Include card fees and any guest fee that makes your total look higher.
  4. Keep your listing details the same everywhere. Same photos, same house rules, same cancellation terms, so nothing contradicts.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I list my home on Airbnb and another site at the same time?

Yes. Airbnb offers calendar import and export (iCal) so you can connect other sites. Keep every calendar connected both ways to avoid double bookings.

Which Airbnb alternative is best for UK hosts?

It depends on your guests. Booking.com has a large audience, Vrbo suits families and whole homes, your own site keeps repeat guests, and niche marketplaces reach guests with specific needs, such as kosher-friendly homes on Book Homes.

Do I need a channel manager?

Not for two or three channels. iCal links are enough for most single-home hosts. A channel manager helps once you run several homes or want prices to update everywhere at once.
