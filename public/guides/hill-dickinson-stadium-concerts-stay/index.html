Concerts and Big Events at Everton's Hill Dickinson Stadium: Travel and Stays

Are there concerts at Everton's Hill Dickinson Stadium? What's confirmed for 2027 and 2028, how to get there by train and shuttle bus, and where to stay.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Here's the honest answer: as of 20 November 2026, no concert at Hill Dickinson Stadium had been announced. Everton has applied for licences to stage live events and has said it wants to host outdoor concerts. A September 2026 update noted that "no performers have been linked to the stadium". The big non-football dates that are confirmed are the Liverpool Half Marathon start on Sunday 14 March 2027 and five Euro 2028 matches in June 2028. Whatever the event, the easiest way there is Merseyrail to Sandhills, about a 15-minute walk, or a shuttle bus.

We'll update this page when a gig is confirmed. In the meantime, here's what we know and how to plan.

The stadium in brief

  • Home of Everton men's team at Bramley-Moore Dock on the north Liverpool waterfront
  • Capacity: 52,769 for football
  • The Plaza: an outdoor space on the Regent Road side that can hold up to 17,000 people
  • Non-match events so far: the Rugby League Ashes Test in autumn 2025, Super League's Magic Weekend on 4 and 5 July 2026, and conferences and exhibitions

Concerts: where things stand

Licensing. Local reporting in December 2025 said Everton had applied to Liverpool City Council for a premises licence covering the stadium area and fan plaza, allowing live events until 11pm, including alcohol sales. A similar bid for the stadium bowl was made in summer 2025. At the time, no date had been set for the council to consider the application.

Ambition. The club has said it will stage "outdoor concerts and events from 2025 and beyond". Fan site reporting in June 2026, updated in September 2026, described 2027 concert plans as an ambition, but said no performers had been linked to the stadium.

What this means for you. If a stadium gig is announced, expect tickets and nearby accommodation to sell quickly. Stadium concerts usually fall on summer weekends, when Liverpool is already busy. Sign up for alerts from the club and official ticket sellers, and check the cancellation policy before you book a room for an unconfirmed date.

Getting to Hill Dickinson Stadium

By train

  • Sandhills is the closest station, about a 15-minute walk. Bank Hall is also about 15 minutes on foot.
  • On match and event days, Merseyrail usually runs extra and longer trains with a special timetable.
  • The Southport, Ormskirk, Hunts Cross and Headbolt Lane lines all run every 15 minutes through Sandhills on a normal timetable.
  • From Bootle New Strand to Sandhills takes about 6 minutes, says Sefton Council.

By shuttle bus

On football match days, shuttle buses run from about three hours before kick-off and after the game:

All of them stop near Great Howard Street and Derby Road. Check with the event organiser whether shuttles run for non-football events.

By car

There's no on-site parking for general fans, and a match parking zone with strict enforcement operates nearby. Roads around Regent Road, Waterloo Road, the Ten Streets and Sandhills station may close. Sefton Council suggests parking at the Bootle Strand multi-storey (open until 11pm on match days) and taking the shuttle from Stand 4 at Bootle Strand bus station. A football travel guide says Merseyrail offers over 4,400 free parking spaces at more than 40 stations.

Can't wait for a gig? Take the stadium tour

Everton runs guided tours of about 90 minutes, covering the dressing room, players' tunnel, pitchside and the press room. A football travel guide published in October 2026 listed prices of £26 for adults, £23 for seniors and under-22s and £16 for under-14s, with under-4s free. Tours run on selected dates only, so book online in advance.
