Kosher Food in Liverpool: Where to Shop and How to Plan

Where to buy kosher food in Liverpool: the Childwall deli, supervision, and the Manchester shops and bakeries most visitors use. Sourced and up to date.

Kosher food in Liverpool

The Deli, Childwall Abbey Road

Liverpool's Jewish community, about 2,000 people according to local press, lives mostly in Childwall and Allerton, with younger families moving to Aigburth, Cressington and Wavertree. The deli sits in the middle of that community, on Childwall Abbey Road.

Local press reports that the shop, now called The Deli and formerly Roseman's, employs someone to check that its products meet Jewish dietary requirements. Its owner told reporters: "If we weren't here, there'd be a whole generation of older Jews who would struggle to keep kosher."

A note on supervision

Kashrut in Liverpool is overseen by the Liverpool Kashrut Commission. Supply has had difficult years. In June 2019, Jewish News reported that the city's main kosher deli had been caught selling non-kosher meat, and the commission revoked its licence after finding "serious breaches of kashrut". In September 2019 the same paper reported that the shop had reopened under new ownership with a new licence from the commission, three months after the original was revoked.

That was several years ago, and we found no newer reports of problems. Still, because of this history, it's sensible to ask the local rabbi or the shul office about the current supervision before you rely on any shop.

Eating out

We couldn't find a kosher restaurant or takeaway in Liverpool in current sources. If you're visiting for a simcha or a long stay, plan to cook, or arrange catering from Manchester (see below).

Kosher food in Greater Manchester

For most visitors, the practical answer is north Manchester, which has one of the largest Jewish communities in the UK. The Manchester Beth Din publishes a list of licensees, and the London Shechita Board also lists some north west shops.

Licensed by the Shechita Board

The London Shechita Board lists Haydn's Kosher Meats in Whitefield and Kosher Deli on Bury New Road, Prestwich, among its north west licensees.

Older directories

You'll find longer lists online, such as the Chabad "Kosher Manchester" directory. It's useful for ideas, but it says itself that details may be out of date. Use the Beth Din and Shechita Board lists as your main reference.

How to plan your food for a Liverpool stay

Before you travel

  • Bring the basics. Long-life milk, wine and grape juice, challah or flour, and any meat you need for Shabbat.
  • Order ahead from Manchester. Many shops and caterers take orders by phone. Ask whether they deliver to Liverpool or whether you'll need to collect.
  • Bring cool bags and ice packs for the drive.

When you arrive

  • Visit The Deli in Childwall for fresh items, and check opening hours before Shabbat and Yom Tov.
  • Supermarkets may stock some kosher-certified products. Check the hechsher on each item rather than assuming.
  • Ask the shul. Liverpool's synagogues are small, close communities and are usually happy to help visitors with practical questions.

In a rental kitchen

A standard holiday home won't have a kosher kitchen. Many observant visitors bring:

  • Their own pots, pans and cutlery, or disposables
  • Foil or liners for oven shelves and worktops
  • A hot plate or urn for Shabbat

Ask the host about the kitchen before you book, and check our Shabbat-friendly stays guide for questions to ask.

Where to stay

If you're keeping Shabbat, stay within walking distance of the shul you'll use. In Liverpool, that usually means Childwall, Allerton or Wavertree in the south of the city. Start with our kosher homes page, which shows what each host has declared. These are host declarations, not certification.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there a kosher shop in Liverpool?

Yes. The Deli, formerly Roseman's, on Childwall Abbey Road in south Liverpool, is the city's kosher deli. Its owner has said it employs someone to check that products meet kashrut requirements. Check current supervision with the local community before you buy.

Are there kosher restaurants in Liverpool?

We couldn't find any kosher restaurant in Liverpool in current published sources. The nearest cluster of supervised restaurants and takeaways is in Prestwich and Salford, Greater Manchester.

Where is the nearest big kosher shopping area to Liverpool?

North Manchester. The Manchester Beth Din lists licensed grocers, butchers, bakeries and fishmongers in Prestwich, Whitefield and Salford. From Liverpool it is a drive along the M62, so check the journey time in a route planner before you set off.

Who supervises kashrut in Liverpool?

The Liverpool Kashrut Commission is the local authority. In 2019 it withdrew the licence of the main deli after breaches were found, then reissued a licence when the shop reopened under new ownership three months later.

Is Zermatt Lodge kosher?

No. Zermatt Lodge has no kosher kitchen or Shabbat features declared, and it's in north Liverpool, far from the Jewish community in the south of the city. See the kosher homes page for homes where hosts have declared these details.
