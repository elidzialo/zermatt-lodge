Liverpool's Royal Court Theatre: A Visitor Guide

Going to Liverpool's Royal Court? The Scouse Nativity runs 6 November 2026 to 16 January 2027. Cabaret stalls, history, tips and a group house to stay in.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Liverpool's Royal Court is an Art Deco theatre on Roe Street, near Lime Street station, best known for big, funny Scouse comedies. This Christmas it is staging The Scouse Nativity by Kevin Fearon, from 6 November 2026 to 16 January 2027, with tickets from £19. The stalls are set out cabaret style, with tables and chairs.

If you want a night out that feels truly Liverpool, the Royal Court is hard to beat. It's known for loud, warm, very local comedies, and the Christmas show is one of the most popular tickets of the winter. Groups of friends and work teams book it year after year.

We checked the details below on 23 November 2026.

The Royal Court at a glance

  • Where: Roe Street, in the city centre, a short walk from Lime Street station and Queen Square
  • Building: an Art Deco theatre with a nautical-themed interior, opened on 17 October 1938 after a fire destroyed the old theatre
  • Architect: James Bushell Hutchins
  • Listed: Grade II, since 1990
  • Capacity: 1,186 seats
  • Known for: Scouse comedies and musicals, including The Scouse Nativity, The Royal, Lennon and Girls Don't Play Guitars

The cabaret stalls

The Royal Court's stalls aren't rows of seats. They're laid out in three tiers of tables and chairs, cabaret style, with a bar at the back. The stalls seat 290 this way. It's a big part of the fun for groups, as you sit together around a table.

Tips for the stalls:

  • Book together so your group gets the same table
  • Arrive early to get drinks in before the show
  • If anyone in your group needs step-free access or a fixed seat, ask the box office before you book

Christmas 2026: The Scouse Nativity

  • Dates: 6 November 2026 to 16 January 2027
  • Written by: Kevin Fearon
  • Tickets: from £19, via the Royal Court's website
  • The story: a Scouse take on the nativity. It was first staged at the Royal Court in December 2017

The cast hadn't been announced when Arts City Liverpool wrote about it. The listings we found give no age guide, so check before booking for children. For family shows, see our Liverpool panto guide.

Planning a group night

  1. Book early. Friday and Saturday nights in December go first
  2. Eat before. There are plenty of restaurants around Queen Square and Liverpool ONE
  3. Plan the trip home. Taxis are in demand after a Christmas show, so pre-book or use the train
  4. Make a weekend of it. Add the Christmas markets or skating at St George's Hall, just up the road

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's on at the Royal Court this Christmas?

The Scouse Nativity by Kevin Fearon, from 6 November 2026 to 16 January 2027. Arts City Liverpool lists tickets from £19.

What is cabaret seating at the Royal Court?

The stalls are laid out in three tiers of tables and chairs, with a bar at the back. The stalls seat 290 in this format. The rest of the theatre has conventional seats.

How big is the Royal Court?

The theatre has 1,186 seats. The current building opened on 17 October 1938, after a fire destroyed the previous theatre.

Is The Scouse Nativity suitable for children?

The listings we checked don't give an age guide, so check the show page or ask the box office before booking for children.

Where is the Royal Court?

On Roe Street in the city centre, a short walk from Lime Street station and Queen Square bus station.
