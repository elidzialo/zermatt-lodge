Working at Speke or Halewood: Accommodation Options

Work at JLR Halewood, Liverpool John Lennon Airport or the Speke science and pharma sites? Getting there by road and rail, and a house for crews with parking.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Speke and Halewood in south east Liverpool are home to Liverpool John Lennon Airport, JLR's Halewood plant (where JLR announced a £500m upgrade for electric vehicle production in 2024) and a cluster of pharmaceutical sites including Elanco, MedImmune and Novartis. Hunts Cross and Liverpool South Parkway are the nearest stations. Crews who want a quieter base with parking can stay in north Liverpool and drive round on the M57 and M62.

South east Liverpool is one of the city region's main industrial areas. Speke has the airport, a pharmaceutical cluster and business parks; next door, Halewood has JLR's car plant. That means a steady demand for shutdown crews, installers, engineers and visiting specialists. This guide covers the main sites, how to get there, and the trade-off between staying close by and staying in north Liverpool.

We used public sources only, checked on 22 November 2026. Always confirm site access details with your contact.

The main work sites

JLR Halewood

  • A JLR factory in Halewood, Merseyside, originally opened by Ford in the 1960s
  • Builds the Range Rover Evoque and Discovery Sport, according to Wikipedia
  • In September 2024, JLR announced a £500m investment to upgrade the factory for electric vehicle production

Upgrade programmes like this bring installation, M&E and commissioning teams. Shift patterns at a car plant can run around the clock, so a quiet base for daytime sleep matters.

Speke

According to Wikipedia:

  • Liverpool John Lennon Airport sits at the base of the estate
  • A cluster of pharmaceutical facilities, including Elanco, MedImmune and Novartis
  • Estuary Banks, a business park developed from 2011
  • Speke Boulevard (A561) is the main road, linking west to Garston and the city, and east to Widnes and Runcorn

Getting there

By rail

  • Hunts Cross (Merseyrail) is the nearest station to Speke, about a mile away
  • Liverpool South Parkway in Garston is served by Merseyrail plus Avanti West Coast and London Northwestern, which helps visiting staff arriving from elsewhere
  • From Fazakerley, take the Northern line to the city centre and change for the Hunts Cross line. Check the journey in a planner, as it's a cross-city trip

By road

  • From north Liverpool, the M57 runs south to junction 1 (Tarbock), where it meets the M62. From there, routes lead towards Halewood and Speke
  • Speke Boulevard and the airport roads get busy at peak times. Test the drive at your actual shift time

From the airport

If your team is flying in, see our airport to Fazakerley guide. The airport is about 10 miles straight-line from Fazakerley.

Stay close, or stay north?

Base Pros Cons
Hotel near the airport Shortest commute Separate rooms, often no kitchen
South Liverpool rental Close to site Fewer whole houses with parking for crews
North Liverpool house Space, kitchen, free parking, near the M57 Longer drive to Speke and Halewood

A north Liverpool base works best for teams that drive, that split time between sites (for example Halewood and Knowsley), or that want a whole house rather than hotel rooms. If every shift starts at 6am at Speke, a closer base may suit you better. Be honest about the commute before you book.

Booking tips

  1. Send the shift pattern so the host knows people will come and go at odd hours
  2. Count the vehicles and ask about vans
  3. Ask for monthly rates on long programmes
  4. Agree invoicing up front. See invoices and VAT for business stays

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What big employers are in Speke and Halewood?

Liverpool John Lennon Airport, JLR's Halewood plant, and a cluster of pharmaceutical facilities in Speke that Wikipedia lists as including Elanco, MedImmune and Novartis. The Estuary Banks business park has been developed there since 2011.

What does JLR make at Halewood?

Wikipedia lists the Range Rover Evoque and the Discovery Sport. In September 2024 JLR announced a £500m investment to upgrade the factory for electric vehicle production.

What's the nearest station to Speke?

Hunts Cross, on Merseyrail, is about a mile away according to Wikipedia. Liverpool South Parkway in Garston is also close and has Avanti West Coast and London Northwestern services.

How far is Speke from Liverpool city centre?

Wikipedia puts Speke about 8 miles south east of the city centre.

Can I stay in north Liverpool and work in Halewood?

Yes, if you drive. The M57 runs from north Liverpool to the M62, which links towards the south of the city. Check the drive at your shift time, as peak traffic varies.
