Ice Skating in Liverpool This Winter: Every Rink for 2026

Ice skating in Liverpool for Christmas 2026: Skate St George's, Icebox at Boxpark and the Pier Head rink, with dates, prices, hours and trains.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Liverpool has three Christmas ice rinks for 2026: Skate St George's on St George's Plateau (21 November 2026 to 3 January 2027), Icebox at Boxpark Liverpool (16 November 2026 to 10 January 2027, from £8 for children and £10 for adults) and a covered rink at Liverpool Christmas Wonderland at the Pier Head. All are closed on Christmas Day.

Liverpool has three Christmas ice rinks for 2026. Skate St George's is the outdoor rink on St George's Plateau, open 21 November 2026 to 3 January 2027. Icebox at Boxpark Liverpool runs 16 November 2026 to 10 January 2027, with sessions from £8 for children and £10 for adults. And the council has announced a covered rink at Liverpool Christmas Wonderland at the Pier Head. All three are closed on Christmas Day.

Here's how they compare, with times and prices where they've been published (checked 19 November 2026).

Skate St George's (St George's Plateau)

The city's headline rink, in front of St George's Hall and opposite Lime Street station, run by Underbelly.

  • Dates: Saturday 21 November 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027
  • Closed: Christmas Day
  • Opening day: Saturday 21 November, 2pm to 11pm

Opening hours

Period Hours
Until Fri 18 Dec Mon to Thu 12pm to 10pm, Fri 12pm to 11pm, Sat 10am to 11pm, Sun 10am to 10pm
From Mon 21 Dec 10am daily, closing 10pm Sun to Thu and 11pm Fri and Sat
Christmas Eve 10am to 6pm
Boxing Day and New Year's Day 12pm to 10pm

The first skating session starts 15 minutes after opening and the last starts 45 minutes before closing.

Prices and age

Skating prices are shown only on the booking page, so check there. Children of all ages are welcome, but the site recommends they're accompanied by someone aged 16 or over at all times.

What else is there

A Christmas market with local traders, a rooftop bar with mulled wine and hot chocolate, and a Big Wheel. Ride tickets, including the Big Wheel, are bought on the day by card. More in our Liverpool Christmas market guide.

Icebox at Boxpark Liverpool

A smaller rink at Boxpark, the street food and bar venue in the Baltic Triangle area.

  • Dates: 16 November 2026 to 10 January 2027, open daily
  • Closed: Christmas Day
  • Sessions: 45 minutes
  • Prices: children (3 to 12) £8, with an adult and a maximum of two children per adult. Adults and juniors (13 to 17 and 18+) from £10. Students £10 with valid ID
  • Skate hire: included

Opening hours

Hours vary by date. As a guide, Boxpark lists Monday to Wednesday mostly 3pm to 11pm, Thursday 10am to 11pm, Friday and Saturday 10am to 1am, and Sunday 10am to 10.30pm. Christmas Eve is 10am to 10pm. Check the full schedule for your date.

Prices are clear and session lengths are fixed, which makes Icebox easy to budget for with a family.

Liverpool Christmas Wonderland at the Pier Head

Liverpool City Council's 2026 festive programme includes Liverpool Christmas Wonderland @ Pier Head, run by Icebox Live, with a covered ice rink, thrill rides, games and experiences beneath the Three Graces.

We couldn't confirm the opening dates, hours or prices when we checked, and the official website didn't load for us. Check liverpoolchristmaswonderland.com before you go. A covered rink is a good option on wet days.

Not ice, but close: curling at Liverpool ONE

Stone's Throw Social in Chavasse Park, Liverpool ONE, has eight ice-free curling lanes, six karaoke huts and two shuffleboard tables, running 3 October 2026 to 3 January 2027. Walk-ins are accepted, but pre-booking is recommended. It's a good choice for mixed-age groups where not everyone wants to skate.

Which rink should you choose?

  • For the full Christmas atmosphere: Skate St George's, with the market and Big Wheel around you
  • For a clear price and a late session: Icebox at Boxpark, open to 1am on Fridays and Saturdays
  • For wet weather: the covered rink at the Pier Head, once details are confirmed
  • For a group that doesn't skate: curling at Stone's Throw Social

Tips for skating with kids

  • Book ahead for weekends and the week before Christmas
  • Gloves are a must. Falls happen, and the ice is cold
  • Thick socks make hire skates much more comfortable
  • Go early in the day in school holidays, when sessions are quieter
  • Eat before or after. Rink-side food is fun but adds up quickly for a family

Getting there by train

From Fazakerley, direct Merseyrail trains take about 15 minutes to Liverpool Central.

  • Skate St George's: opposite Lime Street station, a short walk from Central
  • Icebox at Boxpark: a walk south from Central towards the Baltic Triangle
  • Pier Head: the nearest Merseyrail stations are James Street and Moorfields

Merseyrail takes contactless: tap in and tap out.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is ice skating at Boxpark Liverpool?

Icebox at Boxpark lists children aged 3 to 12 at £8 (with an adult, maximum two children per adult) and adults, juniors and students from £10. Skate hire is included and sessions last 45 minutes.

When is Skate St George's open?

Every day from Saturday 21 November 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027, except Christmas Day. Until 18 December it's open 12pm to 10pm Monday to Thursday, 12pm to 11pm Friday, 10am to 11pm Saturday and 10am to 10pm Sunday.

Is there a covered ice rink in Liverpool?

Liverpool City Council announced a covered rink at Liverpool Christmas Wonderland at the Pier Head for 2026, run by Icebox Live. We couldn't confirm its dates and prices when we checked, so look at liverpoolchristmaswonderland.com.

Can young children skate at Skate St George's?

The site says it's suitable for children of all ages but recommends they're accompanied at all times by someone aged 16 or over.

Are the ice rinks open on Boxing Day?

Skate St George's opens 12pm to 10pm on Boxing Day. Icebox at Boxpark is open every day of its season except Christmas Day.
