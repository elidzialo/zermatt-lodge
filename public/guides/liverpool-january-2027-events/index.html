What's On in Liverpool in January 2027: A Date-by-Date Guide

Liverpool events in January 2027: New Year's Day Proms, Back to the Future at the Empire, Donny Osmond, the Anfield derby and Champions League nights.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

January 2027 in Liverpool opens with the New Year's Day Proms at the Philharmonic on Friday 1 January and Liverpool v Coventry at Anfield on Saturday 2 January. Back to the Future The Musical runs at the Liverpool Empire from 12 January to 6 February, Donny Osmond plays M&S Bank Arena on 23 January, and the Merseyside derby is at Anfield on Saturday 30 January at 12.30pm.

January is quieter than December in Liverpool, but 2027 has plenty to plan around. The month opens with the New Year's Day Proms at the Philharmonic and Liverpool v Coventry at Anfield. Mid month, Back to the Future The Musical arrives at the Liverpool Empire (12 January to 6 February). And it ends with the Merseyside derby at Anfield on Saturday 30 January, now a 12.30pm kick-off.

We checked everything below on 21 November 2026. Dates and kick-off times change, so confirm with the organiser before you book travel.

New Year week: 1 to 3 January

  • Fri 1 Jan: New Year's Day, a bank holiday in England and Wales
  • Fri 1 Jan, 3pm: New Year's Day Proms, listed by Liverpool Philharmonic, with the Manchester Concert Orchestra. Tickets listed at £26 to £56, plus a 10% fee for online and phone orders
  • Sat 2 Jan, 3pm: Liverpool v Coventry City, Anfield (Premier League). Everton are away at Leeds
  • Sun 3 Jan: last day of Skate St George's on St George's Plateau
  • Sun 3 Jan: last days of the Christmas shows, including Mamma Mia at the Empire and Snow White at the Epstein Theatre. See our Liverpool panto 2026 guide

Week 2: 4 to 10 January

  • Tue 5 Jan, 7.30pm: Everton v Aston Villa, Hill Dickinson Stadium (Sky Sports). Liverpool are away at Sunderland that night
  • Weekend of 9 and 10 Jan: FA Cup third round. Opponents and venues depend on the draw
  • Sat 9 Jan: Little Shop of Horrors at Liverpool Playhouse, listed to run until this date
  • Sun 10 Jan: last day of Icebox at Boxpark Liverpool. See our ice skating guide

Week 3: 11 to 17 January

  • Tue 12 Jan: Back to the Future The Musical opens at the Liverpool Empire, Lime Street, running to Saturday 6 February. Our Back to the Future stay guide covers getting there
  • 12 or 13 Jan: Carabao Cup semi-final first legs, if Liverpool beat Bournemouth in the quarter-final at Anfield in December
  • Sat 16 Jan, 3pm: Liverpool v Crystal Palace, Anfield. Everton are away at Coventry (12.30pm)

Week 4: 18 to 24 January

  • Tue 19 Jan: Inter Milan v Liverpool, Champions League (away)
  • Sat 23 Jan: Donny Osmond at M&S Bank Arena
  • Sun 24 Jan, 2pm: Everton v Brentford, Hill Dickinson Stadium (Sky Sports). This game was moved from Saturday 23 January
  • Sun 24 Jan: Manchester United v Liverpool at Old Trafford (4.30pm)

The last week: 25 to 31 January

  • Wed 27 Jan, 8pm: Liverpool v RC Lens, Champions League, Anfield. See our European nights guide
  • Sat 30 Jan, 12.30pm: Liverpool v Everton, Anfield (TNT Sports). This was moved from 3pm in November 2026

Full kick-off details are in our January 2027 fixtures guide.

What hasn't been announced yet

When we checked, M&S Bank Arena's published January list was short, and venues usually add dates through the winter. We also couldn't find full January 2027 listings for the Philharmonic's own orchestra concerts. Check the venue sites nearer the time.

Planning tips for January

  • Big weekends book early. The derby on 30 January will be the busiest weekend of the month
  • Midweek is cheaper and calmer for theatre and museums
  • Expect cold, wet weather and early sunsets. Plan outdoor walks for the middle of the day
  • TV moves games. Check kick-off times about a month ahead

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's on in Liverpool in January 2027?

The New Year's Day Proms at Liverpool Philharmonic (1 January), Back to the Future The Musical at the Liverpool Empire (12 January to 6 February), Donny Osmond at M&S Bank Arena (23 January), and home games at Anfield and Hill Dickinson Stadium, including the Merseyside derby at Anfield on 30 January.

When is the Merseyside derby in January 2027?

Liverpool v Everton is at Anfield on Saturday 30 January 2027. In November 2026 it was moved to a 12.30pm kick-off for TNT Sports. TV changes can still happen, so check the club site.

Is Skate St George's open in January 2027?

Only at the very start. Skate St George's runs to 3 January 2027. Icebox at Boxpark Liverpool runs to 10 January 2027.

Is New Year's Day a bank holiday in 2027?

Yes. New Year's Day, Friday 1 January 2027, is a bank holiday in England and Wales.

When does Back to the Future The Musical play in Liverpool?

The show's official site lists the Liverpool Empire from Tuesday 12 January to Saturday 6 February 2027.
