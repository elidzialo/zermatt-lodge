Sandhills Station: A Match Day Guide for Liverpool and Everton Fans

Sandhills station explained for Anfield and Everton match days: the Soccerbus, walking to Bramley-Moore Dock, step-free access and trains from Fazakerley.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Sandhills is the key station for football in Liverpool. It's the closest station to Everton's new stadium at Bramley-Moore Dock, about a 15-minute walk away, and it's where the matchday Soccerbus to Anfield starts. It sits on Merseyrail's Northern line just north of the city centre, so you can reach it directly from Liverpool Central, Moorfields, Southport, Ormskirk or Kirkby. Where Sandhills is

Sandhills station is in Kirkdale, a short way north of Liverpool city centre, between the docks and Commercial Road. It opened in 1854 and was refurbished most recently in 2008.

The important thing for fans is where it sits on the network. Sandhills is the junction where Merseyrail's Southport branch meets the Ormskirk and Kirkby branches of the Northern line. That means almost every Northern line train calls here, and trains towards the city run very frequently.

The stations heading into town from the Kirkby side are:

Kirkby - Fazakerley - Rice Lane - Kirkdale - Sandhills - Moorfields - Liverpool Central

Facilities at the station

According to the station's published details:

  • Island platform: two platforms on one island, so you won't go to the wrong side
  • Ticket office, toilets, CCTV and live departure screens
  • Covered seating on the platform
  • Step-free access by lifts and ramps, with a lift straight into the booking office
  • Bike rack with about 10 spaces
  • No car park

The station's capacity was increased in 2024, and a new footbridge opened in 2025, as part of preparing for the new Everton stadium and the wider Liverpool Waters development. Passenger numbers have been rising since 2022/23, and you'll see why on a match day.

Sandhills for Everton (Bramley-Moore Dock)

Everton's Hill Dickinson Stadium is on the waterfront at Bramley-Moore Dock. Fan site Grand Old Team's visitor guide puts Sandhills about a 15-minute walk from the stadium, making it the nearest station.

What to expect:

  • Before the game: a steady stream of fans walking down towards the river. Follow the crowd and the stewards' signs.
  • After the game: queues at the station while crowds are managed. This is normal, so be patient or wait it out.
  • Alternative: some fans walk the 30 minutes into the city centre along the waterfront and pick up a train from Moorfields or Liverpool Central once things have calmed down.

For the full picture, including shuttle buses and parking restrictions, see our Everton stadium accommodation guide and the away fans' guide to Everton's stadium.

Sandhills for Anfield: the Soccerbus

Anfield is too far to walk comfortably from Sandhills, so the Soccerbus runs from outside the station on Liverpool match days. It's run by Stagecoach for the 2025/26 season.

Timings (Stagecoach, 2025/26):

  • Buses start 3.5 hours before kick-off
  • The last bus to the ground leaves 30 minutes before kick-off
  • After the game, the Soccerbus back to Sandhills starts about 10 minutes after the final whistle, picking up at Walton Lane at the junction with Anfield Road, until the crowd has gone

Fares (Stagecoach, 2025/26):

Ticket Price
Adult single £2.00
Adult return £4.00
Young person day (MyTicket) £2.20
Group return for 4 adults £12.00

You can pay by the Stagecoach app, contactless or cash. Merseyrail also sells combined rail and Soccerbus tickets from its booking offices, so ask at the station you start from. Fares change, so check before match day.

If you'd rather walk, Kirkdale station (one stop before Sandhills when coming from Fazakerley) has signposted walking routes to Anfield. It's uphill and takes a while, so allow plenty of time.

Match day tips

  1. Travel early. Trains towards Sandhills get busy in the hour before kick-off. Going earlier also means you can take your time on the walk or bus.
  2. Buy your return before you go. It saves queueing at the machines after the game.
  3. Check engineering works. Merseyrail sometimes replaces trains with buses at weekends. Check the Merseyrail website or app the day before.
  4. Know your train home. From Sandhills, take a train towards Kirkby or Headbolt Lane for Fazakerley, and check the destination on the screen, as Southport and Ormskirk trains use the same platform.
  5. Agree a meeting point. Crowds at the station after the game are big. Pick a spot away from the entrance in case your group gets split up.
  6. Evening kick-offs: trains run less often late at night. Check the last train back before you set off.
  7. Don't drive to Sandhills. There's no car park and matchday parking restrictions cover the streets around both grounds. See our match day parking guide.

Getting to Sandhills from north Liverpool

From Fazakerley, Sandhills is a short, direct ride on the Kirkby branch. Trainline puts the whole trip from Fazakerley to Liverpool Central at about 15 minutes, and Sandhills is the stop before the city centre stations.

That makes north Liverpool a practical base for match weekends. You can leave the cars on the drive, take the train in, and avoid the parking zones altogether. On non-match days the same train takes you into the city centre for sightseeing. Read more in our Fazakerley to city centre train guide.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which stadium is Sandhills station for?

Both. It's the closest station to Everton's Hill Dickinson Stadium at Bramley-Moore Dock, about a 15-minute walk, and it's where the matchday Soccerbus to Anfield starts.

How much is the Soccerbus from Sandhills to Anfield?

Stagecoach lists 2025/26 fares of £2.00 adult single and £4.00 adult return, with a £12.00 group return for 4 adults. Merseyrail also sells combined rail and Soccerbus tickets at its booking offices. Check current fares before you travel.

Is Sandhills station step-free?

Yes. The station has lifts and ramps, with a lift leading into the booking office. It's an island platform, so both directions use the same platform.

Can I park at Sandhills station?

No. There's no car park at Sandhills. Park where you're staying or at a station further out and come in by train.

How do I get to Sandhills from Fazakerley?

Take a Merseyrail Northern line train towards Liverpool Central. Sandhills is a few stops down the line, after Rice Lane and Kirkdale, with no change needed.
