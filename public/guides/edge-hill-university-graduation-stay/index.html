Edge Hill University Graduation: A Family Stay Guide

Edge Hill University graduation week is planned for 19-23 July 2027. Timings, getting to Ormskirk and a 4-bed family house in north Liverpool.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Edge Hill University's 2027 graduation ceremonies are planned for Monday 19 to Friday 23 July 2027 on its Ormskirk campus, and families often find Ormskirk itself has very little accommodation for that week. Staying in north Liverpool, close to where the M58 starts, gives you more choice and an easy drive up to campus. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in north Liverpool, sleeping up to 7, with free parking for two cars: room for the graduate, parents, grandparents and siblings together.

This guide covers the ceremony timings, getting to campus, and how to plan the trip so nobody is rushing on the big day.

Edge Hill graduation 2027 at a glance

  • Dates: Monday 19 to Friday 23 July 2027 (Edge Hill says these are subject to change)
  • Timetable: confirmed in the spring, with ceremonies typically spread across the morning, early afternoon and late afternoon
  • Venue: the main theatre of the Faculty of Health, Social Care and Medicine building, on campus
  • Registration: in the Sports Centre
  • Ceremony length: about one hour
  • Contact: the Ceremonies Team on 01695 657390

How the day works

Edge Hill's own guide sets out a clear sequence. Knowing it helps you plan the morning.

  1. Register in the Sports Centre between two hours and one hour before the ceremony starts.
  2. Collect robes and mortar board from Marston Events, also in the Sports Centre. Collection opens two hours before the ceremony.
  3. Official photographs are taken by Marston Events. Studios open two hours before and close 45 minutes before the ceremony.
  4. Be in the theatre at least 30 minutes early. Late arrivals are not admitted and graduate in absentia unless there are exceptional circumstances.
  5. Return robes no later than one and a half hours after the ceremony ends.

Student and guest tickets are emailed through the Marston Events booking portal about a week before the ceremony. Guests can take photos during the ceremony, but without flash and without leaving their seats.

Work out your departure time

For a 10:30am ceremony, the graduate needs to register no later than 9:30am, and ideally earlier to fit in photos. Allow time to park and walk across campus, and check a live route for the drive. Many families aim to arrive two hours before the ceremony.

Getting to campus

By car

Edge Hill's directions say to leave the M58 at junction 3 and take the A570 towards Southport and Ormskirk. From the junction it's 2.8 miles, about 5 minutes. The M58 begins at Switch Island (M57 junction 7) in north Liverpool, so a north Liverpool base keeps you clear of the city centre entirely.

Edge Hill's graduation pages don't set out guest parking arrangements. Check the information sent with your tickets, or call the Ceremonies Team.

By train

Ormskirk station is on Merseyrail's Northern line. Edge Hill says the walk to campus takes about 20 minutes, along Station Approach, Stanley Street and Knowsley Road to St Helens Road. The EL1 bus runs about every 20 minutes between Ormskirk bus station and campus (not on Sundays or bank holidays). It's free with a student or staff card, and other passengers pay the normal fare.

If you're staying near Fazakerley, you'll need to change trains at Sandhills for the Ormskirk branch, so driving is usually simpler on graduation day, especially with grandparents.

Planning the trip

Book early

Graduation week fills accommodation across West Lancashire and north Liverpool, particularly for larger families. Edge Hill has offered its own campus rooms during graduation week in past years, and these go quickly too.

Think about the whole family

  • Grandparents may want a ground-floor room or a short walk. Ask the university about accessibility on campus.
  • Younger siblings will be sitting through a formal hour-long ceremony. Plan something fun for the afternoon.
  • Dress code is up to you, but a smart outfit and comfortable shoes for walking across campus work well.

Celebrate without the stress

Restaurants in Ormskirk and Liverpool get busy during graduation week. Book a table in advance, or celebrate at your accommodation with a family meal. A big kitchen and dining room make it easy to host a celebration dinner for everyone.

Make a weekend of it

If you're coming from far away, add a day in Liverpool. The waterfront, museums and the Albert Dock are about 15 minutes by train from Fazakerley to Liverpool Central.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is Edge Hill graduation in 2027?

Edge Hill says ceremonies are planned for Monday 19 to Friday 23 July 2027. The dates are subject to change, and the full timetable is confirmed in the spring.

Where are Edge Hill graduation ceremonies held?

On the Ormskirk campus, in the main theatre of the Faculty of Health, Social Care and Medicine building. Graduates register in the Sports Centre first.

How early should we arrive?

Edge Hill says graduates register between two hours and one hour before the ceremony and must be in the theatre at least 30 minutes before it starts. Late arrivals aren't admitted, so build in a generous buffer.

How do you get to Edge Hill from Liverpool?

By car, leave the M58 at junction 3 and follow the A570 towards Ormskirk. The university says it's 2.8 miles and about 5 minutes from the junction. By train, Ormskirk station is about a 20-minute walk from campus.

Is Ormskirk close to Liverpool?

Ormskirk is in West Lancashire, north of Liverpool. Staying in north Liverpool, near the start of the M58, means a straightforward drive up the motorway. Check a live route for your timings.
