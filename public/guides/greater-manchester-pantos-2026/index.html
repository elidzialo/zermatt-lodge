Pantos Around Greater Manchester, Christmas 2026

Christmas 2026 pantomimes and family shows across Greater Manchester: Opera House, The Lowry, Stockport Plaza, Octagon Bolton, Albert Halls and smaller theatres, with dates and family homes nearby.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

The big Greater Manchester pantos for Christmas 2026 are Dick Whittington at the Opera House Manchester (5 December 2026 to 3 January 2027), Jack and the Beanstalk at Stockport Plaza (4 December 2026 to 3 January 2027) and Cinderella at the Octagon Theatre Bolton (from 20 November 2026). The Lowry in Salford has The Jolly Christmas Postman (4 December to 3 January) and A Christmas Carol (11 December to 10 January). Book tickets direct with each theatre.

A panto is the easiest way to give a family Christmas trip a centrepiece. Greater Manchester has one big show in almost every town, plus smaller community productions. We checked these dates on 25 November 2026. Theatres sometimes add or drop performances, so check the exact show you want with the box office before you book travel.

Opera House Manchester

Dick Whittington runs from Saturday 5 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027, with Jason Manford and Ben Nickless. This is the city's biggest panto, so weekend and Christmas week seats go first.

Stockport Plaza

Jack and the Beanstalk runs from Friday 4 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 at the Art Deco Plaza on Mersey Square. There are no shows on Sunday 6 December, Thursday 17 December, Christmas Day or New Year's Day. A family listing gives tickets from £21.45 for under-16s and £26.95 for adults, and lists BSL interpreted shows on Saturday 5 December at 7.30pm and Thursday 10 December at 10.30am and 2.30pm. Stockport is a short train ride from Manchester Piccadilly: see our Stockport visitor guide.

Bolton

Bolton has two big Christmas shows:

  • Cinderella: A Fairy Tale at the Octagon Theatre, opening Friday 20 November 2026. It's a modern version of the story, created by Sally Cookson, Adam Peck and the original company, and directed by Sarah Punshon.
  • Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs at the Albert Halls in December 2026, produced by Shone Productions.

More ideas for the town are in our Bolton visitor guide.

The Lowry, Salford

  • The Jolly Christmas Postman, 4 December 2026 to 3 January 2027. It lasts 1 hour with no interval and is aimed at younger children.
  • A Christmas Carol, 11 December 2026 to 10 January 2027.

The Lowry has no car park of its own. Our Salford Quays guide covers trams and parking.

  • The Sorcerer's Apprentice, HOME, Manchester, 27 November 2026 to 2 January 2027
  • Mr Popper's Penguins, Waterside Arts, Sale, 30 November to 27 December 2026

  • Beauty and the Beast, Forum Theatre, Romiley, 12 to 28 December 2026
  • Aladdin, Heywood Civic, 12 to 27 December 2026
  • Sleeping Beauty, Contact Theatre, Manchester, 16 to 31 December 2026

Tips for a panto trip

  • Matinees suit small children and leave the evening free. They also avoid driving home in the dark.
  • Book seats first, then the stay. Christmas week seats sell out before homes do.
  • Combine it with a light trail. See our Christmas light trails guide.
