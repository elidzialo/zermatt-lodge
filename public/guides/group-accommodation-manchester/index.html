Group Accommodation in Manchester: Whole Houses for Families and Groups

Group accommodation in Manchester and Prestwich: whole houses that sleep 8 to 12 for family visits, weddings, reunions and celebrations, with parking and big kitchens.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

For a group of 6 to 12 in Manchester, one whole house is usually simpler and cheaper than several hotel rooms. Book Homes has Greater Manchester houses that sleep 8, 11 and 12, mostly in Prestwich and Crumpsall in the north of the city, with free parking at most of them and Metrolink trams into the centre. They are for family visits, weddings and reunions, not parties.

Getting a big group together in Manchester, for a family visit, a wedding, a reunion or a milestone birthday, is easier when everyone is under one roof. One house means one booking, one kitchen and one living room where everyone can actually sit together.

Book Homes has 8 whole homes in Greater Manchester. The largest sleep 8, 11 and 12 guests, and most list free parking. See them all on the Manchester hub.

Where the group houses are

  • Prestwich. Several of the larger family houses on Book Homes are here, including 4-bedroom homes for 8 and 12 guests. Prestwich and Heaton Park stops are on the Metrolink Bury line, with trams into Manchester Victoria. Heaton Park itself, which Manchester City Council calls "the biggest and best park in the NorthWest", is next door. See homes in Prestwich.
  • Crumpsall. A 5-bedroom house for up to 11, with a pool table and free parking, also in north Manchester.
  • Salford. Smaller homes close to the city centre. See homes in Salford.

One house or several hotel rooms?

For a group of 8, you'd need four hotel rooms or more. A house gives you:

  • One total price on Book Homes, with no guest booking fees
  • A kitchen, so breakfast and at least some dinners cost supermarket prices
  • Space to be together, which a row of hotel rooms doesn't
  • Parking at most of the homes on Book Homes, rather than paying a city centre car park

Our guide Four hotel rooms or one house? runs through the sums, using Liverpool as the example. The same logic applies in Manchester.

Planning tips for groups

  1. Get the bed list first. "Sleeps 12" may include sofa beds. Decide who sleeps where before you book. Our group booking checklist helps.
  2. Agree the money early. Decide how you'll split the cost and the shopping.
  3. Check parking for every car. Ask how many cars fit on the drive and what the street rules are.
  4. Mind the school holidays. Manchester City Council lists the Christmas break as 18 December 2026 to 4 January 2027, and February half term as 12 to 22 February 2027 (school closes and reopens). Book early for those weeks.
  5. Kosher-friendly needs? Some Manchester hosts declare a kosher kitchen or a Shabbat-friendly set-up. Filter for them on the kosher homes page. These are host declarations, not a certification.

House rules

These are family homes on quiet residential streets. Strictly no parties or events - bookings found to be for parties are cancelled. Hen and stag groups should look elsewhere.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest house on Book Homes in Manchester?

The largest Greater Manchester homes on Book Homes sleep 11 and 12 guests. Check each listing for the bed list and parking.

Can we have a party?

No. Strictly no parties or events. Bookings found to be for parties are cancelled. The homes suit family visits, wedding guests, reunions and quiet celebrations.

Where in Manchester are the group houses?

Mostly in Prestwich and Crumpsall in north Manchester. Prestwich is on the Metrolink Bury line, with trams into Manchester Victoria.

Is a house cheaper than hotel rooms?

Often, once you have four or more rooms' worth of people, and you also save on meals by using the kitchen. Compare the total price with no booking fees against the hotel total including breakfast and parking.
