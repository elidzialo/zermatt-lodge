If your child is being treated at Alder Hey and the whole family needs to stay nearby, a self-catering house within a short drive is often the easiest answer. On-site parent rooms tend to be limited to one or two parents, and siblings and grandparents need somewhere too. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Fazakerley, north Liverpool, a short drive from Alder Hey, with free parking for two cars and space for up to seven people.

This guide covers getting to Alder Hey, parking costs and concessions, and how to keep family life ticking over while your child is in hospital.

Getting to Alder Hey Children's Hospital

Alder Hey is on East Prescot Road, West Derby, Liverpool. The hospital gives two sat-nav postcodes:

  • L14 5AB for the main car park
  • L12 2AP (Eaton Road) for the Emergency Department

Driving from Fazakerley

From Fazakerley, Alder Hey is a cross-suburb drive through north-east Liverpool, usually around 15 to 25 minutes. It can take longer at school-run and rush-hour times, so check a live route before setting off for a timed appointment or ward round.

Buses

Alder Hey lists buses stopping on East Prescot Road or Eaton Road: the 15, 61, 61A, 102, 8, 9, 10 and 10A. Use the Merseytravel journey planner to find the best connection from where you're staying.

Trains

The nearest station is Broad Green, about 1.5 miles away, with services to Liverpool Lime Street, Wigan and Manchester. Most families with a car find driving simpler.

Parking at Alder Hey

Alder Hey has a 24/7 multi-storey car park with 1,184 spaces, including 52 Blue Badge bays and 14 electric vehicle charging points. The height limit is 2.2 metres, so check this if you're driving a tall van or a car with a roof box.

Also worth knowing:

  • Blue Badge holders don't pay.
  • Inpatient overnight stays are free. Ask the ward how this applies to your family.
  • You pay at the pay station in the atrium by cash or card. Entry and exit use number plate recognition.
  • Emergency Department parking is limited pay and display, at £4 per visit.

Frequent-visitor concession

If your child has at least 3 outpatient appointments within a month (30 days), spanning at least 3 months, parking is free. Report to the concierge desk to log your vehicle. Families coming back for regular treatment should set this up as soon as possible.

Keeping the family going during a hospital stay

When one child is in hospital, the rest of the family's life doesn't stop. Families often tell us these things help:

Split the roles

One parent stays at the bedside while the other handles siblings, meals and phone calls to relatives, then swap. Separate bedrooms let whoever did the night shift sleep during the day.

Keep siblings' routines

Brothers and sisters can feel forgotten. Normal bedtimes, a film night, a board game or an hour in a garden all help. If they need a proper day out, Croxteth Hall and Country Park is close to both Fazakerley and West Derby.

Cook proper food

Hospital food and takeaways get old fast. A big kitchen means you can cook simple meals, pack lunches for the ward and cater for fussy eaters.

Let grandparents help

Grandparents can often take on school runs or sibling care if they're staying under the same roof. That's much easier in a house than across two hotel rooms.

Look after the paperwork

Keep a notebook of doctors' names, ward numbers and questions. Photograph discharge letters and medication lists.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there family accommodation at Alder Hey?

Alder Hey's getting-here page doesn't describe on-site family accommodation, so ask the ward or call the hospital on 0151 228 4811. Parent rooms are usually limited, which is why extended families with siblings or grandparents often book a house nearby.

How much is parking at Alder Hey?

Alder Hey lists its multi-storey tariff as free for up to 30 minutes, £3.68 up to 2 hours, £5.25 for 2 to 6 hours, £6.30 for 6 to 8 hours and £8.40 for 8 to 24 hours. Blue Badge holders don't pay, and inpatient overnight stays are free.

What postcode should I use for Alder Hey?

Use L14 5AB for the main car park and L12 2AP (Eaton Road) for the Emergency Department.

How far is Fazakerley from Alder Hey?

It's a short drive across north-east Liverpool, usually around 15 to 25 minutes depending on traffic. Check a live route planner at school-run times.

Is there a parking concession for frequent visits?

Yes. Alder Hey says you get free parking if you have at least 3 outpatient appointments within 30 days and the visits span at least 3 months. Register your vehicle at the concierge desk.
