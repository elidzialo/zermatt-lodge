Winter 2026

Christmas in Liverpool

Liverpool City Council's festive programme for 2026 includes:

  • Skate St George's: a new outdoor ice rink at St George's Hall
  • Liverpool Christmas Wonderland at the Pier Head: a covered ice rink, rides and games beneath the Three Graces
  • Pop-up markets on Basnett Street and in the Cunard Building on selected dates
  • Liverpool City Halls events including A Christmas Carol and Carols by Lantern Light

The council hadn't confirmed every opening date when it announced the programme, so check each event's own website. Our Liverpool Christmas markets guide and Christmas family stay guide go into more detail.

Becher Chase Day at Aintree

Aintree's big winter race day, the Becher Chase, is on Saturday 5 December 2026. It's the chance to see horses race over the famous Grand National fences without the April crowds. See our Becher Chase weekend guide.

The Grand National Festival

This is the biggest weekend of the year for accommodation in north Liverpool. The Jockey Club lists the festival as Thursday 8 to Saturday 10 April 2027: Opening Day on Thursday, Ladies Day on Friday and Grand National Day on Saturday.

Staying near Aintree makes a real difference on these days. Fazakerley is a few miles from the racecourse, and you can leave the car at the house. Read our guides to Grand National accommodation, getting to Aintree from Fazakerley and Ladies Day for groups.

Arena events

M&S Bank Arena on Kings Dock hosts touring shows, comedy, sport and concerts all year. For the big ones, a city-centre hotel can cost more than usual, and parking at the waterfront is limited. Our M&S Bank Arena concert accommodation guide covers getting there and back by train.

Tour de France stage finish (3 July 2027)

This one is a first for Liverpool. Liverpool City Council says stage two of the men's race will start in Keswick, run through Lancashire, Knowsley and St Helens, and finish with a sprint along The Strand on the waterfront on Saturday 3 July 2027. Expect road closures across the city region that day, so trains will be the easiest way to get around. Check the council's travel updates nearer the time.

On the Waterfront and Africa Oyé

On the Waterfront is a summer concert series at the Pier Head, with its sixth edition starting on 17 June 2027. Africa Oyé, a free festival of African and Caribbean music and culture, fills Sefton Park on 19 to 20 June 2027.

Anfield concerts

Anfield has become a big summer concert venue. In June 2026 it hosted Zach Bryan (12 June), Foo Fighters (25 and 27 June) and My Chemical Romance (30 June). No 2027 shows were listed on Visit Liverpool at the time of writing, but announcements usually come months ahead. When they do, read our Anfield concert accommodation guide.

Football all season

Liverpool at Anfield and Everton at the Hill Dickinson Stadium on Bramley-Moore Dock bring visitors almost every weekend from August to May, plus midweek cup and European games. Fixture dates move for TV, so book flexible plans where you can. See our guides to Anfield match days, Everton's stadium and the Merseyside derby.

Where to stay for a big event

City centre

Best if you want to walk everywhere and go out late. The downside is price and availability on big dates, and very limited, expensive parking. Groups usually need several rooms, sometimes in different hotels.

Near the venue

For Aintree, Anfield or Everton, staying close sounds ideal, but options right by the grounds are limited and fill first. Streets around both football stadiums have matchday parking restrictions.

North Liverpool with a train line

This is the middle ground. Fazakerley is on the Merseyrail Northern line, about 15 minutes to Liverpool Central according to Trainline, with Sandhills (for Everton and the Anfield Soccerbus) a few stops down the line. Aintree is a few miles away. You get space, parking and a quieter street, and you don't need to drive into the city.

The honest trade-off: you can't walk back from the city centre, so plan your last train or book a taxi after late finishes.

Tips for event weekends

  1. Book early, then confirm the date. Event dates can move. Read the cancellation terms before you pay.
  2. Check minimum stays. Many hosts ask for two nights or more on big weekends.
  3. Plan your trip home. Trains run less often late at night and on Sundays, and some events change train patterns. During the Grand National, the Jockey Club's travel plan says Kirkby-line trains start and end at Sandhills between about 11:00 and 14:00 and 17:00 and 21:00, so you change there for the city.
  4. Use the car for days out, not the city. Park at your base and take the train for events.
  5. Keep it calm at the house. Event weekends are lively in town, but residential streets aren't.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is the Grand National in 2027?

The Jockey Club lists the 2027 Randox Grand National Festival as Thursday 8 to Saturday 10 April 2027 at Aintree, with the Grand National itself on the Saturday.

Is the Tour de France coming to Liverpool?

Yes. Stage two of the 2027 men's race starts in Keswick and finishes on The Strand in Liverpool city centre on Saturday 3 July 2027, according to Liverpool City Council's announcement.

Are there Anfield concerts in 2027?

At the time of writing, no 2027 Anfield concerts were listed by Visit Liverpool. In 2026 the stadium hosted Zach Bryan, Foo Fighters and My Chemical Romance in June, so watch for summer announcements.

When should I book accommodation for a big Liverpool event?

As early as you can. Hotels close to the venue go first for the Grand National, big concerts and major sport. Staying further out with good train links gives you more choice.
