Accommodation for NHS Placement Students in Liverpool

On an NHS placement in Liverpool? How the Learning Support Fund's Travel and Dual Accommodation Expenses work, nightly caps, deadlines and sharing a house.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

If you're a healthcare student in England getting the NHS Learning Support Fund Training Grant, you may be able to claim temporary accommodation costs for a placement through Travel and Dual Accommodation Expenses (TDAE). The NHSBSA cap is up to £82.50 a night for commercial accommodation such as a hotel or B&B, and you have six months from the end of the placement to claim. Check the TDAE guide before you book anything.

Placements are a big part of nursing, midwifery and allied health courses. If yours is in Liverpool and you live too far away to commute, you'll need somewhere to stay for a few weeks. Before you book, it's worth understanding what the NHS might pay back.

We checked the details below on 23 November 2026. This isn't financial advice. Your university placement team and NHS LSF have the final word.

Travel and Dual Accommodation Expenses (TDAE)

TDAE is part of the NHS Learning Support Fund in England. In short:

  • Who can claim: students who attended a placement as part of their course and are already receiving the Training Grant. Eligible Pharmacy and Healthcare Science students can claim some placement travel or accommodation through TDAE, but not the other LSF allowances
  • What counts: "temporary accommodation you paid for to attend your placement"
  • How to claim: sign in to your NHS Learning Support Fund account, choose the academic year and open the TDAE tab

Nightly caps

Type Cap
Commercial (for example, hotel or B&B) up to £82.50 a night
Non-commercial (for example, a friend or relative, but not parents) up to £37.50 a night

Travel is paid at 42p a mile for driving and 30p a mile for cycling.

Deadlines and payment

  • Claim within six months of the last day of the placement
  • Your university submits the claim, and NHSBSA aims to process it within 20 working days
  • Advance payments: you can ask for one, but only after an online 2026/27 TDAE claim has been approved

Before you book a holiday let, check how it would be treated. The NHSBSA page only gives hotels and B&Bs as examples of commercial accommodation, so read the TDAE guide or ask NHS LSF. Keep every receipt and booking confirmation.

Choosing where to stay

  1. Check the placement site first. Liverpool's hospitals are spread across the city. Our Aintree hospital accommodation guide covers one of the biggest sites
  2. Think about shifts. Long days and nights need a quiet bedroom. See our night shift sleep tips
  3. Parking matters. Early and late shifts don't always line up with trains and buses
  4. Share with coursemates. If several of you are placed in Liverpool at the same time, a whole house split between you can cost less per person than separate rooms. Check how TDAE treats shared bookings before you book

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I claim accommodation for an NHS placement?

You may be able to through Travel and Dual Accommodation Expenses (TDAE) if you're already receiving the NHS Learning Support Fund Training Grant and the accommodation is temporary accommodation you paid for to attend your placement.

How much can I claim per night?

The NHSBSA lists up to £82.50 a night for commercial accommodation, such as a hotel or B&B, and up to £37.50 a night for non-commercial accommodation, such as staying with a friend or relative (not parents).

What's the deadline for a TDAE claim?

Six months from the last date of the placement you're claiming for. Your university submits the claim, and NHSBSA aims to process it within 20 working days.

Does a holiday let count as commercial accommodation?

The NHSBSA page we checked only gives hotels and B&Bs as examples. Check the TDAE guide or ask NHS LSF before you book a holiday let or short-let, and keep every receipt.

Can I get money up front?

If you're eligible, NHSBSA says you can ask for an advance payment, but only after an online 2026/27 TDAE claim has been approved.
