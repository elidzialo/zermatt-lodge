What's On in Liverpool in December 2026: A Date-by-Date Guide

Liverpool events in December 2026: Becher Chase, Morrissey, Cinderella at the arena, Mamma Mia, home games at Anfield and Everton, and Boxing Day racing.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

December 2026 in Liverpool runs from Becher Chase Day at Aintree on Saturday 5 December to Boxing Day racing on Saturday 26 December, with Skate St George's open all month except Christmas Day. Big dates include Mamma Mia at the Empire from 8 December, Cinderella at M&S Bank Arena from 12 December, Morrissey on 12 December, and Liverpool home games on 2, 9, 12 and 19 December.

December 2026 in Liverpool starts with Becher Chase Day at Aintree on Saturday 5 December and builds to Boxing Day racing on Saturday 26 December. In between, Skate St George's and its Christmas market are open every day except Christmas Day, the theatres are full of pantos and musicals, and there's Premier League football most weekends.

Below is a week-by-week list of what's been announced, with sources. We checked everything on 19 November 2026. Where an organiser hasn't published 2026 details, we say so.

All month

  • Skate St George's, St George's Plateau: ice rink, Christmas market, rooftop bar and Big Wheel, to 3 January 2027. Closed Christmas Day. Christmas Eve hours are 10am to 6pm
  • Icebox at Boxpark Liverpool: ice skating to 10 January 2027, closed Christmas Day. See our ice skating guide
  • Enchanted at Knowsley Safari: light trail on selected evenings to 23 December, tickets from £9.95
  • Stone's Throw Social, Chavasse Park at Liverpool ONE: ice-free curling and karaoke huts to 3 January
  • Festive Thursdays at Liverpool ONE: free live entertainment throughout December
  • Theatre: the Everyman panto, the Royal Court's Scouse Nativity and more. Full list in our Liverpool panto 2026 guide

Week 1: 1 to 6 December

  • Wed 2 Dec: Liverpool v Sunderland, Anfield, 7.30pm (Sky Sports)
  • Wed 2, Fri 4 and Thu 10 Dec: Evenings of Festive Music at Speke Hall, ticketed
  • Thu 3 Dec: Little Shop of Horrors opens at Liverpool Playhouse (to 9 January)
  • Sat 5 Dec: Becher Chase Day at Aintree, with races over the Grand National fences. See our Becher Chase weekend guide
  • Sat 5 Dec: Wishmas, an all-day free event at Liverpool ONE
  • Sat 5 Dec: Love Actually in Concert at Liverpool Philharmonic
  • Sat 5 Dec: Snow White opens at the Epstein Theatre (to 3 January)
  • Weekend of 5 and 6 Dec: Everton v Fulham at Hill Dickinson Stadium. One fixture list shows Sunday 6 December at 2pm, so check the club site
  • Sun 6 Dec: The Bootleg Beatles at Liverpool Philharmonic

The Jockey Club also lists "Celebrate Christmas at Aintree" events on 5, 11 and 12 December, which are separate from the racing.

Week 2: 7 to 13 December

  • Tue 8 Dec: Mamma Mia opens at the Liverpool Empire (to 3 January)
  • Wed 9 Dec: Liverpool v Porto, Champions League, Anfield, 8pm
  • Sat 12 Dec: Liverpool v Leeds, Anfield, 3pm
  • Sat 12 Dec: Morrissey at M&S Bank Arena
  • Sat 12 Dec: Cinderella opens at the Auditorium, M&S Bank Arena (to 27 December)

Week 3: 14 to 20 December

  • Mon 14 to Wed 23 Dec: extended shopping hours at Liverpool ONE
  • Sat 19 Dec: Liverpool v Tottenham, Anfield, 5.30pm
  • Sat 19 Dec: Liverpool Indoor Funfair Winter opens, running to 9 January 2027
  • Speke Hall house Christmas opening ends on Sunday 20 December

Christmas week: 21 to 27 December

  • From Mon 21 Dec: Skate St George's opens from 10am daily
  • Thu 24 Dec: most attractions close early. Skate St George's closes at 6pm
  • Fri 25 Dec: Christmas Day. Very little is open and trains don't normally run
  • Sat 26 Dec: Boxing Day racing at Aintree, gates 10.30am, first race 11.55am, general admission from £39, under-18s free in the Grandstand and Paddock
  • Sat 26 Dec: Everton v Sunderland at Hill Dickinson Stadium. Liverpool are away at Hull
  • Sun 27 Dec: last day of Cinderella at the arena

Our Boxing Day in Liverpool guide has the full day planned out.

The last few days: 28 to 31 December

  • Tue 29 Dec: Everton v Manchester City, Hill Dickinson Stadium, 8.15pm (Sky Sports)
  • Thu 31 Dec: New Year's Eve. See our New Year's Eve in Liverpool guide

What hasn't been announced yet

When we checked, these 2026 details were still unpublished or unclear:

  • A city-centre Christmas lights switch-on event. The Liverpool BID Company says it funds about 80% of the city's festive lights, but we found no 2026 switch-on date
  • Full dates and times for the Liverpool Christmas Wonderland at the Pier Head, run by Icebox Live
  • 2026 dates for the pop-up markets at Basnett Street and the Cunard Building
  • The festive public transport timetable for 2026. In past years Merseyrail hasn't run on Christmas Day and has run some services on Boxing Day

Planning tips

  • Book the big dates now. Panto weekends, Becher Chase and the final Saturday before Christmas sell out
  • Weekday visits are calmer for the market and skating
  • Check kick-off times about a month ahead. TV changes move games
  • Do your food shop early. Large shops are closed on Christmas Day

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's on in Liverpool in December 2026?

Skate St George's and its Christmas market run all month except Christmas Day. There's racing at Aintree on 5 and 26 December, pantos and musicals across the city's theatres, concerts at M&S Bank Arena and the Philharmonic, and Premier League football at Anfield and Hill Dickinson Stadium.

When is Becher Chase Day 2026?

Saturday 5 December 2026 at Aintree Racecourse. The Jockey Club lists it as William Hill Becher Chase Day.

Which Liverpool FC home games are in December 2026?

Sunderland (Wednesday 2 December, 7.30pm), Porto in the Champions League (Wednesday 9 December, 8pm), Leeds (Saturday 12 December, 3pm) and Tottenham (Saturday 19 December, 5.30pm), as reported in October. Times can change.

Is anything open in Liverpool on Christmas Day?

Very little. Skate St George's, Icebox at Boxpark and most attractions close on Christmas Day, and Merseyrail doesn't normally run. Plan to stay in.

When are the late-night shopping days at Liverpool ONE?

Liverpool ONE has announced extended shopping hours from Monday 14 to Wednesday 23 December 2026. Exact hours had not been published when we checked.
