Why a house works for a winter group break

Winter breaks are as much about the evenings in as the days out. A whole house gives a group:

  • Somewhere warm to gather. One living space instead of separate hotel rooms
  • A kitchen. Breakfasts and a big shared dinner keep costs down. Our meal plan for a group of 7 helps
  • A games room for the dark evenings
  • Parking. Leave the cars and take the train into town

Weighing it up? Read our house vs hotel guide for groups.

Tips for a January break

  1. Book shows early even in January. Weekend panto dates still sell out
  2. Check fixture dates about a month ahead
  3. Pack for weather. Wind, rain and the odd frost are normal
  4. Plan one quiet day. It's winter, and a house day is part of the fun
  5. Agree the house rules with your group before you arrive

Getting into the city

From Fazakerley, direct Merseyrail trains reach Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes, with fares from £4.85 when booked in advance according to Trainline. Merseyrail also takes contactless. For Hill Dickinson Stadium and the Anfield Soccerbus, Sandhills is a few stops down the same line.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is January a good time for a Liverpool group break?

Often, yes. Several Christmas shows run well into January, there's Premier League football most weekends, and demand for accommodation usually eases after New Year. Pack for cold, wet and windy weather.

What's on in Liverpool in January 2027?

The Everyman panto and the Royal Court's Scouse Nativity run to 16 January, Little Shop of Horrors to 9 January, Icebox skating to 10 January and the Indoor Funfair to 9 January. Jack Whitehall plays M&S Bank Arena on 10 January and Donny Osmond on 23 January.

When is the Merseyside derby at Anfield in 2027?

The original fixture list has Liverpool v Everton at Anfield on Saturday 30 January 2027. Like all Premier League games it may be moved for TV, so check before booking.
