Bolton: A Practical Visitor Guide

A day in Bolton, Greater Manchester: free Bolton Museum and its Egyptology collection, Smithills Hall, Hall i' th' Wood, the Octagon Theatre, Bolton Market and Rivington Pike, with homes nearby.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Bolton Museum on Le Mans Crescent (BL1 1SE) is free and open every day, about a 10-minute walk from Bolton Interchange. Its Egyptology collection includes a full-size recreation of the tomb of Pharaoh Thutmose III. Smithills Hall, a Grade I listed manor with an 800-year history, is also free, open Wednesday to Friday 10am to 4pm and Sunday 12pm to 4pm. At Christmas 2026 the Octagon Theatre stages Cinderella and the Albert Halls has Snow White.

Bolton sits in the north west corner of Greater Manchester, with moorland on its doorstep and free museums in the town centre. It's an easy day out, and in December it has two big Christmas shows. We checked these details on 25 November 2026. Opening times change over Christmas, so check before you go.

Getting there

  • Bolton Interchange brings trains and buses together in the town centre. Bolton Museum is about 0.4 miles, a 10-minute walk, from it.
  • By car: the M60, M61 and M6 are all nearby. The museum suggests the Octagon NCP multi-storey (BL1 1TN) for long stays.
  • Plan journeys on the Bee Network app or at tfgm.com. Our transport guide explains fares.

Bolton Museum

  • Where: Le Mans Crescent, Bolton, BL1 1SE.
  • Price: free.
  • Open: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday 9am to 5pm; Wednesday 9.30am to 5pm; Sunday 10am to 4pm.
  • Egyptology: Visit Manchester says the collection has over 12,000 archaeological objects from Egypt and Sudan, including a full-size recreation of the tomb of Pharaoh Thutmose III.
  • Aquarium: the building also houses Bolton's aquarium and archive.
  • Note: the Nature Gallery is partly closed for maintenance.

Historic halls

Smithills Hall

  • One of the oldest manor houses in the North West, with an 800-year history. It's Grade I listed, with medieval, Tudor and Victorian parts, and it's known for ghost stories.
  • Where: Smithills Dean Road, Bolton, BL1 7NP, on the edge of the West Pennine Moors.
  • Open: Wednesday to Friday 10am to 4pm, Sunday 12pm to 4pm.
  • Price: free. There's a car park outside, with disabled spaces.

Hall i' th' Wood

A rare Tudor wood-framed house, now a museum. It's linked with Samuel Crompton, the inventor, and was renovated by William Hesketh Lever, the soap maker. Check opening days before you go.

Theatre

  • Octagon Theatre: the first professional theatre built in the North West after the Second World War. It reopened in May 2021 after a £12 million redevelopment. Its Christmas 2026 show is Cinderella: A Fairy Tale, from 20 November 2026.
  • Albert Halls: Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs in December 2026.

See our Greater Manchester pantos guide for other shows.

Bolton Market

An award-winning food and lifestyle hall, named Best Food Market at the BBC Radio 4 Food and Farming Awards in 2011. A good stop for lunch.

Walks: Rivington Pike

Rivington Pike is a hill over 1,000 feet high. It was once a lookout with a warning beacon, and on a clear day you can see Blackpool Tower and the Isle of Man. Winter Hill, next to it, is higher. Wear boots and check the weather: the moors get cold and windy.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Bolton Museum free?

Yes. Bolton Museum on Le Mans Crescent is free to enter.

What are Bolton Museum's opening times?

Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday 9am to 5pm, Wednesday 9.30am to 5pm and Sunday 10am to 4pm. Part of the Nature Gallery is closed for maintenance.

When is Smithills Hall open?

Wednesday to Friday 10am to 4pm and Sunday 12pm to 4pm. Entry is free and there is a car park outside the hall.

What is the Bolton panto in 2026?

The Octagon Theatre has Cinderella: A Fairy Tale from 20 November 2026, and the Albert Halls has Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs in December.
