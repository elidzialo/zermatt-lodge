Christmas Light Trails Around Manchester 2026: Dates, Prices and Where to Stay

Christmas light trails around Manchester in 2026: Northern Lights at Heaton Park, Christmas at Dunham Massey, RHS Glow at Bridgewater and more, with dates, prices from and family homes nearby.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Visit Manchester lists Northern Lights at Heaton Park from 19 November to 31 December 2026, Christmas at Dunham Massey from 20 November 2026 to 2 January 2027, and RHS Glow at RHS Garden Bridgewater in Salford from 26 November to 30 December 2026. All are ticketed, so book ahead. For a family trip, a whole home in Prestwich puts you next to Heaton Park and on the tram into the city.

Light trails have become one of the easiest Christmas outings with children around Manchester: an hour or two outdoors, hot chocolate and no pressure to buy presents. Here are the main trails for 2026, with dates and starting prices from Visit Manchester's round-up, checked on 23 November 2026.

Prices are "from" prices and usually rise at weekends and close to Christmas. Book on each official site.

Visit Manchester also lists trails a little further out, including Lanterns and Light at Chester Zoo (13 November 2026 to 3 January 2027) and Enchanted at Knowsley Safari in Merseyside (selected dates, 20 November to 23 December).

Northern Lights at Heaton Park

Heaton Park is right next to Prestwich. If you're staying nearby, you can walk there or take the Metrolink Bury line to the Heaton Park stop, which has an entrance to the park. Our Heaton Park visitor guide covers the tram stops, buses and car park postcodes.

Tips for families

  • Book early evening slots for young children. It's dark by late afternoon in December.
  • Dress for the weather. These are outdoor trails, and paths can be muddy.
  • Avoid the last weekend before Christmas if you want a quieter visit. Manchester schools break up on Friday 18 December 2026, according to Manchester City Council.
  • Combine with the markets. The Manchester Christmas Markets are free to enter in the city centre.

Frequently asked questions

When is Northern Lights at Heaton Park in 2026?

Visit Manchester lists it from 19 November to 31 December 2026, with off-peak tickets from £15.25. Book on the official site.

When is Christmas at Dunham Massey 2026?

Visit Manchester lists 20 November 2026 to 2 January 2027, with off-peak tickets from £13 for a child and £19.50 for an adult.

When is RHS Glow at Bridgewater?

26 November to 30 December 2026 at RHS Garden Bridgewater in Salford, according to Visit Manchester, with standard tickets from £9.75 for a child and £17 for an adult.
