Becher Chase Weekend: National Fences at Aintree, Then Christmas in Liverpool

Becher Chase Day is Saturday 5 December 2026 at Aintree. Race times, the National fences, Christmas in Liverpool and planning a winter weekend for a group.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Becher Chase Day 2026 is on Saturday 5 December at Aintree Racecourse, with gates at 10am and the first race at 11.50am, according to the Jockey Club. It is the best chance outside April to see horses jump the Grand National fences, during Liverpool's Christmas season.

Becher Chase Day 2026 is on Saturday 5 December at Aintree Racecourse, with gates opening at 10am and the first race at 11.50am, according to the Jockey Club. It's the best chance outside April to watch horses take on the Grand National fences, and because it lands in Liverpool's Christmas season, it makes a great winter weekend: racing on Saturday, the city's festive lights and markets around it.

This guide explains what makes the day special and how to plan a weekend around it.

Becher Chase Day 2026 at a glance
Date Saturday 5 December 2026
Gates open 10:00
First race 11:50
Last race 15:15
Feature races William Hill Becher Chase and William Hill Grand Sefton Chase, both over the National fences
Venue Aintree Racecourse, Ormskirk Road, L9 5AS

Timings are subject to change, so check the Jockey Club's Becher Chase page nearer the day.

Why it's worth going

The National fences, without the festival crowds

The Becher Chase is run over about 3 miles 2 furlongs, with 21 fences on the Grand National course. It's named after Becher's Brook, the famous fence named after Martin Becher. Our guide to the Grand National fences explains each one.

The race was first run in 1992 and is now a Premier Handicap. It's often seen as an early trial for the Grand National: Amberleigh House and Silver Birch won both.

Two races over the big fences

For 2026, the Jockey Club has moved the Grand Sefton Chase back to Becher Chase Day, with prize money raised to £100,000. That means two races over the National fences on one afternoon.

A festive racecourse

The Jockey Club promises Christmas entertainment on the day, including the Haydock Band playing Christmas classics before the first race and a roaming festive band around the course.

Tickets and children

The Becher Chase page links to tickets and hospitality, but doesn't list prices in its text. The Jockey Club says under-18s go free at most of its racedays. Confirm this for Becher Chase Day when you book.

Getting there

  • Train: Aintree station is across the road from the racecourse. Trains run from Liverpool Central and Moorfields on the Ormskirk branch about every 15 minutes on a normal Saturday.
  • Car: the course is on the A59, about a mile from the M57 and M58. On non-festival days, follow the yellow tourism signs to the car parks.
  • Bus: services 300, 310 and 345 run to and from Liverpool.

Full details are in our guide to getting to Aintree Racecourse.

Watch out: Everton are at home the same afternoon

Sky Sports lists Everton v Fulham at 3pm on Saturday 5 December at Hill Dickinson Stadium. That means extra crowds on Merseyrail's Northern line and busy roads in north Liverpool before and after the match. Allow extra time and check Merseyrail's planner on the day.

Planning a winter weekend

Friday: arrive and settle in

Arrive in the afternoon, do a food shop and have a relaxed night in. If you're arriving by train, see our guide to Lime Street to Fazakerley.

Saturday: racing

  • Late morning: travel to Aintree. Aim to arrive by about 11am to see the first race.
  • Afternoon: the Becher Chase and Grand Sefton Chase. Wrap up warm; it's December on an open racecourse.
  • Evening: back to your accommodation for a warm meal.

Sunday: Christmas in the city

Liverpool's 2026 festive programme includes:

  • Skate St George's, a new outdoor ice rink at St George's Hall run by Underbelly
  • Liverpool Christmas Wonderland @ Pier Head, with a covered ice rink and rides beneath the Three Graces
  • Pop-up markets at Basnett Street and the Cunard Building on selected dates
  • Festive shows and events at Liverpool City Halls

Dates and opening times weren't all published at the time of writing. Our Liverpool Christmas markets guide has the latest we could verify.

Racing fans: more to see

If your group wants more racing history, look at Aintree Racecourse tours. There's also Boxing Day racing at Aintree on Saturday 26 December 2026, if you're planning a longer Christmas stay.

What to wear and bring

  • Layers and a warm, waterproof coat. Aintree is exposed in December.
  • Comfortable shoes. You'll be on your feet and walking between stands and the course.
  • Hat and gloves.
  • A plan for getting home, especially if there's a match on the same day.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is the Becher Chase 2026?

Becher Chase Day is Saturday 5 December 2026 at Aintree. The Jockey Club lists gates opening at 10am, the first race at 11.50am and the last race at 3.15pm, subject to change.

Is the Becher Chase run over the Grand National fences?

Yes. It's run over about 3 miles 2 furlongs with 21 fences on the Grand National course, including Becher's Brook. The Grand Sefton Chase on the same card is also run over the National fences.

Is the Becher Chase a Grand National trial?

It's seen as an early-season trial. Amberleigh House and Silver Birch both won the Becher Chase and the Grand National.

Do children go free at Aintree on Becher Chase Day?

The Jockey Club says under-18s go free at most of its racedays but doesn't confirm this on the Becher Chase page. Check when you book tickets.

What else is on in Liverpool on Becher Chase weekend?

It falls in Liverpool's Christmas season, with Skate St George's, the Christmas Wonderland at the Pier Head and pop-up markets planned for 2026. Everton are also at home to Fulham at 3pm on the same Saturday, so expect busy roads and trains.
