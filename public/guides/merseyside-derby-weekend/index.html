A Merseyside derby weekend needs more planning than an ordinary match: hotels fill early, roads and pubs near both grounds are packed, and kick-off times often move for TV. The two grounds

Hill Dickinson Stadium (Everton)

Everton's men's team moved to their new stadium at Bramley-Moore Dock, on the waterfront north of the city centre, in 2025. It holds 52,769. Goodison Park is now home to Everton Women. The new ground is still a first visit for many travelling fans, so allow extra time to find your way around.

  • Nearest station: Sandhills, on Merseyrail's Northern line, about a 15-minute walk
  • Shuttle buses: routes 919, 929 and 939 have run on match days (£2). Check Merseytravel for derby-day arrangements
  • Parking: a council matchday parking zone operates around the stadium. Check the latest rules before you drive

From Fazakerley, Trainline shows Sandhills about 8 minutes away by train. See getting to Everton's stadium from Fazakerley.

Anfield (Liverpool)

Anfield holds about 61,000, and the expanded Anfield Road stand was fully completed in March 2025.

  • Soccerbus: runs from Sandhills before and after the match. Times vary; check Merseytravel on the day
  • Walking: Kirkdale station is just under a mile from the ground
  • Bus 917: runs from the city centre on match days
  • Parking: no paid parking at Anfield on match days, and the surrounding streets are a residents' zone

Why derby weekends are different

Accommodation goes early

Derbies attract fans from all over the UK and overseas. Many make a weekend of it, and city-centre hotels push prices up. Larger rooms and family suites go first. If you're a group of 5 to 7, a whole house is often easier to find and better value than several hotel rooms. See house vs hotel for groups.

Roads and pubs are under pressure

Both grounds are surrounded by residential streets. On derby days, traffic builds early, pubs near the grounds are rammed, and taxis are hard to find after the final whistle. Plan to use the train, and agree a meeting point in case your group gets split up.

Policing and segregation

Derbies are policed carefully. Away supporters at Anfield sit in a segregated section of the Anfield Road End and enter from the 97 Avenue side, according to the guidance clubs send to visiting fans. Bags must be small (A5 or smaller at Anfield) and are searched. Leave large bags at your accommodation.

Early kick-offs

The November derby kicks off at 12pm on a Sunday. Sunday trains start later and run less often than on weekdays, so check the Merseyrail timetable the night before and leave plenty of time. Staying in Liverpool on the Saturday night avoids a dawn drive.

A sample derby weekend

Saturday - Arrive, check in from 3pm, and do a food shop - Head into the city for the afternoon or evening. The train from Fazakerley reaches Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes - Get back for a quiet night before an early start

Sunday (12pm kick-off) - Cooked breakfast at the house - Train to Sandhills, then walk to Bramley-Moore Dock - After the match, give the crowds time to clear before heading back to Sandhills

Monday - Checkout is 11am. If you've got time, visit the waterfront or the museums before you head home

Tips for a smooth derby day

  1. Only buy tickets through official club channels. Selling football tickets without the club's authority is an offence under section 166 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994, and the clubs act against ticket touting.
  2. Carry a small bag only.
  3. Charge your phone and download the Merseyrail and club apps.
  4. Check the weather. November and January matches can be cold and wet.
  5. Plan the journey back. Agree a meeting point before the match.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When are the Merseyside derbies in 2026/27?

Everton v Liverpool at Hill Dickinson Stadium has been moved for live TV to Sunday 29 November 2026 at 12pm. Liverpool v Everton at Anfield is listed for Saturday 30 January 2027 at 3pm, but all fixtures are subject to change.

Which station is best for each ground?

For Hill Dickinson Stadium at Bramley-Moore Dock, Sandhills is the closest station, about a 15-minute walk. For Anfield, Sandhills also has the Soccerbus, and Kirkdale is just under a mile's walk.

Can I park at Anfield or Everton's stadium on derby day?

There's no paid parking at Anfield on match days and the streets around it are a residents' parking zone. Everton's stadium also sits inside a council matchday parking zone. The train is usually the better option.

Why book accommodation early for a derby?

Derby weekends draw fans from across the UK and abroad, and they often clash with other city events. Hotel prices rise and larger rooms for groups go first, so book as soon as the date is confirmed.

Can our group hold a derby-day gathering at the house?

No. Zermatt Lodge is a quiet residential home with strictly no parties or events. You're welcome to watch the build-up on the TV and come back for a quiet evening.
