Liverpool half term dates for 2027 and a five-day family plan with rainy-day options, opening-day traps to avoid and a base in north Liverpool.

The May half term starts straight after the spring bank holiday on Monday 31 May 2027, so the weekend before is a long one and busier for travel.

Academies, free schools and schools in Sefton, Knowsley and other nearby councils can have different dates. If you're visiting from elsewhere, your own half term may not match Liverpool's, which can mean quieter attractions.

Before you go: three things to check

  1. Opening days. Several attractions don't open every day out of the main season. Art Fund lists World Museum as open Wednesday to Sunday, 10am to 5pm. Knowsley Safari's opening-times page has listed Friday to Sunday opening in winter. Check each one for your exact dates.
  2. What's closed. National Museums Liverpool has closed the Merseyside Maritime Museum and the International Slavery Museum on the Royal Albert Dock for repairs and redevelopment, with both due to reopen in 2028. Don't plan a day around them.
  3. Booking. Stadium tours, soft play, cinemas and popular restaurants book up in half term. Book the one or two things you really care about before you travel.

A five-day half term plan

This plan is for the February week, when the weather is unpredictable and days are short. In May, swap in more outdoor time.

Day 1 (Monday): settle in and get outside

Many museums are closed on Mondays, so make this your outdoor day.

  • Croxteth Hall and Country Park in north-east Liverpool is open all year. The inner park is open 7am to 5.30pm from November to March, and the Victorian walled garden 10am to 3pm daily. Bring a picnic, as the main cafe has been closed for refurbishment and a smaller concession serves refreshments.
  • Do your big food shop on the way back, and cook a simple first-night dinner.

Day 2 (Tuesday): a stadium tour or the waterfront

  • If your family loves football, book a stadium tour. Our football trip with kids guide covers both Anfield and Everton.
  • Otherwise, take the train into town and walk the waterfront from the Pier Head to the Royal Albert Dock. The Museum of Liverpool at the Pier Head covers the city's history, from the docks to the Beatles and football.

Day 3 (Wednesday): World Museum and the city centre

World Museum on William Brown Street is free, and it's one of the best rainy-day options in the North West. Art Fund lists:

  • A full-dome planetarium with shows about the solar system
  • An aquarium
  • Dinosaurs
  • The Bug House, with live insects

It's a few minutes' walk from Lime Street and Liverpool Central. Arrive close to opening at 10am to beat the busiest part of the day, and allow at least two to three hours. Have lunch in town, then head back for a quieter evening.

Day 4 (Thursday): the coast or a slow day

If the forecast is dry, drive to the coast:

  • Crosby Beach to see Antony Gormley's Another Place: 100 cast iron figures along about 2 miles of beach. Sefton Council warns not to walk out to the furthest figures because of very soft sand and mud, and some are underwater around high tide
  • Formby for the pinewoods and red squirrels

Wrap up, as the wind off the Irish Sea in February is cold. If it's wet, make it a home day: films, board games, baking and maybe a short local walk. Children cope much better with a busy week if there's one slow day in the middle.

Day 5 (Friday): Knowsley Safari or a final city trip

Knowsley Safari is off junction 2 of the M57. Its opening-times page lists a 10am opening and last entry to the safari drive at 3pm, and staff will turn you away after that. Go in the morning, do the drive first, then walk around the foot safari.

If animals aren't your thing, finish with a last trip into the city for shopping at Liverpool ONE and tea out.

May half term ideas

In late May and early June there's more light and better weather, so:

  • Spend a full day at Formby or Southport
  • Take a picnic to Croxteth and stay longer
  • Try a Mersey ferry from the Pier Head
  • Check what's on at Sefton Park and the waterfront

Getting around

By car

A car is the easiest way to reach Croxteth, Knowsley Safari and the coast. City-centre parking is expensive, so for city days park at your base and take the train.

By train

From Fazakerley, Merseyrail's Northern line runs to Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes, according to Trainline. Children travel at reduced fares. Check the Merseyrail app for engineering works, which sometimes happen in school holidays.

Half term tips

  • Pick one paid attraction and fill the rest with free museums and parks.
  • Plan lunch. Packed lunches save money and queues.
  • Watch the clocks. In February it gets dark by late afternoon. Do outdoor things in the morning.
  • Pack for wet weather: wellies, waterproofs and a change of clothes in the car.
  • Don't over-plan. One main outing a day is plenty for younger children.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is half term in Liverpool in 2027?

For Liverpool City Council schools, February half term is 15 to 19 February 2027 and May half term is 31 May to 4 June 2027. Academies and free schools can set their own dates, so check with your school.

Is World Museum open every day in half term?

Art Fund lists World Museum as open Wednesday to Sunday, 10am to 5pm, and closed Mondays and Tuesdays. Holiday opening can differ, so check National Museums Liverpool before you plan a Monday or Tuesday visit.

What can families do in Liverpool on a rainy half term day?

World Museum (free, with a planetarium, aquarium, dinosaurs and a bug house), the Museum of Liverpool, and a stadium tour are all indoors. Plan the outdoor days around the forecast.

Is Knowsley Safari open in February half term?

Knowsley Safari's opening-times page has listed winter opening on Fridays to Sundays, from 10am, with last entry to the safari drive at 3pm. Check its website for half term dates, as hours change through the year.
