Exhibiting at Exhibition Centre Liverpool: Build-Up, Breakdown and Where to Stay

Exhibiting at Exhibition Centre Liverpool? Loading bay and hall facts, build-up tips, parking costs and a 4-bed house with parking for your stand team.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

If your company is exhibiting at Exhibition Centre Liverpool, the stay that works best is one where the stand team sleeps under one roof, the vans are parked for free overnight, and there's a kitchen for early build-up mornings. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in north Liverpool, about 15 minutes by train from the city centre, with free parking for two vehicles and beds for up to 7. This guide covers the venue facts exhibitors need and how to plan build-up, show days and breakdown.

If you're attending as a delegate rather than building a stand, see our conference accommodation guide.

The venue in brief

Exhibition Centre Liverpool sits on Kings Dock on the waterfront, linked by a covered bridge to the convention centre and M&S Bank Arena. In 2026 the campus rebranded as Liverpool Experience Campus, and the venue is now officially the Exhibition Centre at Liverpool Experience Campus.

  • Floor space: about 8,100 square metres
  • Halls: three, A, B and C
  • Hall heights: 10.5m for Halls A and B, 18m for Hall C, according to the venue's spec sheet
  • Floor loading: 2 tonnes per square metre
  • Sat nav postcode: L3 4BX

Loading in: what the venue says

Loading Bay 3

The venue's A to Z exhibitor guide says:

  • All halls are reached through Loading Bay 3, which is on the same level as the halls
  • The bay is uncovered, so there's no height restriction in the bay itself
  • It holds a limited number of vehicles. For big shows, organisers may use time slots. Otherwise, traffic managers work on a first come, first served basis
  • There's a separate "ECL Exhibitor Loading Procedure" document with more detail. Ask your organiser for it

Door sizes

The hall spec sheet gives the vehicle loading doors as 4.9m high and 7m wide, while the A to Z guide says 4.6m high. If you're bringing a tall vehicle or oversized stand parts, confirm the current figure with your organiser before you plan the load.

Services must be ordered

There are no permanent connection points. Power, data and water come through floor ducts at 6m intervals, and every service has to be ordered and paid for:

  • Power: through your show's nominated electrical contractor
  • Data: through Guest Relations
  • Water and waste: through the main supplier

Basic Wi-Fi is free throughout the building, but the venue recommends exhibitors who rely on the internet order premium Wi-Fi or a wired connection.

Catering

Stand catering is ordered through the venue's caterer, Centerplate. Bringing your own food or drink in needs Centerplate's permission first, and fees may apply. That's one reason a good breakfast before you leave in the morning matters.

Health and safety

The venue points exhibitors to the AEV e-guide for health, safety and operational planning. Read your organiser's exhibitor manual for PPE rules, working at height and build-up and breakdown times, as these vary by show.

Planning build-up, show days and breakdown

Build-up day

  • Arrive with the right paperwork: your stand plans, risk assessment, method statement and any vehicle passes
  • Get there early. If loading is first come, first served, the first vans in have the easiest time
  • Bring tools, cable ties, gaffer tape and spare bulbs. Don't count on buying them nearby on the day
  • Check your services are live before the end of the day

Show days

  • Agree a rota so people get breaks off the stand
  • Keep lead-capture devices charged overnight
  • Take turns with the car: parking adds up. The 2025 joining instructions list the on-site Kings Dock Car Park at £17 up to 9 hours and £27 up to 24 hours

Breakdown

  • Breakdown is often the busiest time in the loading bay. Have everything packed and labelled before your slot
  • Don't plan a long drive home straight after a late breakdown. A final night's sleep is safer

Getting from north Liverpool to the venue

  • By van or car: drive to the waterfront and follow your organiser's loading instructions. Check a live route for early-morning timings
  • By train: from Fazakerley station, direct Merseyrail trains reach Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes. The venue says James Street station is about a 10-minute walk, and Lime Street about 20 minutes

A common pattern for stand teams: one van drives in for build-up and breakdown, while the rest of the team travels by train on show days.

Why Zermatt Lodge suits exhibitor teams

Zermatt Lodge is a whole house in north Liverpool, near Fazakerley station and the M57/M58. It isn't in the city centre or on the waterfront, so you'll drive or take the train in each day.

  • 4 separate bedrooms, sleeps 7: king master with en-suite, second king, queen and single
  • 2 bathrooms for early starts
  • Free parking for 2 vehicles. We can't promise every van will fit, so call if you're bringing a large van or more than two vehicles

How big are the vehicle doors into the halls?

Can exhibitors bring their own food on to the stand?

How much is parking at the venue?
