Heaton Park and Prestwich: A Practical Visitor Guide

Visiting Heaton Park from Prestwich or Manchester? Opening times, the nearest Metrolink stops, buses, car parks and parking charges, plus homes nearby.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Heaton Park is open from 8am to dusk every day. The Heaton Park and Bowker Vale Metrolink stops on the Bury line are a few minutes' walk from the park. Car parks charge pay and display between 10am and 5pm, up to £3.50 a day, and Blue Badge holders park free.

Heaton Park sits right next to Prestwich in north Manchester. Manchester City Council calls it "the biggest and best park in the NorthWest". If you are staying in Prestwich for a family visit, a celebration or a longer stay, it is the obvious place for a walk, a run or a day out with children.

Opening times

The council says the park is open from 8am to dusk every day. Some attractions and cafes inside the park keep their own hours, so check the council's Heaton Park pages before you plan a day around one.

By tram

  • Heaton Park stop is on the Metrolink Bury line, at the corner of Whittaker Lane and Bury Old Road, with an entrance to the park.
  • Bowker Vale stop is also within a few minutes' walk.
  • Prestwich stop is the next stop towards Bury.

Trams run between Bury and Manchester city centre, so you can be at the park or in town without the car. For live times use TfGM or the Metrolink website. Metrolink also runs free Park and Ride car parks for tram users, including Bury Interchange, Radcliffe and Whitefield.

By bus

The council lists the x35, 59, 90, 135, 149, 156, 164 and 484 buses. Check routes with the TfGM journey planner.

By car

Car park access is from the Sheepfoot Lane, Smithy Lodge or St Margaret's Road entrances. Postcodes for sat nav, with approximate spaces:

Charges: pay and display every day between 10am and 5pm, with a maximum of £3.50 a day. Annual permits are available. Blue Badge holders park free in all the car parks, with the badge on display.

On foot or by bike, the entrances are St Margaret's Road, Bury Old Road, Sheepfoot Lane and Middleton Road.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What time does Heaton Park open?

Manchester City Council says the park is open from 8am to dusk every day.

Which tram stop is nearest Heaton Park?

The Heaton Park stop on the Metrolink Bury line has an entrance to the park. Bowker Vale is also within a few minutes' walk.

How much is parking at Heaton Park?

Pay and display applies every day between 10am and 5pm, with a maximum daily cost of £3.50. Disabled badge holders do not pay.

Which buses go to Heaton Park?

The council lists the x35, 59, 90, 135, 149, 156, 164 and 484. Check routes and times with Transport for Greater Manchester.
