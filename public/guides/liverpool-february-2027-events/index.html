What's On in Liverpool in February 2027: A Date-by-Date Guide

Liverpool events in February 2027: Strictly Come Dancing Live, Disney Worlds Collide, Lunar New Year, half term, Valentine's weekend and home games at Anfield and Everton.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

February 2027 in Liverpool starts with Strictly Come Dancing Live at M&S Bank Arena on 2 and 3 February. Lunar New Year falls on Saturday 6 February, Everton host Newcastle the same day and Leeds on Wednesday 10 February, and Disney Worlds Collide plays M&S Bank Arena on Saturday 13 February, the eve of Valentine's Day. Liverpool City Council schools break for half term from 15 to 19 February, and Liverpool host Hull at Anfield on Saturday 20 February.

February is one of Liverpool's quieter months, which makes it a good time to visit: prices are usually lower than at Christmas, and there's still plenty on. In 2027 the highlights are Strictly Come Dancing Live and Disney Worlds Collide at M&S Bank Arena, Lunar New Year on 6 February, half term from 15 February and four home league games across Anfield and Hill Dickinson Stadium.

We checked every date below on 22 November 2026. Shows can move and football fixtures change for TV, so check again before you book travel.

Week by week

1 to 7 February

  • Strictly Come Dancing Live is listed at M&S Bank Arena on Tuesday 2 and Wednesday 3 February.
  • Back to the Future The Musical finishes its run at the Liverpool Empire on Saturday 6 February, according to the show's official site. See our Back to the Future stay guide.
  • Lunar New Year is Saturday 6 February 2027, the start of the Year of the Goat. Liverpool's Chinatown is home to the oldest Chinese community in Europe. In 2026 the city's celebration was held on the weekend after the new year, so watch for the 2027 dates. Our Chinese New Year in Liverpool guide has what we know so far.
  • Everton v Newcastle, Hill Dickinson Stadium, Saturday 6 February, 3pm. Liverpool are away at Arsenal that day.

8 to 14 February

  • Everton v Leeds, Wednesday 10 February, 8pm. A midweek night game, so plan for a late finish. See our midweek match night guide.
  • Disney Worlds Collide at M&S Bank Arena, Saturday 13 February. Ents24 lists a 5.30pm start, with a 6pm time on one premium package, so check your ticket.
  • Valentine's Day is a Sunday in 2027, so Saturday 13 February is the main night for restaurants. Book early.

15 to 21 February: half term

  • Half term for Liverpool City Council schools runs 15 to 19 February 2027. Academies and free schools may differ. Our half term guide has ideas for families.
  • Liverpool v Hull at Anfield, Saturday 20 February, 3pm. Everton are away at Sunderland.

22 to 28 February

  • Everton v Nottingham Forest, Hill Dickinson Stadium, Saturday 27 February, 3pm. Liverpool are away at Tottenham.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's on in Liverpool in February 2027?

Strictly Come Dancing Live at M&S Bank Arena (2 and 3 February), Lunar New Year (Saturday 6 February), Disney Worlds Collide at M&S Bank Arena (Saturday 13 February), February half term (15 to 19 February for Liverpool City Council schools), and home football at Hill Dickinson Stadium and Anfield.

When is February half term 2027 in Liverpool?

For Liverpool City Council schools, 15 to 19 February 2027. Academies and free schools can set their own dates, so check with your school.

Are Liverpool or Everton at home in February 2027?

Everton host Newcastle on Saturday 6 February (3pm), Leeds on Wednesday 10 February (8pm) and Nottingham Forest on Saturday 27 February (3pm). Liverpool host Hull at Anfield on Saturday 20 February (3pm). All fixtures are subject to change for TV.

When is Chinese New Year 2027?

Lunar New Year is Saturday 6 February 2027, the start of the Year of the Goat. Liverpool's city celebration dates for 2027 hadn't been announced when we checked. In 2026 the celebration was held on the weekend of 21 and 22 February.

Is Valentine's Day 2027 on a weekend?

Yes. Valentine's Day, 14 February 2027, is a Sunday, so Saturday 13 February is the big night out.
