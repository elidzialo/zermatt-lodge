Long Stay Accommodation in Liverpool: What to Ask Before You Book

Need a place in Liverpool for a month or more? What to ask about cleaning, linen, bills and invoices, house vs aparthotel, and a 4-bed house with parking.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

For a long stay in Liverpool, of a month or more, the right place is the one where the boring details are agreed upfront: cleaning, linen, bills, parking and invoices. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in north Liverpool, about 15 minutes by train from the city centre, with free parking for two cars, a big kitchen and room for up to 7. It suits families between homes, project teams and anyone who needs more than a hotel room for several weeks.

This guide explains what to ask any host before booking a long stay, and how a house compares with an aparthotel.

Who books long stays in Liverpool?

  • Families relocating while they buy or rent. See our relocation guide
  • Homeowners during renovation or after a flood or fire. See insurance temporary accommodation
  • Contractors and project teams on multi-week jobs
  • Families supporting someone in hospital for weeks at a time
  • Film and TV crews on location shoots

Each group needs slightly different things, but the questions below apply to all of them.

The questions to ask before you book

1. What's included in the price?

Get it in writing. For a long stay, ask:

  • Are all bills (gas, electricity, water, council tax) included?
  • Is Wi-Fi included? If you're working from the property, ask about the connection and test it on day one
  • Is there a cleaning fee, and is it one-off or per clean? At Zermatt Lodge it's a one-off £129
  • Is a mid-stay clean or linen change available, and at what cost?

2. Cleaning and linen

Over four weeks, towels and bedding need changing. Agree how this works: whether the host arranges a clean, or whether you do it yourself. Ask what laundry facilities there are rather than assuming.

3. Parking

In Liverpool, this matters more than people expect. City-centre aparthotels often charge for parking or have none. Areas near the football grounds have residents' parking zones on match days. A property with its own off-street spaces saves both money and hassle over a month.

4. Cancellation and flexibility

Long stays often change: a house purchase slips, a project overruns or a patient is discharged early. Check:

  • The cancellation terms. Zermatt Lodge uses a Moderate policy: a full refund up to 5 days before check-in, 50% after that
  • Whether you can extend if dates move
  • What happens if you need to leave early

5. Invoices and payment

Business guests should ask for an invoice in the company's name before arriving. If you need a VAT invoice, check whether the host can issue one. See our guide to invoices and VAT for business stays.

6. House rules

Read them carefully. A long stay is still a stay in someone's property. At Zermatt Lodge the rules are simple: no parties or events, no smoking, no pets.

Settling in for several weeks

Healthcare

NHS guidance says you don't need ID or proof of address to register with a GP. You can register as a temporary patient for up to three months, then reapply or register permanently. Our temporary resident GP guide has the details.

Shopping

There's a Lidl close to Zermatt Lodge, which makes a weekly shop easy. Online grocery delivery works well too.

Getting around

From Fazakerley station, Merseyrail trains reach Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes. Since February 2026, Merseyrail has accepted contactless bank cards, phones and watches: tap in and tap out. For longer stays, it's worth comparing weekly or monthly tickets on the Merseytravel website.

Tax and expenses

If you're working away from your usual workplace, tax rules may let you or your employer claim travel and accommodation costs. Tax guidance from LITRG explains that a workplace is treated as temporary unless you work there continuously for more than 24 months. Speak to your employer or accountant about your own situation.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a long stay?

Hosts usually mean 28 nights or more. At that length, cleaning, laundry, bills and invoicing need agreeing upfront, which matters less on a weekend break.

Is a house or an aparthotel better for a long stay in Liverpool?

An aparthotel suits one or two people who want to be in the city centre with reception and housekeeping. A whole house suits families, colleagues or crews who need separate bedrooms, a proper kitchen and parking.

Can I register with a GP during a long stay?

Yes. NHS guidance says you don't need proof of address or ID to register with a GP, and you can register as a temporary patient for up to 3 months.

Can I claim my accommodation as a work expense?

It depends on your circumstances. Tax guidance from LITRG explains that a workplace counts as temporary unless you work there continuously for more than 24 months, which affects what travel and subsistence costs can be claimed. Check with your employer or accountant.
