Manchester Arena Shows This Winter: Dates, Travel and Where to Stay

Going to a Co-op Live or AO Arena show this winter? Confirmed dates from Mariah Carey to Donny Osmond, Disney On Ice, trams, parking and taxis, and whole homes for groups in north Manchester.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Co-op Live (M11 3DU) is next to Etihad Campus tram stop, with trams about every 7.5 minutes from the city centre and until 1am on Fridays and Saturdays. Winter shows include Mariah Carey (2 Dec), Kehlani (10 Dec), Blossoms (11 Dec), Jack Whitehall (2 Jan) and Donny Osmond (24 Jan). The AO Arena sits partly above Manchester Victoria, where Bury line trams from Prestwich and Crumpsall stop, and has Culture Club (10 Dec), Disney On Ice (16 to 20 Dec) and the Christmas Musical Spectacular (22 and 23 Dec).

Manchester has two of the biggest indoor arenas in Europe, and both are busy this winter. A group house means you can travel in together, get back late and not pay for four hotel rooms. We checked these dates and travel details on 24 November 2026. Shows can move or be cancelled, so check your ticket and the venue before you travel.

Getting to Co-op Live

  • Address: 1 Sports City Way, Etihad Campus, Manchester, M11 3DU.
  • Tram: Etihad Campus stop is directly next to the venue on the Ashton to Piccadilly line. Trams run about every 7.5 minutes from the city centre, and until 1am on Fridays and Saturdays. Velopark stop is closed after events.
  • Walk: about 25 minutes from Piccadilly station on the step-free CityLink route. Follow the blue lampposts.
  • Parking: limited official parking, pre-booked only on event days. Free Park and Ride at Ashton Moss, Ashton West and Ladywell. A residents' parking scheme operates around the arena.
  • Taxis: drop-off and pick-up on Edwin Road, M11 3ER. A free pick-up point opens from 10pm.
  • Traffic: Co-op Live says congestion usually builds about 2 hours before a show.

Getting to the AO Arena

  • The AO Arena is immediately north of the city centre, partly above Manchester Victoria station.
  • From Prestwich and Crumpsall: Bury line trams run straight into Victoria, so you can get to the arena with no change.

Venue facts

  • Co-op Live opened on 14 May 2024. It holds about 20,500 seated and 23,500 with standing.
  • The AO Arena opened in 1995 and holds about 23,000.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which tram stop is nearest Co-op Live?

Etihad Campus, directly next to the venue on the Ashton to Piccadilly line. Co-op Live says Velopark stop is closed after events to manage crowds.

Is there parking at Co-op Live?

Only limited official parking, which must be pre-booked. Co-op Live suggests the free Park and Ride sites at Ashton Moss, Ashton West and Ladywell, with a tram in.

Where do taxis drop off at Co-op Live?

At designated spots on Edwin Road, M11 3ER, not outside the venue. A free pick-up point opens from 10pm after events.

How big are Co-op Live and the AO Arena?

Co-op Live opened on 14 May 2024 and holds up to about 23,500. The AO Arena opened in 1995 and holds about 23,000.
