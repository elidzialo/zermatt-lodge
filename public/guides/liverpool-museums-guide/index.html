Liverpool's Museums: What's Open, Free and Closed

A practical guide to Liverpool's museums: free national museums, which are closed for redevelopment (Maritime Museum, Tate's Albert Dock home), opening days and tips.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

National Museums Liverpool venues are free to enter. World Museum, the Walker Art Gallery and the Museum of Liverpool are open, but the Merseyside Maritime Museum and International Slavery Museum on the Royal Albert Dock are closed for repairs and redevelopment, due to reopen in 2028. Tate Liverpool is showing at RIBA North on Mann Island until it returns to the Albert Dock in 2027.

Liverpool has some of the best free museums in the country. But a few big names are closed for redevelopment right now, and older guides still list them as open. Here's what you can actually visit.

What's closed, and why

  • Merseyside Maritime Museum and International Slavery Museum: National Museums Liverpool closed both for "essential repair and maintenance works" ahead of a major redevelopment. Both are due to reopen in 2028, subject to funding
  • Tate Liverpool's Albert Dock gallery: closed for redevelopment. Tate is showing work at Tate Liverpool + RIBA North on Mann Island, open daily 10am to 5.50pm. Tate says the gallery will return to the Albert Dock in 2027. Over Christmas, it closes early on 17 December and is shut from 24 to 26 December 2026

The open museums

World Museum

On William Brown Street, next to St George's Hall. Lonely Planet lists an aquarium on the first floor, live bugs, and a planetarium on the fifth floor with space exhibits including moon rocks. It's one of the best rainy-day options for children.

Walker Art Gallery

Next door to World Museum on William Brown Street. It's the city's main art gallery, with free entry.

Museum of Liverpool

At the Pier Head, opened in 2011. It tells the story of the city and its people across four themes: The Great Port, Global City, People's Republic and Wondrous Place. Highlights include the Lion steam locomotive and Little Liverpool, a gallery for children under six. It's right next to the Mersey Ferry terminal.

Sudley House and the Lady Lever

Sudley House is a historic house in Mossley Hill, south Liverpool. The Lady Lever Art Gallery is in Port Sunlight village on the Wirral. Both are free, and both are better reached by car.

Opening days

A Liverpool World guide lists the national museums as open 10am to 5pm, Tuesday to Sunday, closed on Mondays except bank holidays and Liverpool school holidays. Opening days can change, so check each museum's website before you go, especially over Christmas.

Plan a museum day

  1. Start on William Brown Street. World Museum and the Walker are side by side, with Central Library on the same street and St George's Hall opposite
  2. Walk down to the waterfront for the Museum of Liverpool and a ferry cruise
  3. Finish at Mann Island for Tate Liverpool + RIBA North

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are Liverpool's museums free?

Yes, the National Museums Liverpool venues are free to enter. Some special exhibitions may charge.

Is the Merseyside Maritime Museum open?

No. National Museums Liverpool closed the Maritime Museum and the International Slavery Museum for repairs ahead of a redevelopment. Both are due to reopen in 2028, subject to funding.

Is Tate Liverpool open?

Tate Liverpool + RIBA North is open daily at Mann Island while its Albert Dock home is redeveloped. Tate says the gallery will return to the Royal Albert Dock in 2027. It is closed 24 to 26 December 2026.

Are the museums open on Mondays?

A Liverpool World guide says the national museums open 10am to 5pm Tuesday to Sunday, and close on Mondays except bank holidays and Liverpool school holidays. Check the museum's website before you go.

Which museum is best for young children?

The Museum of Liverpool has Little Liverpool, a gallery for children under six. World Museum has an aquarium, live bugs and a planetarium.
