Liverpool Through the Year: The Best Time to Visit

When to visit Liverpool: weather by month, the big events that push up prices, school holidays and 2027 dates for the Grand National and the Cavern's 70th.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

The best time to visit Liverpool depends on what you want. For mild weather and fewer crowds, May, June and September are hard to beat. For atmosphere, plan around the big events: the Grand National in April, stadium concerts in early summer, Beatleweek in late August, the River of Light in late autumn and the Christmas markets. Winter is the wettest and quietest season, which can mean better value. Wherever you land, book early for event weeks. Liverpool weather by month

The Met Office's 1991-2020 averages for Crosby, on the coast just north of Liverpool, give a fair picture:

Month Avg max (°C) Avg min (°C) Rainfall (mm)
January 7.5 2.8 69
February 7.9 2.7 57
March 9.9 3.9 53
April 12.8 5.6 50
May 15.9 8.3 52
June 18.4 11.3 64
July 20.0 13.5 65
August 19.7 13.5 72
September 17.7 11.2 77
October 14.2 8.5 90
November 10.5 5.7 82
December 8.0 3.1 92

Two takeaways:

  • Spring is the driest time. April and May have the lowest rainfall
  • Autumn and early winter are the wettest. October to December each average over 80mm

Liverpool's waterfront is windy whatever the month, so pack a proper coat.

Spring (March to May)

Weather: cool to mild, and the driest part of the year.

Big events: - The Grand National Festival at Aintree: Thursday 8 to Saturday 10 April 2027. This is one of the busiest weeks of the year for accommodation in north Liverpool - Easter school holidays: Liverpool City Council's 2027 Easter break runs from 26 March to 9 April, overlapping with the Grand National - May half term 2027: 31 May to 4 June

Good for: racing fans, walkers, families wanting days out at Formby or Croxteth Park before the summer crowds.

See our Grand National accommodation guide.

Summer (June to August)

Weather: the warmest months, with July averaging about 20°C. Still expect some rain.

Typical events: the 2026 calendar gives a good idea of what summer looks like:

  • Stadium concerts at Anfield in June (2026 had Zach Bryan, Foo Fighters and My Chemical Romance). See Anfield concert accommodation
  • Africa Oyé in Sefton Park in June
  • On the Waterfront at the Pier Head in June
  • Big gigs in Sefton Park in August
  • International Beatleweek in late August (26 August to 1 September in 2026). See our Beatleweek guide
  • Southport Flower Show and the Southport Air Show in late August

School holidays: Liverpool's 2027 summer holiday runs from 22 July to 31 August.

Good for: festival-goers, families, people who want long light evenings. Downside: higher prices and busier attractions, especially in August.

Autumn (September to November)

Weather: September is often pleasant. October and November get wetter and windier.

Events: - Football season in full swing at Anfield and the Hill Dickinson Stadium - River of Light, a free light festival on the waterfront, ran from 23 October to 1 November 2026 - British Musical Fireworks Championships in Southport in late September - October half term 2026 ran from 26 to 30 October

Good for: football fans, city breaks, museum days. Prices outside event weekends are often lower than summer.

Winter (December to February)

Weather: the coldest months, with December the wettest of the year on average.

Events: - Christmas markets and festive events in the city centre in the run-up to Christmas. See our Christmas markets guide - The Cavern Club turns 70 on 16 January 2027, with the 70 Years Of The Cavern Club exhibition at the Museum of Liverpool from 16 January to 3 October 2027 - Lightport in Southport in February, part of Sefton's events programme - February half term 2027: 15 to 19 February

Good for: Christmas family stays, budget breaks, museum and gallery trips, Beatles fans in 2027.

See our Christmas family stay guide.

When is Liverpool most expensive?

The weeks that tend to fill up first are:

  1. Grand National week (8 to 10 April 2027)
  2. Big football weekends, especially the Merseyside derby and European nights
  3. Stadium concert weekends in early summer
  4. Late August (Beatleweek and the bank holiday)
  5. December weekends

If your dates are flexible, a midweek stay outside those periods is usually the best value.

When is Liverpool quietest?

  • January and early February, after Christmas
  • Midweek in March, before Easter
  • Midweek in November, between half term and the Christmas markets

Planning tips for any season

  • Check fixtures before booking. A home game changes traffic and trains in north Liverpool
  • Book restaurants on event weekends
  • Pack layers and a waterproof all year round
  • Use the train into the centre. From Zermatt Lodge it's an easy ride to Liverpool Central
  • Have a rainy day plan. The waterfront museums are a good bet

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the best month to visit Liverpool?

For mild weather without the peak crowds, May, June and September are good choices. Met Office averages for Crosby, near Liverpool, show maximums of about 16°C in May, 18°C in June and 18°C in September.

What's the wettest time of year in Liverpool?

Autumn and early winter. The Met Office's 1991-2020 averages for Crosby show October, November and December as the wettest months, each with over 80mm of rain.

When are the big events in Liverpool in 2027?

Confirmed so far: the Cavern Club's 70th anniversary exhibition at the Museum of Liverpool (16 January to 3 October 2027) and the Grand National Festival at Aintree (8 to 10 April 2027). Beatleweek usually runs in late August.

When is Liverpool busiest?

Grand National week in April, big football weekends, stadium concerts, late August (Beatleweek and the bank holiday) and the run-up to Christmas. Book accommodation early for those dates.
