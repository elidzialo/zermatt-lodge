Corporate Housing in Liverpool: What It Is and How to Book It

Corporate housing in Liverpool explained: what it includes, typical stay lengths, how it compares with hotels, and what HR and relocation teams should check.

Quick answer

Corporate housing is a fully furnished home rented for work, usually for 30 days or longer, with utilities, wifi and housewares included in one rate. Companies use it for relocating staff, project teams and secondments. In Liverpool, options range from city-centre apartments to whole houses with parking. Corporate housing is a fully furnished home rented for work, usually for 30 days or longer, with utilities, wifi and housewares included in one rate. Companies book it for relocating employees, project teams, secondments and new starters who need somewhere to live while they find a permanent home. The booker is often HR or a relocation agency, not the person who stays.

This guide explains how it works and what to check when booking in Liverpool.

What it includes

A typical corporate let comes with:

  • Furniture, kitchenware and linens
  • Utilities and wifi in the rate
  • Often cleaning and parking
  • One monthly price, so there are no separate bills to set up

That "all-in" set-up is the point: the person can move in with a suitcase and start work the next day.

How long stays run

Industry guides describe corporate housing stays as running from about 30 days to a year, with relocations and project stays often lasting two to three months. Extensions are common when a project overruns or a house purchase is delayed.

Corporate housing vs hotels and serviced apartments

  • Extended-stay hotel: nightly pricing, daily services, compact rooms
  • Corporate housing: a residential home, monthly pricing, a full kitchen and room to live and work
  • Serviced accommodation: a broader term covering stays from a couple of nights up. See what is serviced accommodation?

For stays of several weeks, a furnished home usually costs less per month than a hotel, and it's far easier for a family.

Who uses it in Liverpool

  • Relocating staff and families moving to Merseyside
  • Project teams on construction, port or industrial work
  • NHS and healthcare staff on contracts or placements
  • Consultants and secondees at city-centre offices
  • Families displaced by insurance claims

Checklist for HR and relocation teams

  1. Commute: where is the office or site, and how long is the journey at rush hour?
  2. Beds: check the actual bed layout for a family or team
  3. Parking: city-centre blocks often have none
  4. Wifi and a workspace for remote days
  5. Laundry: a washer and dryer for longer stays
  6. Cleaning: included, weekly or on request?
  7. Paperwork: whose name is on the booking, invoices in the company's name, and VAT. See our invoice and VAT guide
  8. Flexibility: can the stay be extended or shortened, and on what terms?

Choosing an area

Liverpool's city centre suits single professionals working in the business district. Families and teams with cars often prefer a house outside the centre, with gardens, parking and room to spread out. Our moving to Liverpool guide and temporary house for relocating guide cover areas and schools.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is corporate housing?

A fully furnished home rented for work, typically for 30 days or longer, with utilities, internet and housewares included in one rate. The person who signs is often the employer, not the person who stays.

How long are corporate housing stays?

Usually from about a month up to a year. Relocations and project stays often last two to three months, and extensions are common.

Is corporate housing cheaper than a hotel?

For stays of several weeks, it's usually cheaper per month than an extended-stay hotel, and you get a kitchen and separate living space. Compare total costs, including parking and meals.

Can a company book Zermatt Lodge?

Yes. Stays run from 2 to 30 nights, with monthly rates. Ask us before booking about an invoice in your company's name. For stays beyond 30 nights, message us.

What should HR check before booking corporate housing?

Location and commute, the number of beds, parking, laundry, wifi, cleaning, the cancellation terms, who is named on the booking, and whether the host can issue invoices.
