Liverpool Christmas Market 2026: Skating, Markets and How to Plan Your Visit

Liverpool Christmas Market 2026: Skate St George's and its market from 21 November, the Pier Head Wonderland, pop-up markets and getting there by train.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

The Liverpool Christmas Market for 2026 is on St George's Plateau, opposite Lime Street station, as part of Skate St George's, which Underbelly lists from 21 November 2026 to 3 January 2027. Alongside the outdoor ice rink there's a Christmas market with local traders, a rooftop bar and a Big Wheel. The city also has a Christmas Wonderland at the Pier Head and pop-up markets on selected dates.

Liverpool's Christmas line-up changes from year to year, with different operators and sites, so this guide sticks to what's been announced for 2026 and tells you where to check the details.

What's on for Christmas 2026

Skate St George's (St George's Plateau)

  • Dates: 21 November 2026 to 3 January 2027
  • Run by: Underbelly, appointed by Liverpool City Council
  • What's there: an outdoor ice rink, a Christmas market with local traders, a rooftop bar serving mulled wine, hot chocolate and sparkling drinks, and a Big Wheel
  • Tickets: skating needs booking. Opening times, prices and session lengths are on skatestgeorges.co.uk

St George's Hall is one of the city's grandest buildings, and the plateau in front of it is the traditional home of the Christmas market. In previous years, the market ran here under a different operator, so if you've been before, expect a new layout.

Liverpool Christmas Wonderland @ Pier Head

  • Run by: Icebox Live
  • What's there: a covered ice rink, thrill rides, games and experiences, beneath the Three Graces on the waterfront
  • Details: dates and opening times are on liverpoolchristmaswonderland.com

A covered rink is a good bet if the weather turns, which in Liverpool in December it often does. Our ice skating in Liverpool guide compares the rinks.

Pop-up markets

Liverpool City Council has announced pop-up seasonal markets at Basnett Street and the Cunard Building on selected dates, featuring local and independent businesses. Dates were still to be confirmed when we checked, so look at the council's markets pages before you go.

Liverpool City Halls

The council's festive programme also includes events at Liverpool City Halls, such as A Christmas Carol, Carols by Lantern Light and Story Time with Santa. These are ticketed. Our guide to Christmas lights and grottos in Liverpool covers more family events.

The council's regular markets

In 2024 the council's markets team ran festive specials at venues such as Stanley Park and Exchange Flags, and Great Homer Street Market ("Greatie") added extra Sunday openings in December. Check whether similar events are running in 2026.

Getting there

By train

  • St George's Plateau is directly opposite Liverpool Lime Street. If you're on Merseyrail, Lime Street's underground platforms and Liverpool Central are both a short walk away.
  • Pier Head: the nearest Merseyrail stations are James Street and Moorfields.
  • Merseyrail accepts contactless cards, phones and watches. Tap in and tap out.

By car

City-centre car parks fill up on December weekends. If you're staying outside the centre, it's usually easier to park at your accommodation and take the train. Our city centre parking guide lists car parks if you do drive.

Christmas Day and Boxing Day

Merseyrail doesn't normally run on Christmas Day. Check the Liverpool City Region festive transport timetable for Christmas Eve, Boxing Day and New Year before you plan.

Tips for visiting

  • Go on a weekday afternoon if you can. Weekend evenings in December are the busiest.
  • Book skating in advance. Popular sessions sell out, especially at weekends and in the last week before Christmas.
  • Dress for the wind. St George's Plateau and the Pier Head are both exposed.
  • Bring a card and a little cash, in case a stall is card-only or cash-only.
  • Combine it with the city. Liverpool ONE, the waterfront and the museums are all close by.
  • Plan food. Market food is fun but pricey for a family. Eat a proper meal before or after.

Make a weekend of it

December is busy in Liverpool beyond the markets:

  • Becher Chase Day at Aintree on Saturday 5 December 2026, with races over the Grand National fences. See our Becher Chase weekend guide.
  • Boxing Day racing at Aintree on Saturday 26 December 2026.
  • Premier League football at Anfield and Hill Dickinson Stadium most weekends.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is the Liverpool Christmas Market 2026?

Underbelly lists Skate St George's, with its Christmas market, rooftop bar and Big Wheel, on St George's Plateau from 21 November 2026 to 3 January 2027. Check opening times and tickets on the official Skate St George's website.

Where is the Christmas market in Liverpool?

The main Christmas market for 2026 is part of Skate St George's on St George's Plateau, opposite Lime Street station. Liverpool City Council has also announced the Christmas Wonderland at the Pier Head and pop-up markets at Basnett Street and the Cunard Building.

Is there ice skating in Liverpool at Christmas 2026?

Yes, two rinks were announced: an outdoor rink at Skate St George's run by Underbelly, and a covered rink at Liverpool Christmas Wonderland at the Pier Head, run by Icebox Live.

How do I get to the Liverpool Christmas Market by train?

St George's Plateau is right opposite Liverpool Lime Street. On Merseyrail, Lime Street and Liverpool Central are both a short walk away. For the Pier Head, James Street and Moorfields are the nearest Merseyrail stations.

Do trains run in Liverpool on Christmas Day?

Merseyrail doesn't normally run on Christmas Day. Check the Liverpool City Region festive transport timetable each year before planning travel over Christmas.
