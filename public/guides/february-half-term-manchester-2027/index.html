February Half Term 2027 in Manchester

February half term 2027 in Manchester: school dates, the free Super Duper Family Festival on 17 to 20 February, indoor and outdoor days out across Greater Manchester, and family homes to stay in.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Manchester City Council lists schools closing on Friday 12 February 2027 and reopening on Monday 22 February. Visit Manchester has announced the free Super Duper Family Festival for Wednesday 17 to Saturday 20 February 2027, with play zones across the city centre. Other ideas include the free Manchester Museum, The Snow Centre at TraffordCity and RHS Garden Bridgewater, which opens until 5pm from 6 February. Other boroughs may set slightly different dates, so check your child's school.

February half term is a good time for a short family break in Manchester: there's a free festival in the city centre and plenty to do indoors if the weather turns. We checked these details on 25 November 2026. Some events haven't published full plans yet, so check again in January.

Half term dates

  • Manchester City Council lists schools closing on Friday 12 February 2027 and reopening on Monday 22 February 2027.
  • Other Greater Manchester boroughs and academies can set their own dates. Check with your child's school before booking.

The Super Duper Family Festival

  • Dates: Wednesday 17 to Saturday 20 February 2027, as listed by Visit Manchester.
  • Price: free.
  • What it is: themed play zones across the city centre.
  • Last year's zones (2026) were at St Ann's Square, Exchange Square, Printworks, Manchester Arndale, Central Library, Great Northern Warehouse, the People's History Museum, St John's, Aviva Studios and the Science and Industry Museum. Most ran 10am to 5pm. The 2027 venues weren't listed when we checked.

Indoor days out

  • Manchester Museum (free): dinosaurs including Stan the T. rex. Its Vivarium is closed until May 2027.
  • People's History Museum (free): closed on Tuesdays.
  • IWM North at Salford Quays (free).
  • Salford Museum and Art Gallery (free), with a recreated Victorian street. See our Salford guide.
  • The Snow Centre Manchester, the former Chill Factore, for real snow indoors. See our Snow Centre and Trafford Centre guide.

More ideas are in our rainy day guide for kids.

Outdoor days out

  • RHS Garden Bridgewater, Worsley: from 6 February it's open 10am to 5pm (last entry 4pm). Children aged 5 to 16 are £6. See our Bridgewater guide.
  • Dunham Massey: the deer park is open all year. Check the National Trust calendar for house opening in February. See our Dunham Massey guide.
  • Heaton Park, Prestwich: open 8am to dusk. See our Heaton Park guide.
  • Ramsbottom and Peel Tower, for older children who like a proper climb. See our Ramsbottom guide.

Booking tips

  • Book the stay early. Half term is one of the busiest weeks of the winter for family homes.
  • Mix free and paid days to keep the cost down.
  • Check January for updates. Festivals and museums usually publish half-term timetables a few weeks before.

Frequently asked questions

When is February half term 2027 in Manchester?

Manchester City Council lists schools closing on Friday 12 February and reopening on Monday 22 February 2027. Schools in other boroughs and academies can differ, so check with your school.

When is the Super Duper Family Festival 2027?

Visit Manchester lists it for Wednesday 17 to Saturday 20 February 2027. It is free.

What was in the Super Duper Family Festival last year?

In 2026 there were free play zones at places including St Ann's Square, Exchange Square, Printworks, Manchester Arndale, Central Library, Great Northern Warehouse, the People's History Museum and the Science and Industry Museum, mostly 10am to 5pm. Venues for 2027 had not been listed when we checked.

Is Manchester Museum open at half term?

It is normally open Tuesday to Sunday and free. Its Vivarium is closed until May 2027. Check the museum's website for half-term events.
