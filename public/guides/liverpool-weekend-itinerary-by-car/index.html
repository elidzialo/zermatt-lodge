Liverpool Weekend by Car: Avoiding City Parking

Driving to Liverpool for the weekend? Leave the car for the coast and Knowsley Safari, take the train into the city centre, and know the car parks for Albert Dock, Crosby and match days.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

If you're driving to Liverpool, use the car for the places public transport doesn't reach well (Knowsley Safari, Formby, Crosby Beach) and take Merseyrail into the city centre, where parking is pricey and busy. Stay somewhere with free parking. On Saturday, do the city by train; on Sunday, drive to the coast or the safari park. On match days, avoid driving near Anfield and Hill Dickinson Stadium.

A car makes a Liverpool weekend more flexible: you can reach the beaches, the pinewoods and the safari park without juggling trains. But it's a pain in the city centre, where parking is busy and adds up fast. The trick is to use the car for the right days.

We checked the details below on 24 November 2026.

The rule of thumb

Going to Best way
City centre, Albert Dock, Pier Head, Cavern Train (Merseyrail)
Anfield or Hill Dickinson Stadium on a match day Train or bus, not the car
Crosby Beach Either. Car parks by the beach, or Merseyrail
Formby Either, but car parks fill fast on sunny days
Knowsley Safari Car. It's a drive-through

Friday: arrive and park up

Aim to arrive mid-afternoon, before the Friday rush. Do a food shop on the way, park at your accommodation and leave the car there until Sunday. Staying somewhere with free parking saves a lot over a weekend. See our group accommodation with parking guide.

Saturday: the city, by train

  • Morning: train into the city centre. Start at the Royal Albert Dock and The Beatles Story (open from 9am, last winter Saturday entry 5.15pm)
  • Lunch: Liverpool ONE or the dock
  • Afternoon: the Pier Head, the free Museum of Liverpool, or the Mersey Ferry
  • Evening: the Cavern Club or a show, then the train back

No parking charges, no driving after a drink, no circling for a space.

If you do drive into town

Q-Park lists Liverpool ONE (about a 5-minute walk from the Albert Dock), Hanover Street and the John Lewis car park. The Beatles Story also points to the car park opposite the dock entrance. Check prices before you go.

Sunday: drive out

Pick one:

Crosby Beach and Another Place

Antony Gormley's 100 iron figures along more than 3km of beach. Free. Check the tide: at high tide you can't get onto the beach. Sefton Council lists parking at Hall Road (closest to the statues), Crosby Leisure Centre and Crosby Lakeside. See our Crosby Beach guide.

Formby

National Trust pinewoods, dunes and red squirrels. Car parks fill quickly on fine days, so go early. See our Formby guide.

Knowsley Safari

A drive-through route of about 5 miles with lions, rhinos, giraffes, zebras and more. It's been open since 1971. Allow most of the day.

Match days: leave the car behind

If Liverpool or Everton are at home, roads near the grounds get very busy before and after the game, and there are parking restrictions on the streets around both grounds. Plan around it, or take the train or bus. Our match-day parking guide has the details.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it worth driving to Liverpool for a weekend?

Yes, if you want to see the coast or Knowsley Safari too. For the city centre alone, the train is easier and usually cheaper than parking.

Where do I park for the Albert Dock?

Q-Park names Liverpool ONE (about a 5-minute walk), Hanover Street and the John Lewis car park. The Beatles Story also points to the car park opposite the dock entrance.

Where do I park for Crosby Beach?

Sefton Council lists Hall Road car park in Blundellsands (closest to Another Place), Crosby Leisure Centre on Mariners Road, and two car parks by Crosby Lakeside off Cambridge Road in Waterloo.

Should I drive on a match day?

Avoid it if you can. Roads around Anfield and Hill Dickinson Stadium get very busy, and there are parking restrictions on match days. See our match-day parking guide.

Can I drive round Knowsley Safari in my own car?

Yes. Knowsley Safari's main attraction is a drive-through route of about 5 miles, with animals including lions, rhinos and giraffes.
