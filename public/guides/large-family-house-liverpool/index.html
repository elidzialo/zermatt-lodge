Family days out near Fazakerley

Croxteth Hall and Country Park

Croxteth is close to Fazakerley and makes an easy first-day outing. The Victorian Walled Garden opens daily from 10am to 3pm (last admission 2:30pm). The country park is open all year, with inner park hours varying by season. There's also Home Farm, which is run separately, so check its own opening times. The hall itself opens for tours and events, so contact it before visiting. See our Croxteth Hall visitor guide.

Knowsley Safari

Knowsley Safari is a drive-through safari reached via the M57, from junction 2. The safari drive's last entry is 3pm, and you'll be turned away if you arrive later, so set off in good time. See our Knowsley Safari guide.

Crosby Beach and Another Place

Antony Gormley's 100 cast-iron figures stand along more than 3km of Crosby Beach. Sefton Council advises staying close to the promenade and not walking out to the furthest statues, because the sand there is very soft and muddy. Check tide times, as the figures are covered around high tide on big tides. See our Another Place guide.

Liverpool's museums

The Museum of Liverpool, World Museum and the Merseyside Maritime Museum are all in or near the city centre, a short train ride from Fazakerley to Liverpool Central. They're great for rainy days.

Tips for a smooth family stay

  1. Arrive after 3pm and do a big shop. There's a Lidl close by.
  2. Plan one outing per day. Children (and grandparents) tire quickly.
  3. Mix train days and car days. Use the train for the city and the car for the safari or beach.
  4. Book popular activities ahead in school holidays.
  5. Keep a rainy-day plan: board games, the games room and Netflix at home.
  6. Pack for the coast. It can be windy on the beaches even in summer.
  7. Remember checkout is 11am and plan a last-day outing on the way home.

Mealtimes for a big family

Feeding seven people three times a day is the part of a family holiday that catches people out. A few ideas:

  • Breakfast: a big pot of porridge or a fry-up at the weekend, with cereal and toast on busy mornings
  • Lunch: packed lunches on days out are cheaper and quicker than finding a café for seven
  • Dinner: one-pot meals like chilli, curry or a traybake, cooked once and served at the dining table
  • One night off: a takeaway or a meal out, so the cooks get a break

Give each adult a night to cook and get the children to set the table. See our self-catering meal plan for a group of 7.
