Visiting Broadgreen Hospital: A Practical Guide

Visiting Broadgreen Hospital in Liverpool? The car park and charges, Blue Badge parking, the walk from Broad Green station, the shuttle bus and where to stay.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Broadgreen Hospital is on Thomas Drive in east Liverpool (satnav postcode L14 3LB). Its multi-storey car park is open 24/7: under 30 minutes is free, charges run from £4.50 for up to an hour to £8.50 for over six hours, and a 7-day pass is £22.50. Blue Badge holders park free in marked bays. Broad Green station is a 5 to 10 minute walk away.

Broadgreen Hospital is part of Liverpool University Hospitals (UHL), on Thomas Drive in east Liverpool. If you're going for an appointment or visiting someone, the essentials are: park in the 24/7 multi-storey, or walk 5 to 10 minutes from Broad Green station. Blue Badge holders park free in marked bays. For repeat visits, the 7-day parking pass is the best value.

Getting there by car

Use the postcode L14 3LB for satnav. The multi-storey car park is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and charges apply.

Parking charges

UHL lists these visitor prices across its sites, from 1 July 2025:

Time Charge
Under 30 minutes Free
Up to 1 hour £4.50
Up to 2 hours £5.00
Up to 4 hours £6.00
Up to 6 hours £7.00
More than 6 hours £8.50
7-day pass £22.50

UHL says longer-stay prices fell at Broadgreen when these prices came in. Concessions remain for patients with cancer or long-term conditions and for people facing significant clinic delays, among others. Ask at the hospital if you think you qualify.

Blue Badge

Blue Badge holders park free. Display the badge in a marked bay, because the car park uses number plate recognition.

Getting there by train and bus

  • Broad Green station is about a 5 to 10 minute walk, via Thomas Lane, Thomas Drive, South Road and West Road.
  • Buses stop at or near the hospital. Check Merseytravel, Arriva or Stagecoach for routes, or call Traveline on 0871 200 2233.

On site

  • Shuttle bus: runs Monday to Friday, 8am to 5.30pm, stopping at the main building entrances
  • Taxis: a freephone taxi line is at the main entrance
  • Bikes: cycle parking at the two main entrances and the Alexandra Wing, with CCTV
  • Cycle route: links to the city outskirts and the Liverpool Loop Line

Tips for appointments

  • Allow extra time for parking at busy times of day.
  • Bring your letter and know which building or clinic you need.
  • Early appointment? If you live far away, staying nearby the night before is less stressful. See our early morning appointment guide.
  • Moving between sites? Patients are sometimes seen at more than one UHL hospital. See our guides to the Royal Liverpool and Aintree.

Where to stay

Broadgreen is in east Liverpool, a residential area with few large holiday homes. If your family needs a whole house, a house elsewhere in Liverpool with parking can work: check the drive in a map app for your appointment time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is parking at Broadgreen Hospital?

Liverpool University Hospitals lists under 30 minutes free, up to 1 hour £4.50, up to 2 hours £5, up to 4 hours £6, up to 6 hours £7 and over 6 hours £8.50. A 7-day pass is £22.50. These prices have applied since 1 July 2025.

Is Blue Badge parking free at Broadgreen?

Yes. Blue Badge holders park free, but must display the badge in a marked bay, as number plate recognition is in use.

What is the nearest station to Broadgreen Hospital?

Broad Green station, about a 5 to 10 minute walk away via Thomas Lane, Thomas Drive, South Road and West Road.

Is there a shuttle bus at Broadgreen Hospital?

The hospital lists a shuttle bus that runs Monday to Friday, 8am to 5.30pm, stopping at main building entrances. Ask on arrival for the current timetable.
