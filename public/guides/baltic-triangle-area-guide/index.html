The Baltic Triangle, Liverpool: What to Know Before You Go

A plain guide to Liverpool's Baltic Triangle: Cains Brewery Village, Baltic Market, BOXPARK and the ICEBOX rink, how to get there and where to stay with a group.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

The Baltic Triangle is Liverpool's creative quarter, a district of old warehouses south of the city centre that now holds studios, bars, live music venues and food markets. Cains Brewery Village is its busiest corner, with BOXPARK Liverpool and its ICEBOX ice rink (open daily 16 November 2026 to 10 January 2027). Liverpool Central is the closest station today, and a new Liverpool Baltic station is planned for 2027.

If you're visiting Liverpool with a group of friends or older kids, the Baltic Triangle is worth an afternoon or an evening. It's the city's creative quarter: old warehouses turned into studios, bars, music venues and street-food halls, a short walk south of the city centre.

We checked the details below on 25 November 2026. Venues change their hours often, especially over Christmas, so check before you set off.

What's there

Visit Liverpool describes the area as a mix of live music, street art, nightlife and daytime activities, running along Jamaica Street and into Cains Brewery Village. The main names you'll see:

Place What it is
Cains Brewery Village The old Cains Brewery, now coffee shops, food markets and bars
Baltic Market Liverpool's first street food market, with a rotating mix of traders and events
BOXPARK Liverpool Street food, bars and big-screen sport, plus the ICEBOX ice rink in winter
Camp and Furnace Large warehouse venue for live acts and events
Jacaranda Baltic Music venue that hosts showcases and gigs

ICEBOX ice rink, winter 2026

BOXPARK's ICEBOX rink is open daily from 16 November 2026 to 10 January 2027 in Cains Brewery Village.

  • Children (3 to 12): £8, with a maximum of 2 children per adult
  • Adults and juniors (13 and over): from £10
  • Students: £10 with valid ID
  • Every ticket includes skate hire and 45 minutes on the ice
  • Adult-only advanced sessions are also listed

Our ice skating in Liverpool guide compares ICEBOX with Skate St George's and the Pier Head rink.

Eating in the Baltic

The Baltic is mostly about street food and shared tables, which suits groups who all want something different. Baltic Market and the food halls at BOXPARK are the easy options. For other halls and markets across the city, see our Liverpool food markets guide.

Getting there

  • Train: Liverpool Central is the closest station today. Visit Liverpool says a new Liverpool Baltic station is planned to open in 2027.
  • On foot: the district is walkable from the city centre and the Albert Dock.
  • Taxi: the simplest choice late at night, especially in winter.

See our getting around Liverpool guide for train tickets and contactless.

Tips for groups

  • Weekend evenings are the busiest time. For a calmer visit, go at lunchtime or early evening.
  • Some venues are 18+ in the evening. If you have teenagers with you, check each venue's age rules first. Our Liverpool with teenagers guide has more ideas.
  • Wrap up. A lot of the seating is outdoors or in open-sided warehouses.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the Baltic Triangle in Liverpool?

It is a district of repurposed warehouses south of the city centre. Visit Liverpool calls it the city's creative quarter, with offices, studios, hotels, clubs, bars, live music and street art.

What is Cains Brewery Village?

The old Cains Brewery, now turned into a cluster of coffee shops, food markets and bars. BOXPARK Liverpool and its winter ICEBOX rink are there.

When is ICEBOX at BOXPARK Liverpool open?

Daily from 16 November 2026 to 10 January 2027. Child tickets (3 to 12) are £8 and adult or junior tickets (13 and over) start at £10, including skate hire and 45 minutes on the ice.

What is the nearest station to the Baltic Triangle?

Liverpool Central is the closest station for now. Visit Liverpool says a new Liverpool Baltic station is planned to open in 2027.
