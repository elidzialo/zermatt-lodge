A Day Trip to New Brighton from Liverpool

A winter day trip to New Brighton on the Wirral: Fort Perch Rock, the 1830 lighthouse, the Victorian Quarter, the Floral Pavilion's Aladdin panto (5 Dec 2026 to 3 Jan 2027) and how to get there by Merseyrail.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

New Brighton is a seaside town on the Wirral, across the Mersey from Liverpool, with its own Merseyrail station. In winter the highlights are Fort Perch Rock (with a museum and an escape room), the lighthouse built in 1830, the Victorian Quarter's independent bars and shops, and the Floral Pavilion panto. Aladdin runs there from Saturday 5 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027, with tickets from £15.

New Brighton sits on the tip of the Wirral, looking back across the Mersey at Liverpool. It's an easy half-day or full-day trip from the city, and in winter it's at its best on a clear, cold day: a bracing sea walk, a warm café, and maybe a panto in the evening.

We checked these details on 25 November 2026. Check times and tickets before you travel.

What to see and do

  • Fort Perch Rock. A historic coastal fort on the promenade. Merseyrail's guide says its Aviation and Archaeology Museum is open year-round, and Visit Wirral lists Escape Fort Perch, an escape room inside the fort.
  • New Brighton Lighthouse. Built in 1830, it's the classic photo spot at sunrise or sunset.
  • The Victorian Quarter. Visit Wirral describes it as full of independent bars, restaurants, music venues, record shops and vintage stores, with a street art trail.
  • Seafront fun. Championship Adventure Golf, the Mermaid Trail, a bowling alley, an amusement arcade and the beach itself.

Floral Pavilion panto: Aladdin

The Floral Pavilion, Wirral's largest theatre, stages Aladdin from Saturday 5 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027. Sean Jones returns as Wishee Washee for his seventh year. Tickets start at £15.

For pantos on the Liverpool side, see our Liverpool panto 2026 guide.

Getting there

  • Train: New Brighton has its own Merseyrail station. A Merseyrail Day Saver covers a return trip. Prices are in our getting around Liverpool guide.
  • Ferry plus walk: for a scenic route, take a Mersey Ferry across the river and walk along the promenade. See our Mersey Ferry guide for sailings.
  • Car: the Mersey tunnels are tolled, so check the current toll before you go.

Winter tips

  • The wind is strong on the front. Hats, gloves and waterproofs make a big difference.
  • Check the tide before walking out near the fort and lighthouse.
  • Daylight is short in December, so start in the late morning if you want to finish the walk before dark.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I get to New Brighton from Liverpool?

By Merseyrail. New Brighton has its own station on the Wirral side, and trains run from Liverpool city centre stations. You can also drive through the Mersey tunnels, which are tolled.

What is there to do in New Brighton in winter?

Walk the promenade to Fort Perch Rock and the lighthouse, visit the fort's museum or its escape room, explore the Victorian Quarter's independent bars, shops and street art, play adventure golf, or see a show at the Floral Pavilion.

What is the Floral Pavilion panto for 2026?

Aladdin, from Saturday 5 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027. Sean Jones returns as Wishee Washee for his seventh year. Tickets start at £15.

Is Fort Perch Rock open in winter?

Merseyrail's destination guide says the fort's Aviation and Archaeology Museum is open year-round. Check the fort's own opening days before you go, as small museums often keep short winter hours.
