Two Matches, One Trip: Merseyside Double-Headers This Winter

Want to see Liverpool and Everton on one trip? The best Anfield and Hill Dickinson Stadium double-headers from December 2026 to February 2027, plus where to stay.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

The best chances to see both Liverpool and Everton at home on one trip this winter are Everton v Manchester City (Wed 30 Dec 2026, 8pm) then Liverpool v Coventry (Sat 2 Jan 2027, 3pm), or Liverpool v Sunderland (Wed 2 Dec, 8pm) then Everton v Fulham (Sat 5 Dec, 3pm). Premier League kick-offs can move for TV, so book flexible travel.

Liverpool is one of the few cities where you can see two Premier League clubs at home on the same trip. With Everton now at Hill Dickinson Stadium on the waterfront and Liverpool at Anfield, a double-header is a great few days for fans, groundhoppers and overseas visitors.

Fixtures below are from the Sky Sports fixture lists, with TV changes checked on 23 November 2026. Kick-off times can still move.

The best double-headers, December 2026 to February 2027

1. Midweek and weekend: 2 to 5 December

Date Match Ground
Wed 2 Dec, 8pm Liverpool v Sunderland Anfield
Sat 5 Dec, 3pm Everton v Fulham Hill Dickinson Stadium

Bonus: Saturday 5 December is also Becher Chase Day at Aintree.

2. Over New Year: 30 December to 2 January

Date Match Ground
Wed 30 Dec, 8pm Everton v Manchester City Hill Dickinson Stadium
Sat 2 Jan, 3pm Liverpool v Coventry Anfield

Four nights takes in New Year's Eve in the city too. Check the festive train timetable, as services are reduced around the bank holidays.

3. Boxing Day: football and racing

Date Event Venue
Sat 26 Dec Boxing Day racing Aintree Racecourse
Sat 26 Dec, 3pm Everton v Sunderland Hill Dickinson Stadium

You can't easily do both on the same afternoon, so pick one for Boxing Day and see Liverpool or Everton on another day. Our Christmas fixtures guide has the full list.

4. February: two Everton games in five days

Date Match Ground
Sat 6 Feb, 3pm Everton v Newcastle Hill Dickinson Stadium
Wed 10 Feb, 8pm Everton v Leeds Hill Dickinson Stadium

Then Liverpool v Hull at Anfield on Sat 20 Feb, 3pm.

The big one: the derby

Liverpool v Everton, Anfield, Saturday 30 January 2027, 12.30pm (moved for TNT Sports). It's the hardest ticket of the winter. See our Merseyside derby weekend guide.

Tips for a double-header

  1. Tickets first, travel second. Only book non-refundable travel once you have tickets from the official clubs. See our ticket guide
  2. Watch for TV moves. Games are often moved a few weeks ahead
  3. Midweek nights finish late. An 8pm kick-off means getting home close to midnight. See our midweek match night guide
  4. Don't plan to park at the ground. There's no match-day parking at Anfield, and Everton's stadium has a wide match-day parking zone. Use trains, shuttle buses or taxis. Our match-day parking guide explains the options
  5. Fill the day in between. A stadium tour, the museums or a ferry cruise

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I see Liverpool and Everton at home in the same week this winter?

Yes. Liverpool host Sunderland on Wed 2 Dec 2026 and Everton host Fulham on Sat 5 Dec. Everton host Manchester City on Wed 30 Dec and Liverpool host Coventry on Sat 2 Jan 2027.

When is the Merseyside derby?

Liverpool v Everton at Anfield on Saturday 30 January 2027, moved to 12.30pm for TNT Sports.

Can I add racing to a football trip?

Yes. On Sat 5 Dec 2026 it's Becher Chase Day at Aintree, the same day as Everton v Fulham. On Boxing Day there's racing at Aintree and Everton v Sunderland.

Will the kick-off times change?

They can. The Premier League moves games for TV, usually a few weeks ahead. Check the club websites before you book anything you can't change.

How do I get tickets for both?

Use the official club ticketing sites only. Most games need a membership or ballot. See our LFC and Everton ticket guide.
