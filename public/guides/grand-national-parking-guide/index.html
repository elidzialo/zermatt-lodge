Grand National Parking: Pre-Booking, Road Closures and Smarter Alternatives

Driving to the Grand National? Why parking must be pre-booked, the Steeplechase car park crossing, road closures, park and ride and when not to drive.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

For the Grand National Festival, you can only park at Aintree Racecourse if you've booked a space in advance. On festival days, roads around the course close, and the busiest car park has a crossing that shuts around the feature race, so many racegoers find it easier to leave the car at their accommodation and take the train instead.

The 2027 festival runs from Thursday 8 to Saturday 10 April. Here's how parking works, based on the Jockey Club's official travel plan.

The basics

  • Address: Aintree Racecourse, Ormskirk Road, Liverpool L9 5AS
  • Road: the A59, about a mile from the M57 and M58
  • Festival parking: must be booked in advance through the racecourse's online booking
  • On non-festival race days: follow the A59 and yellow tourism signs to the car parks

If you haven't booked, don't drive to the course hoping for a space. Plan to park elsewhere and travel in.

The Steeplechase Car Park and the crossing

The Steeplechase Car Park catches out more people than anything else.

  • You reach it via a course crossing at Melling Road.
  • That crossing closes 30 minutes before the feature race over the Grand National course and reopens 30 minutes after it.
  • No vehicle movement is allowed while the course crossings are closed.
  • After parking, you walk to the Mildmay Course shuttle for the Racecourse Enclosures.
  • On Saturday, ticket holders for the Embankment Enclosure use the course crossing next to the Embankment instead.

What it means for you: if you want to leave early, before the big race, or straight after it, you could be stuck. Plan to stay until the crossing reopens, or park elsewhere.

Road closures

The Jockey Club says a series of road closures in and around Aintree supports the festival transport plan.

  • Closures are applied for through Liverpool City Council, Sefton Council and other councils as needed.
  • A traffic management contractor puts them in place and maintains them.
  • The racecourse publishes a road closure map. The version linked at the time of writing was labelled for an earlier year, so check you've got the current one.

Expect diversions on the A59 and surrounding roads, and allow much longer than a normal journey.

Alternatives to parking at the course

Train

This is how most racegoers get there. On festival days, the Jockey Club says:

  • Trains between Liverpool Central or Moorfields and Aintree run about every 7.5 minutes, roughly 11:00 to 13:00 towards Aintree and 16:30 to 20:30 back.
  • Aintree station is across the road from the racecourse.
  • On the Kirkby line, trains start and end at Sandhills between 11:00 and 14:00 and between 17:00 and 21:00, so passengers to or from Rice Lane, Fazakerley, Kirkby or Headbolt Lane change at Sandhills.

Our guide to getting to Aintree Racecourse covers every route.

Park at a station and ride in

Merseyrail has free car parks at many stations. Everton's matchday guidance mentions over 4,400 free spaces at more than 40 stations. Parking at a station outside the closure area and taking the train avoids the traffic around the course. Check which station car parks are open and how busy they get on festival days.

Bus

Services 300, 310 and 345 run to and from Liverpool. Check with Merseytravel for any festival changes.

Park at your accommodation

If you're staying in Liverpool, the simplest plan is often to leave the car where you're staying. Check before you book that the accommodation has off-street parking, and how you'll get from there to the course.

Tips for drivers

  • Book early. Festival parking sells out.
  • Arrive early. The roads fill well before the first race.
  • Know your exit plan. Decide in advance whether you're leaving before the feature race or waiting until well after.
  • Don't park on residential streets. Local roads near the course are busy, and some will be closed.
  • Pick a designated driver if you're driving home.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book parking for the Grand National?

Yes. The Jockey Club's festival travel page says car parking must be booked in advance. Don't drive to Aintree on a festival day expecting to pay on the gate.

What is the Steeplechase Car Park crossing closure?

Access to the Steeplechase Car Park uses a course crossing at Melling Road. The Jockey Club says it closes 30 minutes before and reopens 30 minutes after the feature race over the Grand National course, and no vehicles can move while it's closed.

Are there road closures around Aintree for the Grand National?

Yes. A series of road closures supports the festival transport plan. They're applied for through Liverpool City Council and Sefton Council and put in place by a traffic management contractor. Check the latest road closure map before you travel.

What's the alternative to driving to the Grand National?

Train. Merseyrail runs trains between Liverpool Central or Moorfields and Aintree about every 7.5 minutes before and after racing on festival days. Many people park at their accommodation or at a station car park outside the closure area and take the train.

When is the 2027 Grand National Festival?

Thursday 8 to Saturday 10 April 2027, with the Grand National itself on the Saturday.
