A Rainy Day in Liverpool with Kids

Rained off in Liverpool with children? Free indoor museums, Little Liverpool for under 6s, the World Museum planetarium, Central Library and a winter panto.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

On a wet day in Liverpool with children, start at World Museum (aquarium, live bugs and a planetarium) and the Museum of Liverpool, where Little Liverpool is a gallery for under 6s. Both are free. Central Library next to World Museum has a children's library, and in winter there's a panto or Christmas show almost every day.

It rains in Liverpool. When it does, you need a plan that keeps children busy without spending a fortune. The good news is that some of the best indoor options in the city are free and close together.

We checked the details below on 23 November 2026.

A rainy day plan

Morning: World Museum

On William Brown Street. Lonely Planet lists:

  • An aquarium on the first floor
  • Live bugs
  • A planetarium on the fifth floor, with space exhibits including moon rocks

Entry is free. Arrive at opening time on wet weekends and school holidays, when it gets busy.

Lunch break: Central Library

Right next door. The children's library is in the building's former lecture theatre, with picture books, story books and free computers. The council says there are special events in the school holidays. Older kids will like the lift to the fourth-floor roof terrace if the rain stops.

Afternoon: the Museum of Liverpool

Down at the Pier Head. Little Liverpool is a gallery for children under six, and older children can see the Lion steam locomotive. Free entry.

Evening: a show

From November to January there's a family show on almost every day. Visit Liverpool's Christmas guide lists, among others:

  • Beauty and the Beast, Everyman: 14 November 2026 to 16 January 2027
  • Snow White, Epstein Theatre: 5 December 2026 to 3 January 2027
  • Cinderella, M&S Bank Arena: 12 to 29 December 2026

See our Liverpool panto guide for the full list.

Good to know

  • Monday closures. The national museums usually close on Mondays, except bank holidays and Liverpool school holidays. Check before you go
  • What's closed. The Maritime Museum and International Slavery Museum are shut until 2028. Older guides still recommend them
  • Toddlers? See our babies and toddlers guide

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's the best free indoor place for kids in Liverpool?

World Museum and the Museum of Liverpool are both free. World Museum has an aquarium, live bugs and a planetarium, and the Museum of Liverpool has Little Liverpool for children under six.

Is there a children's library in Liverpool city centre?

Yes. Central Library on William Brown Street has a fully accessible children's library in its former lecture theatre, with free computers and events in the school holidays.

Are the museums open on Mondays?

A Liverpool World guide says the national museums close on Mondays except bank holidays and Liverpool school holidays. Check before you go.

Is the Maritime Museum open for a rainy day?

No. It is closed for redevelopment until 2028, along with the International Slavery Museum.

Is there a panto on in Liverpool this winter?

Yes, several. For example, the Everyman's Beauty and the Beast runs 14 November 2026 to 16 January 2027. See our Liverpool panto guide for the full list.
