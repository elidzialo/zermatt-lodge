Working in St Helens or Haydock: Accommodation Options

Work at Parkside, Haydock's distribution parks or the St Helens town centre rebuild? What's on site in 2026, the M6 and A580 routes, and a crew house with parking.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

St Helens has two big live jobs: VINCI's town centre rebuild, with a 120-bedroom Hampton by Hilton and 56 apartments going up in 2026, and Parkside in Newton-le-Willows, where J F Hunt started £15m of enabling works in March 2026 for an 800,000 sq ft logistics first phase linked to M6 junction 22. Haydock's distribution centres sit by M6 junction 23 on the A580 East Lancashire Road. A Liverpool base works for crews who drive, using the A580 or the M57 and M62.

The borough of St Helens sits between Liverpool and Manchester, on the M6 and the A580 East Lancashire Road, and it's busy with construction. There's a town centre rebuild in St Helens itself, a huge logistics scheme at Parkside in Newton-le-Willows, and established distribution parks in Haydock. This guide sums up what's on site and how crews can base themselves.

We used public sources only, checked on 22 November 2026. Always check the latest programme with your project lead.

St Helens town centre

St Helens Borough Council and the English Cities Fund (ECF) are leading a 20-year regeneration programme for the town centre. In July 2026 the council reported:

  • VINCI Building is the main contractor
  • Two tower cranes went up between 30 June and 3 July 2026
  • They support concrete frame work for a 120-bedroom Hampton by Hilton hotel and a 56-apartment block on the Phase 1 site
  • VINCI said the job was moving from substructure, drainage and piling into the main frame

No completion date was given. Expect follow-on packages (fit-out, M&E, public realm) as the frames go up.

Parkside, Newton-le-Willows

Parkside is a former colliery site being developed by Parkside Regeneration, a joint venture between St Helens Borough Council and Langtree. In March 2026 the council reported:

  • J F Hunt started a £15m enabling works package: clearance, earthworks, highways, drainage, utilities and landscaping
  • Phase one: about 800,000 sq ft, with three consented logistics units to follow under a separate main works tender
  • Phase two: about 1.6m sq ft, approved in February 2026
  • Jobs: up to 1,300 gross operational jobs forecast for phase one
  • Access: a £60m link road to M6 junction 22 opened in May 2025

Haydock

According to Wikipedia:

  • Haydock is a village in the borough of St Helens, north east of the town
  • Distribution centres have grown on the north side, close to M6 junction 23
  • The A580 East Lancashire Road, a dual carriageway linking Liverpool and Manchester, crosses the north of the village
  • There's the Haydock Industrial Estate and the Old Boston Trading Estate on a former colliery site
  • Haydock Park Racecourse is here too, so check the racing calendar if your shifts clash with a big meeting

Getting there from Liverpool

  • A580 East Lancashire Road: the direct route east from Liverpool towards Haydock
  • M57 and M62: the M57 runs from north Liverpool to the M62 at junction 1 (Tarbock), which continues east. From there, roads lead to St Helens and the M6
  • Drive times depend heavily on traffic. Test the route at your start time before committing a crew

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is being built in St Helens town centre?

St Helens Borough Council and the English Cities Fund are leading a phased regeneration with VINCI Building as main contractor. In July 2026 tower cranes went up for a 120-bedroom Hampton by Hilton hotel and a 56-apartment block on the first phase site.

What is Parkside in Newton-le-Willows?

A former colliery site being developed for logistics by Parkside Regeneration, a joint venture of St Helens Borough Council and Langtree. J F Hunt began a £15m enabling works package in March 2026 for a first phase of about 800,000 sq ft, with a second phase of about 1.6m sq ft approved in February 2026.

How does Parkside connect to the motorway?

A £60m link road connecting the site to junction 22 of the M6 opened in May 2025, according to St Helens Borough Council.

Where is Haydock?

A village in the borough of St Helens, north east of St Helens town. Its distribution centres are close to M6 junction 23, and the A580 East Lancashire Road crosses the north side of the village. It's also home to Haydock Park Racecourse.

Can I stay in Liverpool and work in St Helens?

Yes, if you drive. The A580 East Lancashire Road links Liverpool with Haydock, and the M57 and M62 are another route. Test the drive at your shift time before booking.
