Christmas in Liverpool with Kids: A 2026 School Holiday Planner

Christmas in Liverpool with kids, 2026: the indoor funfair, pantos, skating, Santa at Knowsley Safari and free race-day entry for under-18s, with dates.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

For Christmas 2026 with kids in Liverpool, the big draws are the Liverpool Indoor Funfair (19 December to 9 January), Cinderella at M&S Bank Arena (to 27 December), skating at Skate St George's and Icebox at Boxpark, Santa and the Enchanted light trail at Knowsley Safari (to 23 December), and Boxing Day racing at Aintree, where under-18s go free in the Grandstand and Paddock.

The best things to do with kids in Liverpool over Christmas 2026 are the Liverpool Indoor Funfair (19 December to 9 January), a panto such as Cinderella at M&S Bank Arena, ice skating at Skate St George's or Icebox at Boxpark, Santa and the Enchanted light trail at Knowsley Safari (to 23 December), and Boxing Day racing at Aintree, where under-18s go free in the Grandstand and Paddock.

This planner is for families spending the school holidays in or around Liverpool. If you're planning Christmas Day itself, including cooking and transport, see our Christmas family stay guide. Everything below was checked on 19 November 2026.

When are the school holidays?

Christmas holiday dates vary between schools and academies, so check with your child's school or the Liverpool City Council term dates page. Christmas Day 2026 is a Friday, so many families will plan around the week of Monday 21 December and the first days of January.

Big days out

Liverpool Indoor Funfair (19 December to 9 January)

Liverpool Experience Campus lists Liverpool Indoor Funfair Winter from 19 December 2026 to 9 January 2027, with tickets through Ticket Quarter. Times and prices weren't on the listing when we checked. An indoor funfair is a lifesaver on a wet Liverpool day.

Knowsley Safari: Santa and Enchanted

  • Santa's Grotto Daytime: 21 November to 24 December, with Safari admission or membership. Each child gets a large plush toy
  • Santa's Grotto Evening: 20 November to 23 December, £21.50 per child
  • Enchanted light trail: selected evenings to 23 December, from £9.95. Refreshments, rides and Santa are extra
  • SEN options: a Santa's Grotto SEN Evening on 9 December, and a new Enchanted SEN Night

More in our Santa grottos and Christmas lights guide.

Boxing Day at Aintree

Racing on Saturday 26 December: gates 10.30am, first race 11.55am, last race 3.20pm. General admission from £39, and Grandstand and Paddock tickets are free for under-18s. It's a big family day out a short drive from north Liverpool. See our Boxing Day guide.

Skating

  • Skate St George's: to 3 January, closed Christmas Day. Children of all ages, but the site recommends they're accompanied by someone aged 16 or over
  • Icebox at Boxpark: to 10 January, 45-minute sessions, £8 for children aged 3 to 12 (maximum two children per adult)

Compare both in our ice skating guide.

Free and low-cost ideas

  • Liverpool ONE's 30-metre walk-through Christmas tree at Paradise Place, plus the lights around the centre
  • Festive Thursdays at Liverpool ONE: free live entertainment through December
  • Wishmas at Liverpool ONE on Saturday 5 December: free festive entertainment all day
  • A winter walk at Formby, with pine woods and red squirrels. Parking is £9, free for National Trust members. See our winter walks guide
  • Speke Hall at Christmas: the house is open 12.30pm to 4pm, Wednesdays to Sundays until 20 December, with a Merry Mice family trail. Free for National Trust members and under-5s

A sample holiday week

Sunday Indoor Funfair or a Formby walk

Tips for a smooth family Christmas in Liverpool

  • Book the big things now: panto weekends, Santa slots and skating sessions sell out
  • Mix busy and quiet days. Children (and adults) need a rest day after a big outing
  • Go early. Morning sessions are calmer in the school holidays
  • Do a big food shop before Christmas Eve. Large shops close on Christmas Day
  • Check transport over Christmas. Merseyrail hasn't run on Christmas Day in past years

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What can kids do in Liverpool over Christmas 2026?

Skating at Skate St George's or Icebox at Boxpark, the Liverpool Indoor Funfair from 19 December, pantos like Cinderella and Snow White, Santa and the Enchanted trail at Knowsley Safari, and Boxing Day racing at Aintree with free entry for under-18s in the Grandstand and Paddock.

When is the Liverpool Indoor Funfair over Christmas 2026?

Liverpool Experience Campus lists Liverpool Indoor Funfair Winter from 19 December 2026 to 9 January 2027. Times and prices are on Ticket Quarter.

When do Liverpool schools break up for Christmas 2026?

Dates vary between schools and academies. Check your child's school or the Liverpool City Council term dates page. Many families plan around the week of 21 December.

What's open in Liverpool on Christmas Eve with kids?

Skate St George's is open 10am to 6pm and Icebox at Boxpark 10am to 10pm on Christmas Eve. Knowsley Safari's daytime Santa runs to 24 December. Most things close on Christmas Day.

Which panto is best for toddlers in Liverpool?

Cinderella at M&S Bank Arena says it's suitable for all ages, but uses strobe and pyrotechnics. Snow White at the smaller Epstein Theatre may suit very young children better.
