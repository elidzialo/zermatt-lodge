The Kings Dock car park at the Exhibition Centre has 1,450 spaces, but a 24-hour stay costs £27, so free parking at the house matters on a multi-day build. Plan for fatigue

Crews often work long days back to back. The HSE's good practice guidelines for shift work include:

  • Cap shifts at 12 hours including overtime, or 8 hours for night work or safety-critical tasks
  • Keep long, night and early shifts to 2 or 3 in a row
  • Allow two nights of uninterrupted sleep when switching between day and night shifts

The HSE also notes that fatigue has been implicated in 20% of accidents on major roads. After a late get-out, a short trip to a bed is safer than a long drive home. Our night shift sleep tips cover how to rest in a shared house.

What to look for in a crew house

  1. Real beds, not sofa beds, and enough bathrooms for staggered starts
  2. Off-road parking for vans or splitter vans. See our van parking guide
  3. A washer and dryer for blacks and hi-vis
  4. Self check-in for late arrivals
  5. A kitchen for early breakfasts and late meals
  6. An invoice in the company's name. See invoices and VAT for business stays
  7. Clear house rules, especially on noise after a late finish

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where are Liverpool's main event venues?

The Kings Dock campus has the Exhibition Centre Liverpool, opened in September 2015 with 8,100 square metres of space, linked to the convention centre and M&S Bank Arena, which holds about 11,000 people. Aintree Racecourse, Anfield and Hill Dickinson Stadium also host big events.

How much is parking at the Exhibition Centre Liverpool?

The venue lists Kings Dock Car Park tariffs from £4 for up to 1 hour to £27 for up to 24 hours, with 1,450 spaces. Prices can change, so check the venue website.

How long should event crew shifts be?

HSE good practice guidance suggests capping shifts at 12 hours including overtime, or 8 hours for night work or safety-critical work, and limiting long or night shifts to 2 or 3 in a row.

Can a company book Zermatt Lodge for an event crew?
