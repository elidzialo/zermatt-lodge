About Zermatt Lodge

Zermatt Lodge is a spacious, luxury 4-bedroom house in Liverpool with room for up to 7 guests - made for groups, families and business or contractor stays. There is a king master bedroom with en-suite, a second king, a queen room and a single, plus two bathrooms in total. Downstairs you get a big kitchen, a separate dining room and a games room with a pool table and board games, and the 55" Smart TV has Netflix. Out back there is a large private garden, and there is free parking for 2 cars.

Liverpool city centre is about 15 minutes away by Merseyrail train (Northern line to Liverpool Central), and the M57 and M58 are close by for getting around Merseyside. The house is also near Aintree University Hospital, The Walton Centre and Aintree Racecourse. It suits families, groups, contractors and anyone visiting the hospitals. Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is by 11am. You book direct here, so you deal with the host, ZALO Stays, from start to finish.

Ideal for families, groups of friends or work crews who want room to spread out without paying for multiple hotel rooms. Two bathrooms means no queue for the shower.

Come back after a day of exploring to a warm, welcoming space where you can relax together, play some games, cook a meal, or unwind in your own room.

Every stay comes with fresh towels and hotel-quality bedding. Our cleaning crew makes sure the house is spotless for every stay, and our local team inspects the property before each arrival.

Our property management team is available 24/7 by email or phone if you need anything during your stay.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many people does Zermatt Lodge sleep?

Zermatt Lodge sleeps up to 7 guests. It is an entire 4-bedroom house with 4 beds: a king in the master bedroom (with en-suite), a second king, a queen and a single.

How many bedrooms and bathrooms are there?

There are 4 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. The king master bedroom has its own en-suite, and there is a second bathroom for the other three bedrooms.

Is it the entire house or a shared space?

You get the entire house to yourselves - all four bedrooms, both bathrooms, the kitchen, dining room, games room and the large private back garden. Nothing is shared with other guests.

Where is Zermatt Lodge?

Zermatt Lodge is a spacious house in Liverpool, in the Fazakerley area of north Liverpool, Merseyside. Liverpool city centre is about 15 minutes away by Merseyrail train, or about 4.5 miles by road. The house is close to Aintree University Hospital, The Walton Centre, Aintree Racecourse and the M57 and M58. The full address is sent to confirmed guests.

Is the house in Liverpool city centre?

No. Zermatt Lodge is a residential house in north Liverpool, not in the city centre. Liverpool Central is about 15 minutes away by Merseyrail train, and the city centre is about 4.5 miles by road, so it suits guests who want more space, a garden and free parking with an easy trip into town.

Is it close to Aintree University Hospital?

Yes. Aintree University Hospital is about 0.6 miles away in a straight line. It is a good base for patients' families, visitors and NHS staff who want a whole house with free parking.

How far is it from The Walton Centre?

The Walton Centre shares the Aintree University Hospital site on Lower Lane, so it is about 0.6 miles away in a straight line. Fazakerley station is the closest station to the hospital site.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is free parking for 2 cars at the house. That is useful for hospital visits, as parking on the Aintree hospital site is chargeable.

How do I get to Liverpool city centre?

Fazakerley station is on the Merseyrail Northern line, with trains to Liverpool Central taking about 15 minutes. You can tap in and out with a contactless card or phone. By car, the city centre is about 4.5 miles away.

Is it good for the Grand National?

Yes. Aintree Racecourse is very close, and Aintree station runs extra trains on race days. The 2027 Grand National Festival runs 8-10 April 2027, so book early as it is the busiest week of the year.

Is it close to Anfield and Everton's stadium?

Anfield is about 3 miles away. Everton's Hill Dickinson Stadium at Bramley-Moore Dock is about 4 miles away - take the train from Fazakerley to Sandhills (around 8 minutes) and walk from there.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3pm and check-out is by 11am.

Is the house suitable for contractors and work crews?

Yes. It sleeps 7 across 4 bedrooms, has free parking for 2 cars, a big kitchen and a dining room, and is close to the M57 and M58 for getting around Merseyside and the North West.

Do you take longer stays for work crews?

Yes. Get in touch on +44 7479 905477 or eli@zelr.co.uk for weekly and monthly stays.

Is it good for families and groups?

Yes. There is a games room with a pool table, board games, a 55" Smart TV with Netflix, a big kitchen, a separate dining room and a large private back garden.

What entertainment is there?

The games room has a pool table, there are board games for all ages, and the living area has a 55" Smart TV with Netflix.

Can we have a party or event?

No. Strictly no parties or events - bookings found to be for parties are cancelled. Zermatt Lodge is a home in a residential area for families, groups and work stays, so please respect the neighbours.

Is smoking allowed?

No. Smoking is not allowed anywhere in the house.

Are pets allowed?

No, pets are not allowed.

What do I need to provide before my stay?

All guests booking need to provide ID, sign a rental agreement, and either pay a damage deposit or take the damage waiver. We will ask for these after you book.

What is the cancellation policy?

Our cancellation policy is Moderate: you get a full refund if you cancel up to 5 days before check-in, a 50% refund if you cancel after that, and no refund after check-in. No pets and no smoking. Strictly no parties or events - bookings found to be for parties are cancelled. Guest ID and damage deposit may be required.

Who runs Zermatt Lodge?

Zermatt Lodge is run by ZALO Stays. You can call +44 7479 905477 or email eli@zelr.co.uk.

Are there shops nearby?

Yes. Lidl, Tesco Express and Co-op are on Longmoor Lane, close to the house.

How far is Liverpool John Lennon Airport?

Liverpool John Lennon Airport is about 10 miles away in a straight line. Most guests drive or take a taxi; by public transport you can go via the city centre.

Are there parks nearby?

Yes. Walton Hall Park has 130 acres and two lakes, and Croxteth Hall and Country Park, about 2.5 miles away, has a working farm, a walled garden and a free Saturday parkrun.
