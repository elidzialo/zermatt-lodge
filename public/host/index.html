Using Guesty? How to link it

Guesty to Book Homes (stops double bookings here)

  1. In Guesty, switch to Operations mode and open Properties.
  2. Open the listing, then Calendar in the left menu.
  3. Choose "Calendar sync (iCal)" and click Export.
  4. Click the sync icon to make the link, then the copy icon.
  5. Paste it above under Import, choose Guesty, and tap Import calendar.

Book Homes to Guesty (closes dates booked here)

  1. Copy your Book Homes link from Export above.
  2. In Guesty: the listing, Calendar, "Calendar sync (iCal)", then Import.
  3. Click "Add an iCalendar link", name it Book Homes, paste the link and turn on Sync calendar.
  4. Click Save.
