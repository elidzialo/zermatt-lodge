Planning a Friends' Reunion Weekend in Liverpool

Planning a reunion weekend with old friends in Liverpool? Dates, splitting the cost of a house, a 2-day plan with shows, football and sights, and the house rules.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

For a friends' reunion in Liverpool, book a whole house for two nights and split the cost, then build the weekend around one big thing: a Royal Court comedy, a match, a concert or a night at the Philharmonic. Fill the rest with free sights, such as the waterfront, both cathedrals and Another Place on Crosby Beach. Keep it a reunion, not a party: most houses ban parties.

Old school friends, uni housemates, a group who used to work together: whoever they are, getting everyone together gets harder every year. A weekend in Liverpool is easy to reach from most of the UK and has enough going on that there's something for everyone.

We checked the details below on 23 November 2026.

Step 1: Fix the date and the money

  • Pick two dates and vote. Give people a deadline
  • Agree the budget up front, including the house, food and one big night out
  • One person books, everyone pays their share straight away. It avoids awkward chasing later
  • Decide rooms early. Who gets the en-suite? Do couples pay more for the bigger rooms?

Step 3: A simple 2-day plan

Saturday

  • Morning: walk the waterfront from the Pier Head to the Royal Albert Dock
  • Lunch: somewhere in town, booked ahead for a big table
  • Afternoon: the free Museum of Liverpool, or a Mersey Ferry cruise
  • Evening: your big night out

Sunday

  • Late breakfast at the house, which is often the best catch-up of the weekend
  • A walk: Liverpool Cathedral (free, with a paid tower), or Another Place on Crosby Beach at low tide. Don't walk out to the furthest statues, as Sefton Council warns of soft sand and mud
  • Head home after lunch

More ideas are in our things to do with a group and free things to do guides.

Step 4: Keep it a reunion, not a party

A reunion is about catching up. Most holiday houses have strict rules on parties, noise and extra guests. Share the house rules with everyone before you arrive.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is a house cheaper than hotel rooms for a group of friends?

Often, yes, once you split the cost and add savings from cooking breakfast and some meals. Compare the total for your dates. See our house vs hotel guide.

What's a good group night out in Liverpool that isn't just drinking?

A show at the Royal Court, where the stalls are set out with tables, a concert at the Philharmonic, a match, or the Strictly Live Tour in February 2027.

What can we do for free?

Walk the waterfront, visit Liverpool Cathedral or the Metropolitan Cathedral, the free national museums, Central Library's roof terrace, or Another Place on Crosby Beach at low tide.

Can we have a party at the house?

How should we split the cost?
