Visiting Liverpool's Knowledge Quarter for Work: Paddington Village Guide

Visiting or working at Paddington Village in Liverpool's Knowledge Quarter? What's there, the HEMISPHERE lab build, Paddington South, and where teams can stay.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Paddington Village is Liverpool City Council's flagship site in the Knowledge Quarter (KQ Liverpool), on the east side of the city centre. It already has The Spine offices and a Novotel, with HEMISPHERE One, a £61m lab building by Morgan Sindall, and the 9-acre Paddington South phase coming next. The Royal Liverpool University Hospital is nearby. Teams who need parking can stay in north Liverpool and take the train to the city centre in about 15 minutes.

The Knowledge Quarter (KQ Liverpool) is the city's innovation district, home to universities, hospitals and research buildings on the east side of the city centre. Paddington Village is its main development site, and it brings a steady flow of visiting researchers, consultants, fit-out teams and site crews. Here's what's there, what's being built, and how a team can stay without paying city centre parking every day.

We used public reports only, checked on 22 November 2026. Check your own site details with your host organisation.

What's at Paddington Village now

Liverpool Express describes Paddington Village as central to KQ Liverpool and lists these existing buildings:

  • The Spine: WELL Platinum-rated Grade A offices, which won a BCO Innovation Award
  • A clean energy multi-storey car park
  • The Novotel Liverpool Paddington Village hotel

The Royal Liverpool University Hospital is also in the Knowledge Quarter. If you're visiting the hospital for work or as a family member, see our Royal Liverpool University Hospital accommodation guide.

What's being built

HEMISPHERE One

In March 2025, Liverpool Express reported that Morgan Sindall had been appointed to build HEMISPHERE One, a £61m, 117,190 net sq ft building, supporting developer Sciontec:

  • Phase one of a two-phase scheme
  • With HEMISPHERE Two, a planned 216,693 sq ft of chemistry, data, bio and innovation labs, plus incubator, grow-on and commercial lab space
  • Appointed through the North West Construction Hub High Value Framework

Paddington South

Paddington South is the next phase of the £1bn KQ Liverpool development, led by Liverpool City Council with the University of Liverpool:

  • A 9-acre site
  • Morgan Sindall chosen for masterplanning and enabling works
  • A revamped green space around Grove Street at its centre, with a target of a 10% biodiversity improvement
  • The former Smithdown Lane police station was bought to make way for the scheme

KQ Liverpool has said the wider development could help create upwards of 10,000 skilled jobs in the city region, in areas like life sciences, robotics and materials chemistry.

Who visits the Knowledge Quarter for work

  • Researchers and academics on visits or short secondments
  • Lab fit-out and specialist M&E teams
  • Construction crews on HEMISPHERE and Paddington South
  • NHS and hospital contractors
  • Conference delegates at university venues

Getting there from north Liverpool

  • By train: from Fazakerley, direct trains reach Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes. Paddington Village is on the east side of the centre, so allow time to walk or take a bus or taxi from there
  • By car: the area has paid parking, including the multi-storey at Paddington Village. Check rates before committing a team to driving in daily

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is at Paddington Village in Liverpool?

Liverpool Express lists The Spine, a Grade A office building, a clean energy multi-storey car park and the Novotel Liverpool Paddington Village. A £61m lab building, HEMISPHERE One, is planned, and Paddington South is the next phase.

What is HEMISPHERE One?

A £61m, 117,190 net sq ft lab and innovation building at Paddington Village. Morgan Sindall was appointed contractor in March 2025, supporting developer Sciontec. With HEMISPHERE Two, the scheme is planned to offer 216,693 sq ft of lab space.

What is Paddington South?

The next phase of the Knowledge Quarter development, a 9-acre site led by Liverpool City Council with the University of Liverpool. Morgan Sindall was chosen for masterplanning and enabling works, with a new green space around Grove Street at its centre.

How many jobs could the Knowledge Quarter create?

KQ Liverpool has said the development could help create upwards of 10,000 highly skilled jobs in the city region.

Where can a team stay with parking near the Knowledge Quarter?

City centre parking is charged, so some teams stay outside the centre in a house with free parking and take the train in. From Fazakerley, Liverpool Central is about 15 minutes by train.
