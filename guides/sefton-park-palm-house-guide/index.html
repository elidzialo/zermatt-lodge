Sefton Park and the Palm House in Winter: Opening Times and Tips

Visiting Sefton Park Palm House in winter 2026: opening days and times, the Friday and Saturday closures, the café, and Lark Lane Market dates in November and December.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Sefton Park Palm House is a Victorian glasshouse in Sefton Park, south Liverpool. It opens to visitors Sunday to Wednesday 10am to 5pm and Thursday 10am to 3pm, and is closed to the public on Fridays and Saturdays for private celebrations. The café closes an hour before the building. Nearby Lark Lane Market runs on 28 November and 19 December 2026, 9am to 2pm.

Sefton Park is one of Liverpool's big Victorian parks, and its Palm House is the place to head on a cold, bright winter day: a glasshouse full of plants where you can warm up between walks. It's an easy, low-cost outing for families and mixed-age groups.

We checked these times on 25 November 2026. The Palm House updates its calendar often, so look again before you travel.

Day Palm House Café
Sunday to Wednesday 10am to 5pm 10am to 4pm

The grounds open at 8am on open days.

Dates to note:

  • Wed 25 and Sun 29 November 2026: Palm House open 12pm to 5pm
  • Thu 3 December 2026: Palm House and café closed, grounds 8am to 12pm

The Palm House website has a Christmas section, so check it for dates later in December.

Why go in winter

  • It's warm inside. A good break from the wind on a winter walk.
  • The park is big and open, so it's easy to let children run around before or after.
  • It's not ticketed in the usual way. The website doesn't list an entry price, so check there if you're bringing a large group.

Lark Lane Market

Lark Lane, by the park, has a monthly Saturday market run with the council. The next dates listed are:

  • Saturday 28 November 2026, 9am to 2pm
  • Saturday 19 December 2026, 9am to 2pm

Our Liverpool food markets guide lists the other council markets running this winter.

Plan your day

  • Avoid Friday and Saturday if the Palm House is the main reason for your visit.
  • Thursday closes early, at 3pm.
  • Bring layers and waterproofs. Paths can be muddy after rain.
  • For more outdoor ideas, see our winter walks near Liverpool guide.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is Sefton Park Palm House open?

Sunday to Wednesday 10am to 5pm and Thursday 10am to 3pm. It is closed to visitors on Fridays and Saturdays, when it hosts private celebrations. Special dates can differ, so check the Palm House website before you go.

Is the Palm House café open every day?

It follows the building's open days: Sunday to Wednesday 10am to 4pm and Thursday 10am to 2pm. It closes an hour before the building.

Are there any closures in December 2026?

The Palm House lists Thursday 3 December 2026 as closed for the building and café, with the grounds open 8am to 12pm. It also lists shorter hours (12pm to 5pm) on 25 and 29 November.

When is Lark Lane Market?

Liverpool City Council lists Lark Lane Market on Saturday 28 November and Saturday 19 December 2026, 9am to 2pm.
