Keeping Kosher on a Business Trip to Liverpool

Travelling to Liverpool for work and keeping kosher? Where to buy food, how to handle conference catering, finding a shul, and choosing a base for the week.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Liverpool has one kosher deli, The Deli, and no kosher restaurant that we could find, so most business travellers bring food, order from shops on the Manchester Beth Din's official licensee list, or stay somewhere with a kitchen. For shul and service details, contact the local community directly. For conferences, ask the venue early about sealed kosher meals, and for a week-long trip consider a self-catering base.

A work trip to Liverpool is easy to plan, until you need to keep kosher for the week. Kosher supply in the city is limited and we couldn't find a kosher restaurant. Here's how to plan food, shul and a base.

This guide uses published sources only. Check current supervision before you travel, and contact the local community directly for shul and service details. For homes where hosts have declared a kosher kitchen or Shabbat features, see our kosher homes page.

Food: three ways to manage

1. Shop locally

The Deli, formerly Roseman's, is Liverpool's kosher deli. Check opening hours and current supervision with the Liverpool Kashrut Commission before you go. Our kosher food in Liverpool guide has more.

2. Order from Manchester

The Manchester Beth Din (MBD) publishes an official licensee list of butchers, bakeries, grocers, restaurants, takeaways and caterers. For a week-long trip, many travellers:

  • Order a box of meals or Shabbat food in advance
  • Ask whether the shop delivers to Liverpool or needs collection
  • Bring cool bags and ice packs for the drive

The licensee list changes, so check the MBD's site before you order.

3. Self-cater

A kitchen gives you the most freedom. A standard rental won't have a kosher kitchen, so observant travellers usually bring:

  • Their own pans, cutlery and a chopping board, or disposables
  • Foil or liners for oven shelves and worktops
  • Simple meals that need little equipment

See our guide to keeping kosher in a holiday rental kitchen.

Conferences and client meals

If you're attending a conference at the waterfront convention centre (rebranded in 2026 from ACC Liverpool to the convention centre at Liverpool Experience Campus) or any other venue:

  1. Tell the organiser early, ideally when you register
  2. Ask exactly what will be served: ask for a sealed meal from a supervised caterer, with the hechsher shown
  3. Get it in writing, and bring a backup in case it doesn't arrive
  4. For client dinners, suggest a venue where you can order something simple, or host at your accommodation if you're self-catering

We couldn't find a published kosher catering policy for Liverpool's conference venues, so don't assume. Ask.

Davening and shul

Contact the local community directly before you travel for shul and service details, especially if you need to say kaddish for a yahrzeit.

Shabbat on a work trip

If your trip runs over a weekend, you'll need to be within walking distance of the shul you'll use. Our Shabbat in Liverpool guide covers the details, and our Shabbat-friendly holiday home checklist lists what to ask a host. In winter, Shabbat starts mid afternoon, so plan to finish work early on Friday.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I get kosher food in Liverpool for a work trip?

Yes, with planning. The Deli is the city's kosher deli. For more choice, use the Manchester Beth Din's official licensee list of butchers, bakeries, grocers, takeaways and caterers. Check current supervision before you buy.

Are there kosher restaurants in Liverpool?

We couldn't find one in current published sources. Plan to cook, bring food, or order from a licensed caterer.

Can a Liverpool conference venue provide kosher meals?

Ask the organiser and venue well before the event. Ask for a sealed meal from a supervised caterer, with the hechsher shown. We couldn't find a published kosher catering policy for Liverpool's conference venues, so get confirmation in writing.

How do I find a shul in Liverpool?

Contact the local community directly for shul and service details before you travel.
