Strictly Come Dancing Live Tour 2027 in Liverpool

Strictly Come Dancing The Live Tour plays M&S Bank Arena Liverpool on 2 and 3 February 2027. Show times, tickets, getting there and a group house to stay in.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Strictly Come Dancing The Live Tour 2027 plays M&S Bank Arena Liverpool on Tuesday 2 and Wednesday 3 February 2027, with 7.30pm shows on both nights. Janette Manrara hosts, and Shirley Ballas, Anton Du Beke and Craig Revel Horwood are named as judges. Both are weeknights, so if you're travelling in, booking a stay saves a late drive home.

The Strictly Come Dancing Live Tour is back in Liverpool in February 2027. It's a popular night out for mums and daughters, groups of friends and three generations of the same family, and in Liverpool both shows fall midweek. If you're coming from outside Merseyside, staying over is usually easier than a late drive home.

We checked these details on 23 November 2026. Always check your ticket for the final times.

Both shows are at M&S Bank Arena Liverpool on the waterfront. Ents24 lists tickets from ATG Tickets, and a separate Skyline Experience at 6pm on both dates, sold by Seat Unique.

Who's on the tour

The Irish News reported that Janette Manrara will host, with Shirley Ballas, Anton Du Beke and Craig Revel Horwood judging. The tour has 29 shows in all. The judges score each routine, and the audience can text vote for the winner of the tour's own Glitterball.

Planning a Strictly night

  • Eat early. With a 7.30pm start, book dinner for about 5pm, or eat before you head into town
  • Plan the trip home. Check the running time with the venue, and pre-book a taxi or know your last train, as everyone leaves at once
  • Dress for February. The arena is on the river, and it's cold walking back to a car or a station
  • Weeknight traffic. A 7.30pm start on a Tuesday or Wednesday means rush-hour roads on the way in. Leave plenty of time
