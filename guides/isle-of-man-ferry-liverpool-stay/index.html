Isle of Man Ferry from Liverpool: Where to Stay the Night Before

Catching the Isle of Man ferry from Liverpool? Terminal address, check-in times, parking, TT 2027 dates and a 4-bed house with parking the night before.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Staying in Liverpool the night before an Isle of Man sailing means no rush up the motorway to make check-in. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in north Liverpool, about 15 minutes by train from the city centre, with free parking for two cars and room for up to 7.

If you're sailing to the Isle of Man from Liverpool, staying in the city the night before means you won't be racing up the motorway to make check-in. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in north Liverpool, about 15 minutes by train from the city centre, with free parking for two cars and room for up to 7. It suits families, groups of friends and work crews heading over, or coming back late and not wanting to drive home the same night.

This guide covers the Liverpool terminal, check-in, parking and the extra planning needed around TT.

The Liverpool ferry terminal

The Isle of Man Steam Packet moved into a new purpose-built terminal on Liverpool's waterfront in June 2024.

  • Address: Isle of Man Ferry Terminal, 1 Triskelion Way, Liverpool, L3 0BN
  • Sat nav: the Steam Packet says some don't recognise L3 0BN. Use L3 0AY (Jesse Hartley Way) or the what3words address on its website
  • Terminal opening hours: 09:00 to 19:15 from March to October and 09:00 to 16:00 from November to February, varying with sailings

Crossing times

Vessel When Crossing to Douglas
Manannan (fast craft) Seasonal service, daily About 2 hours 45 minutes
Manxman Covers the route in winter, with weekend sailings About 4 hours 15 minutes

Check the Steam Packet timetable for your date. Weather and tides can change sailings, so sign up for service updates.

Check-in times

From the Steam Packet's port page:

  • Vehicles: check in at the booths at the port entrance. Last check-in is 45 minutes before departure
  • Foot passengers: check in inside the passenger terminal. Last check-in is 25 minutes before departure
  • Example: check-in opens at 08:45 for the 11:15 sailing and 16:45 for the 19:15 sailing

Getting to the terminal from north Liverpool

Driving with a vehicle on the ferry

If you're taking your car, van or bike across, staying in north Liverpool makes the morning easy. You drive through the north end of the city towards the waterfront, avoiding the motorway rush from Manchester or the M6. Check a live route the night before and leave a margin for traffic.

Foot passengers

If you're travelling on foot, don't plan to leave a car at the port. The Steam Packet says long-term parking isn't available there. It suggests:

  • Princes Dock multi-storey, about a 5-minute walk, open 24/7 with CCTV and EV charging, bookable through JustPark
  • Q-Park Liverpool ONE, a short walk away, with a discount code listed on the Steam Packet site

Alternatively, leave the car at your accommodation and take the train. From Fazakerley, Merseyrail reaches Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes, and Moorfields a stop earlier. The Steam Packet says a council bus (£2, pay on board) runs to the terminal from Skelhorne Street via Lime Street and Liverpool ONE bus station, starting two hours before departure. It also names local taxi firms and Uber.

Left luggage

The terminal offers left luggage at £2.50 per bag, for use on the day of travel only.

Planning around TT

TT is the busiest fortnight of the year on the route. TT 2027 runs from Monday 31 May to Saturday 12 June 2027, with the Senior TT on Saturday 12 June.

  • Book the ferry first. The Steam Packet opens TT sailings well in advance and vehicle space goes fast
  • Then book your Liverpool stay around your sailing, especially for early departures or late returns
  • Bikes and vans: the house has free parking for two vehicles. If you're travelling with trailers or several bikes, call us before booking to check what will fit
  • Coming back: a late arrival into Liverpool after a long day at the races is a good reason to stay one more night rather than drive home tired

A simple plan for a morning sailing

  1. Arrive the afternoon before. Check in from 3pm, do a food shop and load the car properly so nothing is in the way at the vehicle booths.
  2. Check the Steam Packet's service updates in the evening for any weather or tide changes.
  3. Eat breakfast at the house, then leave with plenty of margin before last check-in.
  4. Keep tickets and ID handy in the front of the car, not packed in the boot.

Other reasons people cross from Liverpool

  • Family visits to relatives on the island
  • Work trips for crews and contractors
  • Hospital appointments the other way. Some Isle of Man patients are treated in Liverpool hospitals. Our guide to Aintree Hospital from the Isle of Man covers that journey
  • Holidays. See our Isle of Man trip guide

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where does the Isle of Man ferry leave from in Liverpool?

From the Isle of Man Ferry Terminal, 1 Triskelion Way, Liverpool, L3 0BN, on the waterfront north of the Pier Head. The Steam Packet says some sat navs don't recognise the postcode and suggests L3 0AY instead.

How long is the crossing from Liverpool to Douglas?

The Steam Packet lists 2 hours 45 minutes on Manannan, which runs the seasonal service, or 4 hours 15 minutes on Manxman, which covers the route in winter with weekend sailings.

What time should I check in?

The Steam Packet says last check-in is 45 minutes before departure for vehicles and 25 minutes before for foot passengers. For example, check-in opens at 08:45 for an 11:15 sailing.

Can I leave my car at the Liverpool terminal?

No. The Steam Packet says long-term parking isn't offered at the port. It points foot passengers to Princes Dock multi-storey, about a 5-minute walk, and Q-Park Liverpool ONE.

When is the TT in 2027?

TT 2027 runs from Monday 31 May to Saturday 12 June 2027, with the Senior TT on the final Saturday. Ferries sell out very early for TT.
