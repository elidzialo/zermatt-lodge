How to Get to Aintree Racecourse: Trains, Buses, Driving and Race-Day Changes

How to get to Aintree Racecourse from Liverpool city centre, Lime Street, the airport or north Liverpool, with race-day train changes, buses and parking.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

The easiest way to Aintree Racecourse is the Merseyrail train to Aintree station, under a five-minute walk from the course. Trains from Liverpool Central take about 17 to 18 minutes, running about every 15 minutes normally and every 7.5 minutes on Grand National Festival days.

The easiest way to get to Aintree Racecourse is by Merseyrail train to Aintree station, which the Jockey Club says is across the road from the course, under a five-minute walk. Trains leave Liverpool Central and Moorfields about every 15 minutes on normal days and about every 7.5 minutes on Grand National Festival days, and the journey from Central takes about 17 to 18 minutes.

Below is a route for every starting point, plus the festival-day changes that catch people out.

The racecourse at a glance

  • Address: Aintree Racecourse, Ormskirk Road, Aintree, Liverpool L9 5AS (the sat nav postcode)
  • Main entrance: Grand National Avenue, off Ormskirk Road
  • Phone: 0151 523 2600
  • Road: on the A59, about a mile from the M57 and M58
  • Nearest station: Aintree (Merseyrail Northern line, Ormskirk branch)

Aintree is in north Liverpool, not the city centre. Allow time for the journey out, especially on big race days.

From Liverpool city centre

This is the route most racegoers use.

  1. Go to Liverpool Central or Moorfields.
  2. Catch a Northern line train towards Ormskirk. Not every Northern line train goes this way, so check the destination board.
  3. Get off at Aintree. Trainline lists the trip from Central at about 17 to 18 minutes.
  4. Cross the road to the course.

Merseyrail accepts contactless bank cards, phones and watches. Tap in and tap out at the gates or validators.

From Liverpool Lime Street

Lime Street is the mainline station for trains from London, Manchester and beyond. It doesn't have Northern line platforms, so walk the short distance to Liverpool Central, then follow the steps above.

From Liverpool John Lennon Airport

The Jockey Club says the airport is about 20 minutes away by road. By public transport, take a bus to Liverpool South Parkway, then a train into Liverpool Central or Moorfields, and change onto an Ormskirk train to Aintree. The Jockey Club estimates about an hour. Our Liverpool airport guide has more on the airport buses.

Manchester Airport is roughly 45 minutes by road, according to the Jockey Club, though traffic on the M62 varies.

By bus

The Jockey Club lists services 300, 310 and 345 to and from Liverpool. Bus routes and timetables change, so check with Merseytravel (0151 236 7676 or merseytravel.gov.uk) before relying on them.

By car

The racecourse is on the A59. The Jockey Club's directions:

  • From the south: M6 to junction 21A, M62 west, leave at junction 6 for the M57, then follow race signs.
  • From the north: M6 to junction 26, M58, then follow race signs.
  • From the Mersey Tunnels: follow signs for Preston, then the A59, then race signs.

On normal race days, follow the yellow tourism signs to the car parks. On festival days, it's a different story (see below).

From north Liverpool (Fazakerley, Kirkby and nearby)

If you're staying in north Liverpool, the racecourse is close, but the rail lines don't join up neatly.

  • Fazakerley and Kirkby are on the Kirkby branch. Aintree is on the Ormskirk branch. The two branches meet at Sandhills, so by train you go in towards the city, change at Sandhills and come back out. It works, but it isn't the most direct.
  • Taxi or a short drive is often quicker outside festival week. Book a licensed private hire car in advance; in Liverpool, private hire journeys must be pre-booked.
  • Walking is possible from parts of north Liverpool, but distances depend on exactly where you start and which entrance you use. Use a live map before you set out, and wear sensible shoes.

Grand National Festival: what changes

The festival (Thursday 8 to Saturday 10 April 2027) brings huge crowds to north Liverpool. The Jockey Club's official travel plan includes:

Trains

  • Every 7.5 minutes between Liverpool Central/Moorfields and Aintree, roughly 11:00 to 13:00 towards Aintree and 16:30 to 20:30 back.
  • Kirkby line: between 11:00 and 14:00 and again between 17:00 and 21:00, trains start and end at Sandhills. If you're heading to or from Rice Lane, Fazakerley, Kirkby or Headbolt Lane, you change at Sandhills.
  • Wirral line: normal timetable. Change at Moorfields for Aintree.
  • Live times are on Merseyrail's Aintree races page.

Car parking

  • Parking must be booked in advance. Don't turn up hoping for a space.
  • The Steeplechase Car Park is reached by a course crossing at Melling Road, which closes 30 minutes before and reopens 30 minutes after the feature race over the Grand National course. No vehicles move while it's shut.
  • Road closures run around Aintree, managed under council permissions. Check the latest closure map.

See our Grand National parking guide for more.

Getting home

Everyone tries to leave after the last race. Options:

  • Stay for a drink and leave later. Queues for the station ease as the evening goes on, and trains run until about 20:30 on the enhanced timetable.
  • Walk to a meeting point away from the station for a pre-booked taxi.
  • Stay locally, so your journey home is short.

A simple race-day plan

  • Late morning: travel in, aiming to arrive well before the first race. Check your ticket for gate opening times.
  • Afternoon: racing. Pre-book food if you're in a hospitality area.
  • Early evening: let the first crush clear, then head for the train or your taxi.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the nearest station to Aintree Racecourse?

Aintree station on Merseyrail's Northern line (Ormskirk branch). The Jockey Club says it's across the road from the racecourse, under a 5-minute walk. Trains run from Liverpool Central and Moorfields about every 15 minutes on normal days.

How often do trains run to Aintree on Grand National days?

The Jockey Club's festival travel plan says trains between Liverpool Central or Moorfields and Aintree run about every 7.5 minutes, roughly 11:00 to 13:00 towards Aintree and 16:30 to 20:30 back to Liverpool. Check Merseyrail's live planner before you travel.

Can I drive to Aintree for the Grand National?

Yes, but car parking must be booked in advance for the festival, and road closures operate around the course. On quieter race days, follow the A59 and the yellow tourism signs to the car parks.

Which buses go to Aintree Racecourse?

The Jockey Club lists services 300, 310 and 345 to and from Liverpool. Check routes and times with Merseytravel on 0151 236 7676.

How do I get from Fazakerley to Aintree Racecourse by train?

Fazakerley is on the Kirkby branch and Aintree is on the Ormskirk branch, so you change at Sandhills. On festival days the Kirkby line starts and ends at Sandhills at certain times, so a change there is needed anyway. A short taxi or drive can be simpler outside the festival.
