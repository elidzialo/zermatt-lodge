Locum and Agency Nurse Accommodation in Liverpool

Short-term accommodation for locum doctors, agency nurses and NHS contractors in Liverpool. Shift-friendly house near Aintree Hospital with free parking.

For locum doctors, agency nurses and other NHS contractors working in Liverpool, the best accommodation is usually somewhere quiet near your main hospital, with parking and a kitchen, rather than a city-centre hotel. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Fazakerley, close to Aintree University Hospital, The Walton Centre and Fazakerley station. It suits clinicians on placement, alone or sharing with colleagues.

This guide covers how Liverpool's main hospital sites connect to a north Liverpool base, what shift workers need from accommodation, and how agencies can book.

Liverpool's main hospital sites and how they connect

Aintree University Hospital and The Walton Centre (Fazakerley)

These share one campus on Lower Lane and Longmoor Lane in Fazakerley, near junction 6 of the M57. The hospital says Fazakerley station is across the road from the Longmoor Lane entrance, about 15 minutes' walk from the main entrance. Woodlands Hospice is on the same site. If you're placed here, staying in Fazakerley means a short walk or drive to work, even before a 7am handover.

Royal Liverpool University Hospital (city centre)

The Royal is about a mile from both Lime Street and Liverpool Central. From Fazakerley you can take the Northern line to Central, about 15 minutes, then walk. The hospital also lists the Paddington Village car park (open 24 hours) and the Q-Park on Prescot Street (6am to 10pm) for drivers. Check staff parking arrangements with your trust, because visitor parking adds up quickly.

Alder Hey Children's Hospital (West Derby)

Alder Hey is on East Prescot Road in West Derby. From Fazakerley it's a cross-suburb drive rather than a train journey. The nearest station, Broad Green, is about 1.5 miles away. Allow time for school-run and rush-hour traffic.

Liverpool Women's and other sites

Liverpool University Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust also runs Liverpool Women's Hospital and Broadgreen, among others. If you're rotating between sites, a base with parking near the M57 and a Merseyrail station gives you the most options.

What shift workers need from accommodation

Hotels are designed for people who sleep at night and go out during the day. Clinicians on rotas need something different:

  • Your own bedroom, ideally away from the kitchen, so you can sleep after a night shift
  • A proper kitchen for meal prep, because hospital canteens close and takeaways get expensive
  • Free parking, so you can drive in when trains aren't running
  • Laundry facilities for scrubs and uniforms (check what your trust provides)
  • Flexibility on dates, because rotas change
  • Quiet neighbours and house rules, so a party next door doesn't wreck your sleep

Getting around at odd hours

Trainline lists the first Fazakerley to Liverpool Central train at 04:46 and the last at 23:36, with direct journeys of about 15 minutes. Times vary at weekends and on holidays, so check Merseyrail the day before. For night shifts at the Royal, many people drive. For Aintree and The Walton Centre, staying in Fazakerley largely solves the problem.

Sharing with colleagues

If several clinicians from the same agency or trust are placed in Liverpool at once, sharing a house usually beats booking several hotel rooms:

  • One booking, one invoice for the agency or trust
  • Separate bedrooms, with two bathrooms between them
  • Two parking spaces, so people can car-share or drive themselves
  • A shared living space to unwind after difficult shifts

Agree a few house rules on day one: quiet hours for night workers, a kitchen rota and whose shelf is whose. Our guide to house-sharing etiquette for work crews has a simple checklist.

Tips for a smoother placement

  1. Ask your agency for the exact site and department before booking. "Liverpool" covers several hospitals miles apart.
  2. Check staff parking rules with the trust. They're often different from visitor tariffs.
  3. Do a dry run of your commute the evening you arrive, at the same time as your first shift.
  4. Stock up on day one. There's a Lidl near Zermatt Lodge.
  5. Plan for sleep. See our night-shift sleep tips.
  6. Keep receipts for any expenses your agency covers.

Questions to ask your agency or trust

  • Which site and department will I be working in, and where do staff park?
  • What are my shift times, and will they change during the placement?
  • Is there a staff ID or parking permit process on day one?
  • Will the agency book and pay for accommodation, or do I claim it back?
  • What's the process if the placement is extended or ends early?

Knowing this before you book makes it much easier to choose the right base and dates.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where should a locum stay for a placement at Aintree Hospital?

Fazakerley is the obvious choice. The hospital is in Fazakerley, and Fazakerley station is across the road from its Longmoor Lane entrance. Staying in the same suburb takes the commute out of early and late shifts.

Can several agency staff share a house in Liverpool?

Yes. A whole house with separate bedrooms is often better value and more comfortable than several hotel rooms. Zermatt Lodge sleeps up to 7 in 4 bedrooms, with 2 bathrooms and parking for 2 cars.

How early do trains run from Fazakerley?

Trainline lists the first Fazakerley to Liverpool Central train at 04:46 and the last at 23:36, with a journey of about 15 minutes. Weekend and holiday times can differ, so check Merseyrail before a shift.

Is Zermatt Lodge suitable for night-shift workers?

It's a quiet, residential house with no parties allowed, and each sharer gets their own room. Bring an eye mask and earplugs as you would anywhere, and agree quiet hours with housemates on different rotas.
