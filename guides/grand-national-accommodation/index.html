Grand National Accommodation: Where to Stay Near Aintree for the Festival

Grand National accommodation for groups: a 4-bed house in Fazakerley near Aintree with free parking. Festival dates, race-day trains and parking explained.

Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Fazakerley, the suburb next to Aintree, sleeping up to 7, with free parking for two cars. This guide covers festival dates, how to get to the course from a north Liverpool base, parking rules and how to plan a group stay.

Grand National Festival dates and format

The Randox Grand National Festival runs over three days at Aintree Racecourse. According to the Jockey Club, the next festival is Thursday 8 to Saturday 10 April 2027.

  • Opening Day (Thursday): the first day of the festival
  • Ladies Day (Friday): when style and fashion take the spotlight, with the Style Award presented
  • Grand National Day (Saturday): the main event

Many groups stay Thursday to Sunday so they aren't driving home on Saturday evening. If your group is going for Ladies Day, see our Ladies Day group stay guide.

Where Aintree Racecourse is

Aintree Racecourse is on Ormskirk Road, Aintree, Liverpool, L9 5AS, on the A59. The Jockey Club says it's about a mile from the M57 and M58. Fazakerley is the neighbouring suburb to the east, which makes it one of the closest residential areas to the course.

Getting to the races from Fazakerley

By train (recommended on festival days)

Fazakerley station is on Merseyrail's Northern line (Kirkby branch). Aintree station is on the Ormskirk branch. The two branches meet south of Fazakerley, so you need one change at Sandhills.

The Jockey Club's festival travel plan says:

  • Trains run every seven and a half minutes between Liverpool Central/Moorfields and Aintree on each festival day.
  • Going to the races, these run from about 11:00 to 13:00. Coming back, they run from about 16:30 to 20:30.
  • On the Kirkby line, between 11:00 and 14:00 and between 17:00 and 21:00, services start and end at Sandhills. Passengers returning to Rice Lane, Fazakerley, Kirkby or Headbolt Lane must change there.

So from Fazakerley you take a train towards the city, change at Sandhills onto an Aintree train, and reverse it on the way home. Check the Merseyrail journey planner or app before you go, because race-day timetables are amended. Our step-by-step guide to getting to Aintree from Fazakerley has more detail.

By bus

The Jockey Club lists services 300, 310 and 345 to and from Liverpool. Merseytravel has up-to-date times.

By car or taxi

There's a series of road closures around Aintree on festival days. If you're getting a lift or a taxi, you'll be dropped off and directed to the shuttle on the Mildmay Course, which runs to the Racecourse Enclosures. A short taxi ride from Fazakerley might sound easy, but closures and heavy traffic after the last race can make the train quicker.

Parking at the Grand National

The Jockey Club is clear: car parking must be booked in advance online. Other things to know:

  • Follow the A59 towards Liverpool and the yellow tourism signs to your car park.
  • At the Steeplechase Car Park, access via the Melling Road course crossing closes 30 minutes before the feature race and reopens 30 minutes after it.
  • No vehicles can move while the course crossings are closed, so leaving early isn't always possible.

Many groups staying nearby leave the cars at the house and take the train. Our Grand National parking guide goes into the options.

Planning a group stay for the festival

Book early

Festival week is one of the busiest times for accommodation in north Liverpool. Whole houses for 6 or 7 people go first. If your group has tickets, sort your accommodation straight away.

Think about the morning

Getting a group ready for the races takes time: showers, outfits, hair and breakfast. Two bathrooms, an en-suite master and plenty of bedroom space make a real difference compared with sharing hotel rooms.

Plan the evening

Restaurants and pubs near Aintree get very busy on race evenings. Plenty of groups come back, cook a simple dinner and spend the evening at home with a pool table and board games instead. If you do want to eat out, book well ahead.

Make it a long weekend

Many visitors add a city day on the Sunday: the waterfront, the Albert Dock or a Mersey ferry. See things to do in Liverpool with a group.

Grand National checklist

  • Race tickets, plus pre-booked parking if you're driving
  • Check the dress code for your enclosure (see our dress code guide)
  • Layers and a waterproof. April weather in Liverpool can change quickly.
  • Comfortable shoes for walking on grass, or a spare pair in a bag
  • A fully charged phone and the Merseyrail app
  • An agreed meeting point in case the group gets separated

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is the next Grand National Festival?

The Jockey Club lists the next Randox Grand National Festival as Thursday 8 to Saturday 10 April 2027. Thursday is Opening Day, Friday is Ladies Day and Saturday is Grand National Day.

How close is Fazakerley to Aintree Racecourse?

Fazakerley is a neighbouring suburb, a couple of miles from the racecourse on Ormskirk Road (L9 5AS). On race days, road closures and crowds mean the trip takes longer than the distance suggests, so plan your route in advance.

Can I get the train from Fazakerley to Aintree on race days?

Yes, with a change. Fazakerley is on the Kirkby branch and Aintree station is on the Ormskirk branch. On festival days, the Jockey Club says Kirkby-line trains start and end at Sandhills between 11:00 and 14:00 and between 17:00 and 21:00, so change at Sandhills for the frequent Aintree trains.

Do I need to book parking for the Grand National?

Yes. The Jockey Club says car parking must be booked in advance online.

When should I book Grand National accommodation?

As early as you can. Rooms near Aintree are in very high demand for festival week. Larger houses that sleep a whole group are especially scarce.
