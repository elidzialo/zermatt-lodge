Go-Karting in Liverpool for Groups

Planning go-karting in Liverpool for a group? TeamSport's multi-level electric track, ages and inside-leg rules, Grand Prix and combat kart formats, and e-karting at Gravity MAX.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

The main indoor karting track in Liverpool is TeamSport Liverpool: a 475m multi-level electric track with 10 corners, plus a 275m starter track. Juniors can drive from age 8 (inside leg at least 25 inches) and adult karts need age 13 and a 29-inch inside leg. Group formats include Grand Prix, Ultimate Race and Combat Karts. Gravity MAX at Liverpool ONE also has e-karting alongside bowling, darts and VR.

Go-karting is a great group activity: it's competitive, it's over in a couple of hours and everyone has a story afterwards. Here's what's on offer in Liverpool and how to pick the right format for your group.

Age and size rules

  • Juniors: from age 8, with an inside leg of at least 25 inches
  • Adult karts: from age 13, with an inside leg of at least 29 inches
  • Combat Karts: 13+

Measure inside legs before you book if you have younger teens in the group.

Group formats

Format Track What happens
Grand Prix Main Qualifying, then a grid-start race and a podium presentation
Ultimate Race Experience Main Two sessions with a pit lane start. Fastest lap wins
Combat Karts Starter Two 12-minute sessions, medals for the top three. 13+
Rookie Race Starter Two sessions, good for younger drivers. Parties need 6 drivers or more

For a group of adults who all want to win, go for the Grand Prix. For a mixed group, the Ultimate Race is more relaxed.

Prices and booking

TeamSport says prices vary by day, time, experience and group size. It recommends pre-booking large groups, especially at weekends, and offers 10% off online pre-bookings with a code shown on its site.

Gravity MAX at Liverpool ONE

If you'd rather stay in town, Gravity MAX in Liverpool ONE has an e-karting track with downhill twists and fast corners, across a two-floor venue that also has:

  • AR darts and AR bowling
  • Immersive GameBox and Hologate VR
  • Urban Street Golf
  • Shuffleboard and batting cages
  • Food and bars, including a rooftop terrace

It's a good option for a group with mixed interests, because not everyone has to kart. Check age rules and opening times on its booking site.

Make a day of it

  • Morning: karting
  • Lunch: a booked table for the group
  • Afternoon: an escape room or crazy golf and darts
  • Evening: back to the house to cook

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How old do you have to be to go karting at TeamSport Liverpool?

Junior karting starts at age 8, with an inside leg of at least 25 inches. Adult karts need a minimum age of 13 and an inside leg of 29 inches. Combat Karts is 13+.

Are the karts electric?

Yes. TeamSport says all its Liverpool karts are electric, with a top speed of 45mph on the main track, 30mph on the starter track and 25mph for junior karts.

What's the best format for a group?

For competitive adults, the Grand Prix: a qualifying session, then a grid-start race and a podium. For a relaxed or mixed group, the Ultimate Race Experience, where the fastest lap wins. Combat Karts adds a game element on the starter track.

How much does go-karting cost in Liverpool?

TeamSport says prices vary by day, time, experience and group size, so check its booking page. It offers 10% off online pre-bookings with a code shown on its site.

Is there go-karting in Liverpool city centre?

Gravity MAX at Liverpool ONE has an e-karting track with downhill twists, plus darts, bowling, VR and street golf in the same building.
