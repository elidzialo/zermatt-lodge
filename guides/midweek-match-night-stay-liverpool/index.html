Midweek Night Matches in Liverpool: Dates, Late Travel and Staying Over

Going to an evening game at Anfield or Hill Dickinson Stadium this winter? Midweek dates for Dec 2026 and Jan 2027, getting home late and where to stay.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

This winter's midweek games in Liverpool include Liverpool v Sunderland (Wed 2 Dec 2026, 7.30pm, moved for Sky Sports), Everton v Manchester City (Tue 29 Dec, 8.15pm, moved for Sky Sports) and Everton v Aston Villa (Tue 5 Jan 2027, reported as 7.30pm). An evening kick-off ends late, so staying over avoids a rush for the last train or a long drive home. Check dates again, as fixtures move for TV.

An evening kick-off is a great atmosphere and a hard trip home. A 7.30pm or 8pm game finishes after 9.30pm, and then you've got crowds, queues for buses and trains, and a long drive if you've come from outside Merseyside. If you live more than an hour or so away, staying over turns a stressful midweek dash into an easy night out, and you can travel home the next morning.

Getting home late

From Anfield

The matchday Soccerbus links Anfield with Sandhills station. It runs back to Sandhills for about 90 minutes after the game, with the last bus from Walton Lane about 75 minutes after the final whistle. From Sandhills, Merseyrail's Northern line runs north towards Kirkby and Fazakerley.

From Hill Dickinson Stadium

Everton's stadium is at Bramley-Moore Dock on the north docks. Sandhills is the nearest Merseyrail station for most fans and is walkable from the ground. Trainline lists the Fazakerley to Sandhills trip at around 8 minutes.

Check the last train

Late-evening Merseyrail services run less often than daytime trains. Check the last train from Sandhills to your station before the game, not after it. If you miss it, taxis near the grounds are in high demand straight after the match.

Why stay over

  • No late drive. You won't be on the motorway at midnight after a long day.
  • Beat the crowds. Have a drink, wait for the rush to clear, then head back.
  • Make a trip of it. Spend the day in the city, see the stadium tour or the waterfront, then go to the match.
  • Work the next day? If you're travelling for work anyway, a midweek match fits around meetings.

Frequently asked questions

What time is Liverpool v Sunderland in December 2026?

Liverpool FC confirmed Liverpool v Sunderland for Wednesday 2 December 2026 at 7.30pm GMT, shown on Sky Sports.

When is Everton v Manchester City?

Manchester City say the game at Hill Dickinson Stadium was brought forward to Tuesday 29 December 2026, kicking off at 8.15pm and shown live on Sky Sports.

How do I get back from Anfield after an evening game?

The matchday Soccerbus runs from Anfield back to Sandhills station for about 90 minutes after the game, with the last bus from Walton Lane about 75 minutes after the final whistle. From Sandhills, Merseyrail's Northern line runs north to Fazakerley. Check the last trains on the night.

Is it worth staying over after a midweek match?

If you live more than an hour or so away, often yes. An evening game finishes late, crowds slow the trip out of the city, and you avoid driving tired. A whole house shared by a group can cost less per person than several hotel rooms.
