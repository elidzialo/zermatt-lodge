The Beatles Story: A Visitor Guide for Winter 2026 and 2027

Visiting The Beatles Story at the Royal Albert Dock? Ticket prices, winter and Christmas opening times, how long to allow, access, parking and what changes in 2027.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

The Beatles Story is on the Royal Albert Dock. Tickets are £20 for adults (16+), £16 for seniors and students and £11 for children aged 5 to 15, and must be booked online. In winter (5 October 2026 to 1 March 2027) it opens 9am to 5.30pm Sunday to Friday and to 6.30pm on Saturdays and school holidays. It is closed on 25 and 26 December. Most people spend about 1.5 hours inside.

The Beatles Story is the big Beatles museum on Liverpool's waterfront, inside the Royal Albert Dock. It tells the band's story, and part of the route is a Cavern Club area. It's a good first stop before you head to Mathew Street.

We checked the details below on 24 November 2026. Times and prices can change, so check the official site before you go.

Winter and Christmas opening times

From 5 October 2026 to 1 March 2027:

  • Sunday to Friday: 9am to 5.30pm (last entry 4.15pm)
  • Saturdays and Liverpool school holidays: 9am to 6.30pm (last entry 5.15pm)

From 2 March 2027 it opens 9am to 6.30pm, last entry 5pm.

How long to allow

The attraction says most people spend about 1.5 hours, and up to 3 hours if you read and listen to everything. Your ticket also covers the Discovery Zone, which has free children's workshops, plus the Fab4 Stores and Fab4 Cafe.

Access

When we checked, the attraction said its entrance lift was out of order, so the exhibition was reached by steps only. If anyone in your group needs step-free access, check the lift status page on the official site before you book. Hearing loops and large-print guides are available on request.

Getting there and parking

The Beatles Story has no parking of its own. It suggests the car park opposite the Albert Dock entrance, the arena car park and Q-Park Liverpool ONE. Coaches can drop off on Salthouse Quay or Gower Street.

Our Royal Albert Dock guide covers the rest of the dock, including what's open and what's closed for redevelopment.

What changes in 2027 and 2028

The Beatles Story says it will stay at the Royal Albert Dock until 2028. Apple Corps, the Beatles' company, plans to take over the attraction in 2027, and a new attraction is planned for St Johns Shopping Centre in 2028, subject to approval. If you're visiting this winter, nothing changes for you.

Make a Beatles day of it

  1. Morning: The Beatles Story at the Albert Dock
  2. Lunch: around the dock or in Liverpool ONE
  3. Afternoon: walk into the city centre to Mathew Street and the Cavern Club, where live music starts at 11.15am every day
  4. Evening: back to your base for dinner

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much are Beatles Story tickets?

The Beatles Story lists £20 for adults (16 and over), £16 for concessions (seniors and students), £11 for children aged 5 to 15, and free entry for babies. Prices can change, so check the booking site.

Do I need to book The Beatles Story in advance?

Yes. The FAQs say tickets must be bought online in advance. Card-only kiosks on site sell the next available slot on the day, and the admissions desk doesn't take cash.

How long does The Beatles Story take?

The attraction says the main exhibition takes 1 to 3 hours, with the average visitor spending about 1.5 hours.

Is The Beatles Story open at Christmas?

It's open 10am to 3pm on Christmas Eve, closed on Christmas Day and Boxing Day, and open 10am to 4pm on New Year's Eve and New Year's Day.

Is The Beatles Story moving?

The attraction says it stays at the Royal Albert Dock until 2028. Apple Corps plans to take it over in 2027, and a new attraction is planned for St Johns Shopping Centre in 2028, subject to approval.

Is there parking at The Beatles Story?

There's no dedicated parking. The attraction points visitors to the car park opposite the Albert Dock entrance, the arena car park and Liverpool ONE.
