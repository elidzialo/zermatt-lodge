Shabbat Over Christmas and New Year in Liverpool: How to Plan

Christmas Day and New Year's Day 2026-27 both fall on a Friday. How Jewish visitors can plan Shabbat in Liverpool around shop closures and festive transport.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

In 2026, Christmas Day falls on Friday 25 December and New Year's Day on Friday 1 January 2027, so both are erev Shabbat. Large shops must close on Christmas Day and trains haven't run on Christmas Day in past years, so arrive and do your Shabbat shopping by Thursday 24 December. The Christmas school holidays are a popular time for Jewish families to get away, and in 2026 the calendar adds a twist: Christmas Day is Friday 25 December and New Year's Day is Friday 1 January 2027. Shabbat times

Winter Shabbat in Liverpool starts early. Sunset is at its earliest on 13 December 2026, at 3.54pm, so candle lighting on both Fridays is mid afternoon. Check the exact time on a Jewish calendar.

Food: plan for both weeks at once

Kosher supply in Liverpool is limited: the city has one kosher deli. Most visitors bring food or order from shops on the Manchester Beth Din's official licensee list.

Over Christmas, that takes extra planning:

  1. Ask shops about Christmas week hours before you travel. Don't assume normal hours
  2. Order early. Bakeries and butchers get busy before a long weekend
  3. Bring enough for both Shabbatot, or plan a second shop on Tuesday 29 or Wednesday 30 December
  4. Freeze what you can when you arrive
  5. Check the rental kitchen. See our guide to keeping kosher in a holiday rental

Travel

The 2026 festive timetable for Merseyrail and buses hadn't been published when we checked. In past years Merseyrail hasn't run on Christmas Day, and the Combined Authority has warned of very limited buses around the holidays.

  • Travel by Wednesday 23 or Thursday 24 December
  • If you're driving from Manchester, avoid the M62 on Christmas Eve afternoon if you can
  • For New Year, arrive on Thursday 31 December or earlier

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Christmas Day 2026 on a Friday?

Yes. Christmas Day is Friday 25 December 2026, a bank holiday, so Shabbat begins that afternoon. New Year's Day 2027 is also a Friday.

Can I buy food for Shabbat on Christmas Day?

Don't plan on it. Large shops over 280 square metres must close on Christmas Day in England and Wales. Do your shopping by Thursday 24 December, and earlier for kosher items.

Will trains run on Christmas Day so I can travel before Shabbat?

The 2026 festive timetable hadn't been published when we checked. In past years Merseyrail hasn't run on Christmas Day. Plan to arrive by Christmas Eve.

When does Shabbat start in Liverpool in late December?

Mid afternoon. Sunset in Liverpool is at its earliest in mid December, at 3.54pm on 13 December 2026 and stays early through late December. Get the exact candle lighting time from a Jewish calendar.

Is the Boxing Day bank holiday on a Saturday in 2026?

Boxing Day itself is Saturday 26 December, which is Shabbat, so the bank holiday moves to Monday 28 December. Shops and services may be closed or on reduced hours that Monday.
