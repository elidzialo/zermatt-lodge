How to Get Direct Bookings for Your Holiday Let

Practical ways UK hosts get direct bookings: repeat guests, your own site, Google, local partners and marketplaces, plus the rules on not moving Airbnb guests.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Direct bookings come from guests who already know you or find you by name: past guests who opted in to hear from you, your own booking page, Google, local businesses who send people your way, and marketplaces that charge less. Do not ask Airbnb guests to book or pay outside Airbnb: build direct demand from your own channels instead.

Direct bookings come from people who already know your home or can find it by name. Building them takes months, not days, but every one saves you a platform fee and gives you a guest you can welcome back.

First, the rule to respect

Airbnb does not allow hosts to ask Airbnb guests to book or pay outside Airbnb. Keep your Airbnb messages about the stay. Build direct demand from your own channels instead, and you keep your Airbnb account safe.

Seven ways to build direct bookings

  1. Your own booking page. A clear page with photos, the total price, house rules and a secure card payment. A low-fee marketplace page can do this job while you build your own.
  2. Google. Make your home easy to find by name. A consistent name across every listing helps.
  3. Guests who booked direct. Ask them, with clear consent, if they want to hear about dates and offers. Keep the list under UK data protection rules and make it easy to unsubscribe.
  4. Local partners. Hospitals, wedding venues, event organisers, contractors and relocation agents often need somewhere for families or crews. Offer a simple weekly or monthly rate.
  5. Work stays. Companies booking crews want invoices, van parking and separate beds. Say so clearly.
  6. Niche audiences. If your home suits a specific group, such as observant families with a kosher kitchen, list it where those guests search.
  7. Repeat seasons. Note the events that fill your area each year and update your page early.

Keep the calendar safe

The more places you take bookings, the more important calendar sync is. Connect every channel both ways with iCal links. See sync your Airbnb calendar with another site.

Do the basics properly

Direct guests rely on your own terms, so have a written cancellation policy, clear house rules, holiday let insurance and the safety checks set out in the GOV.UK guidance for self-catering hosts.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I give Airbnb guests my website for next time?

Airbnb's rules do not allow you to ask guests to book or pay outside Airbnb. Build your direct audience from your own channels, such as guests who booked direct, your website and local partners.

Do I need a website for direct bookings?

It helps but is not essential. A listing on a low-fee marketplace and a simple page with your details can be enough to start.

How do I take payment safely for a direct booking?

Take card payments through a proper payment provider with clear terms and a cancellation policy. Card payments also protect your guest better than a bank transfer.
