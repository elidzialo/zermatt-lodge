Working on Liverpool's Waterfront Projects: What's Happening and Where to Stay

Central Docks at Liverpool Waters: what GRAHAM is building, the 2028 timeline, the north docks development zone, and where crews can stay with parking.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

The main live job on the waterfront is Central Docks at Peel's Liverpool Waters, where GRAHAM is building £81m of roads, utilities, drainage and bridge foundations, funded by Peel and Homes England, with the infrastructure due to finish in 2028. It will open up land for 2,350 homes and a 4.7-acre park. Hill Dickinson Stadium sits at the northern end. North Liverpool is a practical base: Sandhills station is about 8 minutes by train from Fazakerley.

Liverpool's northern waterfront is one of the biggest regeneration areas in the North West. Everton's Hill Dickinson Stadium is open at Bramley-Moore Dock, and the next big job is Central Docks, the first major neighbourhood at Peel's Liverpool Waters. Behind that sits a much wider plan for the north docks. This guide sums up what's on site now and how crews can stay close by.

We used public reports only, checked on 22 November 2026. Always check the current programme with your project lead.

Central Docks: what's on site now

According to Place North West in November 2026:

  • Main contractor: GRAHAM
  • Value: £81m, with £25.9m from Peel for the infrastructure and £55.2m from Homes England
  • Done after a year on site: about 12 miles of utility ducting, 1.7 miles of drainage, new highways and foundations for two bridges
  • Timeline: GRAHAM expects to finish the infrastructure in 2028
  • The end result: a neighbourhood planned for 2,350 homes and a 4.7-acre park designed by Planit, with a play area, community garden and open parkland

Peel is talking to developers and registered providers about plots, including build-to-rent, affordable and later living housing. That means more building contracts are likely once the infrastructure is ready.

The wider north docks

In December 2025, the Metro Mayor and the leader of Liverpool City Council announced a proposed North Docks Mayoral Development Corporation, as reported by Liverpool Express:

  • About 174 hectares, mostly brownfield
  • Potential for more than 17,500 homes and 5 million sq ft of commercial space over 15 years
  • Named schemes including Central Docks, the King Edward Triangle and Pall Mall

For the office scheme, see our Pall Mall project stays guide. For the full regional picture, see Liverpool's construction pipeline.

Getting to the waterfront

  • Train: Sandhills station on the Northern line is the usual stop for Hill Dickinson Stadium. From Fazakerley it's about 8 minutes (fastest 7). Our Sandhills station guide covers the walk
  • Road: the dock road is busy with port traffic. Allow extra time at shift change
  • Match days: Hill Dickinson Stadium holds 52,769. On Everton home days, roads and trains around Sandhills get very busy, so check the fixture list against your shifts

What crews need from a base

  • Off-street parking for vans and cars, with tools out of sight
  • Separate beds for colleagues
  • A washer and dryer for workwear. See our laundry guide for workers
  • A big kitchen for early breakfasts and evening meals
  • Monthly rates for long jobs

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is being built at Central Docks, Liverpool Waters?

Infrastructure first. Place North West reported in November 2026 that GRAHAM had installed about 12 miles of utility ducting and 1.7 miles of drainage, built highways and laid foundations for two bridges. The finished neighbourhood is planned to have 2,350 homes and a 4.7-acre park.

Who is the contractor at Central Docks?

GRAHAM is the main contractor for the infrastructure works, according to Place North West.

When will Central Docks be finished?

GRAHAM expects to complete the infrastructure in 2028. Homes and other buildings will follow on plots that Peel is discussing with developers.

What is the North Docks development zone?

In December 2025 the Metro Mayor and Liverpool City Council proposed a North Docks Mayoral Development Corporation covering about 174 hectares, with potential for more than 17,500 homes and 5 million sq ft of commercial space over 15 years, according to Liverpool Express.

What's the nearest station to Liverpool Waters?

Sandhills, on Merseyrail's Northern line, is the usual station for Hill Dickinson Stadium at Bramley-Moore Dock. From Fazakerley it is about 8 minutes by train.
