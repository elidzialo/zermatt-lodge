Keeping Kosher on a Business Trip to Manchester

Keeping kosher on a work trip to Manchester or Salford? How to use the Manchester Beth Din licensee list, ask for sealed meals at conferences, and book a home with a host-declared kosher kitchen.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

For kosher food on a Manchester work trip, start with the Manchester Beth Din's official licensee list, which covers butchers, bakeries, caterers, restaurants and takeaways, and groceries. Check current supervision before you buy. For conferences and client lunches, ask the organiser early for a sealed meal from a supervised caterer. For a week or longer, a home with a host-declared kosher kitchen or Shabbat-friendly features, filtered on our kosher homes page, lets you cook for yourself. For shul and service details, contact the local community directly.

A few days of meetings in Manchester or at MediaCityUK is much easier when you've sorted food before you arrive. This guide covers the practical side. We checked it on 24 November 2026.

Food: use the official lists

  • The Manchester Beth Din (MBD) publishes an official licensee list, with sections for butchers, bakeries, caterers, restaurants and takeaways, groceries, milk, confectionery and fishmongers.
  • Other kashrut authorities, including KLBD and the London Beth Din, publish their own lists and product guides.
  • Licences change, so check the current list before you buy, and look for the certificate in the shop.
  • Our kosher food in Manchester guide explains how the different authorities work.

Meetings, conferences and client meals

  1. Ask early. Tell the organiser or venue at least a couple of weeks ahead that you need kosher meals.
  2. Ask for sealed meals from a supervised caterer, with the hechsher shown on the packaging.
  3. Get it in writing. A confirmation email avoids surprises on the day.
  4. Client dinners. Suggest a restaurant on the licensee list, or a lunch you can bring in.

Where to stay

On a trip of more than a night or two, a kitchen of your own makes a big difference.

  • On Book Homes, hosts can declare a kosher kitchen and Shabbat-friendly features, such as an urn, a hotplate, lamp timers, a non-electric key and no motion-sensor lights.
  • These are host declarations, not certification. Ask the host exactly how the kitchen is set up and decide with your own rabbi.
  • Filter for these homes on our kosher homes page. Our kosher-friendly holiday homes guide explains what to ask.
  • If you're staying over Shabbat, see Shabbat in Manchester.

Shul, mikvah and services: contact the local community or the Manchester Beth Din directly. We don't publish community locations or times.

  • For invoices in your company's name, see invoices and VAT for business stays.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find kosher food in Manchester for a work trip?

Use the Manchester Beth Din's official licensee list. It covers butchers, bakeries, caterers, restaurants and takeaways, groceries, milk, confectionery and fishmongers. Check current supervision before you buy.

Can a Manchester conference venue provide kosher meals?

Ask the organiser and the venue well before the event for a sealed meal from a supervised caterer, with the hechsher shown, and get confirmation in writing.

Are Book Homes kosher homes certified?

No. Kosher kitchens and Shabbat features on Book Homes are declared by the host, not certified by us or by a kashrut authority. Ask the host for details and check with your own rabbi.

How do I find a shul or minyan on a work trip?

Contact the local community or the Manchester Beth Din directly before you travel. We don't publish community locations or times.
