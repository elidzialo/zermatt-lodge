Fazakerley to Liverpool City Centre by Train: A Simple Guide

Fazakerley to Liverpool city centre by Merseyrail: stations on the line, journey time, first and last trains, which stop to use and ticket tips.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Getting from Fazakerley to Liverpool city centre by train is easy. Merseyrail's Northern line runs direct from Fazakerley to Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes, calling at Rice Lane, Kirkdale, Sandhills and Moorfields on the way. Trainline lists the first train at 04:46 and the last at 23:36 on its standard timetable. This guide explains the line, which stop to use for where you're going, and tips for evenings, Sundays and event days.

The line at a glance

Fazakerley is on the Kirkby branch of Merseyrail's Northern line. Merseyrail lists the stations on this route as:

Headbolt Lane - Kirkby - Fazakerley - Rice Lane - Kirkdale - Sandhills - Moorfields - Liverpool Central

Trains towards the city are usually shown as going to Liverpool Central. Trains in the other direction go to Kirkby or Headbolt Lane. Headbolt Lane opened in 2023 as an extension beyond Kirkby.

Fazakerley station is also the station for Aintree University Hospital. The hospital says the station is across the road from its Longmoor Lane entrance.

Journey times

  • Fazakerley to Liverpool Central: about 15 minutes, direct
  • Fazakerley to Sandhills: a few stops, for Everton's stadium and the Anfield Soccerbus
  • Fazakerley to Kirkdale: two stops, with walking routes to Anfield
  • Fazakerley to Kirkby: one stop the other way

How often trains run

During the day there are several trains an hour. Frequency drops in the evening and on Sundays, when you might wait up to half an hour. Merseyrail has changed this route's timetable over the last few years, so always check the Merseyrail app or journey planner for your exact day. See our tips for evenings and Sundays.

Tickets

  • Buy at the station machine or ticket office, on the Merseyrail app, or use contactless where it's accepted.
  • If you're making several trips in a day, a day ticket can work out cheaper. See our Merseyrail tickets guide.
  • Children travel at reduced fares.
  • Keep your ticket until you exit. Some city stations have barriers.

Event days and disruption

  • Grand National Festival: the Jockey Club's travel plan says that on festival days, Kirkby-line trains start and end at Sandhills between about 11:00 and 14:00 and between 17:00 and 21:00. If you're travelling between Fazakerley and the city then, you'll need to change at Sandhills.
  • Football match days: Sandhills and Kirkdale get very busy before and after games. Allow extra time, or travel at a different time if you aren't going to the match.
  • Engineering works: Merseyrail sometimes replaces trains with buses, especially at weekends. Check before you travel.

Common trips, step by step

Arriving at Lime Street by mainline train Walk a few minutes from Lime Street to Liverpool Central. Take a Northern line train towards Kirkby or Headbolt Lane and get off at Fazakerley, about 15 minutes later.

A day in the city centre Fazakerley to Liverpool Central, then walk to Liverpool ONE, Bold Street and down to the Royal Albert Dock. Come back from Central.

Visiting the Pier Head and the Three Graces Get off at Moorfields for a shorter walk to the waterfront, or at Central if you're shopping first.

Everton match day Fazakerley to Sandhills, then walk to Bramley-Moore Dock. Expect queues after the final whistle.

Liverpool match day Fazakerley to Sandhills for the Soccerbus, or to Kirkdale to walk.

Going to Kirkby Take a train in the other direction, towards Kirkby or Headbolt Lane. Kirkby is the next stop.

Tips for visitors

  1. Check the train's destination. Into town, you want Liverpool Central. Heading home, you want Kirkby or Headbolt Lane.
  2. Plan your last train home on nights out, concerts or evening matches.
  3. Use a taxi as a back-up for late nights. See taxis in Liverpool.
  4. Travelling with luggage? There's space on the trains, but avoid rush hour if you can.
  5. Step-free access: check station accessibility on Merseyrail's website before travelling.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long is the train from Fazakerley to Liverpool Central?

About 15 minutes on a direct Merseyrail Northern line train, according to Trainline.

What stations are between Fazakerley and Liverpool Central?

Heading into the city, the line runs Fazakerley, Rice Lane, Kirkdale, Sandhills, Moorfields, then Liverpool Central.

What time is the first and last train from Fazakerley?

Trainline lists the first train from Fazakerley to Liverpool Central at 04:46 and the last at 23:36 on its standard timetable. Times differ at weekends and on holidays.

How often do trains run?

Several trains an hour during the day, with fewer in the evening and on Sundays. Check the Merseyrail app for your date.

Which station is best for Lime Street?

Get off at Liverpool Central and walk a few minutes to Lime Street, or use Moorfields for the business district and waterfront.
