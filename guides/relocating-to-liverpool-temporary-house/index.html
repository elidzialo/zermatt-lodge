Relocating to Liverpool: Using a Short-Stay House as Your Base

Moving to Liverpool? How to use a furnished short-stay house as your relocation base: viewings, school transfers, GP registration and areas to research.

This guide walks through a practical relocation plan.

Why a house beats a hotel for relocating

Relocation stays are different from holidays:

  • They're longer. Weeks, not nights.
  • They're busy. Viewings, work, admin and school runs.
  • They involve normal life. Cooking, laundry, homework and bedtimes.
  • Dates move. Completions slip and start dates change.

A whole house gives children their own rooms, lets you cook proper meals and gives you a garden and living space at the end of a long day of viewings. Free parking means you can drive to viewings across the city region.

Step 1: Choose your base location

Being central to the areas you're researching saves a lot of driving. North Liverpool works well if you're looking at:

  • North Liverpool suburbs: Aintree, Walton, West Derby, Croxteth and Fazakerley itself
  • Sefton: Maghull, Crosby, Formby and Southport, via Switch Island and the M58
  • Knowsley: Kirkby, Prescot and Huyton, via the M57
  • West Lancashire: Ormskirk and Skelmersdale, via the M58

And when you need the city centre, the Northern line from Fazakerley reaches Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes.

Step 2: Plan your viewing loops

Group viewings by area to cut down on driving. For example:

  • Day 1: Sefton coast (Crosby, Formby)
  • Day 2: north Liverpool suburbs (Aintree, Maghull, West Derby)
  • Day 3: second viewings and admin

Visit at different times of day. School-run traffic, train frequency and evening noise can all feel very different from a Sunday morning. See our house-hunting trip planner.

Step 3: Sort school places

School admissions depend on which council area you're moving into.

Liverpool City Council says:

  • If you're moving into Liverpool from another council area or an independent school, apply online through the council's Citizen Portal.
  • Applications should be processed in no longer than 15 school days, under the School Admissions Code.
  • Transfers can't be fully processed during school holidays, so plan around them.
  • For Years 10 and 11, the council recommends talking to your current headteacher first, because a move can affect courses and exam boards.

Sefton (Crosby, Formby, Maghull, Southport) and Knowsley (Kirkby, Prescot, Huyton) run their own in-year admissions, so check the right council for your new address. See our school places guide.

Step 4: Register with a GP

You don't need to wait until you've moved permanently. The NHS says:

  • You don't need ID, proof of address or proof of immigration status to register with a GP surgery.
  • You can register as a temporary patient for up to 3 months, then reapply or register permanently.
  • Use the NHS Find a GP service to search by postcode.

Register near your temporary base if you need care now, and move to a surgery near your new home once you know where you'll live.

Step 5: Handle the rest of the admin

  • Bank and post: set up mail redirection from your old address.
  • Work: do a trial commute at rush hour from your shortlisted areas.
  • Transport: look at Merseyrail lines near each area. The Northern line and its branches serve much of north Liverpool and Sefton.
  • Utilities and council tax: these apply once you have your new home. Check with the council for your new address.

Our first-month guide to moving to Liverpool has a full checklist.

Step 6: Look after the family

Moving is stressful, especially for children. Keep some normality:

  • Regular mealtimes and bedtimes
  • A weekend outing: Croxteth Hall, Crosby Beach or a city museum
  • Downtime at home with board games, pool or a film

A sample first fortnight

  • Days 1 to 2: settle in, food shop, register with a GP if you need care, and do a trial commute
  • Days 3 to 6: viewing loops by area, with evenings for research
  • Day 7: a family day out to recharge
  • Days 8 to 10: second viewings, offers or rental applications, school applications
  • Days 11 to 14: admin (post redirection, school uniforms, utilities for the new place) and planning the move

Everyone's timeline is different, but writing a rough plan like this helps you decide how long to book.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where can a family stay while relocating to Liverpool?

A furnished whole house is usually more comfortable than a hotel for a relocating family. Zermatt Lodge in Fazakerley sleeps 7 in 4 bedrooms, with a full kitchen, garden and free parking for 2 cars, and it's near the M57/M58 and a Merseyrail station.

Can I register with a GP while in temporary accommodation?

Yes. The NHS says you can register as a temporary patient for up to 3 months, and you don't need ID or proof of address to register with a GP surgery.

How do I apply for a school place when moving to Liverpool?

Liverpool City Council says families moving into Liverpool apply for an in-year place online through its Citizen Portal. Processing should take no longer than 15 school days, and transfers can't be fully processed during school holidays. Sefton and Knowsley councils run their own processes.

How long should I book a relocation stay for?

Enough time to view homes, settle work and sort schools, often two to six weeks. Talk to us about your dates so there's some flexibility if a move slips.
