Liverpool vs Manchester for a Group Weekend: Which Base Suits You?

Liverpool vs Manchester for a group or family weekend: travel between the two, what each city does best, and whole houses for groups in Prestwich, Crumpsall and Liverpool.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Pick the city where most of your plans are, and day-trip to the other: TransPennine Express says Liverpool Lime Street to Manchester Piccadilly takes as little as 51 minutes, about 31 miles. Choose Manchester for two Premier League grounds, big arenas and a larger choice of group houses on Book Homes (8 in Greater Manchester, sleeping up to 12). Choose Liverpool for the waterfront, Anfield, Aintree and the Hill Dickinson Stadium, with Zermatt Lodge in Liverpool sleeping 7 with free parking.

Liverpool and Manchester are close enough that you don't have to pick just one. The real question is where to sleep. Base yourself where most of your plans are, and take the train or drive to the other city for a day.

How close are they?

TransPennine Express says Liverpool Lime Street to Manchester Piccadilly takes as little as 51 minutes, a distance of about 31 miles, with direct trains through the day. It warns that journeys can take longer at weekends, on public holidays and during engineering work. Check the last train back before you set off, especially on a Sunday. By road, the M62 links the two cities.

Choose Manchester if...

  • You're going to the football. Manchester City and Manchester United both play at home through the winter. See our Manchester football winter guide for dates and trams.
  • You want a bigger house. Book Homes has 8 whole homes in Greater Manchester, sleeping 4 to 12, including 4 and 5-bedroom houses in Prestwich and Crumpsall with free parking. See our Manchester group accommodation guide.
  • You need kosher-friendly options. Some Manchester hosts declare a kosher kitchen or Shabbat-friendly features. See the kosher homes page.
  • You're at a show or concert at the Co-op Live, the AO Arena or one of the city centre theatres.
