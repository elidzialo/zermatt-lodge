Contractor Accommodation: Airbnb or Book Direct?

Booking crew accommodation? How Airbnb receipts and VAT invoices work for business, what booking direct gives you, and the checks to make either way.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

For a one-off short stay, Airbnb is quick and familiar. For crews, long jobs and anything your finance team needs to reconcile, booking direct is usually easier: you can agree an invoice in the company's name, monthly rates and flexible dates with the host. On Airbnb, business details must be added before the booking is confirmed to appear on the receipt, and Airbnb's VAT invoices cover its service fees, not the accommodation itself. Crew managers and site leads book a lot of accommodation, and the choice between Airbnb and booking direct with the host comes up every time. Neither is always better. Airbnb is quick for a short, simple stay. Direct is usually easier for crews, long jobs and anything your accounts team needs to reconcile. Receipts and invoices

On Airbnb

  • Add business details before you book. Airbnb's help centre says business details added after a reservation is confirmed won't appear on the receipt. To get them on, you'd have to cancel and rebook, which may be subject to the host's cancellation policy
  • VAT invoices cover Airbnb's fee. Airbnb says it provides a VAT invoice whenever VAT is charged on its service fees, and that VAT invoices can't be changed once issued
  • The accommodation charge comes from the host. If you need a VAT invoice for the stay itself, the host must be VAT registered and issue one

Booking direct

  • Agree the invoice before you pay: company name and address, guest names, dates, and a purchase order number if you use them
  • Ask whether the host is VAT registered. Many small hosts aren't, so there may be no VAT to reclaim
  • One invoice for the whole crew is often possible

Our invoices and VAT guide explains what a valid VAT invoice must show.

Price

Airbnb says hosts who set prices through property management software now pay a single 15.5% service fee, and guests see the host's price without a separate guest fee on top. Hosts usually build platform fees into their prices, so the same home can cost less booked direct. Compare the total for the same dates and the same number of guests.

Safety and protection

Booking direct means relying on the host's own terms, so check:

  • A real business with a working phone number and clear terms
  • Card payment on a secure page. MoneySavingExpert warns about holiday booking scams, and bank transfers give you very little protection
  • Cancellation, deposit and damage terms in writing

Never pay a stranger by bank transfer for a booking you found on social media.

Our view

  • One person, two nights, no invoice needed: use whichever is quicker
  • A crew, a week or more, invoice needed: book direct and agree terms first
  • Not sure about the host? Book through a platform you trust

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a business receipt from Airbnb?

Yes, but add your business details before the reservation is confirmed. Airbnb says details added after confirmation won't appear on the receipt, and to get them on it you'd need to cancel and rebook, which may be subject to the host's cancellation policy.

Does Airbnb give a VAT invoice for the stay?

Airbnb says it provides a VAT invoice whenever VAT is charged on its service fees. That covers Airbnb's fee, not the host's accommodation charge. Whether the accommodation itself carries reclaimable VAT depends on whether the host is VAT registered.

Is booking direct cheaper than Airbnb for contractors?

Often, because the host isn't paying platform commission. Airbnb says hosts who set prices through property management software pay a single 15.5% service fee, and many hosts build platform fees into their prices. Always compare the total for the same dates.

What should a contractor check before booking direct?

That the host is a real, contactable business, that you pay by card on a secure page, that the invoice will be in your company's name, and what the cancellation and damage terms are.
