Liverpool and Everton in January 2027: Every Game and Kick-Off

Every Liverpool and Everton game in January 2027, with the Anfield derby moved to 12.30pm, Champions League nights and where to stay for a match.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

In January 2027 Liverpool play at Anfield against Coventry (Sat 2 Jan, 3pm), Crystal Palace (Sat 16 Jan, 3pm), RC Lens in the Champions League (Wed 27 Jan, 8pm) and Everton (Sat 30 Jan, 12.30pm). Everton's home games at Hill Dickinson Stadium are Aston Villa (Tue 5 Jan, 7.30pm) and Brentford (Sun 24 Jan, 2pm). Times can still change for TV.

January 2027 has six home games across Anfield and Hill Dickinson Stadium, plus cup ties still to be drawn. The big one is Liverpool v Everton at Anfield on Saturday 30 January, which was moved to 12.30pm on 19 November 2026 for TNT Sports. Liverpool also host RC Lens in the Champions League on Wednesday 27 January.

Here's every game we could confirm on 21 November 2026. All fixtures are subject to change.

The derby: what the 12.30pm kick-off means

An early kick-off changes the weekend.

  • Arrive on Friday. With a 12.30pm start, you'll want to be near the ground by late morning. Driving in on Saturday morning is a gamble
  • Eat early. Pubs and cafes near Anfield fill fast before a lunchtime game
  • Plan the afternoon. You're out by about 2.30pm, which leaves the rest of the day for the city or a night in

Our Merseyside derby weekend guide has more on both grounds.

Midweek games

Three January games kick off in the evening: Everton v Villa (7.30pm), Liverpool v Lens (8pm) and the trip to Sunderland. For the home ones, the final whistle comes around 9.30pm to 10pm. Staying over beats a late drive home, especially in winter weather.

Getting to the grounds

  • Hill Dickinson Stadium: Sandhills is the usual station, on the Merseyrail Northern line
  • Anfield: there's no station at the ground. Many fans walk, get a bus or taxi, or park away from the stadium. See our match day parking guide

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is Liverpool v Everton in January 2027?

Saturday 30 January 2027 at Anfield. On 19 November 2026 the kick-off was moved from 3pm to 12.30pm for live coverage on TNT Sports.

How many home games do Liverpool have in January 2027?

Four that are fixed so far: Coventry (2 January), Crystal Palace (16 January), RC Lens in the Champions League (27 January) and Everton (30 January). An FA Cup third round tie on the weekend of 9 and 10 January and a possible Carabao Cup semi-final could add more.

When are Everton at home in January 2027?

Aston Villa on Tuesday 5 January at 7.30pm and Brentford on Sunday 24 January at 2pm, both at Hill Dickinson Stadium. The Brentford game was moved from Saturday 23 January.

When is the FA Cup third round in 2027?

The weekend of 9 and 10 January 2027. Ties and venues depend on the draw.

Are these kick-off times final?

No. Premier League fixtures are subject to change for TV and cup runs. Check the club's website about a month before you travel.
