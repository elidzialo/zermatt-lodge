Getting Groceries Delivered to Your Holiday House

Ordering a supermarket delivery to your holiday home? Minimum spends, booking the slot, what to tell the host, and a first-night shopping list for groups.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

You can have a supermarket shop delivered to a holiday home: set the rental's postcode as a delivery address, book a slot after check-in, and add the host's delivery notes. Watch the minimum spend: Tesco's minimum basket for home delivery is £50, with a £5 charge if you spend less, and Sainsbury's Delivery Pass customers need to spend £50 or pay £7.50. Ask the host first whether deliveries are allowed and where drivers should park.

Arriving at a holiday house with a group, tired from the drive, and then having to find a supermarket is nobody's idea of a good start. A supermarket delivery booked for just after check-in solves it. Here's how to plan one, what it costs, and what to order.

We checked the delivery terms below on 22 November 2026. They change, so check your supermarket's site when you order.

Step by step

  1. Ask the host whether deliveries are fine, and where the driver should park and knock
  2. Add the rental as a new address in your supermarket account
  3. Book a slot after check-in. If check-in is 3pm, book from 4pm or later to allow for traffic
  4. Add delivery notes: house name or number, your mobile, and any parking instructions
  5. Say whether substitutions are OK. Turn them off for allergy or kosher items
  6. Make sure someone is in for the whole slot

Minimum spends

Supermarket Minimum basket If you spend less
Tesco £50 £5 minimum basket charge
Sainsbury's (Delivery Pass customers) £50 for slots from 7 April 2026 £7.50 for home delivery

Delivery charges on top vary by slot. For a group of six or seven, a first shop will usually be over £50 anyway.

Timing tips

  • Book early for busy dates. Christmas week, bank holidays and Grand National week fill up fast
  • Don't book the morning of arrival. You won't be in yet
  • Last day: don't over-order. Plan to use everything up by checkout

What to order for the first night

A simple list for a group of six or seven:

Breakfast - Milk, bread, butter, cereal, eggs, bacon or a veggie alternative, fruit, juice

First dinner (easy) - Two big pasta bakes or curries with rice, a bag of salad, garlic bread

Basics - Tea, coffee, sugar, cooking oil, salt and pepper - Washing-up liquid and dishwasher tablets, if the host doesn't provide enough - Kitchen roll and bin bags

Packed lunches - Wraps or rolls, fillings, crisps, fruit, water

Kosher or special diets

Supermarkets stock some kosher-certified products, but check the hechsher on each item and turn off substitutions. For fresh kosher food in the north west, check the Manchester Beth Din (MBD) licensee list on its official website. See our kosher food in Liverpool guide and kosher food in Manchester guide.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a supermarket delivery to an Airbnb or holiday home?

Usually, yes. Add the rental's address as a new delivery address in your supermarket account and book a slot after your check-in time. Check with the host first, and give the driver clear instructions.

What is the minimum spend for Tesco delivery?

Tesco says its minimum basket value for home delivery is £50. If your basket is under that, a £5 minimum basket charge is added.

What is the minimum spend for Sainsbury's delivery?

Sainsbury's says the minimum order for Delivery Pass customers is £50 for slots from 7 April 2026, and orders below that pay £7.50 for home delivery. Check current terms if you don't have a Delivery Pass.

When should I book the delivery slot?

After your check-in time, so someone is there to take it. If the check-in time is 3pm, book a slot from about 4pm to give yourself time to arrive. Book early for bank holidays and Christmas week.

Can I get kosher food delivered to a holiday home?

Supermarkets stock some kosher-certified products, so check each hechsher. For fresh kosher food, order from licensed shops in Manchester and ask whether they deliver or whether you need to collect.
