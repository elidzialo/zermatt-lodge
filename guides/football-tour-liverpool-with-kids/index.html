A Football Trip to Liverpool with Kids: Matches, Tours and Tips That Work for Families

Taking children on a Liverpool football trip? Junior memberships, easier women's matches, stadium tours for little ones, match day tips and a family base.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Plan a Liverpool football trip around your children's ages: a women's match or stadium tour often suits young children better than a sold-out Premier League game. Whatever you choose, arrive early, dress warmly and have an easy route home.

A football trip to Liverpool with kids works best when you plan around their age: a women's match or a stadium tour is often a better first experience for young children than a sold-out Premier League game, while older kids will remember a big match at Anfield or Hill Dickinson Stadium for life. Whatever you choose, arrive early, dress warmly and have an easy route home.

Here's how families can get the most from a football weekend in Liverpool.

Pick the right football for their age

Under 5s: tours and parks

Very young children often enjoy the stadium more without 50,000 people in it. A stadium tour, a kickabout in Stanley Park and a look at the stadiums from outside can be plenty. See our Liverpool for kids under 5 guide for more.

Ages 5 to 10: women's football and junior events

Women's matches are usually smaller, calmer and easier to get tickets for, making them a great first live game.

  • Liverpool FC Women play home matches at St Helens Stadium. Liverpool FC's ticket page lists fixtures including Liverpool v Chelsea on 21 November 2026 and Liverpool v Crystal Palace on 20 December 2026. See our LFC Women match guide.
  • Everton Women play at Goodison Park, the club's historic home. See our Goodison Park guide.

Everton also runs junior events. During Easter 2026, its Junior Fan Takeover Week at Hill Dickinson Stadium offered Mini Tunnel Tours for younger children (with less walking), plus free activities such as crafts, a games zone and football sessions with Everton in the Community coaches. Keep an eye on the club's site for future school-holiday events.

Ages 10 and up: a Premier League match

Older children are more likely to enjoy a full match day: the build-up, the singing and 90 minutes of football.

Tickets and junior memberships

Premier League tickets in Liverpool are hard to get. Start early.

  • Check each club's ticketing policy for age rules, accompanying adult rules and junior prices before you buy. They're set by the clubs and can change.
  • Liverpool FC junior membership covers children up to and including 16, in four tiers: Little Liver (0 to 3), Mighty Red (4 to 8), Red Squad (9 to 12) and Teen Red (13 to 16).
  • Only buy from official sources. Tickets from unofficial sellers can be cancelled, and you may be refused entry.

Our LFC and Everton ticket guide explains how ticket sales work at both clubs.

Stadium tours with children

Anfield

The Anfield Stadium Tour & Museum includes the home dressing room, the tunnel and This Is Anfield sign, the dugout and the museum. Football Ground Guide quotes £17 for a child under 16 and £77 for a family ticket (two adults, two children), and says the tour takes 70 to 90 minutes plus museum time. That can be long for little ones, so plan a snack break. Our Anfield stadium tour guide has full details.

Everton

Everton runs tours at Hill Dickinson Stadium, and its Easter 2026 Mini Tunnel Tours were designed specifically for younger children. See our Everton stadium tour guide.

Match day tips for families

Getting there

  • Don't drive to the ground. Liverpool FC says there's no paid parking at Anfield on match days, and the area is a residents' zone. Everton advises fans to park in the city centre or Bootle and travel in.
  • Use the train and shuttle. For Anfield, the Soccerbus runs from Sandhills station from two hours before kick-off. For Everton, Sandhills is about a 15-minute walk, and shuttle buses run from the city centre and Bootle.
  • Arrive early. Queues and crowds are easier with kids when you're not rushing.

What to bring

  • Warm layers and a waterproof. Stadiums are cold, especially for evening games.
  • Ear defenders for younger or noise-sensitive children. Crowds get very loud.
  • Snacks for the journey. Food queues at half-time are long.
  • Check the bag rules. Everton allows bags up to A4 size only, with no rucksacks and no storage. Liverpool FC sets its own rules, so check before you go.

Staying safe in the crowd

  • Agree a meeting point in case you get separated.
  • Put your phone number in your child's pocket or on a wristband.
  • Leave a few minutes before the end or wait until the crowds clear, rather than joining the crush at the final whistle.

Plan downtime around the football

Kids get tired. Mix football with other things:

  • Stanley Park, between Anfield and Goodison, for a run around. See our Stanley Park guide.
  • Rainy day options in the city. See rainy day Liverpool with kids.
  • Downtime back at your accommodation: a film, a board game, an early night.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What age can children go to Anfield?

Liverpool FC sets its own ticketing and child policies, which can change, so check the club's ticketing policies before you buy. Junior membership covers children up to and including 16, split into Little Liver (0 to 3), Mighty Red (4 to 8), Red Squad (9 to 12) and Teen Red (13 to 16).

What's the easiest live football for young children in Liverpool?

Women's matches are often a gentler first game. Liverpool FC lists LFC Women home fixtures at St Helens Stadium, including Liverpool v Chelsea on 21 November 2026 and v Crystal Palace on 20 December 2026. Everton Women play at Goodison Park.

Is the Anfield stadium tour good for kids?

Most children love the dressing room, tunnel and dugout. The standard tour takes about 70 to 90 minutes plus the museum, which can feel long for very young children. Football Ground Guide quotes a family ticket at £77 for two adults and two children.

Does Everton run tours for younger children?

Everton ran Mini Tunnel Tours designed for younger children, with less walking, during its Easter 2026 Junior Fan Takeover Week at Hill Dickinson Stadium. Check the club's site for future junior events.

What should I bring for kids at a match?

Warm layers, a waterproof, snacks for the journey and something to keep them occupied during the wait. Many parents bring ear defenders for younger children. Check the bag rules first: Everton only allows bags up to A4 size.
