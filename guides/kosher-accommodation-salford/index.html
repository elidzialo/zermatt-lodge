Kosher Accommodation in Salford: A Guide for Visitors

Staying in Salford for Shabbat, a simcha or a family visit? What to ask a host, how to find kosher food, which hechsher is which and who to contact for shul details.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

For a kosher or Shabbat stay in Salford, contact the local community or the Manchester Beth Din (MBD) directly for shul, mikvah and eruv details, then book a home within walking distance of the shul you'll use. Find kosher food on the official MBD licensee list, and ask your host about the kitchen, locks and lights before you book.

Kosher food in Salford

The Manchester Beth Din publishes an official licensee list of grocers, bakeries, butchers, restaurants and caterers. Use it as your main reference, as businesses open, close and change supervision. See our kosher food guide for Manchester visitors.

Which hechsher?

Three authorities certify locally: the Manchester Beth Din, Machzikei Hadass Manchester and Badatz Igud Rabbonim (KIR). If you're eating with family, ask which one they keep before you shop.

What to ask a host before you book

A normal holiday home won't be set up for Shabbat. Ask:

  1. How many minutes' walk is it to my shul? Get the walk time, not the drive time.
  2. Is there a kosher kitchen, or can I bring my own pots, or use disposables?
  3. Are there motion-sensor lights, a keypad lock or a doorbell camera that switches on automatically?
  4. Is there an urn or hot plate, and can I set timers on the lights?
  5. How many beds and bathrooms are there, really? Simcha weekends need a lot of both.

Things to see

The Manchester Jewish Museum tells the story of Jewish life in Manchester, in a former synagogue completed in 1874. It reopened in 2021 after a redevelopment. Book and check opening times via its official website.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there an eruv in Salford?

Contact the local community or the Manchester Beth Din directly for current eruv details and status. Ask your host too, and check before every Shabbat.

Where can I buy kosher food in Salford?

Use the Manchester Beth Din's official licensee list, which covers grocers, bakeries and more. Lists change, so check it before you shop.

Which kashrut authorities work in Salford?

The Manchester Beth Din, Machzikei Hadass Manchester and Badatz Igud Rabbonim (KIR) all certify in Greater Manchester. If you're eating with family, ask which hechsher they keep.
