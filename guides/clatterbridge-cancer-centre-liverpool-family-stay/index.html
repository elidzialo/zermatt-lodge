Family Stays for Clatterbridge Cancer Centre Liverpool

Here for Clatterbridge Cancer Centre Liverpool treatment? Free Paddington Village parking, the shuttle bus and a 4-bed family house in north Liverpool.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

During treatment at Clatterbridge Cancer Centre Liverpool, a base with free parking and a kitchen helps families rest between appointments. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in north Liverpool, about 15 minutes by train from the city centre, sleeping up to 7.

If someone you love is starting a course of treatment at Clatterbridge Cancer Centre in Liverpool, the most practical base is one where you can park for free, cook proper meals and rest between appointments. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in north Liverpool, about 15 minutes by train from Liverpool city centre, with free parking for two cars and room for up to 7 people.

This guide covers where the hospital is, how its free car park and shuttle work, and how to organise a family stay that might last several weeks.

Where Clatterbridge Cancer Centre Liverpool is

The hospital is at 65 Pembroke Place, Liverpool, L7 8YA, in the Knowledge Quarter on the eastern edge of the city centre. The main switchboard is 0151 556 5000.

Many families still think of Clatterbridge as the Wirral hospital. The Liverpool building is a separate, newer site, so check your appointment letter for which one you need.

Parking: how the free car park works

This is the single most useful thing to know. According to the centre's Where to Find Us page:

  • Paddington Village Car Park is free for patients and visitors. The address is Elm Grove, Smithdown Lane, L7 3FA, and it's open 24 hours a day.
  • Levels 9 to 12 are reserved for the cancer centre, so head up there if the lower floors are full.
  • To get the free rate, give your registration at a hospital reception desk, or take your ticket to Main Reception (Level M2), Outpatients Reception (Level M1) or Chemotherapy Reception (Level 6).
  • Some sat navs get the car park postcode wrong. The hospital suggests searching for "Paddington Village Car Park" instead.
  • There are Blue Badge bays and EV charging points in the car park, but no Blue Badge bays directly outside the hospital.

The nearby Mount Pleasant car park is not part of the scheme. The hospital confirmed in 2022 that patients there pay the normal public rate.

The free shuttle bus

The hospital runs a free, wheelchair-accessible shuttle every 15 minutes, Monday to Friday, during the day. It runs between Smithdown Lane (outside the car park) and the hospital stop on Pembroke Place.

  • Car park to hospital: from 7:15am, then every 15 minutes, last departure 6:45pm
  • Hospital to car park: from 7:25am from the stop outside the Dental Hospital, last departure 6:55pm

If you'd rather walk, the hospital says it's about 0.4 miles (0.6km).

Drop-off at the front door

There's a small pick-up and drop-off area at the main entrance on Pembroke Place. Cars can pull in, but they can't wait there. A common routine is for one person to drop the patient at the door, then park and catch the shuttle.

Getting there from north Liverpool

By car

From the north of the city, you'll drive in towards the Knowledge Quarter. Allow extra time at rush hour, and check a live route the evening before your first appointment. Because the car park is free and open 24 hours, driving is often the simplest choice for daily treatment.

By train

The hospital lists Liverpool Lime Street and Liverpool Central as its nearest stations. From Fazakerley station, on Merseyrail's Northern line, direct trains reach Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes. That's handy for relatives who don't want to drive, or for days when the patient is too tired for a car journey and someone else is attending a meeting with the team. See our guide to Fazakerley to Liverpool city centre by train.

Planning a stay around a treatment course

Radiotherapy and chemotherapy are often given over several weeks, with appointments on most weekdays. A hotel room works for a night or two. Over a month, families tend to need more.

Things to sort out in week one

  1. Get the parking routine right. Know which reception validates your ticket and keep a note of your registration.
  2. Ask about help with travel costs. The NHS Healthcare Travel Costs Scheme can refund travel for patients on certain benefits or low incomes. NHS guidance says visitors aren't covered, but an escort can be if a healthcare professional confirms they're needed.
  3. Save the key numbers. The switchboard, your treatment team and the ward if the patient is admitted.
  4. Plan food. Treatment can change appetite and taste. A full kitchen lets you cook what the patient can actually face.
  5. Agree a rota. If several relatives are helping, decide who drives, who stays home and who handles calls.

Why space matters

  • Rest. Patients often need to sleep in the afternoon. Separate bedrooms let them rest while others carry on.
  • Infection worries. Some patients want to avoid crowded places. A private house is quieter than a busy hotel lobby.
  • Children and teenagers. A garden, games and a TV help younger family members cope with a long stay away from home.
  • Laundry and routine. Living in a house feels closer to normal life.

For more ideas, see our tips for long hospital stays in Liverpool.

Honest comparison: city centre vs north Liverpool

If the patient is attending alone, without a car, a city-centre hotel within walking distance of Pembroke Place is hard to beat. A house in north Liverpool makes more sense when:

  • Several family members are travelling together
  • You're driving and can use the free car park
  • The stay is long enough that cooking and laundry matter
  • Some of the family also need to visit Aintree University Hospital or The Walton Centre, which are on the north side of the city

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is Clatterbridge Cancer Centre Liverpool?

It's at 65 Pembroke Place, Liverpool, L7 8YA, in the city's Knowledge Quarter next to the Royal Liverpool University Hospital. The main phone number is 0151 556 5000.

Is parking free at Clatterbridge Cancer Centre Liverpool?

The centre's Where to Find Us page says Paddington Village Car Park is free for patients and visitors, as long as you validate your ticket or give your registration at a hospital reception desk. Levels 9 to 12 are reserved for the cancer centre.

Is there a shuttle bus from the car park to the hospital?

Yes. The centre runs a free, wheelchair-accessible shuttle every 15 minutes, Monday to Friday in the daytime, between Smithdown Lane outside the car park and the hospital stop on Pembroke Place. The walk is about 0.4 miles.

Can I stay in north Liverpool and still get to Clatterbridge easily?

Yes, if you plan for it. You can drive in and use the free car park, or take the Merseyrail train from Fazakerley to Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes. The hospital names Lime Street and Liverpool Central as its nearest stations.

Does Clatterbridge have accommodation for families?

The centre's travel page doesn't list visitor accommodation. Call 0151 556 5000 or ask your treatment team what's available before you book elsewhere.
