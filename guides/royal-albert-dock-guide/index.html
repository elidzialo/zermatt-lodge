Royal Albert Dock Guide: What's There and How to Get There

Visiting the Royal Albert Dock in Liverpool this winter? What's open, what's closed until 2027 and 2028, The Beatles Story, Tate Liverpool, history and where to park.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

The Royal Albert Dock is Liverpool's restored 1846 dock, the largest group of Grade I listed buildings in the UK. This winter The Beatles Story and the dock's shops, bars and restaurants are open, but the Merseyside Maritime Museum and International Slavery Museum are closed until 2028, and Tate Liverpool is showing at RIBA North on Mann Island until it returns to the dock in 2027. There's no dedicated parking at The Beatles Story; use the car parks nearby or Liverpool ONE.

The Royal Albert Dock is one of the first places most visitors head to in Liverpool: red-brick warehouses around the water, the Mersey on one side and the Three Graces just along the river. It's free to walk around and easy to combine with the Pier Head, Liverpool ONE and the city centre.

This winter is a little different, because two of the dock's big museums are closed for redevelopment. Here's what's open and what isn't, checked on 24 November 2026.

A quick history

  • Designed by Jesse Hartley and Philip Hardwick
  • Opened in 1846 by Prince Albert
  • Five warehouses that make up the largest group of Grade I listed buildings in the UK
  • Closed in 1972 as Liverpool's docks declined
  • Restored in the 1980s after the Merseyside Development Corporation was set up in 1981
  • Became the "Royal" Albert Dock in 2018, when it was given a royal charter

Tate lists RIBA North's hours as 10am to 5.50pm daily. Our museums guide has more on what's open across the city, including the free Museum of Liverpool at the Pier Head.

Things to do at the dock

  • The Beatles Story: the dock's biggest attraction. Allow about 1.5 hours
  • Walk the waterfront: from the Albert Dock along to the Pier Head and the Three Graces. The Royal Liver Building was finished in 1911, with a Liver Bird on each clock tower
  • Museum of Liverpool: free, at the Pier Head, opened in 2011
  • Mersey Ferry: the River Explorer Cruise leaves from the Pier Head. See our Mersey Ferry guide
  • Eat and drink around the dock, or walk into Liverpool ONE for more choice

More free ideas are in our free things to do in Liverpool guide.

Parking and getting there

  • Car: Q-Park lists Liverpool ONE (about a 5-minute walk), Hanover Street and the John Lewis car park as nearby options. The Beatles Story also mentions the car park opposite the dock entrance and the arena car park. City-centre parking adds up over a full day, so check prices before you go
  • Coach: short-stay drop-off on Salthouse Quay and Gower Street
  • Train: the nearest Merseyrail stations are in the city centre, with a walk down to the waterfront
  • Postcode for the dock: L3 4AA

Winter tips

  • Wrap up. It's on the river and can be very windy
  • Christmas and New Year: The Beatles Story is closed on 25 and 26 December. Check opening times for restaurants and shops over the holidays
  • Rainy day? The Beatles Story is indoors, and the shops and restaurants are under cover, so the dock works well in bad weather

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is there to do at the Royal Albert Dock?

The Beatles Story, shops, cafes, bars and restaurants, waterside walks and the views across to the Pier Head. Tate Liverpool returns to the dock in 2027 and the Maritime Museum in 2028.

Is the Merseyside Maritime Museum open?

No. National Museums Liverpool closed the Maritime Museum and the International Slavery Museum for repairs ahead of a redevelopment. Both are due to reopen in 2028.

Where is Tate Liverpool now?

Tate Liverpool is showing at RIBA North on Mann Island, a short walk from the dock, while its Albert Dock gallery is redeveloped. Tate says it will reopen at the Royal Albert Dock in 2027.

Where do I park for the Albert Dock?

Q-Park names Liverpool ONE (about a 5-minute walk), Hanover Street and the John Lewis car park as nearby options. The Beatles Story also points to the car park opposite the dock entrance.

Why is it called the Royal Albert Dock?

Prince Albert opened the dock in 1846. It was given a royal charter and the 'Royal' name in 2018.
