Liverpool John Lennon Airport to Fazakerley: How to Get There

How to get from Liverpool John Lennon Airport to Fazakerley: taxi, car hire, the 500 bus plus Merseyrail, or the 86A via South Parkway. Times and tips.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

The quickest way from Liverpool John Lennon Airport to Fazakerley is by taxi or hire car, around 20 to 40 minutes depending on traffic. By public transport, take the 500 bus to the city centre, then a Merseyrail Northern line train from Liverpool Central, roughly an hour and a quarter in total.

The quickest way from Liverpool John Lennon Airport to Fazakerley is by taxi or hire car. Expect around 20 to 40 minutes depending on traffic. By public transport, take the 500 bus to the city centre, walk to Liverpool Central, and catch a Merseyrail Northern line train to Fazakerley (about 15 minutes). Allow roughly an hour and a quarter in total. Here are all the options in detail.

Option 1: Taxi or pre-booked transfer (fastest)

The airport is in Speke, south Liverpool, and Fazakerley is in the north. A taxi goes across or around the city, often using the motorways on the eastern side (the M62 and M57), or through the suburbs.

  • Journey time: the Jockey Club describes the airport as about a 20-minute drive from Aintree Racecourse, which is next door to Fazakerley. Allow 25 to 40 minutes at busy times.
  • Best for: families with children, groups with luggage, late arrivals and anyone visiting a patient at Aintree Hospital who needs to get there quickly.
  • Tip: for 5 to 7 people with suitcases, pre-book a larger vehicle or minibus. A standard car won't fit everyone.

See our guide to taxis in Liverpool for how hackney and private hire cabs work.

Option 2: Hire car

If you're planning days out to Knowsley Safari, Formby or North Wales, a hire car from the airport makes sense. You can then park free at Zermatt Lodge (there's space for two cars) and take the train into the city centre when you want to.

Route outline: follow signs from the airport towards the M62, join the M57 north and leave at the Kirkby/Fazakerley junction. Your sat nav will give you the best route for the time of day.

Option 3: 500 bus plus Merseyrail (simplest public transport)

  1. Bus 500 from the airport to the city centre. The airport says the 500 runs every 30 minutes, 4am to midnight, and takes about 30 minutes to the city centre. It stops at Liverpool ONE Bus Station and Great Charlotte Street (for Lime Street station).
  2. Walk to Liverpool Central station. It's a short walk from either stop. Ask the driver or follow signs for Liverpool Central.
  3. Merseyrail Northern line to Fazakerley. Catch a train towards Kirkby or Headbolt Lane. Direct trains to Fazakerley take about 15 minutes.

Total: roughly 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes, depending on connections.

Best for: solo travellers and couples with light luggage.

Option 4: 86A or 80A bus plus Merseyrail via South Parkway

  1. Bus 86A or 80A to Liverpool South Parkway. The airport lists the 86A at about every 15 minutes on weekday daytimes and the 80A at about every 20 minutes. Both continue to Liverpool ONE.
  2. Merseyrail Northern line from South Parkway towards the city centre.
  3. Change in the city centre (Liverpool Central is easiest) onto a train for Kirkby or Headbolt Lane, and get off at Fazakerley.

This route involves an extra change, so it's handy mainly if you're already heading to South Parkway or the 500 isn't due. See our Liverpool South Parkway guide.

Travelling with children or lots of luggage

  • Pre-book a taxi or minibus with child seats if you need them.
  • Avoid rush-hour trains into Liverpool Central if you've got big cases.
  • Use the lifts at stations where available, and check step-free access on Merseyrail's website.
  • Pack a snack bag, as the bus and train route can take over an hour with waits.

Arriving late?

  • The 500 runs until about midnight, according to the airport.
  • Merseyrail has published late-evening departures from Liverpool Central towards Headbolt Lane (via Fazakerley) until just before midnight, but evening trains are less frequent and timetables change. Check the last train for your date in the Merseyrail app.
  • If you land late, a pre-booked taxi is usually the safest choice.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to drive from Liverpool Airport to Fazakerley?

The airport is in south Liverpool and Fazakerley is in the north. The Jockey Club puts the airport about a 20-minute drive from Aintree Racecourse, next door to Fazakerley. Allow longer at rush hour, typically 25 to 40 minutes.

Is there a direct bus from Liverpool Airport to Fazakerley?

Not one we'd recommend. The easiest public transport route is the 500 bus to Liverpool ONE Bus Station, then a short walk to Liverpool Central for a Merseyrail Northern line train to Fazakerley.

How often does the 500 airport bus run?

Liverpool John Lennon Airport says the 500 runs every 30 minutes from 4am to midnight, taking about 30 minutes to the city centre. It serves Liverpool ONE Bus Station and Great Charlotte Street for Lime Street.

Can I take the train from Liverpool Airport?

There's no station at the terminal. Take the 86A or 80A bus to Liverpool South Parkway, then a Merseyrail Northern line train towards the city centre, changing onto a Kirkby or Headbolt Lane train for Fazakerley.

Is a taxi worth it for a group?

Often, yes. For 4 to 7 people with luggage, a pre-booked taxi or minibus can cost about the same as several bus and train fares and takes you door to door.
