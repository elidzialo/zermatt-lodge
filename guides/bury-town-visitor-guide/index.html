Bury: A Practical Visitor Guide

A day in Bury, Greater Manchester: Bury Market opening days, the East Lancashire Railway from Bolton Street, free museums, the tram from Manchester and homes to stay in nearby Prestwich.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Bury is at the northern end of the Metrolink Bury line, which Visit Manchester says is 34 minutes from Manchester city centre. Bury Market dates back to 1444: the outdoor market runs Wednesday to Saturday, and the Fish and Meat Hall Monday to Saturday (closing at 12pm on Tuesdays). The East Lancashire Railway runs heritage trains from Bury Bolton Street, and Bury Art Museum is free. Prestwich, about 4 miles south on the same tram line, is a handy base.

Bury is a proper market town at the top of the tram line. You can do the market, a steam train and a museum in one day, and be back in Prestwich or Manchester for dinner. We checked these facts on 24 November 2026. Opening times can change, especially over Christmas, so check before you go.

Getting there

  • Tram: the Metrolink Bury line ends at Bury. Visit Manchester says Bury is 34 minutes from Manchester city centre on the line from Victoria.
  • From Prestwich: Prestwich is about 4 miles south on the same line, so it's a short ride.
  • Plan it: use the Bee Network app for trams and buses.

Bury Market

  • Visit Manchester says the market was established in 1444.
  • Outdoor Market: Wednesday to Friday, 9am to 4.30pm; Saturday, 9am to 5pm.
  • Fish and Meat Hall: Monday to Saturday, 9am to 4.30pm, closing at 12pm on Tuesdays.

East Lancashire Railway

  • Heritage trains run from Bury Bolton Street station (BL9 0EY), in the town centre.
  • Stations on the line: Heywood, Bury Bolton Street, Burrs Country Park, Summerseat, Ramsbottom, Irwell Vale and Rawtenstall.
  • Parking at the station is limited and charged; it's a small council pay-and-display car park. The railway suggests coming by Metrolink.
  • Bury Transport Museum, with vintage buses, trams and coaches, opens 10am to 4pm on days the railway runs public services (not dining trains).
  • In December, the railway runs Santa Specials. See our Santa Specials 2026 guide.

Museums and venues

  • Bury Art Museum and Sculpture Centre: free, open weekdays 10am to 5pm and Saturday 10am to 4.30pm; closed Sunday and Monday.
  • The Fusilier Museum: 300 years of military history of the Lancashire Fusiliers. Open weekdays 10am to 5pm and Saturday 10am to 4pm; closed Sunday.
  • The Met: an arts venue in the Victorian Derby Hall, with theatre, comedy, folk and live music.
  • Victoria Wood statue: a life-size bronze in Library Gardens.

Further out

  • Ramsbottom is about 4 miles north, with Peel Tower and Nuttall Park, and is also on the steam railway.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is Bury Market open?

Visit Manchester lists the outdoor market as Wednesday to Friday 9am to 4.30pm and Saturday 9am to 5pm. The Fish and Meat Hall is open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 4.30pm, closing at 12pm on Tuesdays.

How do I get to Bury from Manchester?

Take the Metrolink Bury line from Manchester Victoria. Visit Manchester puts Bury 34 minutes from the city centre.

Where does the East Lancashire Railway start?

Bury Bolton Street station, BL9 0EY, in the town centre. The line's stations are Heywood, Bury Bolton Street, Burrs Country Park, Summerseat, Ramsbottom, Irwell Vale and Rawtenstall.

Is Bury Art Museum free?

Yes. Visit Manchester lists Bury Art Museum and Sculpture Centre as free, open weekdays 10am to 5pm and Saturday 10am to 4.30pm.
