Boxing Day in Liverpool 2026: How to Plan the Day

Boxing Day 2026 in Liverpool: Aintree racing from 10.30am, Everton v Sunderland, skating from 12pm, transport warnings and a family house to come back to.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Boxing Day 2026 is a Saturday. Aintree has Boxing Day racing (gates 10.30am, first race 11.55am, general admission from £39, under-18s free), Everton host Sunderland at Hill Dickinson Stadium, and Skate St George's opens from 12pm to 10pm. Liverpool are away at Hull. Public transport runs a reduced service, so plan travel carefully.

Boxing Day 2026 falls on a Saturday, and Liverpool has a busy one. Aintree has Boxing Day racing, with gates at 10.30am and the first race at 11.55am. Everton host Sunderland at Hill Dickinson Stadium. Skate St George's opens from 12pm to 10pm. Liverpool FC are away at Hull. The catch is transport: festive services are reduced, so a base close to where you're going makes a big difference.

Here's how to plan the day, checked on 19 November 2026.

Boxing Day racing at Aintree

Aintree's Boxing Day meeting is a jump racing card a short drive from north Liverpool.

  • Date: Saturday 26 December 2026
  • Gates: 10.30am
  • First race: 11.55am, the William Hill Mares' Handicap Hurdle
  • Last race: 3.20pm, the William Hill Open National Hunt Flat Race
  • General admission: from £39 (cheaper if you book online in advance)
  • Hospitality: from £150
  • Under-18s: free in the Grandstand and Paddock

Times and races are subject to change. The Jockey Club gives the address as Ormskirk Road, Aintree, L9 5AS, on the A59, about a mile from the M57 and M58. Follow the A59 and the yellow tourism signs to the car parks.

Getting to Aintree on Boxing Day

On normal days, Merseyrail trains run to Aintree station, a short walk from the course. On Boxing Day services may be reduced, and the 2026 timetable wasn't out when we checked. Our guide to getting to Aintree covers the options. From north Liverpool, Aintree is close by car or taxi, which helps if trains are thin.

Racing finishes mid-afternoon, so you're back before dark for a family evening, or in time to watch Liverpool's 5.30pm game at Hull on TV.

Football: Everton v Sunderland

  • Venue: Hill Dickinson Stadium, Bramley-Moore Dock
  • Kick-off: most sources list 3pm. The Premier League's fixture page appeared to show an earlier 12.30pm slot, so confirm with Everton
  • Getting there: Sandhills is the usual station. From Fazakerley it's a few stops on the same line, if trains are running

Liverpool are away at Hull City at 5.30pm (Sky Sports). There's no game at Anfield on Boxing Day 2026. For every festive fixture, see our Liverpool and Everton Christmas fixtures guide.

Clash warning: racing at Aintree and football at Hill Dickinson Stadium on the same day means busy roads in north Liverpool around midday. Leave early.

Family options in the city

Skating

  • Skate St George's: 12pm to 10pm on Boxing Day
  • Icebox at Boxpark: open daily to 10 January 2027 except Christmas Day, 45-minute sessions from £8 for children and £10 for adults

A panto

Cinderella at the Auditorium, M&S Bank Arena runs to 27 December. Check Ticket Quarter for Boxing Day performances. Our Liverpool panto 2026 guide lists other shows.

The sales

Many shops open on Boxing Day for the sales, but opening times vary by store. Check before you go.

A Boxing Day walk

If you'd rather blow the cobwebs away, the coast north of Liverpool is close. Formby, run by the National Trust, has pine woods (home to red squirrels) and wide beaches. Its car parks cost £9 (free for members and Blue Badge holders), and the page we checked showed winter car park hours of 9am to 4pm. It doesn't mention Christmas closures, so check before you set off. See our winter walks near Liverpool guide for more.

Transport on Boxing Day

This is the part to plan most carefully.

  • Trains: the 2026 festive timetable hadn't been published when we checked. In past years Merseyrail has run some services on Boxing Day
  • Buses: in past years the Combined Authority has warned of "very limited" bus services on Boxing Day
  • Taxis: expect higher demand and pre-book where you can
  • Driving: possible for Aintree, with car parks off the A59. For the football, avoid driving near the stadium

Check merseyrail.org and the Liverpool City Region Combined Authority website in mid-December.

A sample Boxing Day plan

  1. Morning: leftovers breakfast, then a short drive or taxi to Aintree for gates at 10.30am
  2. 11.55am to 3.20pm: racing (under-18s free in the Grandstand and Paddock)
  3. Late afternoon: back to base, feet up, Liverpool on TV at 5.30pm
  4. Evening: board games, pool and more leftovers

Or for football fans: Everton at Hill Dickinson Stadium, then the Liverpool game on TV in the evening.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there racing at Aintree on Boxing Day 2026?

Yes. William Hill Boxing Day Racing is on Saturday 26 December 2026. The Jockey Club lists gates at 10.30am, the first race at 11.55am and the last at 3.20pm, subject to change.

How much are Aintree Boxing Day tickets?

General admission starts from £39 and hospitality from £150, with savings for booking online in advance. Grandstand and Paddock tickets are free for under-18s.

Is there football in Liverpool on Boxing Day 2026?

Everton host Sunderland at Hill Dickinson Stadium. Most sources list a 3pm kick-off, but check the club website. Liverpool are away at Hull City at 5.30pm.

Do trains run in Liverpool on Boxing Day?

The 2026 festive timetable hadn't been published when we checked. In past years, Merseyrail has run some services on Boxing Day and buses have been very limited. Check merseyrail.org nearer the time.

Is Skate St George's open on Boxing Day?

Yes, from 12pm to 10pm. It's closed on Christmas Day.
