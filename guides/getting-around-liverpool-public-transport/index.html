Getting Around Liverpool by Public Transport: A Visitor's Guide

A plain guide to getting around Liverpool by train, bus, ferry and taxi: Merseyrail Day Saver prices, Tap and Go contactless, peak-time rules, buses under public control and tips for groups.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Merseyrail is the easiest way around Liverpool. Since February 2026 you can tap in and out with a contactless bank card, phone or watch. A Merseyrail Day Saver costs £5.95 for an adult across all areas (£4.45 for one area) and £2.95 for 5 to 18 year olds, but it isn't valid between 06.31 and 09.29 on weekdays. Buses are moving under public control, starting in St Helens in 2026.

Liverpool is a compact city, and between Merseyrail trains, buses, the Mersey Ferry and taxis you can get almost anywhere without a car. This guide covers the basics for visitors, especially families and groups staying a few nights.

We checked these prices and rules on 25 November 2026. Fares can change, so check Merseyrail and Merseytravel before you travel.

Merseyrail: the quickest option

Merseyrail runs the local trains. In the city centre, the key stations are Liverpool Central, Lime Street, Moorfields and James Street. Lines run out to Southport, Ormskirk, Kirkby, Hunts Cross, the Wirral, Chester and Ellesmere Port.

Tap and Go contactless

Since February 2026, Merseyrail's Tap and Go works with contactless bank cards, watches and smartphones, as well as MetroCards.

  • Tap in at the gates or platform reader at the start of every journey
  • Tap out at the end
  • Use the same card or device both times
  • Ticket offices are still open for paper tickets

Day Saver prices

Ticket 1 area All areas
Adult (19+) £4.45 £5.95
Child or young person (5 to 18) £2.20 £2.95

Day Savers are not valid between 06.31 and 09.29, Monday to Friday. Outside those times, and all day at weekends and on bank holidays, they give unlimited travel. Merseyrail also says 15 to 18 year olds can travel at half the adult price.

Tip for families: for a day of sightseeing that starts after 9.30am, a Day Saver each usually keeps things simple.

Buses

Buses in the Liverpool City Region are moving into public control. ITV reported in September 2026 that the change starts in St Helens, then the Wirral, and reaches the rest of the city region in 2027. New yellow buses are being introduced.

The region's £2 bus fare cap was last extended to the end of 2025 in the announcements we found, so check Merseytravel for today's fares.

Ferries

The Mersey Ferry is a great way to see the waterfront and cross to the Wirral. See our Mersey Ferry cruise guide and New Brighton day trip.

Taxis and late nights

  • Black cabs wait at ranks at Lime Street and in the city centre.
  • App-based cabs are widely used.
  • After a big match or concert, expect queues. Walking to a station or a quieter street often helps.

Driving and parking

If you're coming by car, it's often easier to leave it at your accommodation and take the train into town. Our Liverpool weekend by car guide and match day parking guide cover this in more detail.

Day trips by train

  • Chester: Merseyrail says about 45 minutes, last train back at 23:00. See our Chester day trip guide.
  • Southport: Merseyrail says about 45 minutes, with trains every 15 minutes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I use contactless on Merseyrail?

Yes. Since February 2026 Merseyrail's Tap and Go works with contactless bank cards, watches and smartphones as well as MetroCards. Tap in at the start and tap out at the end of every journey. Ticket offices still sell paper tickets.

How much is a Merseyrail Day Saver?

Adults (19+) pay £5.95 for all areas or £4.45 for one area. Children and young people (5 to 18) pay £2.95 for all areas or £2.20 for one area.

When can I use a Day Saver?

Any time except between 06.31 and 09.29 Monday to Friday. Weekends and bank holidays are unlimited all day.

Are Liverpool bus fares still £2?

The Liverpool City Region £2 bus fare cap was last extended to the end of 2025 in the announcements we found. Check Merseytravel for the current fare before you travel.

Is there a station in the Baltic Triangle?

Not yet. Liverpool Central is the closest station. Visit Liverpool says a Liverpool Baltic station is planned to open in 2027.
