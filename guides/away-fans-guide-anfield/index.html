Away Fans Guide to Anfield

Following your team to Anfield? Where away fans sit, entrances, bag rules, trains from Lime Street, parking, and staying over in Liverpool after the match.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Away fans at Anfield sit in the lower tier of the Anfield Road End, with an allocation of around 3,000 for league games and Premier League tickets capped at £30. The ground is in north Liverpool, away from the city centre, with no station on the doorstep, so most travelling fans come by train to Sandhills or Kirkdale, by club coach, or by taxi. This guide covers getting in, getting there and back, and the option of staying over in Liverpool rather than facing a long drive home after the final whistle.

Where away fans sit

According to Football Ground Guide:

  • Away fans are in the lower tier of the Anfield Road End, on the side next to the Main Stand
  • Liverpool supporters are alongside and in the tier above, in segregated sections
  • The standard allocation is around 3,000
  • The rebuilt Anfield Road stand was fully completed in March 2025 and now holds about 15,700

The renovation dealt with the old complaints about the overhanging upper tier, though the back rows still feel some way from the pitch.

Facilities

The away concourse has food and drink kiosks, open before the match and at half-time. Football Ground Guide says there are up to 24 pitchside wheelchair spaces in the away section, with access via Gate DA6 on Anfield Road.

Getting in

Information Leeds United published for its supporters travelling to Anfield says:

  • Away fans enter via entrances Q and R
  • Approach Anfield Road from the 97 Avenue direction. There's no access from Arkles Lane
  • Coach parking and Stanley car park both lead along Dahlia Walk to 97 Avenue
  • After the game, turn left

Your own club will publish the latest arrangements for your fixture, so check them before you travel.

Bags and banned items

  • Bags: small handheld bags no bigger than A5, plus clear LFC Superstore bags with purchases. All bags are searched
  • Medical bags: if you need a larger bag for medical reasons, email Liverpool's Disability Team at least 48 hours before
  • Not allowed: laptops and tablets, buggies and prams, video recording equipment and large cameras. Flares, pyrotechnics and smoke bombs are strictly forbidden
  • Flags: small flags are allowed. Larger flags only in the away section and must be checked first

Behaviour

Stay in your own seat and don't stand on seats or block aisles. Discriminatory language, tragedy chanting and offensive gestures can lead to a football banning order.

Getting to Anfield

By train

From Liverpool Lime Street, walk or change to Liverpool Central, then take a Northern line train:

  • Sandhills: the Soccerbus runs from here to the ground on match days, starting a couple of hours before kick-off and running back after the match. Check Merseytravel for the exact times for your game
  • Kirkdale: just under a mile's walk to the ground

By bus

The 917 runs from the city centre to Anfield on match days.

By car

There's no paid parking at Anfield on match days, and the surrounding streets are residents-only. Football Ground Guide says the Stanley Park car park is now for permit holders only. Pre-book a secure car park before you set off. See our Liverpool match day parking guide.

By club coach

Many away fans travel on official club coaches, which use dedicated coach parking. It's often the least stressful option for a long trip.

Before the match

Football Ground Guide lists pubs near the ground that have welcomed away fans, but many visiting supporters prefer to eat and drink in the city centre, around Bold Street, Concert Square or Mathew Street, before heading up to Anfield. Arrangements can change for high-profile games, so follow your club's and the police's advice on the day.

Tickets

Only buy away tickets through your own club. Under section 166 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994, it's an offence to sell a ticket for a designated football match without the organiser's authority.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where do away fans sit at Anfield?

Football Ground Guide says away fans are in the lower tier of the Anfield Road End, on the side next to the Main Stand, with Liverpool fans alongside and above in segregated sections. The usual league allocation is around 3,000.

How much are away tickets at Anfield?

Premier League away tickets are capped at £30. Cup prices are set by the home club. Buy through your own club's ticket office.

What's the best station for Anfield?

There's no station right by the ground. Sandhills has the Soccerbus shuttle on match days, and Kirkdale is just under a mile's walk. From Lime Street, go via Liverpool Central for both.

What size bag can I take into Anfield?

Information published for visiting supporters says only small bags no bigger than A5 are allowed, plus clear LFC Superstore bags with purchases. All bags are searched.

Can I park at Anfield as an away fan?

Street parking is very difficult because of residents' zones, and there's no paid parking at Anfield on match days. Pre-book a car park, use your club's coach, or come by train.
