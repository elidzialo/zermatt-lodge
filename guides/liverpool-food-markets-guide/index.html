Liverpool Food Markets: Where to Go This Winter

Where to eat and shop at Liverpool's food markets in winter 2026: Baltic Market, Duke Street Market, BOXPARK and the council's markets, including Lark Lane and Woolton dates in November and December.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

For street food, Liverpool's best-known halls are Baltic Market and BOXPARK in the Baltic Triangle, and Duke Street Market near the Ropewalks. For daytime markets, the council lists the Cunard Building Market on Wednesdays (10am to 4pm), Basnett Street Artisan Market on Saturdays (10am to 6pm), Lark Lane Market on 28 November and 19 December 2026, and Woolton Village Market on 12 December 2026.

Food markets are one of the easiest ways to feed a group in Liverpool. Everyone picks their own stall, you share long tables, and nobody has to agree on one menu. In winter there are also plenty of daytime markets for local produce and gifts.

We checked the dates below on 25 November 2026. Hours change often over Christmas, so check before you go.

Tips for groups

  • Go early in the evening at weekends. Halls fill up later on.
  • Some venues become 18+ at night. If you have children or teenagers with you, check first.
  • Card payment is normal, but a little cash helps at smaller farmers' markets.
  • Combine a market with a walk. Lark Lane is next to Sefton Park. See our Sefton Park and Palm House guide.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the best street food market in Liverpool for a group?

Baltic Market, BOXPARK and Duke Street Market all have lots of different traders under one roof, which suits groups who want different things. Duke Street is the closest to the main shopping streets.

When is Lark Lane Market?

Liverpool City Council lists Saturday 28 November and Saturday 19 December 2026, 9am to 2pm.

When is Woolton Village Market?

The council lists Saturday 12 December 2026, 9am to 2pm.

Is there a market at the Cunard Building?

Yes. The council lists the Cunard Building Market on Wednesdays, 10am to 4pm. Check the council page for dates.

Are food market opening times the same every week?

Not always. Street food halls change hours over Christmas and for events, so check each venue's website or social media before you go.
