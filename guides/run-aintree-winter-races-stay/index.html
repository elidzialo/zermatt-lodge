Run Aintree This Winter: Race Day Tips and Where to Stay

Running the Run Aintree half marathon, 10k or 5k on 13 December 2026 or 3 January 2027? Start times, the course, parking and where to stay the night before.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

RunThrough holds Run Aintree half marathon, 10k and 5k races at Aintree Racecourse on Sunday 13 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027. For the January race, findarace lists start times of 10am (half), 10.45am (10k) and 10.55am (5k), and warns that free parking on site is limited. Staying nearby the night before saves an early drive. If you've entered a Run Aintree race this winter, the key facts are simple: two Sunday races, an early start, a flat lapped course and limited parking. Times can change, so always follow your race-day email.

Getting there

Parking

findarace says there is limited free parking on site and recommends arriving early. On a winter Sunday morning that means leaving in the dark if you're driving in from further away.

By train

Aintree station, on Merseyrail's Northern line (Ormskirk branch), is the closest station to the racecourse. Trains usually run every 15 minutes Monday to Saturday and every 30 minutes on Sundays, so check the Sunday timetable and allow a buffer before your start time.

From Fazakerley

Fazakerley, home of Zermatt Lodge, borders Aintree. Fazakerley station is about 0.8km in a straight line from the centre of the racecourse, though the walk to the entrance you need will be longer. See our guide to getting to Aintree Racecourse from Fazakerley.

Winter race tips

  • Dress for standing around. Bring an old layer or bin bag for the wait before the start.
  • Plan for wind and rain. Racecourses are open sites with little shelter.
  • Eat at home. A kitchen means you can have your usual breakfast at the usual time.
  • Get dry after. A hot shower and somewhere to dry kit makes the trip home far better.
  • Bring supporters? A lapped course is good for spectators, who see you several times.

Why a whole house suits a running group

If you're travelling as a club or a group of friends, a whole house lets you cook a pasta dinner on the Saturday, store bikes or kit, and stagger showers after the race. Split the cost between the group and compare it with several hotel rooms.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When are the next Run Aintree races?

RunThrough lists Run Aintree half marathon, 10k and 5k events on Sunday 13 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027, then Sunday 9 May 2027, Sunday 11 July 2027, Sunday 31 October 2027 and Sunday 19 December 2027.

What time does the Run Aintree half marathon start?

For 3 January 2027, findarace lists the half marathon at 10am, the 10k at 10.45am and the 5k at 10.55am, and asks runners to be on site before 10am. Check your race email for final times.

Is the Run Aintree course hilly?

No. findarace describes a flat, lapped course inside the racecourse: 1 lap for the 5k, 2 for the 10k and 4 for the half marathon.

Is there parking at Aintree for the race?

findarace says there is limited free parking on site and recommends arriving early. Staying close by, or coming by train to Aintree station, avoids the parking rush.
