Conference Accommodation for ACC Liverpool (Liverpool Experience Campus)

Attending a conference at ACC Liverpool, now Liverpool Experience Campus? Parking prices, trains to James Street and a 4-bed house for your team.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

For a team at ACC Liverpool (now branded Liverpool Experience Campus), one shared house can replace several waterfront hotel rooms. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Liverpool, an easy train ride from Liverpool city centre, sleeping up to 7 with free parking for two cars.

If your team is heading to a conference at ACC Liverpool, now branded Liverpool Experience Campus, you can either pay waterfront hotel prices for several rooms or share a house and commute in. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Liverpool, an easy train ride from Liverpool city centre, sleeping up to 7 with free parking for two cars. It's a practical option for teams of colleagues who'd rather have one base, one invoice and space to prepare in the evenings.

This guide is for delegates and speakers attending conferences. If your team is building a stand, see our separate exhibitor stay guide.

The venue: ACC Liverpool is now Liverpool Experience Campus

The complex on Kings Dock opened in 2008 as ACC Liverpool. In 2026, the operator rebranded as Liverpool Experience Campus (LEX):

  • The convention centre is now the Convention Centre at Liverpool Experience Campus
  • The exhibition halls are now the Exhibition Centre at Liverpool Experience Campus
  • M&S Bank Arena keeps its name

Many invitations, apps and taxi drivers still say "the ACC", so you'll hear both names. The campus includes an auditorium for 1,500, a multipurpose hall and 21 breakout rooms, with the Exhibition Centre linked by a covered bridge.

Arriving and checking in

The venue's joining instructions say:

  • The sat nav postcode is L3 4BX (the address is Kings Dock, Liverpool Waterfront)
  • There's a riverside drop-off point for taxis and coaches
  • Visitors to meetings report to the Guest Relations desk in the Atrium. The phone number given is 0151 239 6002

Getting there

By train

Trains from the house run into Liverpool Central. From there you can:

  • Walk to the waterfront, or
  • Use the Wirral line to James Street. The venue says James Street is about a 10-minute walk from the campus

Lime Street, for mainline arrivals, is about a 20-minute walk from the venue, according to the joining instructions. Merseyrail now takes contactless cards: tap in and tap out.

By car

The Kings Dock Car Park sits on the campus. The 2025 joining instructions list:

It has 1,450 spaces and is open 24 hours, with accessible bays and EV charging on every floor. On big event days it can fill, and the venue also points to Q-Park sites around Liverpool ONE. For a team of five over three conference days, parking two cars on site adds up quickly, so many teams drive to their accommodation and take the train in.

By bus

The venue says Liverpool ONE bus station is about a 5-minute walk and Queen Square bus station about 15 minutes.

Shared house vs hotel rooms for a conference team

When hotels make sense

  • You're a solo delegate or a pair
  • You want to walk back to your room between sessions
  • Your company has a negotiated hotel rate

When a house makes sense

  • You're a team of 4 to 7. One booking replaces several rooms
  • Big conference weeks push hotel prices up near the waterfront, and there's limited choice
  • You need to prepare together. A dining table for slides, rehearsals and post-event debriefs is more useful than a hotel bar
  • You want to cook. Conference food gets repetitive. A proper kitchen helps over a long event
  • Parking. Two free spaces at the house, against daily charges in town

Tips for conference teams

  1. Download the agenda early and plan who covers which sessions.
  2. Agree a train plan. Leaving the house together saves coordination.
  3. Pack layers. Waterfront wind is real, even if the halls are warm.
  4. Book evening tables ahead. Restaurants around the waterfront fill up during large conferences.
  5. Keep receipts for parking, trains and meals for expense claims.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is ACC Liverpool still called ACC Liverpool?

The operator has rebranded as Liverpool Experience Campus (LEX), with the full launch in April 2026. The venues are now the Convention Centre and the Exhibition Centre at Liverpool Experience Campus. M&S Bank Arena kept its name. Many people and event invites still say ACC Liverpool.

How much is parking at the conference centre?

The venue's 2025 joining instructions list the Kings Dock Car Park (1,450 spaces, open 24 hours) at £4 up to 1 hour, £9 up to 2 hours, £13 up to 5 hours, £17 up to 9 hours and £27 up to 24 hours. Check current prices before you go.

What's the nearest station to the conference centre?

The venue says James Street (Wirral line) is about a 10-minute walk, and Liverpool Lime Street is about 20 minutes on foot or a short taxi ride.

Is it cheaper for a team to share a house than book hotel rooms?

Often, for teams of 4 to 7 staying several nights, especially during big conferences when waterfront hotel prices rise. Compare the total cost, including parking, and remember a house means one invoice instead of several.
