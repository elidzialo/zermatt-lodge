Prestwich for Visitors: A Practical Area Guide

Visiting Prestwich in north Manchester? How the Metrolink Bury line works, Heaton Park, Prestwich Clough and Philips Park, driving tips and whole homes with free parking.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Prestwich is a town in the Borough of Bury, just north of Manchester city centre. Prestwich tram stop is on the Metrolink Bury line in fare zone 3, with trams mostly every 12 minutes and every 6 minutes between Bury and Manchester at peak times. Heaton Park, Prestwich Clough and Philips Park nature reserve are all close by. Book Homes has several whole houses in Prestwich, some with free parking, for families, groups and work stays.

Prestwich sits between Manchester and Bury, on the edge of Heaton Park. It is a good base if you want a quieter, residential street to sleep on but still need to get into the city quickly. We checked the facts below on 24 November 2026.

Getting around

  • Tram: Prestwich tram stop is on the Metrolink Bury line, in fare zone 3. Trams run mostly every 12 minutes, with a 6-minute service between Bury and Manchester at peak times. The stop first opened as a railway station in 1879 and became a Metrolink stop in 1992.
  • Into the city: the Bury line runs into Manchester Victoria, next to the AO Arena and the National Football Museum.
  • Bury: Visit Manchester puts Prestwich about 4 miles south of Bury. Stay on the tram northbound for Bury Market and the East Lancashire Railway.
  • Buses: buses from Prestwich run to Bury, Manchester and Salford. Plan journeys on the Bee Network app.

Parks and green space

  • Heaton Park is right next to Prestwich. See our Heaton Park visitor guide for opening times, tram stops and parking charges.
  • Prestwich Clough is a Bury Council wood of about 9.8 hectares. The Woodland Trust describes it as an ancient wooded valley and a Site of Biological Importance, running from St Mary's Park down to Drinkwater Park.
  • Philips Park is Bury's largest local nature reserve, and the first to be declared, in October 1999. It has mixed woodland, meadows, streams and ponds, Bury's first mountain bike trail and a Countryside Centre.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I get from Prestwich to Manchester city centre?

Take the Metrolink Bury line from Prestwich tram stop. It is in fare zone 3, and trams run mostly every 12 minutes, with a 6-minute service between Bury and Manchester at peak times.

What is there to do in Prestwich?

Heaton Park is next door, and Prestwich Clough is an ancient wooded valley owned by Bury Council. Philips Park is Bury's largest local nature reserve, with woodland, meadows, ponds and a mountain bike trail.

Is Prestwich far from Bury?

No. Visit Manchester puts Prestwich about 4 miles south of Bury, and both are on the same Metrolink line.
