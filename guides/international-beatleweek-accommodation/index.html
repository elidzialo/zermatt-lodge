International Beatleweek: How to Plan Your Stay

Planning a trip for International Beatleweek in Liverpool? When it runs, how it works, Cavern 70th anniversary plans for 2027 and where groups can stay.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

What International Beatleweek is

Beatleweek is organised every year by Cavern City Tours, the company that runs the Cavern Club. The festival brings tribute bands, special guests and fan events to Liverpool, with the Cavern at the centre and events spread across the city. In 2026 the festival won Event/Festival of the Year at the Liverpool City Region Tourism Awards, according to the Cavern Club.

What 2026 looked like

The 2026 programme gives a good idea of the format. Ticketed events listed on Skiddle included:

  • Get Back - The Return of the Fab Four, a tribute show at the Philharmonic Hall, with acts from Argentina, Brazil and the USA
  • The Annual Beatles Convention Day at the Adelphi Hotel, with live music, guest speakers, film screenings, a Beatles marketplace and "Ringo Bingo"
  • 1966 - A Year in Music at The Dome, marking the year Revolver came out

There was also an International Beatleweek Showcase at the Museum of Liverpool as part of its Summer of Sound series.

2027: the Cavern turns 70

2027 is a big year for Beatles fans. The Cavern Club turns 70 on 16 January 2027, and the Museum of Liverpool is running an exhibition, 70 Years Of The Cavern Club, from 16 January to 3 October 2027. It covers the club's beginnings as a jazz cellar on Mathew Street, the Beatles years, and its closures and reopening.

If you're planning a Beatleweek trip in August 2027, you'll be able to see the exhibition the same week. Expect extra interest in the city's Beatles sights all year.

When to book

Beatleweek falls over the late August bank holiday period, which is already busy in Liverpool. In 2026 the same weeks also had Creamfields at Daresbury and the Southport Air Show, according to the city region's events calendar. Our advice:

  1. Watch for the 2027 dates. Follow the Cavern Club and official Beatleweek channels.
  2. Book flexible accommodation early, ideally with a cancellation policy that lets you adjust once dates are confirmed.
  3. Buy event tickets as soon as they go on sale. Headline shows and the convention can sell out.
  4. Plan your nights out. Mathew Street is packed late in the evening that week.

Where to stay for Beatleweek

City centre

Staying near Mathew Street means you can walk everywhere. The downsides are price and noise. Hotels near the Cavern Quarter are in high demand that week, and late-night crowds make for broken sleep.

A short train ride out

A base in north Liverpool can work well, especially for groups:

  • Trains to Liverpool Central take about 15 minutes from Fazakerley on Merseyrail's Northern line, calling at Moorfields on the way
  • Moorfields is the handy stop for Mathew Street and the business district; Liverpool Central is closest to Bold Street and Liverpool ONE
  • You get quiet nights, parking and space to spread out

The catch is the last train. Trainline lists the last train from Liverpool Central towards Fazakerley in the late evening on its standard timetable, so check the night you need. For very late gigs, agree a taxi plan in advance.

Why groups often choose a house

Beatles fans often travel in groups: friends, families with grown-up children, or fans meeting up from different countries. A house means:

  • One booking instead of several hotel rooms
  • A kitchen for breakfasts before long festival days
  • A shared living space to swap stories and plan the next day
  • Parking if some of you are driving

Tips for Beatleweek

  • Carry ID. Some venues and late events check it.
  • Use contactless on Merseyrail. It saves queueing for tickets on busy days.
  • Book restaurant tables. Central restaurants are busy over the bank holiday.
  • Pack for rain. Late August in Liverpool can be warm or wet, often both.
  • Keep a slow day. A week of gigs is tiring. Build in a quieter day at the house.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is International Beatleweek?

It runs each year in late August. The 2026 festival ran from 26 August to 1 September 2026. The organisers hadn't confirmed 2027 dates when we checked, so watch the official Beatleweek and Cavern Club channels before booking travel.

Who organises Beatleweek?

Cavern City Tours, the company that runs the Cavern Club, organises the festival every year, with the Cavern as its hub and events across the city.

What's happening for the Cavern Club's 70th anniversary?

The Cavern Club turns 70 on 16 January 2027. The Museum of Liverpool is running an exhibition, 70 Years Of The Cavern Club, from 16 January to 3 October 2027.

Do I need tickets for Beatleweek?

Many headline shows and the Beatles Convention Day are ticketed, and tickets go on sale months ahead. Some events and Cavern sessions are sold separately, so check each event's listing.

Is it better to stay in the city centre for Beatleweek?

It's the most convenient but also the most expensive and noisiest choice that week. A base a short train ride away can be better value for groups, as long as you plan your late journeys back.
