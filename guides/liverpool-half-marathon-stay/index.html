Liverpool Half Marathon 2027: Race Day Logistics and Where Runners Can Stay

Liverpool Half Marathon 2027 is on Sun 14 March, starting at Hill Dickinson Stadium at 9.30am. Start, finish, bag drop, trains and where to stay.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

The 2027 Liverpool Half Marathon is on Sunday 14 March. The half starts at 9.30am at Hill Dickinson Stadium on Regent Road and finishes at the Pier Head, with a new 10K starting at 8.45am. Sandhills is the nearest station, about 14 minutes by train from Fazakerley. Book a bed for the Saturday night, as the race sold out at 15,000 places in 2025.

The 2027 Liverpool Half Marathon is on Sunday 14 March. The half starts at 9.30am at Hill Dickinson Stadium on Regent Road and finishes at the Pier Head, in front of the Three Graces. A new Liverpool 10K starts at 8.45am from the Pier Head the same morning. The nearest station to the start is Sandhills. Course

BTR says the first four miles of the half were updated for 2025 and pass Liverpool Town Hall, the Chinese Arch and Liverpool Cathedral. The 10K heads along The Strand and follows parts of the half course, including Upper Parliament Street, Princes Road and Sefton Park Road, turning on Croxteth Drive and passing through Princes Park before returning to the Pier Head.

How big is it?

  • First held in 1994; 2027 is the 34th edition
  • 12,000 places sold out in 2023 and 2024
  • Capacity rose to 15,000 in 2025, when the start moved to Hill Dickinson Stadium, and it sold out
  • 2026 was the biggest race yet, and BTR expects a fourth sell-out in 2027
  • The new 10K has 3,000 places; entries opened on 14 October 2026

Getting to the start

By train. Sandhills, on the Merseyrail Northern line, is the nearest station. From Fazakerley, trains to Sandhills take about 14 minutes on average (fastest around 9 minutes), with advance fares from about £4.30. It's a Sunday, so services are less frequent than on weekdays. Check the timetable the night before and aim for a train that gets you there well before the 9.10am bag drop deadline.

By shuttle. BTR lists an Arriva shuttle bus from Liverpool ONE to the start line at £2 per person, one way.

On foot. The start is about 1.4 miles (around 30 minutes) from the city centre.

By car. BTR lists several city-centre car parks between 0.3 and 2.2 miles from the stadium. Roads around the event close from 8.30am, so arrive early or leave the car where you're staying.

Bag drop

Bag drop is on Regent Road, north of the stadium, and closes at 9.10am. After the start it moves to the Cruise Liverpool car park near the finish, so your bag is waiting at the end.

For supporters

  • At the start: Regent Road by Hill Dickinson Stadium is the place to see everyone set off at 9.30am
  • On the course: the early miles pass Liverpool Town Hall, the Chinese Arch and the Cathedral, all walkable from Liverpool Central
  • At the finish: the Pier Head, in front of the Three Graces. Agree a meeting point away from the finish funnel, as it gets very busy
  • Children: a games room or garden back at your accommodation helps if they've had an early start

After the race

Walk it off along the waterfront, refuel, and give yourself time before travelling home. If you're driving a long way on Sunday afternoon, a late checkout or an extra night on the Sunday makes the day far less rushed.

Where to stay

Think about the morning, not the night before.

  • City centre: walkable to the finish and the 10K start, but you'll still need to get to Regent Road for the half
  • North Liverpool: handy for the start. The Northern line runs through Sandhills, so places like Fazakerley, Walton and Kirkdale have a direct train to the start station
  • A whole house for a running club: if several of you are running, a house means a shared kitchen for pasta on Saturday, porridge on Sunday and space to stretch

Supporters can watch at the start, then take the train or walk into town to see runners finish at the Pier Head.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is the Liverpool Half Marathon 2027?

Sunday 14 March 2027. It is the 34th Liverpool Half Marathon, organised by BTR Liverpool.

Where does the Liverpool Half Marathon start and finish?

The half marathon starts at Hill Dickinson Stadium on Regent Road at 9.30am and finishes at the Pier Head, in front of the Three Graces.

What is the nearest station to the start?

Sandhills, on the Merseyrail Northern line. Trains from Fazakerley to Sandhills take about 14 minutes on average. BTR also lists an Arriva shuttle bus from Liverpool ONE to the start for £2 one way.

Is there a 10K as well?

Yes. A new Liverpool 10K runs on the same morning, starting at 8.45am from Canada Boulevard at the Pier Head, with 3,000 places at its first edition.

What is the cut-off time?

BTR lists a 3-hour cut-off for the half. Runners who fall behind may be asked to move onto the pavement or withdraw.
