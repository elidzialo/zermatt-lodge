This guide explains what crews need, where the work is in the Liverpool City Region, and how a north Liverpool base helps.

What work crews actually need

Hotels are built for tourists and business travellers. Crews on site need something different:

  • A bed each, not a sofa bed. Tired workers make mistakes.
  • Off-street parking for vans or cars, at no extra cost
  • Fast motorway access, so a 7am start doesn't mean a 5:30am alarm
  • A proper kitchen to batch cook, make packed lunches and keep food costs down
  • Two bathrooms, so everyone isn't queueing for the shower before work
  • Space to switch off after a long shift: a TV, a garden, something to do
  • One booking and one invoice for the company

Where the work is around Liverpool

The Liverpool City Region has a busy construction and regeneration pipeline. Some examples from public announcements:

  • Pall Mall, city centre: VINCI Construction was appointed design and build partner in 2026 for the first phase of this commercial development, which includes net zero carbon-enabled offices and new green space.
  • Housing growth: in January 2026, the Liverpool City Region mayor set out a £2bn plan to unlock 64,000 homes across the region.
  • Knowsley: Knowsley Council describes Knowsley Business Park as one of the largest employment sites in Europe, with over 800 businesses and 18,000 jobs. It's next door to Fazakerley, and our Kirkby and Knowsley Business Park guide covers it in more detail.
  • The northern docklands: the area around Bramley-Moore Dock and the north docks continues to see major investment.
  • Hospitals and public buildings: sites such as Aintree University Hospital, near the M57, regularly need maintenance and fit-out crews.

For an overview, see Liverpool's construction pipeline.

Why north Liverpool works for crews

Motorway access

Fazakerley sits near the end of two motorways:

  • M57: south to the M62 for Warrington and Manchester, past Knowsley and Prescot
  • M58: east to Skelmersdale and the M6 for Wigan, Preston and the north
  • Switch Island, where the M57 and M58 meet, leads to the north docks, Bootle and Seaforth, plus Crosby and Southport

You can reach a lot of the city region without going near the city centre.

Train into town

If the job is in the city centre, Fazakerley station is on Merseyrail's Northern line, with direct trains to Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes. Trainline lists the first train at 04:46. That's handy when city sites have tight parking or delivery restrictions.

Supplies

There's a Lidl near the house, plus retail parks and trade counters around north Liverpool for anything you need.

Living well on a long contract

Long contracts are easier when the house works for everyone:

  • Set a shower rota for early starts.
  • Batch cook on Sundays. See our self-catering guide for work crews.
  • Agree quiet hours if anyone works nights.
  • Keep work boots and hi-vis by the door. Don't take muddy kit upstairs.
  • Check out washing arrangements before you arrive. See our laundry guide for workers.
