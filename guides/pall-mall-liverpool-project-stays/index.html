Pall Mall Development: Where Contractors and Consultants Can Stay

Working on One Pall Mall in Liverpool's business district? What's being built, who's involved, and where project teams can stay with parking and a train into town.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

One Pall Mall is a planned 111,000 sq ft Grade A office building on a 1.2-hectare site behind the former Exchange Station in Liverpool's business district, with a half-acre park. Kier Property and Liverpool City Council are joint developers, and VINCI was named design and build contractor for the £55m first phase in July 2026. Teams who need parking often stay outside the centre: Fazakerley is about 15 minutes by train to Liverpool Central, via Moorfields.

Pall Mall is one of the biggest office schemes in Liverpool city centre for years, and it will bring design teams, consultants and site crews to the business district through 2027 and beyond. This guide sums up what's been published about the scheme and how a project team can stay nearby without paying city centre parking every day.

We used public reports only, checked on 22 November 2026. Programmes change, so always check with your project lead.

What's being built

According to Kier and Place North West:

  • One Pall Mall: a Grade A office building of about 111,000 sq ft off Bixteth Walk
  • Site: about 1.2 hectares behind the former Exchange Station, in Liverpool's commercial business district
  • Features: ground floor retail, an amenity floor (flexible workspace, business lounge, meeting rooms), a level-eight roof terrace, and a new half-acre public park, Pall Mall Gardens
  • Net zero carbon enabled, according to the developer
  • Later phases: the full masterplan could deliver up to 400,000 sq ft of Grade A offices, plus retail and leisure, in phases

Who's involved

  • Joint developers: Kier Property and Liverpool City Council
  • Contractor: in July 2026, VINCI was named design and build contractor for the £55m first phase, after a two-stage tender
  • Project team named by Place North West: WSP, Allies and Morrison (architect), Stantec and Re-form
  • Funding: support from the Liverpool City Region Combined Authority's investment fund, a £15m council grant and a rent guarantee scheme, according to Place North West

In March 2026, Kier said the refreshed RIBA Stage 3 design was complete and the project was moving into Stage 4. The July reports didn't give a start on site date, and we couldn't find a completion date.

Getting to site

Pall Mall is in the business district, a short walk from Moorfields station.

  • By train: Merseyrail's Northern line runs through Moorfields to Liverpool Central. From Fazakerley, direct trains reach Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes, stopping at Moorfields on the way
  • Early starts: on a sample day the first train from Fazakerley left at 04.46. Check the current timetable for your shift pattern
  • By van: the city centre has restricted streets and paid parking. Agree parking or a compound with the site team before you send vans in

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is being built at Pall Mall in Liverpool?

The first phase, One Pall Mall, is a Grade A office building of about 111,000 sq ft with ground floor retail, amenity space, a rooftop terrace and a new half-acre park called Pall Mall Gardens. Kier says the full masterplan could deliver up to 400,000 sq ft of offices in phases.

Who is the contractor for One Pall Mall?

In July 2026 Place North West and Construction Enquirer reported that VINCI had been chosen as design and build contractor for the £55m first phase, working for Kier Property and Liverpool City Council.

When will One Pall Mall be finished?

We couldn't find a published completion date. The July 2026 reports did not give a start on site date either, so check with the project team.

Where is the nearest station to Pall Mall?

Moorfields, on Merseyrail's Northern and Wirral lines, is the station for the business district. Northern line trains from Fazakerley run through Moorfields to Liverpool Central.

Is there parking near Pall Mall for contractors?

City centre parking is limited and charged. Many teams stay outside the centre where a house has free off-street parking, and take the train in.
