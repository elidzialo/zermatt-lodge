New Year's Day in Liverpool 2027: How to Spend It

New Year's Day 2027 in Liverpool is a Friday bank holiday. The Philharmonic's New Year Proms, skating, shows, Liverpool v Coventry next day and travel tips.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

New Year's Day 2027 is Friday 1 January, a bank holiday. Liverpool Philharmonic lists the New Year's Day Proms at 3pm, Icebox at Boxpark is open daily to 10 January, and Skate St George's runs to 3 January. Liverpool host Coventry at Anfield the next day, Saturday 2 January, at 3pm. Festive transport is reduced, so check times before you travel.

New Year's Day 2027 falls on Friday 1 January, a bank holiday, which turns it into the start of a long weekend. In Liverpool you can ease in with the New Year's Day Proms at 3pm at the Philharmonic, go skating, catch one of the last Christmas shows, or get a walk in before Liverpool v Coventry at Anfield on Saturday 2 January.

We checked the details below on 21 November 2026. Opening hours on bank holidays change, so confirm before you go.

New Year's Day Proms

Liverpool Philharmonic lists the New Year's Day Proms on Friday 1 January 2027 at 3pm.

  • Performers: the Manchester Concert Orchestra, conducted by John Andrews
  • Programme highlights: the finale of Puccini's La boheme, the finale of Tchaikovsky's Swan Lake, Elgar's Nimrod, Holst's Jupiter and Pomp and Circumstance March No. 1
  • Tickets: listed at £26, £31, £41.50, £49 and £56, with a 10% fee on online and phone orders

A 3pm start suits families and anyone who stayed up late the night before.

Skating

  • Icebox at Boxpark Liverpool: open daily to 10 January 2027 except Christmas Day
  • Skate St George's, St George's Plateau: runs to 3 January 2027, so New Year's weekend is one of the last chances

Our ice skating guide has prices and session times.

Shows still running

Several Christmas shows run into the new year, including Mamma Mia at the Empire and Snow White at the Epstein Theatre (both to 3 January) and Little Shop of Horrors at the Playhouse (listed to 9 January). Check each theatre for New Year's Day performances. See our panto guide.

A New Year's Day walk

A bracing walk is a Liverpool tradition for many. The coast north of the city, at Crosby and Formby, is close by car. Check car park hours and tide times, and go in the middle of the day while it's light. Our winter walks guide has routes.

Getting around

The festive timetable for Merseyrail and buses hadn't been published when we checked. Expect a reduced service on New Year's Day, and plan for taxis being in short supply late on New Year's Eve. If you're staying a short train ride out, check the first and last trains before you set off.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is New Year's Day 2027 a bank holiday?

Yes. Friday 1 January 2027 is a bank holiday in England and Wales, so many offices and some shops and services will be closed or on reduced hours.

What's on in Liverpool on New Year's Day 2027?

Liverpool Philharmonic lists the New Year's Day Proms at 3pm with the Manchester Concert Orchestra. Icebox at Boxpark is open daily to 10 January except Christmas Day, Skate St George's runs to 3 January, and the Christmas shows are still on at several theatres.

Is there football in Liverpool around New Year 2027?

Liverpool host Coventry City at Anfield on Saturday 2 January 2027 at 3pm. Everton are away at Leeds that day, and host Aston Villa on Tuesday 5 January.

Do trains run on New Year's Day in Liverpool?

How much are New Year's Day Proms tickets?
