Aintree Hospital Parking: What It Costs and How to Pay Less

Aintree University Hospital parking explained: visitor and short stay tariffs, the weekly ticket, free parking concessions, Blue Badges and avoiding fines.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Parking at Aintree University Hospital is free for under 30 minutes, then charged by the hour, with a weekly ticket available for regular visitors. The hospital's own page lists £3 for up to 1.5 hours rising to £5.50 for over 4.5 hours, and a weekly ticket at £17.50. Several groups of patients and visitors can park free, and a couple of simple habits avoid the most common fines.

This guide is about the car parks on the Aintree site in Fazakerley, north Liverpool, which are shared with The Walton Centre.

Prices at a glance

Aintree University Hospital publishes these tariffs on its location page:

Visitor parking

Time Charge
Under 30 minutes Free
Up to 1 hour 30 minutes £3.00
Up to 2 hours 30 minutes £4.00
Up to 4 hours 30 minutes £4.50
Over 4 hours 30 minutes £5.50
Weekly ticket £17.50

Short stay car park (Emergency Department and Sefton Suite)

Time Charge
Under 30 minutes Free
Up to 1 hour 30 minutes £3.00
Over 1 hour 30 minutes £10.00

The short stay car park is meant for quick visits. Don't use it if you're going to be in A&E or on a ward for hours, because the charge jumps to £10.

Why the prices you see online don't match

The Walton Centre, which shares the site, publishes a different tariff: £4.50 up to 1 hour, £5 up to 2 hours, £6 up to 4 hours, £7 up to 6 hours, £8.50 over 6 hours, and a 7-day pass at £22.50. One of the two pages is probably out of date. The signs and machines on the day are what count.

How paying works

  • Number plate recognition. There's no ticket on entry. Cameras read your registration.
  • Pay before you leave, at a machine, entering your registration.
  • Payment method: the hospital's page says the machines take cash and card. The Walton Centre's page says card only. Bring a card.
  • Note your registration on your phone, especially if you're in a hire car or a relative's car.

Free parking: who qualifies

Aintree University Hospital lists these concessions.

Cancer treatment

Free for patients being treated for cancer, including conditions caused directly by the treatment. Pre-existing conditions aren't covered. The hospital suggests asking specialist nurses or Macmillan Cancer Support on site for details.

Long-term treatment

Free for outpatients with at least three appointments within a month and a total treatment period of at least three months.

Blue Badge holders

Free in a marked bay with the badge displayed. Bring your registration and a photo, copy or screenshot of the badge, and give these to the clinic receptionist before leaving so you can exit free. Parking on double yellow lines with a Blue Badge isn't covered, because the site is private land.

Exceptional circumstances

A ward or clinic nurse in charge, or a manager, can grant free parking at their discretion. The hospital gives examples such as carers, private patients and distressed relatives called in at short notice. If you're in that position, ask.

Clinic delays

If your clinic runs more than an hour late, clinic managers can reduce your charge by the length of the delay, up to two hours.

Help with travel costs

Eligible patients can claim travel costs under the hospital's Patient Travel Cost Policy at the Cash Office, Ground Floor, Aintree Lodge.

How to avoid a parking notice

The hospital issues breach of contract notices to vehicles that obstruct access or park carelessly. Don't park:

  • in Blue Badge bays without a badge on display
  • on double yellow lines
  • on yellow or hatched areas
  • on grass verges or pavements
  • in loading bays

If the car parks are busy, it's better to drop your passenger at the entrance and circle back than to leave the car somewhere you'll get a notice.

Entrances and drop-off

  • Sat nav: L9 7AL
  • Main entrance: Lower Lane (bike parking is here too)
  • Second entrance: Longmoor Lane, across the road from Fazakerley station
  • Drop-off and pick-up points: outside all main entrances, for people who struggle to walk far
  • Traffic: the hospital warns of congestion at peak times, so allow extra time

For a step-by-step guide to the site, see visiting Aintree hospital: a practical guide.

Avoiding the car park altogether

  • Train: Fazakerley station on the Merseyrail Northern line is opposite the Longmoor Lane entrance, about 15 minutes from Liverpool Central.
  • Lifts: if a relative drops you off and collects you, you only need the drop-off points.
  • Stay nearby: if you're visiting for days or weeks, staying close by and walking or taking a short lift in can cut costs.

Visiting for more than a few days?

If you're travelling in to visit someone over a week or more, parking is only part of the cost. Fuel, meals and accommodation add up fast. Our long-stay hospital visiting tips cover budgeting and routines.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is parking at Aintree hospital?

Aintree University Hospital's location page lists visitor parking as free for under 30 minutes, £3 up to 1.5 hours, £4 up to 2.5 hours, £4.50 up to 4.5 hours and £5.50 over that. The Walton Centre's page for the same site shows higher figures, so check the signs on the day.

Is there a weekly parking ticket at Aintree hospital?

Yes. The hospital lists a weekly ticket at £17.50, bought from the pay machines. The Walton Centre's page lists a 7-day pass at £22.50.

Who gets free parking at Aintree hospital?

The hospital lists free parking for patients having cancer treatment, outpatients with frequent appointments over at least three months, and Blue Badge holders in marked bays. Ward or clinic managers can also grant free parking in exceptional circumstances.

How do I pay for parking at Aintree hospital?

Pay before you leave. The site uses number plate recognition, so you need your registration. The hospital's page says machines take cash and card, but the Walton Centre's page says card only, so carry a card to be safe.

Can I get a fine at Aintree hospital car park?

Yes. The hospital issues breach of contract notices for obstructive or careless parking, including on double yellows, hatched areas, verges, pavements, loading bays and Blue Badge bays without a badge.

What's the postcode for Aintree hospital parking?

Use L9 7AL for Aintree University Hospital. The main entrance is on Lower Lane, with another entrance on Longmoor Lane.
