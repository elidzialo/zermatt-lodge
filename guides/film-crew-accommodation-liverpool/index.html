Film Crew Accommodation in Liverpool: Housing a Small Crew Together

Housing a film or TV crew in Liverpool? Why the city is so busy for filming, The Depot studios, getting around for location shoots and a 4-bed crew house.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

For a small film or TV crew in Liverpool, a whole house gives everyone a bedroom, a kitchen for early calls and parking outside. The best base depends on the shoot: The Depot studios off Edge Lane, city-centre locations and wider Merseyside each suit different areas.

For a small film or TV crew working in Liverpool, a whole house is often better than a block of hotel rooms: everyone has a proper bedroom, there's a kitchen for early call times, and you can park outside. The best location depends on where you're shooting. Studio work at The Depot off Edge Lane, city-centre locations and shoots across Merseyside each favour slightly different bases.

Liverpool is one of the busiest filming locations in the UK, so crew accommodation fills up. This guide is for production managers, location managers and freelancers finding somewhere to stay.

Why Liverpool is so busy for filming

The Liverpool Film Office's impact report, published in February 2026 and covering 2019 to 2025, gives a sense of scale:

  • More than 1,600 productions in the Liverpool City Region since 2019
  • Over 5,000 full-time equivalent jobs supported
  • About £150m added to the local economy
  • Productions including The Batman, Clayface, House of Guinness, This City Is Ours, The Responder and Time

The city's streets, waterfront and docks are versatile, which helps explain why productions keep coming back. Our list of films to watch before visiting Liverpool shows how varied the locations are.

The studios

The Depot

The Depot opened in October 2021 with two sound-proofed production units of 20,000 sq ft each, off Edge Lane, east of the city centre. The Liverpool Film Office says it has generated £29.1m for the city region and helped 15 businesses start up or grow.

Littlewoods Studios (planned)

The former Littlewoods building next to The Depot is planned as a studio campus with six sound stages and post-production facilities. Check progress before assuming it's open.

Location shoots across the city region

Many productions don't stay in one place. A crew might be on the waterfront one day, on a terraced street in north Liverpool the next and on the Sefton coast after that. That's where a base with good road links really helps.

  • Motorways: the M57 runs around the east of Liverpool, connecting to the M62 at its southern end and to the M58 at Switch Island in the north. The M62 runs east towards Manchester.
  • City centre: filming in town often means closures, permits and expensive parking. A crew base with trains into the centre lets people who aren't driving the kit travel separately.
  • Coast and countryside: the Sefton coast and West Lancashire are reached via the A59 and M58 from north Liverpool.

Always check live journey times for your call times. Morning traffic on routes into Liverpool can be slow.

What a crew needs from accommodation

Sleep

Crews work long, irregular days. Separate bedrooms with proper beds matter more than anything else. Ask how many people would be sharing rooms, and whether anyone's on a sofa bed.

Bathrooms

For a crew of five to seven with the same early call time, two bathrooms is the realistic minimum.

Parking

You'll probably have at least one car or van per two people. Check:

  • how many vehicles can park off-street
  • whether a van will fit (height and length)
  • whether kit can be left in vehicles overnight, and how secure the area feels

Our van parking accommodation guide covers what to ask.

A kitchen

Catering usually covers the working day, but not 5am breakfasts or late suppers after a night shoot. A full kitchen saves money and keeps people going.

Flexibility

Schedules slip. Ask about extending, shortening and cancellation terms before you sign anything.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Liverpool a busy city for filming?

Yes. The Liverpool Film Office's impact report, published in February 2026, says more than 1,600 productions have filmed in the city region since 2019, including The Batman, House of Guinness, The Responder and Time.

Where are the film studios in Liverpool?

The Depot, run with the Liverpool Film Office, has two sound stages of 20,000 sq ft each off Edge Lane, east of the city centre. A larger studio campus is planned at the former Littlewoods building next door.

What should a production look for in crew accommodation?

Separate bedrooms so people can sleep after split shifts, enough bathrooms, off-street parking, a kitchen for early starts, and flexible dates. Also check access to the motorways and to where you're actually filming.

Can a crew house have vans parked outside?

It depends on the property. Zermatt Lodge has free parking for 2 cars. If you're bringing vans or a larger vehicle, call first to check before booking.
