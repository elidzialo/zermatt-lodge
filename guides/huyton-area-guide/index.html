Huyton, Merseyside: A Practical Guide for Visitors and Business Travellers

Huyton guide: where it is, City Line trains to Liverpool and Wigan, M57 and M62 junctions, the village centre, Harold Wilson's statue and where to stay nearby.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Huyton is a town of about 33,000 people in the borough of Knowsley, 6 miles east of Liverpool city centre. It sits just west of the M57, with three motorway junctions nearby (M57 J2, M62 J5 and J6), and Huyton station has City Line trains to Liverpool, St Helens and Wigan. For business stays, it's well placed for Knowsley, the M62 corridor and south and east Liverpool.

Huyton is a town in the borough of Knowsley, about 6 miles east of Liverpool city centre. It's mainly residential, but its location makes it useful for business travellers and contractors: it sits on the M57 and M62, and Huyton station has direct trains into Liverpool and out towards St Helens and Wigan. Locals still call the town centre "the village", a name from its rural past.

Key facts

  • Where: Metropolitan Borough of Knowsley, Merseyside, part of the Liverpool Built-up Area
  • Population: 33,193, according to figures cited on Wikipedia from the Office for National Statistics
  • Distance to Liverpool: 6 miles (9.7 km) west via the M62
  • Border: the M57 marks the town's eastern edge
  • Local government: Huyton-with-Roby became part of Knowsley on 1 April 1974. The Huyton Municipal Building was completed in 1963
  • Famous MP: Harold Wilson, MP for Huyton from 1950 to 1983 and Labour Prime Minister in 1964 to 1970 and 1974 to 1976. A statue of him was put up in the town centre in 2006

Getting around

By road

Huyton has three motorway junctions close by: M57 junction 2 and M62 junctions 5 and 6. The M62 links Liverpool with Warrington and Manchester, and the M57 runs north around the east of Liverpool to Switch Island, where the M58 begins. Fazakerley and Kirkby are on the M57 corridor north of Huyton.

That makes it easy to reach sites across Knowsley, the M62 corridor and the north of the city.

By train

Huyton station (formerly Huyton Gate) has City Line services to Liverpool, St Helens and Wigan. Huyton and Roby both had stations on the original Liverpool and Manchester Railway, which opened in 1830.

The town centre

The centre, "the village" or "the villie" to locals, has an Asda complex and around 100 independent shops. It's a practical place for supplies rather than a destination.

Where to stay

Huyton is mostly housing, with few large holiday homes. For a team, the M57 corridor to the north, around Kirkby and Fazakerley, has whole-house options with parking and quick motorway links south to Huyton. Always check the drive at the time of day you'll travel.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is Huyton?

Huyton is a town in the Metropolitan Borough of Knowsley, Merseyside, about 6 miles (9.7 km) east of Liverpool city centre via the M62. The M57 marks its eastern border.

How do I get from Huyton to Liverpool?

By train from Huyton station on the City Line to Liverpool, or by road on the M62. Huyton station also has trains towards St Helens and Wigan.

Which motorway junctions are near Huyton?

Three: M57 junction 2, and M62 junctions 5 and 6.

Is there business accommodation in Huyton?
