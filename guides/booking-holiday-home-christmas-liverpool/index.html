Booking a Holiday Home for Christmas in Liverpool: What to Check

Renting a house for Christmas 2026 in Liverpool? Bank holidays, shop and transport closures, what to ask the host and how to plan the food and the days out.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

For a Christmas holiday home in Liverpool, arrive by Christmas Eve, as large shops must close on Christmas Day and trains haven't run on Christmas Day in past years. Check the oven and dining space for Christmas dinner, the bed layout, parking and check-in times, and book early. In 2026, Christmas Day is a Friday and the Boxing Day bank holiday moves to Monday 28 December.

A rented house can make Christmas easier: one kitchen, one table and room for the whole family. But Christmas changes the rules of a normal holiday. Shops close, trains stop and check-in days matter. In 2026, Christmas Day is a Friday, and the Boxing Day bank holiday moves to Monday 28 December. Here's what to check before you book a holiday home in Liverpool.

Before you book: ask the host

  1. The oven: is it full size? Will a turkey tray fit?
  2. The hob and kitchen kit: roasting tins, a big pan for sprouts, enough plates for everyone
  3. The table: does it seat your whole group at once?
  4. The beds: the real layout, room by room, including cots for babies
  5. Heating: how does it work, and can you control it?
  6. Check-in on Christmas Eve: what time, and is it self check-in?
  7. Decorations: can you bring a tree or lights?
  8. Parking: for how many cars, as you'll be bringing presents and food

Food planning

  • Book a supermarket delivery slot early, timed for the day you arrive. Christmas slots go fast
  • Or shop on arrival. Large shops must close on Christmas Day in England and Wales, and some may close early on Christmas Eve
  • Bring the specialist bits you can't find in a hurry: your gravy, your stuffing, the special crackers
  • Plan leftovers for Boxing Day and the bank holiday Monday

Getting around

The 2026 festive timetable hadn't been published when we checked. In past years Merseyrail hasn't run on Christmas Day, and the Combined Authority has warned of very limited buses around the holidays. A house with parking and a car gives you options, or pick a base close to where you want to go on Boxing Day.

Things to do around Christmas

  • Skate St George's and its Christmas market, open daily to 3 January 2027 except Christmas Day

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When should I arrive for Christmas in a holiday home?

By Christmas Eve, Thursday 24 December 2026, at the latest. Large shops must close on Christmas Day, and in past years Merseyrail hasn't run on Christmas Day. Arriving on the 23rd gives you a calmer day to shop.

What are the bank holidays over Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day on Friday 25 December, Boxing Day observed on Monday 28 December, and New Year's Day on Friday 1 January 2027, in England and Wales.

What should I ask the host before booking a Christmas house?

Ask about the oven and hob, the dining table size, the exact bed layout, parking, heating, check-in on Christmas Eve, and whether you can put up decorations or a real tree.

Is anything open in Liverpool on Christmas Day?

Very little. Skate St George's and most attractions close on Christmas Day. Plan to stay in, cook and play games.

Can I host a Christmas party in a holiday house?

Not at Zermatt Lodge. Strictly no parties or events, at Christmas or any other time. Christmas dinner for the guests staying is what the dining room is for.
