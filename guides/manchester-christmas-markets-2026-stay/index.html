Manchester Christmas Markets 2026: Dates, Sites and Where to Stay

Manchester Christmas Markets run 6 November to 22 December 2026, with Albert Square and Cathedral Gardens open to 3 January. Sites, tips and family homes.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

The Manchester Christmas Markets open on Friday 6 November 2026 and run until Tuesday 22 December 2026. Albert Square and Cathedral Gardens stay open until Sunday 3 January 2027. Entry is free. For a family or group trip, a whole home with parking in north Manchester or Prestwich, near the Metrolink, is an easy base.

The Manchester Christmas Markets are one of the biggest festive days out in the north of England. Here are the 2026 dates, the main sites and some practical tips for a family or group trip, plus where to stay if you want a whole home rather than hotel rooms.

Dates for 2026

  • Opens: Friday 6 November 2026.
  • Most sites close: Tuesday 22 December 2026.
  • Albert Square and Cathedral Gardens: stay open until Sunday 3 January 2027.

Entry to the market sites is free, and you do not need to book. Opening hours can differ between sites, so check the official Visit Manchester Christmas page before you set off.

The market sites

Manchester's Finest lists these city centre locations for 2026:

  • Albert Square, the centrepiece, with a 50-metre Ferris wheel, a helter-skelter, food, drink and craft stalls.
  • Lincoln Square, new for 2026, just off Brazennose Street.
  • King Street and St Ann's Square, which has a new layout with more craft, food and drink traders.
  • Exchange Street, Exchange Square and New Cathedral Street.
  • Corn Exchange.
  • Cathedral Gardens, where Skate Manchester's covered ice rink returns until 3 January.
  • Market Street.

Visit Manchester describes "over 200 stalls" across the sites. Lists can change, so check before you go.

Tips for families and groups

  • Go early in the week if you can. Weekends in December are the busiest times.
  • Use the tram. Visit Manchester recommends public transport because parking in the centre gets busy.
  • Plan a meeting point. With sites spread across the centre, it is easy to lose people. Pick one square to meet at.
  • Book skating in advance if you want a set time at Skate Manchester.
  • The Christmas Parade was reported for Sunday 6 December 2026, with details to be confirmed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When are the Manchester Christmas Markets in 2026?

From Friday 6 November to Tuesday 22 December 2026. Albert Square and Cathedral Gardens stay open until Sunday 3 January 2027, according to Visit Manchester.

Are the Manchester Christmas Markets free?

Yes, entry to the market sites is free. You pay for food, drink, gifts and activities such as ice skating.

What are the opening hours?

Hours can vary by site and some close earlier than others. Check the official Visit Manchester page before you go.

When is the Manchester Christmas Parade?

Manchester's Finest reported it will take place on Sunday 6 December 2026, with details still to be announced. Check official channels nearer the day.

Is it better to drive or take the tram?

Visit Manchester recommends public transport, as city centre parking can be busy at weekends and peak times. Metrolink trams run into the centre from Prestwich, Salford and most of Greater Manchester.
