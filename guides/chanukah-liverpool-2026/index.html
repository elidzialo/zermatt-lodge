Chanukah 2026 in Liverpool: A Practical Guide for Visitors

Chanukah 2026 runs from Friday 4 to Saturday 12 December. Friday lighting, menorah safety in a rental, kosher food and staying in Liverpool for the festival.

Friday and Saturday lighting

Both Fridays need a little planning. Chabad.org's Chanukah guide says:

  • Light the Chanukah candles before the Shabbat candles on Friday
  • Use larger candles or extra oil, so they burn until half an hour after nightfall. Standard 30-minute Chanukah candles aren't suitable on Friday
  • Don't relight, move or prepare the menorah from Shabbat candle lighting until havdalah
  • On Saturday night, light only after Shabbat ends after nightfall

Liverpool's winter afternoons are short. Sunset is at its earliest on 13 December, at 3.54pm, so on both Fridays you'll be lighting mid afternoon. Get the exact Shabbat time from a Jewish calendar.

Lighting a menorah in a rental

Most holiday homes have smoke alarms, and many have rules on candles. Before you book:

  1. Ask the host whether you can light candles, and where
  2. Find a safe spot. Chabad.org advises a sturdy, fireproof surface, out of reach of children, away from curtains and anything flammable
  3. Windowsills and doorways are the traditional places. Check the sill is deep enough and clear of blinds
  4. Ask where the smoke alarms are, and never disable one
  5. Bring a foil tray to sit the menorah on, and long candles for the Fridays

If open flames aren't allowed, ask your rabbi what to do before you travel.

Chanukah and the rest of December

Chanukah overlaps a busy week in the city. Becher Chase Day is at Aintree on Saturday 5 December, and Liverpool play Porto at Anfield on Wednesday 9 December. Expect busy roads and booked-up hotels. See our December 2026 events guide.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is Chanukah 2026?

Chanukah 2026 begins at sundown on Friday 4 December, when the first candle is lit. The eighth candle is lit on Friday 11 December, and the festival ends at nightfall on Saturday 12 December.

How do you light Chanukah candles on a Friday?

Chabad.org's guide says the Chanukah lights are kindled before the Shabbat candles, using larger candles or extra oil so they burn until half an hour after nightfall. Standard 30-minute Chanukah candles are not suitable on Friday.

When are Saturday night Chanukah candles lit?

Only after Shabbat ends after nightfall. The menorah shouldn't be relit, moved or prepared from Shabbat candle lighting on Friday until havdalah.

Can I light a menorah in a holiday rental?

Ask the host before you book. Many rentals have rules on open flames. Place the menorah on a sturdy, fireproof surface away from curtains and out of reach of children, and ask where the smoke alarms are.
