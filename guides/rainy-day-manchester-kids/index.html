Rainy Day in Manchester with Kids: Indoor Ideas That Work

Wet day with children in Manchester? Free museums with dinosaurs and history, the National Football Museum, IWM North, Christmas opening dates, and family homes to come back to.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Manchester Museum on Oxford Road is free, with Stan the T. rex in its Fossils and Dinosaurs gallery; it's closed on Mondays and from 24 to 28 December. The People's History Museum is free and open every day except Tuesday. IWM North at Salford Quays is free. The National Football Museum, a two-minute walk from Manchester Victoria, is paid (under-5s free) and open daily 10am to 5pm. LEGOLAND Discovery Centre and SEA LIFE are other paid options.

Manchester gets its share of rain, but the city is full of indoor places that children actually enjoy, and several are free. We checked these opening details on the venues' own websites on 24 November 2026. Christmas hours differ, so check before you set off.

Manchester Museum (free)

  • Where: The University of Manchester, Oxford Road, M13 9PL.
  • Price: free. The winter experience Magic Place (28 November to 17 January) needs booking.
  • Hours: closed Monday. Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Sunday 10am to 5pm; Wednesday 10am to 9pm; Saturday from 8am (only some galleries before 10am) to 5pm.
  • For kids: Stan the T. rex and April the Tenontosaurus in Fossils and Dinosaurs, and a life-sized moose in Living Worlds. The Vivarium is closed until May 2027.
  • Christmas: closed 24 to 28 December and 1 January. Closes at 4pm on 16 and 17 December.

National Football Museum (paid)

  • Where: Urbis Building, Cathedral Gardens, M4 3BG, about a two-minute walk from Manchester Victoria.
  • Hours: daily 10am to 5pm, last entry 4pm. Closed 24 and 25 December 2026 and 1 January 2027.
  • Price: summer 2026 prices were £14 adult and £8.75 child online in advance (higher on the door), with other rates at other times. Under-5s free. Tickets bought direct work as season tickets for 12 months.
  • Handy: from Prestwich or Crumpsall, Bury line trams run straight into Victoria.

People's History Museum (free)

  • Where: corner of Left Bank and Bridge Street, Spinningfields, M3 3ER.
  • What: the national museum of democracy.
  • Hours: 10am to 5pm every day except Tuesday.
  • Price: free; the museum asks for donations.
  • Closed: 24 to 29 December 2026, 1 January, and 4 to 15 January 2027.

IWM North (free)

  • At Salford Quays, across the canal from The Lowry. Designed by Daniel Libeskind and opened in 2002.
  • General admission is free. See our Salford Quays guide for trams and parking.

Science and Industry Museum

Visit Manchester describes it as exploring 250 years of innovations that started in Manchester. Check the museum's own website for current opening, booking and which galleries are open.

Paid indoor attractions

LEGOLAND Discovery Centre and SEA LIFE Manchester sit next to each other at the Trafford Palazzo by the Trafford Centre. Our LEGOLAND and SEA LIFE guide covers tickets, the adult rule and winter closure dates.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Manchester Museum free?

Yes. All galleries and exhibitions are free. Its winter experience, Magic Place (28 November to 17 January), needs booking in advance.

When is Manchester Museum closed over Christmas?

Closed 24 to 28 December and on 1 January. It closes at 4pm on 16 and 17 December, and opens 10am to 5pm on 23 and 29 to 31 December. Normal hours resume on 2 January.

How much is the National Football Museum?

The museum lists summer 2026 prices of £14 adult and £8.75 child (5 to 15) online in advance, and £15.75 and £10.50 on the door, with other rates at other times. Under-5s are free. Tickets bought direct work as season tickets for 12 months. Check current prices before you go.

Is the People's History Museum open over Christmas?

It's closed 24 to 29 December 2026, and on 1 January and 4 to 15 January 2027. Otherwise it opens 10am to 5pm every day except Tuesday.
