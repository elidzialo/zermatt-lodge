Spending Shabbat in Manchester: What Visitors Need to Know

Visiting Manchester for Shabbat? Which areas to stay in, the eruvs, finding a shul, kosher food for Friday night and what to check in a holiday home.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

For Shabbat in Manchester, stay in north Manchester: Broughton Park and Higher Broughton in Salford, or Prestwich and Whitefield in Bury. These areas have many shuls, Manchester Beth Din licensed shops and two eruvs: the Manchester community eruv, covering Broughton Park, Prestwich and surrounding areas, and the smaller Whitefield eruv. Book within walking distance of your shul, order food by Thursday, and check the eruv status before Shabbat.

Manchester is the easiest place in the north of England to keep a full Shabbat as a visitor. Greater Manchester's Jewish population was 28,072 in the 2021 Census, according to Jewish News, and most of it lives in a compact area of north Manchester with dozens of shuls, licensed kosher shops and two eruvs. Your one big decision is where to sleep: book within walking distance of the shul you'll use. The eruvs

  • Manchester community eruv: described by the eruv directory as "the largest Eruv in the UK, covering Broughton Park, Prestwich and surrounding areas", certified by the Manchester Beth Din. The Jewish Chronicle reported a 13-mile perimeter across Salford, Prestwich and Crumpsall when it went live in January 2014. Status hotline: 0161 883 1333.
  • Whitefield eruv: inaugurated on 12 December 2013, covering no more than 2 square kilometres. It's checked every Thursday, and Whitefield Hebrew Congregation shows its status on the front page of its website.

Check the status every week, and confirm with your host that the house is inside the boundary.

Finding a shul

An older community directory lists congregations from Adath Yisroel and Machzikei Hadass in Broughton Park to Prestwich Hebrew Congregation on Bury New Road and Whitefield Hebrew Congregation on Park Lane. It notes its listings may be out of date, so:

  • Contact the shul before Shabbat. Ask about times, seating and any visitor or security arrangements.
  • Ask about meals. Many communities can put visitors in touch with hosts for a Shabbat meal.

Food for Shabbat

The Manchester Beth Din licensee list includes caterers such as Celia's Kitchen, Niru and Orange Catering in Prestwich, bakeries in Prestwich and Salford, and restaurants and takeaways mostly on and around Bury New Road. Halpern's in Salford and Kayes Kosher in Prestwich are listed as grocers.

Practical tips:

  • Order by Thursday. Friday is short in winter, and shops get busy.
  • Bring cool bags if you're driving in from elsewhere.
  • Check the hechsher. Machzikei Hadass and Badatz Igud Rabbonim (KIR) also certify locally, alongside the Manchester Beth Din.

Setting up a holiday home for Shabbat

A standard holiday let isn't built for Shabbat. Before you book, ask the host about:

  1. Motion-sensor lights, outside lights and doorbell cameras
  2. Door locks: a keypad or smart lock may not work for you; ask for a key
  3. An urn or hot plate, and whether you can set light timers
  4. The fridge light, and whether it can be switched off
  5. Kitchen: kosher, or can you bring your own equipment?

Our Shabbat-friendly holiday home checklist covers each one.

Manchester or Liverpool?

Liverpool's community is much smaller, with about 2,000 people according to local press, and one kosher deli. If you have a choice, Manchester is easier for Shabbat. If you have to be in Liverpool, read our Shabbat in Liverpool guide.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which area of Manchester is best for Shabbat?

North Manchester. Broughton Park and Higher Broughton in Salford and Prestwich and Whitefield in Bury have the most shuls and kosher shops. Pick the area by the shul you want to walk to.

Is there an eruv in Manchester?

Yes, two. The Manchester community eruv covers Broughton Park, Prestwich and surrounding areas and is certified by the Manchester Beth Din; its status hotline is listed as 0161 883 1333. The Whitefield eruv, inaugurated in December 2013, is checked every Thursday and its status is shown on Whitefield Hebrew Congregation's website.

Can I get kosher takeaway for Friday night?

Yes. Most Manchester Beth Din licensed restaurants and caterers are in Prestwich, with bakeries in both Prestwich and Salford. Order early on Thursday or Friday morning, as winter Fridays are short.

Is Manchester better than Liverpool for a Shabbat stay?

For most observant visitors, yes. Liverpool's community is much smaller, with about 2,000 people according to local press and one kosher deli. Greater Manchester's Jewish population was 28,072 in the 2021 Census.
