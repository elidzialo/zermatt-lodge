Self-Catering Meal Plan for a Group of 7

A three-day self-catering meal plan for a group of 7, with a shopping list, one-pot dinners, a cooking rota, leftovers rules and shops near Fazakerley.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

The easiest way to feed a group of 7 on a self-catering trip is to keep breakfasts and lunches simple, cook one big one-pot dinner each evening, and share the cooking in pairs so no one is stuck in the kitchen. Do one main shop on arrival day with a list, then top up as you go. Below is a three-day plan with a shopping list, a rota and food safety tips, written for a group staying in a house with a full kitchen and a dining table, such as our 4-bedroom house in north Liverpool.

The principles

  1. One big shop, one top-up. Shop on arrival with a list, and pop back for bread and milk on day two or three.
  2. Breakfasts are self-service. Put everything out and let people help themselves.
  3. Lunches are flexible. Some days you'll be out, so pack food or buy it. Other days, sandwiches at home.
  4. Dinners are one-pot or tray-bake. They scale easily to 7, use few pans and leave little washing up.
  5. Pairs cook, others clear. Rotate so everyone does one dinner and one clear-up.
  6. Plan for leftovers. Turn night one's extras into day two's lunch.

Before you shop: ask the group

  • Allergies and intolerances
  • Vegetarians, vegans and anyone avoiding particular foods
  • The fussy eaters, especially children
  • Who's bringing what from home (spices, oil, coffee, tea, condiments)
  • Budget per person for food

Bringing basics from home saves money. A small box with oil, salt, pepper, stock cubes, dried herbs, tea, coffee and sugar covers most recipes.

The three-day plan

Day 1 (arrival)

  • Lunch: on the road or before you arrive
  • Dinner: Chilli con carne (or a bean chilli) with rice, tortilla chips, grated cheese and soured cream. One big pot, and easy to keep warm if people arrive at different times.

Day 2

  • Breakfast: cereal, toast, yoghurt, fruit. Bacon or veggie sausage sandwiches if you have time.
  • Lunch: wraps with leftover chilli (eat within the safe time, see below), or a packed lunch for a day out.
  • Dinner: Chicken and vegetable tray-bake with potatoes. Two large trays in the oven, with a vegetarian tray of halloumi or chickpeas if needed.

Day 3

  • Breakfast: a cooked breakfast for whoever's up: eggs, beans, toast, mushrooms and tomatoes.
  • Lunch: soup and crusty bread, or out.
  • Dinner: Pasta bake with a tomato and vegetable sauce, mozzarella on top, and a big salad. Make two dishes if your oven allows.

Departure morning

Use up what's left: eggs, bread, fruit. Keep it simple so you can clean the kitchen and leave on time.

Shopping list for 7 people, 3 days

This list covers the plan above. Adjust for appetites and diets.

Breakfast

  • 2 boxes of cereal
  • 2 loaves of bread, plus 1 for top-up
  • 6 to 8 litres of milk across the stay
  • Butter or spread, jam, peanut butter
  • 2 large pots of yoghurt
  • Fruit: bananas, apples, grapes
  • 1 to 2 dozen eggs
  • Bacon or vegetarian sausages, beans, mushrooms, tomatoes
  • Orange juice

Lunches

  • Wraps or rolls
  • Sliced cheese and ham (or vegetarian fillings)
  • Salad: lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes
  • Crisps and snacks
  • 2 to 3 cartons of soup, or ingredients for a big pot

Dinners

  • Chilli: about 1kg of mince or 3 to 4 tins of beans, onions, garlic, tinned tomatoes, kidney beans, chilli spices, rice, tortilla chips, cheese, soured cream
  • Tray-bake: chicken thighs or a vegetarian alternative, potatoes, peppers, onions, courgettes, oil and seasoning
  • Pasta bake: about 1kg of pasta, tinned tomatoes or passata, vegetables, mozzarella and cheddar, salad

Extras

  • Tea, coffee, sugar
  • Squash and soft drinks
  • Bin bags, washing-up liquid and dishwasher tablets if the house doesn't provide enough
  • Foil and cling film for leftovers

A simple cooking rota

Day Cooks Clear up
Day 1 dinner Pair A Pair B
Day 2 dinner Pair B Pair C
Day 3 dinner Pair C Pair A

The seventh person can be the "breakfast captain" or take a night off. Post the rota in the group chat before you travel.

Food safety for groups

With more people and bigger batches, food safety matters more. The Food Standards Agency advises:

  • Cool, cover and refrigerate or freeze leftovers within one to two hours of cooking
  • Keep the fridge at 5°C or below
  • Eat or freeze leftovers within 2 days, or within 1 day for rice dishes
  • Reheat previously cooked and frozen meat only once
  • Eat defrosted food within 24 hours

With rice, cook only what you need and get any extra into the fridge quickly.

Also keep raw meat on the bottom shelf of the fridge and use separate chopping boards for raw meat and vegetables.

Shopping near Fazakerley

If you're staying in Fazakerley, north Liverpool, the nearest supermarkets include:

  • Lidl, Longmoor Lane: listed as open 8am to 10pm Monday to Saturday and 10am to 4pm on Sunday. It's good for the main shop.
  • Tesco Express, Longmoor Lane: listed as open 7am to 11pm every day. Handy for top-ups.

Larger stores are a short drive away. Bigger shops, like this Lidl, often have shorter Sunday hours, and times change on bank holidays and over Christmas (the Lidl's listing shows it closed on Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day). Check before you go.

You can also book an online grocery delivery to arrive soon after check-in. Book the slot early if you're coming on a busy weekend.

Eating out without breaking the budget

Self-catering doesn't mean never eating out. Many groups:

  • Self-cater breakfasts and most dinners
  • Eat lunch out on city days
  • Book one restaurant dinner as the treat night, well ahead for a table of 7

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do you feed 7 people on a self-catering holiday?

Plan breakfasts and lunches simply, cook one big one-pot dinner each night, and give each pair a night to cook. Do one main shop on arrival day and top up as you go.

How long can we keep leftovers?

The Food Standards Agency says to cool, cover and refrigerate or freeze leftovers within one to two hours, keep the fridge at 5°C or below, and eat or freeze them within 2 days, or within 1 day for rice dishes.

Where can we shop near Fazakerley?

There's a Lidl on Longmoor Lane, listed as open 8am to 10pm Monday to Saturday and 10am to 4pm on Sunday, and a Tesco Express on Longmoor Lane open 7am to 11pm daily. Check times before you go, especially on bank holidays.

Is it cheaper to self-cater than eat out with a group?

Usually, especially for breakfasts and at least some dinners. Many groups self-cater most meals and book one or two restaurant meals as treats.
