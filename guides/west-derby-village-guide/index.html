West Derby Village Guide: History, Croxteth Park and Getting Around

West Derby village guide: the 1586 courthouse, Croxteth Hall and Country Park, the Casbah Club's Beatles history, Alder Hey, buses and where to stay.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

West Derby is a historic village in east Liverpool, older than Liverpool itself, with a 16th-century courthouse, a listed Victorian village cross and a cluster of independent restaurants and cafes at its heart. On its doorstep is Croxteth Hall and Country Park, one of the city's biggest green spaces, and it's home to Alder Hey Children's Hospital. Beatles fans know it for the Casbah Club, where the band played before they were famous. There's no train station, so most visitors drive or take the bus. A little history

West Derby is mentioned in the Domesday Book and was an important place long before Liverpool grew into a city. Its name is linked to a royal deer park that is now Croxteth Park.

A few things survive from its long history:

  • The Courthouse (1586): a small stone building in the village centre. It was restored in 2005 and is described as the only freestanding post-medieval courthouse in Britain.
  • The stocks: Victorian cast iron stocks opposite the courthouse, put there to mark the coronation of Edward VII in 1902.
  • The Village Cross: a Victorian monument built between 1861 and 1870 to a design by William Eden Nesfield, and listed Grade II*.
  • The castle site: a motte-and-bailey castle once stood near Castlesite Road and Meadow Lane, but nothing is left. It was reported to be in ruins by 1327.

West Derby became part of Liverpool in stages, and the parish was merged into the city in 1922.

Things to do

Croxteth Hall and Country Park

Croxteth Park, the former estate of the Earls of Sefton, is the big draw. Opening times published by Croxteth Hall:

  • Country park: open all year
  • Inner park around the hall: 7am to 6.30pm (April to October), 7am to 5.30pm (November to March)
  • Car park: 7am to 7pm (April to October), 7am to 6pm (November to March)
  • Victorian walled garden: 10am to 3pm daily, last admission 2.30pm
  • Home Farm: run separately, so check its own hours and prices

The hall runs "Upstairs Downstairs" tours and events, but can close for weddings, filming and private events, so check before you go. The main cafe has been closed for refurbishment, with a smaller concession serving drinks and snacks, and you're welcome to bring a picnic.

The hall is also a wedding venue. See our Croxteth Hall wedding guest guide.

The Casbah Coffee Club

At 8 Haymans Green, Mona Best opened a members' coffee club in her cellar on 29 August 1959. John Lennon, Paul McCartney and George Harrison, then the Quarrymen, helped paint the walls in return for a booking, and their designs of spiders, dragons, rainbows and stars are still there. The club closed on 24 June 1962, with the Beatles as the last band to play. Mona's son, Pete Best, was the Beatles' first drummer.

The cellar was Grade II listed in 2006 and has a blue plaque. It has been run as an attraction for booked groups only, and in 2024 the property was converted into holiday flats. Check current visiting arrangements before you go, and please respect that it's a residential street.

Walking and cycling

The old North Liverpool Extension Line railway, which once had a West Derby station, is now part of National Cycle Route 62, a traffic-free route for walking and cycling.

Football history

Liverpool FC trained at Melwood in West Derby for decades, and Everton trained at Bellefield until 2007. Both clubs have since moved their training grounds. Bill Shankly lived in West Derby.

Alder Hey Children's Hospital

Alder Hey is on the south side of West Derby, on East Prescot Road, with its trust headquarters on Eaton Road. Families visiting a child in hospital often look for a base nearby with a kitchen and parking. See our Alder Hey family accommodation guide.

Food and drink

The village centre around the courthouse has a mix of restaurants, cafes, bars and pubs, making it a pleasant place for lunch after a walk in Croxteth Park or for a relaxed evening meal. It's busy on Friday and Saturday nights, so book for a group.

Getting there and around

By bus

West Derby is served by several bus routes, including:

  • 12, 13 and 15: to Liverpool city centre, Huyton and Stockbridge Village (the 15 also serves Alder Hey)
  • 61: to Bootle Strand and Aigburth Vale
  • 18: from Croxteth Park to the city centre

Check current timetables with Merseytravel before you travel.

By train

There's no station in West Derby today. Alder Hey says its nearest station is Broad Green, about 1.5 miles away, with trains to Lime Street, Wigan and Manchester. If you're staying in north Liverpool, Fazakerley station has direct Merseyrail trains to Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes, according to Trainline, and West Derby is a short drive from there.

By car

West Derby sits between Liverpool city centre and the M57. Parking in the village centre can be tight at busy times. Croxteth Park has its own car park.

Living in West Derby

West Derby is popular with families for its village centre, green space and choice of schools. It's within Liverpool City Council, so council tax, bins and school admissions are run by Liverpool. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is West Derby known for?

West Derby is one of Liverpool's oldest districts. It's in the Domesday Book, has a 1586 courthouse, a Grade II* listed Victorian village cross and Croxteth Hall and Park. It's also home to Alder Hey Children's Hospital and the Casbah Club, where the Beatles played in their early days.

Is there a train station in West Derby?

Not any more. West Derby's old station was on the North Liverpool Extension Line; the track has been lifted and the route is now part of National Cycle Route 62. Buses link West Derby with the city centre, Huyton, Bootle and Aigburth.

Can you visit the Casbah Coffee Club?

The Casbah, in the cellar of the Best family home on Haymans Green, has been run as an attraction for booked groups only, and part of the property was converted into holiday flats in 2024. Check current arrangements before you go.

When is Croxteth Country Park open?

Croxteth Hall says the country park is open all year. The inner park is open 7am to 6.30pm from April to October and 7am to 5.30pm from November to March, and the Victorian walled garden 10am to 3pm daily.
