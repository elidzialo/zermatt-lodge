RHS Garden Bridgewater: A Winter Visitor Guide

Visiting RHS Garden Bridgewater in Worsley, Salford: winter opening times for 2026 to 2027, ticket prices, car-free discount, RHS Glow dates and family homes nearby.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

RHS Garden Bridgewater (Occupation Road, Worsley, Salford, M28 2LJ) is open every day except Christmas Day. From 26 November to 3 January it opens 10am to 3.30pm, then 10am to 4pm from 4 January to 5 February. Adult tickets are from £13.60 booked online in advance or £18.40 on the day, children aged 5 to 16 are £6 and under-5s are free. Arrive car-free and adult entry is £12.90. The Glow light trail runs from 26 November to 30 December 2026.

RHS Garden Bridgewater is the Royal Horticultural Society's garden in Worsley, Salford. It's a good winter walk with children or older relatives, and in December the after-dark Glow trail adds a festive evening option. We checked these details on the RHS website on 25 November 2026.

Where it is

RHS Garden Bridgewater, Occupation Road, off Leigh Road, Worsley, Salford, M28 2LJ. Use the RHS "Getting here" page or the Bee Network app to plan a bus journey.

Winter opening times

The garden is open every day except Christmas Day. Last entry is 3pm for most of the year.

Dates Garden open

The garden centre is free to enter. From 2 November to 5 February it opens Monday to Saturday 9am to 4.30pm and Sunday 10.30am to 4.30pm.

  • Book ahead: it's cheaper than paying on the day.
  • Car-free: show proof of how you travelled on arrival.
  • £1 tickets: for people receiving Universal Credit, Pension Credit or ESA, for up to 6 in the party, with proof on arrival.
  • Wheelchairs and mobility scooters can be booked online.
  • Dogs: the RHS welcomes guide, assistance and service dogs. Check the RHS website for other dogs before you bring one.

RHS Glow at Christmas

RHS Glow runs from 26 November to 30 December 2026, with standard tickets from £9.75 for a child and £17 for an adult, as listed by Visit Manchester. It's ticketed separately from daytime entry. Our Christmas light trails guide compares it with Heaton Park and Dunham Massey.

Tips for a winter visit

  • Go early. Daylight is short and the garden closes at 3.30pm in December.
  • Wear boots. Paths away from the main routes can be wet.
  • Combine it with Ordsall Hall and Salford Museum on another day for a Salford weekend.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is RHS Bridgewater open on Christmas Day?

No. The garden is open all year except Christmas Day.

How much are RHS Bridgewater tickets?

Adults from £13.60 booked online in advance, or £18.40 on the day. Children aged 5 to 16 are £6, under-5s are free, and RHS members get in free.

Is there a discount for coming by bus?

Yes. The RHS says car-free visitors (bus, train, bike or on foot) pay £12.90 for an adult ticket, with proof shown on arrival.

What time is last entry in winter?

Last entry is 3pm for most of the year, except in early March.
