New Year's Eve in Manchester, 2026

Planning New Year's Eve 2026 in Manchester? What the city ran last year in St Peter's Square and Albert Square, what to check for 2026, family-friendly ideas and homes to stay in.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

For New Year's Eve 2025, Manchester City Council ran a free, alcohol-free fireworks and light event in St Peter's Square from 10pm to 12.30am, with fireworks at midnight, and a separate event in Albert Square with a 50-metre Ferris wheel. Bags had to be A4 or smaller and entry closed when the square was full. As of 25 November 2026 we have not seen the council's plans for 31 December 2026, so check its New Year's Eve page before you travel.

New Year's Eve is one of the busiest nights of the year in Manchester. This guide sets out what the city ran last year, what is not confirmed yet for 2026, and how to plan a calm night if you're travelling with family or a group. We checked the sources on 25 November 2026.

What happened last year (31 December 2025)

Manchester City Council ran two city centre events to welcome in 2026:

  • St Peter's Square: a free, family-friendly and alcohol-free fireworks and light event, 10pm to 12.30am, with fireworks at midnight. There was a DJ set hosted by BBC Radio Manchester and two food stalls.
  • Albert Square: open 10am to 12.30am, with food, drink, fairground-style rides including a 50-metre Ferris wheel and craft stalls.
  • Entry rules: bags no larger than A4, with bag searches. Entry to St Peter's Square closed once it was full, and there was no direct walk-through between the two squares.

What we don't know yet for 2026

On 25 November 2026 we could not find the council's plans for 31 December 2026. Don't assume last year's format will repeat. Check the council's New Year's Eve in Manchester page and Visit Manchester's New Year guide before you book anything around it.

Ticketed nights

Visit Manchester's New Year guide lists plenty of paid options each year, from rooftop bars and hotel gala dinners to candlelit concerts at Manchester Cathedral and club nights. These sell out, and prices are often higher on the night itself, so book early if you have one in mind.

Family-friendly ideas

  • An early night out: some families do an early dinner and a Christmas show, then watch the midnight fireworks on TV at home. Our panto guide lists shows that run into the new year.
  • New Year's Day: a walk in Heaton Park or a museum visit works well. Check opening first, as many venues close on 1 January. See our January and February guide.

Getting home

  • Trams and trains are busy after midnight and late services vary each year. Check the Bee Network app or TfGM for New Year's Eve times on the day.
  • Taxis are hard to get just after midnight. Agree a meeting point and a plan before you go into a crowd.
  • Our Greater Manchester transport guide covers trams, buses and fares.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are there fireworks in Manchester on New Year's Eve 2026?

The council held free fireworks in St Peter's Square for New Year's Eve 2025. We have not seen 2026 plans yet, so check the council's New Year's Eve in Manchester page nearer the date.

Was the St Peter's Square event free?

Yes. Last year it was free, family-friendly and alcohol-free, from 10pm to 12.30am with fireworks at midnight. Entry closed once the square reached capacity.

Can I take a bag to the Manchester NYE fireworks?

Last year bags had to be no larger than A4 and there were bag searches. Check the rules for 2026 before you go.

Is it better to stay outside the city centre on New Year's Eve?

It can be. City centre rooms are in high demand that night. A home on a tram line, such as Prestwich on the Bury line, lets you get into town and back without a taxi queue, as long as you check the late tram times first.
