What Is Serviced Accommodation? A Plain-English Guide

Serviced accommodation explained: what's included, how it differs from a hotel, an Airbnb and corporate housing, and what to check before booking in Liverpool.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Serviced accommodation is a furnished, self-contained home or apartment let for short or medium stays, with utilities, wifi, bedding and cleaning included in the price. Unlike a hotel room, you get your own kitchen and living space. It's popular with business travellers, contractors, relocating families and insurance stays, and in Liverpool it ranges from city-centre flats to whole houses with parking.

Serviced accommodation is a furnished, self-contained home or apartment that you book like a hotel, with utilities, wifi, bedding and cleaning included. The difference from a hotel room is space: you get your own kitchen, living area and often several bedrooms. It's widely used by business travellers, contractors, relocating families and people placed by insurers after a flood or fire.

This guide explains the term in plain English and what to check before you book one in Liverpool.

A working definition

The Valuation Office Agency, which values property for business rates, describes serviced apartments as self-contained accommodation that is "fully serviced in terms of services, cleaning and provision of utilities". It notes they grew from hotel users wanting more independence, and that they usually aim at business users wanting short-term, good-quality accommodation.

In practice, "serviced accommodation" in the UK also covers whole houses run the same way.

What's usually included

  • Furniture and a full kitchen: pots, pans, crockery, oven, fridge
  • Bedding and towels
  • Utilities and wifi in the price
  • Cleaning between stays, and sometimes during longer ones
  • Self check-in or a host on call, rather than a reception desk

Serviced accommodation vs Airbnb

The line is blurry. Many serviced operators list on Airbnb and Booking.com. The difference is how the place is run: a serviced operator usually expects business guests, issues invoices, takes longer stays and keeps a consistent standard. When you're booking for work, ask those questions whatever the platform.

Who uses it

  • Contractors and crews on a job for weeks
  • Business travellers who want to cook and spread out
  • Relocating families waiting for a rental or a purchase
  • Insurance stays when a home is unliveable
  • Hospital visitors and NHS staff on placements

What to check before you book

  1. Where is it? City-centre flats rarely have free parking. See our serviced accommodation with parking guide
  2. Beds, not just bedrooms. Check how many people it sleeps and how
  3. Laundry: a washer and dryer matter on longer stays
  4. Cleaning: between stays only, or mid-stay too?
  5. Invoices and VAT: HMRC has specific VAT rules for holiday and serviced accommodation, and not every host is VAT registered. See our invoice and VAT guide
  6. Minimum and maximum stay, and monthly rates
  7. House rules: parties, smoking, pets

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does serviced accommodation mean?

It means a furnished, self-contained apartment or house you book like a hotel, with services such as cleaning, utilities and wifi included in the price. The Valuation Office Agency describes serviced apartments as self-contained accommodation that is fully serviced in terms of services, cleaning and utilities.

Is serviced accommodation the same as an Airbnb?

They overlap. Many serviced accommodation providers list on Airbnb and Booking.com, and some Airbnb homes are run like serviced accommodation. The difference is mostly in how they're run: serviced operators usually aim at business stays, with invoices, longer stays and consistent standards.

Is serviced accommodation cheaper than a hotel?

Often, per person, for groups and longer stays, because you share one property and can cook. For one person for one night, a hotel can be cheaper. Compare the total cost, including parking and meals.

Does serviced accommodation include cleaning?

Cleaning between stays is standard. Mid-stay cleans vary, from daily to weekly to on request, so ask before you book.

Is there a trade body for serviced apartments?

Yes. The Association of Serviced Apartment Providers (ASAP) is the not-for-profit trade body for the sector.
