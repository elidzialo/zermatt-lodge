Kosher Accommodation in Prestwich and Whitefield

Staying in Prestwich or Whitefield? What to ask a host, how to find kosher food from the official licensee list, tram links and who to contact for shul details.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

Prestwich and Whitefield are in the borough of Bury, on the Metrolink Bury line. For shul, mikvah and eruv details, contact the local community or the Manchester Beth Din (MBD) directly. Prestwich and Whitefield are in the borough of Bury, just north of Salford. If you're coming for Shabbat or a simcha, the key is to book within walking distance of the shul you'll use. Our kosher homes page shows homes where hosts have declared kosher kitchens and Shabbat set-ups.

Kosher food

The Manchester Beth Din publishes an official licensee list of restaurants, takeaways, grocers, butchers, fishmongers, bakeries and caterers. Use it as your main reference, as businesses open, close and change supervision. Machzikei Hadass and Badatz Igud Rabbonim (KIR) also certify locally.

More detail is in our kosher food guide for Manchester visitors.

Getting around

Prestwich tram stop is on the Metrolink Bury line, between Heaton Park and Besses o' th' Barn. Trams run mostly every 12 minutes, with a 6-minute service between Bury and Manchester at peak times.

Ask your host about parking, especially on a busy weekend.

What to ask a host

  1. Walking time to my shul, in minutes
  2. Kitchen: kosher, or can I kasher, bring my own or use disposables?
  3. Shabbat set-up: urn, hot plate, light timers, no motion sensors, a key rather than a keypad
  4. Beds and bathrooms for the whole family
