Whitefield: A Visitor's Area Guide

Whitefield in the Borough of Bury: Metrolink stops, the M60 and M66 junction, All Saints' Church at Stand, the Irwell Sculpture Trail and whole homes nearby in Prestwich.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Whitefield is a town in the Metropolitan Borough of Bury, about 5 miles north-west of Manchester city centre and 3 miles south-east of Bury. It has two Metrolink stops on the Bury line, Whitefield and Besses o' th' Barn, and sits right by the junction of the M60 and M66 motorways. For a whole home with a kitchen and parking, Book Homes has houses in neighbouring Prestwich.

Whitefield is the next town up the Bury line from Prestwich. Visitors mostly come here for family, for work on sites around the M60, or as a stop between Manchester and Bury. We checked these facts on 24 November 2026.

Where it is

  • Whitefield is in the Metropolitan Borough of Bury, about 3 miles south-east of Bury and 5 miles north-west of Manchester city centre.
  • It sits on the south bank of the River Irwell, with Prestwich and the M60 to the south.
  • Local areas include Besses o' th' Barn, Stand, Park Lane and Hillock.

Getting around

  • Tram: two stops on the Metrolink Bury line, Whitefield and Besses o' th' Barn. Metrolink services on the line began in 1992. Bury line trams run mostly every 12 minutes, with a 6-minute service between Bury and Manchester at peak times.
  • Driving: Whitefield lies on the west side of the junction of the M60 and M66. That makes it handy for the ring road, Bury, Rochdale and the route north into Lancashire.
  • Buses: use the Bee Network app to plan bus trips.

Things to see

  • All Saints' Church, Stand was designed by Sir Charles Barry and has a tower of about 57 metres. It is the centre of the All Saints' conservation area.
  • The Irwell Sculpture Trail and a local nature trail pass through the area.
  • Philips Park, Bury's largest local nature reserve, is close by on the Prestwich side, with woodland walks, ponds and a mountain bike trail.
  • Heaton Park is a short tram ride south. See our Heaton Park guide.
