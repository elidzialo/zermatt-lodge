East Lancashire Railway Santa Specials 2026: A Family Planning Guide

ELR Santa Specials 2026 from Bury Bolton Street: every date from 28 November to Christmas Eve, from-prices, what's included, the Heywood shuttle and homes nearby in Prestwich.

ZBy Book Homes, hosts of Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool

Quick answer

The East Lancashire Railway's 2026 Santa Specials run on 28 and 29 November, 5 and 6, 12 and 13, and 19 to 24 December. Steam trains start and finish at Bury Bolton Street, with a ride to Rawtenstall and back of about 80 minutes and about 1 hour 30 minutes for the whole experience. Adult fares start from £25 and child fares from £24.50 on the first weekend, rising to £41.50 and £41 on 20 and 24 December. Every child gets a gift.

A steam train, a brass band and a visit to Santa is hard to beat for young children. The East Lancashire Railway (ELR) runs its Santa Specials from Bury, at the top of the Metrolink Bury line. We took the details below from the ELR's own page on 24 November 2026. Popular dates sell out, so book early.

A £2.50 booking fee applies per order. The ELR offers Pay in 3 through PayPal. British Sign Language interpreters are available on 28 and 29 November and 5 and 22 December, with limited places; contact the railway to ask.

What's included

  • A winter wonderland walkthrough at Bury Bolton Street station.
  • Time with festive characters, including elves, Mrs Claus and the Ice Princess.
  • The steam train ride from Bury to Rawtenstall and back, about 80 minutes.
  • A visit from Santa, with time for photos.
  • A gift for each child, chosen by age band, and an activity book.
  • A brass band on board.

Allow about 1 hour 30 minutes for the whole experience. Passengers stay on the train throughout.

Getting there

  • Station: Bury Bolton Street, BL9 0EY, in Bury town centre.
  • Tram: the ELR recommends the Metrolink from Manchester. Bury is the last stop on the Bury line.
  • Parking: limited and charged at the station.
  • Santa Shuttle: a diesel railcar from Heywood, which has free parking, takes about 15 minutes to Bury. Shuttle tickets are open return and booked separately. It doesn't run on 24 December.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When are the ELR Santa Specials in 2026?

Saturday and Sunday 28 and 29 November, 5 and 6 December and 12 and 13 December, then every day from Saturday 19 to Thursday 24 December (Christmas Eve).

How long is the Santa Special?

The steam ride from Bury to Rawtenstall and back takes about 80 minutes. The full experience from Bury Bolton Street is about 1 hour 30 minutes. Passengers stay on the train throughout.

How much are ELR Santa Special tickets?

From £25 adult and £24.50 child on 28 and 29 November, up to £41.50 adult and £41 child on 20 and 24 December. An adult with an under-3 on their knee starts from £39. A £2.50 booking fee applies per order.

Is there parking for the Santa Specials?

Parking at Bury Bolton Street is limited and charged. The railway runs a Santa Shuttle diesel railcar from Heywood, which has free parking, taking about 15 minutes. The shuttle doesn't run on 24 December, and its tickets are booked separately.
