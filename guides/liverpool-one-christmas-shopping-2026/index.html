Liverpool ONE at Christmas 2026: What's On and How to Plan It

Planning a Christmas shopping trip to Liverpool ONE in 2026: the new 30-metre walk-through tree at Paradise Place, curling at Chavasse Park until 3 January 2027, Christmas Day closures and tips for groups.

BBy Book Homes, Liverpool guides

Quick answer

Liverpool ONE is the city's main open-air shopping district. For Christmas 2026 it has a new 30-metre Christmas tree you can walk through at Paradise Place, and Stone's Throw Social curling at Chavasse Park from 3 October 2026 to 3 January 2027. Large shops in England can't open on Christmas Day by law, so Friday 25 December 2026 is a closed day for shopping.

Liverpool ONE is the big open-air shopping district in the city centre, between the main shopping streets and the waterfront. At Christmas it's a full day out: shops, food, a new walk-through tree and curling in the park.

We checked these details on 25 November 2026. Opening hours over Christmas are usually announced closer to the time, so check Liverpool ONE's website before you go.

Christmas 2026 at Liverpool ONE

  • The tree: a new 30-metre Christmas tree at Paradise Place that you can walk through the middle of. The new decorations went up from Friday 6 November.
  • Curling: Stone's Throw Social at Chavasse Park, from 3 October 2026 to 3 January 2027. It has eight ice-free curling lanes, plus karaoke huts and shuffleboard tables. Pre-booking and packages are available, and walk-ins are welcome.

The Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004 stops shops over 280 square metres opening on Christmas Day in England and Wales.

Tips for a Christmas shopping trip

  • Go on a weekday morning if you can. Weekends in December are the busiest time.
  • Take the train. Liverpool Central is close by. See our getting around Liverpool guide.
  • Make a day of it with the Christmas markets and lights. See our Christmas markets guide and Christmas lights and grotto guide.
  • Plan your Christmas food shop separately. If you're self-catering, our Christmas dinner in a holiday house guide has a shopping timetable.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's on at Liverpool ONE for Christmas 2026?

A new 30-metre walk-through Christmas tree at Paradise Place, with decorations up from 6 November, and Stone's Throw Social curling at Chavasse Park from 3 October 2026 to 3 January 2027.

Is Liverpool ONE open on Christmas Day?

Expect it to be closed. The Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004 stops shops over 280 square metres opening on Christmas Day in England and Wales.

Do you need to book curling at Liverpool ONE?

Visit Liverpool says packages and pre-booking are available, as well as walk-ins on the day. Booking is safer at busy times.

What day is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day is Friday 25 December 2026, and Boxing Day is Saturday 26 December.
