The Liverpool City Region Freeport explained

The word "freeport" gets used loosely, so it helps to know what's where.

Tax sites

The government's freeport maps name three tax sites, designated with effect from 22 March 2022:

Tax site Where Notes from the combined authority
Parkside St Helens Large strategic employment site near the M6 and M62, aimed at advanced manufacturing and logistics
3MG Widnes Motorway and West Coast Main Line rail access, involved in the hydrogen economy
Wirral Waters Birkenhead Built around Birkenhead Docks, with port connectivity and M53 access

The Liverpool City Region Combined Authority says the freeport became operational in December 2022, covers a 45km area from Wirral Waters to Port Salford, and is estimated to add £850m to the local economy. Its focus sectors include advanced manufacturing, pharmaceuticals and green energy.

The Port of Liverpool and Seaforth

Seaforth Dock, at the north end of Liverpool's dock system, is described as part of the Port of Liverpool and Liverpool Freeport. It's home to the port's container terminals, including Liverpool2, the extension that opened in November 2016. Peel Ports describes Liverpool2 as a deep-water container terminal and says the terminals run round-the-clock operations.

The dock also handles containers, oils, timber, grain and animal feed, and is served by the Canada Dock freight rail branch.

Who ends up needing a work stay?

Port and freeport projects bring in people from well outside Merseyside, often at short notice:

  • Construction and fit-out crews building or adapting units on the tax sites
  • Engineers and technicians maintaining cranes, terminal equipment and rail sidings
  • Shutdown and maintenance teams working fixed windows
  • Consultants, surveyors and auditors on multi-day assignments

Most need the same things: bedrooms for everyone, parking for vehicles and a kitchen that copes with early starts.

Which base suits which site?

Be realistic about travel. The city region is big, and the M62, M57 and Mersey tunnels all add time at peak hours.

  • Seaforth, the north docks and Bootle: north Liverpool is a good fit. Seaforth is reached via the A5036 and A565. Switch Island (M57 junction 7) is where the M57 and M58 meet the A5036 towards Bootle
  • Parkside, St Helens: consider a base nearer St Helens or the M62. See Haydock and St Helens work stays
  • 3MG, Widnes: further south and east. A north Liverpool base works for a short job but means a longer daily drive
  • Wirral Waters, Birkenhead: across the river. You'll pay a Mersey tunnel toll each way. See our Mersey tunnel tolls guide

Check live drive times at the hours you'll actually travel, especially for early and late shifts.

Practical tips for port and logistics work stays

Shift patterns

Port operations run around the clock. If your crew works nights or rotating shifts:

  • Give day sleepers the quietest bedrooms
  • Agree kitchen times so cooking doesn't wake anyone
  • Blackout blinds or an eye mask help in summer

Our guide to night shift sleep tips has more.

Site access

Ports are secure sites. Before day one, confirm with your client:

  • Which gate or entrance you should use
  • What ID and site inductions are required
  • What PPE you need
  • Whether there's parking on site for contractors, or whether you need to share vehicles

Arriving at the wrong gate with the wrong paperwork can cost you a morning.

Vehicles

Zermatt Lodge has free parking for two vehicles. We don't promise that every van or high-top will fit, so if you're bringing larger vehicles or more than two, call before booking. See van parking accommodation in Liverpool.

Food and laundry

A crew of five or six eating out every night gets expensive. A big kitchen and dining room let you batch-cook. There's a Lidl close to the house for a weekly shop. See self-catering for work crews.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where are the Liverpool City Region Freeport tax sites?

The government lists three: Parkside in St Helens, 3MG in Widnes and Wirral Waters in Birkenhead. They were designated with effect from 22 March 2022.

Is the Port of Liverpool part of the freeport?

Seaforth Dock, home of the Port of Liverpool's container terminals including Liverpool2, is described as part of the Port of Liverpool and Liverpool Freeport. The combined authority describes the freeport as covering a 45km area from Wirral Waters to Port Salford.

Is north Liverpool a good base for port work?

For Seaforth and the north docks, yes. They're on the north side of the city, reached via the A5036 and the A565. For Parkside, 3MG or Wirral Waters, a base closer to those sites may suit you better.

Can a crew of 7 stay together?
