About this home

Welcome to Zermatt Lodge, a spacious private luxury 4-bed home in Liverpool with room for everyone. Enjoy the games room with pool table, Netflix on the 55" Smart TV, a big kitchen, dining room and large private gardens. Free parking for 2 cars, minutes from the M57/M58, Aintree Hospital, the train station and Lidl, with city nightlife a short ride away.

Ideal for families, groups of friends or work crews who want room to spread out without paying for multiple hotel rooms. Two bathrooms means no queue for the shower.

Come back after a day of exploring to a warm, welcoming space where you can relax together, play some games, cook a meal, or unwind in your own room.

Every stay comes with fresh towels and luxurious, hotel-quality bedding. Our cleaning crew makes sure the house is spotless for every stay, and our local team personally inspects the property before each arrival.

Our property management team is available 24/7 by email or phone if you need anything during your stay.
