## Method for distances

- Reference point: **Fazakerley railway station** (53°28′08″N 2°56′12″W = 53.4689, -2.9367) - source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fazakerley_railway_station . We do not publish the house address, so all distances are "from the Fazakerley area", not from the front door.
- Distances below are **straight-line (haversine) distances** calculated from that point to each destination's published coordinates. Road distance is normally 20-40% longer. Drive times are **estimates only [UNVERIFIED]** - check in Google Maps from the real address before publishing, and re-check at peak times.
- Rail times come from Trainline route pages (they vary by service; fastest and typical shown).
- Copy rule: say "about", round up, and never claim "minutes away" for anything not checked from the real address.

## Rail (Merseyrail)

- **Fazakerley station**: Northern line, Headbolt Lane branch (via Kirkby). Usually 4 trains/hour each way off-peak, 2/hour late evenings and Sundays. Next stop towards town: Rice Lane. Cycle racks (4) + secure storage (20); no car park listed. - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fazakerley_railway_station
- Fazakerley to Liverpool Central: ~15 min direct, ~69 trains/day, first 04:46, last 23:36 (sample day) - https://www.thetrainline.com/train-times/fazakerley-to-liverpool-central
- **Aintree station**: Northern line, Ormskirk branch. Every 15 min Mon-Sat, 30 min evenings/Sundays. 104-space car park, step-free. 6-car trains and extra services on Grand National days. - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aintree_railway_station
- Aintree to Liverpool Central: ~17-18 min, ~62 trains/day - https://www.thetrainline.com/train-times/aintree-to-liverpool-central
- Moorfields and Liverpool Central are on all Northern line routes and interchange with the Wirral line. Sandhills links to SoccerBus for Anfield and is walkable to Hill Dickinson Stadium. New Class 777 trains. - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merseyrail
- **Tap & Go**: from 25 Feb 2026 Merseyrail accepts contactless bank cards, watches and phones - tap in and tap out (gates or platform validators). Fares frozen until March 2027. - https://www.merseytravel.gov.uk/news/new-easier-to-use-era-starts-on-merseyrail-as-tap-and-go-extended-to-bank-cards-watches-and-smartphones/
