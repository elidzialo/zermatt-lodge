Getting the whole family in one place doesn't happen often. Whether it's cousins flying in from abroad, a big birthday or simply time together, Liverpool works well: it's easy to reach, has plenty to do for every age, and many of the best things are free.

We checked the details below on 23 November 2026.

## 1. Pick the right dates

- **Avoid the busiest weekends.** The **Grand National Festival runs 8 to 10 April 2027**, and big match days at Anfield and Hill Dickinson Stadium also push prices up
- **Think about school holidays.** Families with school-age children can only travel in holidays, which are also the busiest times
- **Two or three nights** is usually right. One night is rushed, and longer is hard to fit around everyone's work
- **Check our monthly guides** for [December](/guides/liverpool-december-2026-events), [January](/guides/liverpool-january-2027-events) and [February](/guides/liverpool-february-2027-events)

## 2. Choose a house, not hotel rooms

A reunion is about being together. In a hotel, everyone disappears to separate rooms. In a **whole house**, you share breakfast, sit round one table at dinner and stay up talking.

Things to check:

1. **Beds:** how many real beds, and who needs their own room (grandparents, young families)
2. **Bathrooms:** two bathrooms make a big difference for 6 or 7 people
3. **Stairs:** check bedroom layout if anyone has mobility needs. See our [accessible group stay questions](/guides/accessible-group-stay-liverpool-questions)
4. **Parking:** family groups often arrive in several cars
5. **Kitchen and dining table:** big enough for everyone to eat together

If the family is bigger than one house sleeps, book two homes near each other. Our [group booking checklist](/guides/group-booking-checklist) has more.

## 3. Plan meals

- **One big home-cooked dinner** is often the highlight. Our [meal plan for 7](/guides/self-catering-meal-plan-group-of-7) makes it easy
- **Get a supermarket delivery** on arrival day. See our [grocery delivery guide](/guides/grocery-delivery-to-holiday-house)
- **Book one meal out** for the big night, and book early on weekends

## 4. Outings for every age

Keep it simple: one outing a day, then home.

- **The free national museums:** World Museum, the Walker Art Gallery and the Museum of Liverpool. See our [museums guide](/guides/liverpool-museums-guide)
- **Liverpool Cathedral:** free to visit, with a paid Tower Experience
- **The Mersey Ferry:** a 50-minute River Explorer Cruise with commentary, good for grandparents and children alike. See our [ferry guide](/guides/mersey-ferry-cruise-guide)
- **A show:** from November to January there are pantos and Christmas shows across the city

More ideas are in our [free things to do](/guides/liverpool-free-things-to-do) guide.

## 5. Agree house rules up front

Share the host's rules with everyone before you arrive: check-in time, quiet hours, parking and who's sleeping where. It saves arguments on the day.
