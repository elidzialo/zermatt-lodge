England is getting a national register for short-term lets. GOV.UK guidance, updated on 2 October 2026, says the mandatory scheme is expected to begin in March 2027. Here is what is known, what is not, and what to do now.

## What we know

- **It will be mandatory** across England for short-term lets.
- **Expected start:** March 2027.
- **Not for hotels, hostels or B&Bs**, the government has said.
- **Light touch:** the government says it wants the scheme to be "as light touch, low cost and simple to use as possible".
- **Not a planning control.** Clyde and Co notes that the scheme "is not itself a planning control measure". Your council still decides planning.

## What is still undecided

- the fee, if any
- whether hosts who let their home only now and then will be exempt
- what information you will have to give, and how often you renew
- what booking platforms will have to check

Clyde and Co says the government "has not yet published all of the operational details of the scheme". We will update this guide when it does.

## Get ready now

Most of what a register is likely to ask for is what you should already have:

1. **Consents:** lease, landlord, lender and freeholder permission in writing.
2. **Safety:** gas safety record, electrical checks, fire risk assessment and smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, following the [GOV.UK guidance for self-catering hosts](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/letting-out-a-self-catering-holiday-home-in-england-rules-and-regulations).
3. **Insurance:** holiday let cover with public liability.
4. **Records:** a simple log of nights let and nights available, which also tells you whether business rates apply (140 nights available and 70 let in England).
5. **Local rules:** see our guides for [Liverpool](/guides/liverpool-short-let-rules-for-hosts), [Greater Manchester](/guides/manchester-short-let-rules-for-hosts) and [London's 90-night rule](/guides/london-90-night-rule-short-lets).
