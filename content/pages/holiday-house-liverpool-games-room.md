If you want a Liverpool holiday house where the evenings are as good as the days, look for one with a games room. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Fazakerley, north Liverpool, with a games room and pool table, a cupboard of board games and a 55" Smart TV with Netflix. It sleeps 7, has a big kitchen, a dining room and a garden, and there's free parking for two cars. Trains reach the city centre in about 15 minutes.

This page is about making the most of the time you spend at the house, whether that's a rainy afternoon, a post-match evening or a family games night.

## Why a games room makes a group trip

Days out in Liverpool are full on: museums, the waterfront, football, racing, shopping. But the bit people often remember most is the evening when everyone's together. A games room gives you:

- **Something to do without spending money** after a day of paying for everything
- **Mixed-age fun**, so grandparents, parents and teenagers can all play
- **A rainy-day back-up** when the weather turns
- **An alternative to a night out**, especially if you have children, drivers or early starts
- **A bit of friendly competition** that gets people talking

## Pool night ideas

You don't need to be good at pool to have fun with it. Try these formats:

- **Doubles:** pair a confident player with a beginner, and rotate partners each game.
- **Killer:** everyone gets three lives. Pot a ball on your turn or lose a life. It's great with five to seven people.
- **Round-the-table handicap:** better players have to pot with their weaker hand.
- **Family league:** keep a scoreboard on the fridge for the whole stay, with a silly prize at the end.

Our [pool night ideas for families and friends](/guides/pool-table-tournament-ideas) explains the rules simply.

**House tip:** keep drinks on a side table, not the edge of the pool table, and chalk away from the cloth.

## Board game evenings

Board games work for every age and need no screens. A good evening might look like this:

1. **Quick warm-up** with a card or dice game while dinner cooks
2. **Main game** after dinner, at the big dining table
3. **Party-style game** to finish, the kind where teams guess or draw

There are board games at the house, and you're welcome to bring your own favourites. If your group loves games, see our [board game weekend guide](/guides/board-game-weekend-liverpool).

## Pairing days out with nights in

### Rainy day

Morning: take the train from Fazakerley to Liverpool Central, then visit one of the free national museums, such as the Museum of Liverpool or World Museum.
Afternoon: back to the house for pool and a board game.
Evening: a big home-cooked meal and a film.

See [rainy day things to do in Liverpool](/guides/rainy-day-liverpool).

### Match day

Morning: a big breakfast at the house, then the train towards Sandhills.
Afternoon: the match.
Evening: back to watch the late game or highlights on the big TV, with a pool rematch.

### Waterfront day

Morning: the Pier Head and a Mersey ferry River Explorer cruise. Mersey Ferries says it takes about 50 minutes with commentary.
Afternoon: the Royal Albert Dock.
Evening: board games and a takeaway at home.

## Evening ideas for different groups

**Families with young children**
- An early "kids' cup" on the pool table, with a short cue and adult help
- Simple card or picture games at the dining table
- A family film with the lights down, then bed

**Families with teenagers**
- A proper pool league with a scoreboard, where teens usually beat the adults
- Strategy board games after dinner
- Teens choose the film, adults choose the takeaway

**Friends and couples**
- Doubles pool while dinner's in the oven
- A big board game that runs all evening
- The match, the highlights or a series binge on Netflix

**Work crews and contractors**
- A quick game of killer to unwind after site
- The football on the big screen
- Early nights during the week, with a longer pool session on Thursday or Friday

The point is that nobody has to go out, spend money or book a table to have a good evening together.
