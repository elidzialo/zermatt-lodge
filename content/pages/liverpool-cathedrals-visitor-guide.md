Liverpool has **two cathedrals linked by one street**. The Anglican **Liverpool Cathedral** stands at one end of Hope Street and the Catholic **Metropolitan Cathedral of Christ the King** at the other, so you can see both in a single walk. Both are good choices on a cold or wet day.

We checked the details below on 25 November 2026. Cathedrals change their hours for services and events, especially in December, so check before you go.

## Liverpool Cathedral

**Opening hours:** daily, **10am to 6pm**. Last entry is 15 minutes before closing. On Sundays the building is open for worship from 10am to 12pm, and the cathedral suggests arriving from 12pm for the full visitor experience.

**Entry:** you don't need to book and entry is **usually free**. Some special events, such as organ recitals, may charge or ask for a donation.

**Shop, bistro and tower:**

| | Monday to Saturday | Sunday |
|---|---|---|
| Shop | 10am to 5pm | 12pm to 5pm |
| Bistro | 10am to 4.30pm | 12pm to 4.30pm |
| Tower (last entry) | 4.30pm | 4.30pm |

The tower closes 30 minutes after last entry.

### Christmas period 2026

For the **Noël period, 25 November to 5 December 2026**:

- **Wed 25 November:** closes at 4pm for filming
- **From Thu 26 November:** closes at **3pm**, with last tower entry at **2.30pm**
- Services still take place in the Lady Chapel

### The Cathedral Tower Experience

| Ticket | Price |
|---|---|
| Standard | £8 |
| Concession | £6.50 |
| Family (2 adults, up to 3 children) | £22.50 |

You go up in **two lifts and then 108 stairs**. The cathedral advises care if you have asthma or other chest conditions, heart problems or are pregnant. Visitors who can't manage the climb can see the view through a camera facility. The tower is closed while the bells are being rung.

## The Metropolitan Cathedral

The **Metropolitan Cathedral of Christ the King** is a striking circular modernist building with a stained-glass lantern tower. Creative Tourist lists it as **open daily, 7.30am to 6pm**.

Under it is the **Lutyens Crypt**. Work on Sir Edwin Lutyens' huge original design began in 1933, but rising costs meant only the crypt was built. Sir Frederick Gibberd's design was later finished in under five years.

## A Hope Street walk

1. Start at **Liverpool Cathedral** and go up the tower if the weather is clear.
2. Walk along **Hope Street**, past the **Philharmonic Hall** and the **Everyman Theatre**.
3. Finish at the **Metropolitan Cathedral**.
