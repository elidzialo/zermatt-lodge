For a golf group trip to Liverpool, base yourselves somewhere between the Wirral and the Sefton coast, with parking for two cars, and you can play several of England's best-known links courses without changing hotels. Royal Liverpool at Hoylake, Royal Birkdale, Hillside and Formby are all within reach of north Liverpool by car, though each is a drive rather than a hop, so check live routes for your tee times.

This guide covers the courses, how to book them, getting around with clubs, and how to plan the days.

## The courses

Visit Liverpool markets the coast from the Wirral up to Southport as a golf coast. Here's what it highlights.

### Royal Liverpool (Hoylake)

On the Wirral, at the mouth of the Dee. Visit Liverpool describes it as one of the oldest seaside courses in England. It has hosted The Open many times, most recently in **2023**, and Tiger Woods won here in 2006. From Liverpool you reach it through one of the Mersey tunnels.

### Royal Birkdale (Southport)

Royal Birkdale was formed in 1889 as a nine-hole course, and Visit Liverpool says it's currently ranked first in England. It was named as host of **The Open in 2026**. See our [Open Championship guide](/guides/the-open-golf-liverpool-stay) for future championship visits.

### Hillside (Southport)

In Southport, close to Birkdale. Visit Liverpool describes a 7,000-yard course of sandhills and pinewoods, founded in 1911.

### Formby

A classic coastal course where pine trees shelter several holes from the wind.

### More on the coast

Southport and Ainsdale, West Lancashire and others line the same stretch. Our [Sefton coast golf courses guide](/guides/sefton-coast-golf-courses) covers them in more detail.

## Booking the big clubs

The championship clubs run tight booking systems. Royal Birkdale's visitor terms are a good example:

- A provisional enquiry is held for **14 days**.
- **Full payment is due 60 days before play.** Inside 60 days, payments are non-refundable and tee times can't be moved.
- Groups can **reduce numbers without penalty up to 60 days** before play.
- Bookings of fewer than four may occasionally be combined with others.
- **Caddies, buggies and trolleys** can be arranged in advance.
- From **1 November to 31 March**, winter maintenance may mean hole closures or a shorter course.

Other clubs have their own rules. Many ask visitors about handicaps, dress codes and the days visitors can play. Ask each club directly when you book, and get everything confirmed in writing.

### Booking tips

- **Book the headline course first**, then build the rest of the trip around it.
- **Mix it up.** One or two championship rounds plus a couple of more relaxed courses keeps costs and tired legs in check.
- **Leave a spare half-day** for weather. Links golf in a strong wind is hard work.

## Getting around with clubs

Golf trips here work best by car. You'll be crossing from the Wirral to Southport and back, and nobody wants to carry a full tour bag on a train.

- **Two cars for six to eight golfers** is typical.
- **North to Formby and Southport:** from north Liverpool, the Switch Island interchange at the end of the M57 and M58 connects to the A5758 towards Formby and Southport, and to the A59.
- **South-west to Hoylake:** through a Mersey tunnel into the Wirral. Tunnel tolls apply.
- **Leave early.** Morning traffic around Liverpool varies, and you don't want to rush your warm-up.

## What a golf group needs from accommodation

- **Parking for every car.** Off-street, so you're not unloading clubs onto a busy road.
- **Somewhere to keep clubs and wet shoes.** Ask the host where kit can go.
- **Proper beds.** Golfers sharing a sofa bed after 36 holes don't stay friends for long.
- **Two bathrooms** for a group with the same tee time.
- **A kitchen and dining table** for early breakfasts and relaxed evenings.
