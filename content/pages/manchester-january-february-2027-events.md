The new year is quieter in Manchester than December, which can make it a good time for a family visit or a group weekend: easier dinner bookings and fewer crowds in town. Here's what's on in January and February 2027, checked on 23 November 2026. Always confirm dates on the official sites.

## Early January: the last of Christmas

- **Christmas markets:** Albert Square and Cathedral Gardens stay open until **Sunday 3 January 2027**, according to Visit Manchester.
- **Dick Whittington** at the Opera House: last show **Sunday 3 January**.
- **Billy Elliot the Musical** at the Palace Theatre: until **Saturday 9 January**.
- **Lightroom** at Aviva Studios: Factory International lists its two Lightroom shows until **10 January**.
- **Schools go back** on **Monday 4 January 2027**, according to Manchester City Council.

## Arena shows

- **Jack Whitehall**, Co-op Live: **Saturday 2 January 2027**. Co-op Live says the show moved from 9 January because of filming commitments, and existing tickets are valid for the new date.
- **Donny Osmond**, Co-op Live: **Sunday 24 January 2027**, show at 7pm.

Check the Co-op Live and AO Arena websites for other dates.

## Football

The FA lists the **FA Cup third round** on **Saturday 9 January 2027** and the **fourth round** on **Saturday 13 February 2027**.

Home Premier League dates from the fixture lists published by Sky Sports (these often move for TV, so check the clubs' websites):

- Sat 16 Jan: v Nottingham Forest
- Sat 30 Jan: v Arsenal
- Sat 20 Feb: v Newcastle

**Manchester United (Old Trafford):**

## Lunar New Year

Lunar New Year falls on **Saturday 6 February 2027**, the Year of the Goat, according to Royal Museums Greenwich. Manchester's Chinatown usually celebrates with a parade and events, but Visit Manchester says 2027 plans are still to be announced. Check its Lunar New Year page nearer the time.

## It's a Sin at Aviva Studios

Rambert and Factory International's world premiere of **It's a Sin** runs at Aviva Studios from **Tuesday 16 February** (preview) to **Saturday 27 February 2027**.

## February half term

Manchester City Council lists schools closing on **Friday 12 February** and reopening on **Monday 22 February 2027**. If you're planning a family break, book early: homes with several bedrooms go first.
