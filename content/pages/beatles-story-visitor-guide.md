The Beatles Story is the big Beatles museum on Liverpool's waterfront, inside the Royal Albert Dock. It tells the band's story, and part of the route is a Cavern Club area. It's a good first stop before you head to Mathew Street.

We checked the details below on 24 November 2026. Times and prices can change, so check the official site before you go.

## Tickets

| Ticket | Price |
|---|---|
| Adult (16 and over) | £20 |
| Concession (seniors and students) | £16 |
| Child (5 to 15) | £11 |
| Baby | Free |

- **Book online in advance.** The attraction says tickets must be pre-booked for a date and time. On-site kiosks are card only and sell the next free slot
- **Groups of 10 or more** get a group rate
- **Blue Light Card and Defence Discount** holders get 20% off online
- **Carer tickets** are available with one supporting document
- **Free multimedia guide** in 12 languages comes with your ticket

## Winter and Christmas opening times

From **5 October 2026 to 1 March 2027**:

- **Sunday to Friday:** 9am to 5.30pm (last entry 4.15pm)
- **Saturdays and Liverpool school holidays:** 9am to 6.30pm (last entry 5.15pm)

Over Christmas and New Year:

| Date | Opening |
|---|---|
| Christmas Eve | Entry 10am to 3pm |
| Christmas Day | Closed |
| Boxing Day | Closed |
| New Year's Eve | Entry 10am to 4pm |
| New Year's Day | Entry 10am to 4pm |

From **2 March 2027** it opens 9am to 6.30pm, last entry 5pm.

## How long to allow

The attraction says most people spend about **1.5 hours**, and up to 3 hours if you read and listen to everything. Your ticket also covers the **Discovery Zone**, which has free children's workshops, plus the Fab4 Stores and Fab4 Cafe.

## Access

When we checked, the attraction said its **entrance lift was out of order**, so the exhibition was reached by steps only. If anyone in your group needs step-free access, check the lift status page on the official site before you book. Hearing loops and large-print guides are available on request.

## Getting there and parking

The Beatles Story has **no parking of its own**. It suggests the car park opposite the Albert Dock entrance, the arena car park and Q-Park Liverpool ONE. Coaches can drop off on Salthouse Quay or Gower Street.

Our [Royal Albert Dock guide](/guides/royal-albert-dock-guide) covers the rest of the dock, including what's open and what's closed for redevelopment.

## What changes in 2027 and 2028

The Beatles Story says it will **stay at the Royal Albert Dock until 2028**. Apple Corps, the Beatles' company, plans to take over the attraction in 2027, and a new attraction is planned for St Johns Shopping Centre in 2028, subject to approval. If you're visiting this winter, nothing changes for you.

## Make a Beatles day of it

1. **Morning:** The Beatles Story at the Albert Dock
2. **Lunch:** around the dock or in Liverpool ONE
3. **Afternoon:** walk into the city centre to Mathew Street and the [Cavern Club](/guides/cavern-club-visitor-guide), where live music starts at 11.15am every day
4. **Evening:** back to your base for dinner
