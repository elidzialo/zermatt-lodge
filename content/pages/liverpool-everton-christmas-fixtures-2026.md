**Liverpool are not at home on Boxing Day 2026**: they're away at Hull City at 5.30pm. Their festive home games at Anfield are **Sunderland (Wed 2 Dec), Porto (Wed 9 Dec, Champions League), Leeds (Sat 12 Dec), Tottenham (Sat 19 Dec) and Coventry (Sat 2 Jan 2027)**. **Everton** are at home on Boxing Day to Sunderland, then host Manchester City on Tuesday 29 December and Aston Villa on Tuesday 5 January.

If you searched for "Boxing Day at Anfield", this is the key point: the Boxing Day football in Liverpool this year is at **Hill Dickinson Stadium**, not Anfield.

All dates and times below were checked on 19 November 2026. Premier League games can be moved for TV, sometimes only a few weeks before, so confirm with the club before you book travel.

## Tickets

Liverpool home tickets are sold through the club's membership schemes and are very hard to get for most games. Everton tickets are sometimes easier. Always buy through official club channels. Our [LFC and Everton ticket guide](/guides/lfc-and-everton-ticket-guide) explains the options.

## Getting to the grounds

### Hill Dickinson Stadium (Everton)

Sandhills is the usual station, on the Merseyrail Northern line. Our [Everton stadium guide](/guides/everton-stadium-accommodation) has the walking route.

### Anfield (Liverpool)

There's no station right next to Anfield. Take the train to Sandhills and use the matchday Soccerbus, or walk in from Kirkdale. Our [Anfield match day guide](/guides/anfield-match-day-accommodation) has the details. If you're driving, read our [match day parking guide](/guides/liverpool-match-day-parking-guide), as parking is restricted around the ground.

### Festive transport

The 2026 festive timetables hadn't been published when we checked. In past years, Merseyrail hasn't run on Christmas Day and has run a reduced service on Boxing Day. Check merseyrail.org before Boxing Day travel.
