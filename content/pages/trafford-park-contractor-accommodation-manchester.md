Trafford Park is one of the biggest employment areas in the North West, with factories, warehouses, distribution centres and offices. Shutdowns, fit-outs and long installs bring crews in from all over the country. We checked these facts on 24 November 2026.

## Trafford Park at a glance

- About **4.7 square miles** (12 square km) on the **south side of the Manchester Ship Canal**, opposite Salford Quays.
- About **3.4 miles south-west** of Manchester city centre.
- As of 2025, about **1,400 companies** employed around **40,000 people** in the park.
- It is often described as the **first planned industrial estate in the world**.

## Getting there

- **Tram:** the **Trafford Park line** opened in **March 2020**. It runs from **Pomona** to the **Trafford Centre**, stopping at **Wharfside, Imperial War Museum, Village, Parkway and Barton Dock Road**.
- **Bus:** buses **250** and **X50** link the Trafford Centre with Manchester city centre.
- **Driving:** most crews drive, especially with tools. Check site parking and any permit rules with your client before you arrive.

## Where to base a crew

Book Homes has homes north and west of the city:

- **Crumpsall:** a **5-bedroom house** with **free parking** and a pool table for downtime.
- **Prestwich:** several **3 and 4-bedroom houses**, some with **free parking**.
- **Salford:** an apartment handy for MediaCityUK and the Quays.

Drive times vary a lot with M60 traffic at shift change, so check a route planner for your start time.

## Before you book

1. **Count beds, not bedrooms.** Make sure each worker has a proper bed.
2. **Vehicles.** Confirm how many vans and cars can park.
3. **Kitchen and washer.** See our [self-catering guide for work crews](/guides/self-catering-for-work-crews).
4. **Invoices.** Ask for an invoice in your company's name.
5. **House rules.** Homes on Book Homes are in residential areas: strictly no parties or events - bookings found to be for parties are cancelled.
