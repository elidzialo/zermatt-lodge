"Short-term let" covers furnished homes booked by the night: holiday homes, serviced apartments and whole houses. For contractors, it is often the easiest way to keep a crew together near a job. Here is how it works on Book Homes.

## What you are booking

A whole, furnished home for set dates. The crew has the place to themselves: bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen and living room. Bills, Wi-Fi and cleaning are included in the price. You are guests, not tenants, so there is no tenancy agreement.

## How the price works

- The price at checkout is the **total for the stay**, including cleaning.
- There is **no guest booking fee**.
- Some hosts set a **weekly discount** (7 nights or more) or a **monthly discount** (28 nights or more). It is taken off automatically and shown before you pay.

## How long you can stay

Each host sets a minimum and a maximum number of nights. Many allow stays of around a month. If the job will run longer, ask the host early about the following dates.

## Paperwork for the company

On most homes you can tick a box at checkout and add your company name for a **company receipt (PDF)**. If you need a full VAT invoice, ask the host before booking. Our [invoices and VAT guide](/guides/invoice-and-vat-for-business-stays) explains what to ask for. HMRC's VAT Notice 709/3 treats houses and flats let for holiday or leisure use as holiday accommodation for VAT.

## Cancellation

Each listing shows its policy (Flexible, Moderate or Strict) before you pay. If the host cancels a confirmed booking, you get a full refund.

## Rules of the house

Short-term lets are usually on residential streets. Crews need to keep noise down late at night, park considerately and follow the host's rules on smoking and bins. Parties and events are not allowed. Our [house share etiquette guide](/guides/trades-team-house-share-etiquette) is worth sharing with the team.
