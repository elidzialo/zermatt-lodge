Crosby is a coastal town just north of Liverpool, best known for Antony Gormley's Another Place: 100 cast iron figures standing along about 2 miles of Crosby Beach. Together with Waterloo and Blundellsands, it has a coastal park, a watersports lake, a village high street and a good choice of places to eat on South Road in Waterloo. It's on Merseyrail's Southport line, so it's easy to reach from Liverpool city centre, and it's a popular place for families to move to. This guide covers what to see, how to get there and what it's like to live in.

## Where Crosby is

Crosby is in the borough of **Sefton**, between Bootle to the south and Formby to the north. Visit Southport describes Waterloo and Crosby as the start of the **22-mile coastline** that runs up to Southport.

The area is usually thought of as several neighbourhoods:

- **Waterloo:** the southern end, with South Road's bars and restaurants and the marine lake
- **Crosby village:** the main shopping centre, inland
- **Blundellsands:** quieter residential streets near the beach
- **Hall Road:** the northern end of the coastal park, with its own station

## Things to do

### Another Place (the Iron Men)

Antony Gormley's **Another Place** is made up of **100 cast iron figures**, all facing out to sea, spread across more than 3km of beach. You can see them from the promenade, which is the safest way to enjoy them.

Sefton Council's safety advice:

- **Don't walk out to the furthest figures.** The sand and mud there are very soft.
- **Check the tide.** At a high tide of around 9 to 10 metres, the figures are underwater for about an hour either side of high water and you can't get onto the beach. At a 4-metre tide or lower, you'll see all of them.
- **Follow warning signs** and any instructions from lifeguards, coastguards and rangers.

Late afternoon and sunset are popular times for photos, with views across to North Wales.

### Crosby Coastal Park

The coastal park runs along the shore. Sefton Council lists:

- A **children's play area**
- The **promenade**, coastal footpath and **cycleway**
- **Crosby Lakeside Adventure Centre**
- **The Bus Yard**, an outdoor bar and street food spot with sea views

It's also a protected wildlife area, part of the Sefton Coast Special Area of Conservation.

### Crosby Lakeside

Crosby Lakeside Adventure Centre, at the Waterloo end of the coastal park, is a watersports and leisure centre. Visit Southport describes a **24-hectare lake** for watersports and adventure activities, and the centre also has a gym and fitness studios. The **Curlew Cycle Route** starts here.

### Walks and heritage

- The **Sefton Coastal Path** runs north along the shore. Visit Southport mentions Honest Coffee at Burbo Bank as a stop for refreshments.
- The **Great Crosby Heritage Trails** offer two walking routes through the area's history, which goes back about 1,000 years to the Vikings.
- The **Waterloo Heritage Trail** focuses on local architecture.

### Golf

Crosby is close to some well-known links courses. **West Lancashire Golf Club**, founded in 1873, is among the ten oldest golf clubs in England. See our [golf group trip guide](/guides/golf-group-trip-liverpool).

## Food, drink and shopping

- **South Road, Waterloo:** a street of restaurants and bars, about 10 minutes' walk from the adventure centre.
- **The Lake House** at the marine lake has a cafe bar, restaurant and dog-friendly bistro.
- **The Bus Yard:** street food from a converted bus by the beach.
- **Crosby village:** independent shops and high-street names for everyday shopping.

## Getting there

### By train

Crosby is on the **Southport branch** of Merseyrail's Northern line. Useful stations:

- **Waterloo:** for South Road and the lake end of the beach
- **Blundellsands and Crosby:** for Crosby village and the middle of the beach
- **Hall Road:** for the northern end of the coastal park

Sefton Council says the coastal park is within easy walking distance of all three. Trains run direct from Liverpool Central and Moorfields.

From **Fazakerley** in north Liverpool, take the train towards the city and change at **Sandhills**, where the Kirkby and Southport branches meet, onto a Southport train.

### By car

Sefton Council lists parking off **Cambridge Road, Blucher Street, Mariners Road and Hall Road West**, and asks visitors to plan ahead and park considerately, especially on sunny days. Crosby Lakeside's car park lists charges of **30p for up to 30 minutes, £1 for up to 2 hours, £1.90 for up to 4 hours and £3.80 for longer**.

## Living in Crosby

Crosby is popular with families moving to the Liverpool area because of its beach, parks, village feel and train link to the city. Things to know:

- **It's in Sefton, not Liverpool.** Council tax, bins and schools are run by Sefton Council.
- **School places** are coordinated by Sefton for all its schools, including academies. If you're moving mid-year, Sefton asks for proof of a permanent address, such as a completed purchase or a tenancy of at least 6 months. See our [school admissions guide](/guides/liverpool-school-admissions-relocating).
- **Commuting:** the Southport line runs direct into Liverpool city centre.
- **House-hunting:** visit on a weekday and a weekend, and walk the streets near the station at rush hour. Our [house-hunting trip planner](/guides/house-hunting-trip-liverpool) suggests a full viewing loop.

## Visiting Crosby from north Liverpool

If you're staying in Liverpool, Crosby makes an easy half-day out: by car for a beach walk and lunch on South Road, or by train with a change at Sandhills. Combine it with **Formby** for the pinewoods and red squirrels, a little further up the coast.
