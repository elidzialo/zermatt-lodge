"Competitive socialising" means bars and venues built around a game: crazy golf, darts, bowling, shuffleboard. They're ideal for groups because there's something to do besides drink, and they work for friends, families with older kids and work teams.

We checked these venues on 24 November 2026. Prices and opening times change, so check before you book.

## Crazy golf

| Venue | Where | What's special |
|---|---|---|
| **Junkyard Golf Club** | Liverpool ONE | Three themed courses made from reclaimed junk. Tickets from £9.50 |
| **Golf Fang** | Cains Brewery, Baltic Triangle | 18 holes with big, art-filled obstacles. Formerly Ghetto Golf |
| **One Under** | Lyceum Place, near Bold Street | Neon course from £9.95, plus pool, shuffleboard and arcade games |
| **Boom Battle Bar** | St George's Place, near St Peter's Square | Neon course plus axe throwing, beer pong and shuffleboard |
| **Urban Street Golf at Gravity MAX** | Liverpool ONE | App-tracked scores. Smaller clubs for children from age 4 |

### Outdoor options

- **Otterspool Adventure Centre**, by the river in Aigburth: a family-focused 12-hole course, plus a petting farm
- **Championship Adventure Golf**, New Brighton: 18 holes across two courses, with river views. Adult tickets from £7.50 for one course

## Darts

- **Flight Club**, Liverpool ONE: social darts
- **Gravity MAX**, Liverpool ONE: AR darts among many other games

## Bowling

- **Roxy Ball Room:** bowling, beer pong and arcade games, with several city-centre venues
- **Pins Social Club**, Duke Street: bowling, karaoke, table tennis and live music
- **Gravity MAX:** AR bowling

## Everything under one roof: Gravity MAX

Gravity MAX at Liverpool ONE is a two-floor venue with **e-karting, AR darts, AR bowling, street golf, VR, shuffleboard, batting cages** and an arcade, plus food and bars. It's a good choice for a mixed group, as people can split up and meet again for food. For more on the karting, see our [go-karting guide](/guides/go-karting-liverpool).

## Tips for booking a group

- **Book ahead** for Friday and Saturday nights
- **Check age rules.** Bar venues set their own, and they can be stricter in the evening. Daytime slots are the safer bet with teenagers
- **Pick one venue with several games** if your group has mixed interests
- **Combine** with an [escape room](/guides/escape-rooms-liverpool-groups) for a full day
- **Agree the budget** before you go, as games and drinks add up
