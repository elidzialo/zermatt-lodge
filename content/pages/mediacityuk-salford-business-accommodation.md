Production crews, freelancers, visiting lecturers and contractors all end up at MediaCityUK. A week or a month in a hotel adds up fast, and a crew of five or six rarely wants five or six separate rooms. We checked these facts on 24 November 2026.

## MediaCityUK at a glance

- A **200-acre** mixed-use development on the banks of the **Manchester Ship Canal**, in Salford and Trafford.
- The first 36-acre phase was finished in **2011**. BBC staff began moving in during May 2011.
- **Main tenants:** the **BBC** (about 2,700 staff as of 2023), **ITV** (ITV Granada and ITV Studios) and the **University of Salford**, which runs a MediaCity campus.

## Getting in each day

- **Tram:** **MediaCityUK** stop is at the end of a spur from the **Eccles line**. **Broadway** and **Harbour City** stops are close by. Salford City Council says there is a **six-minute service** to the Quays at peak times.
- **Across the canal:** the **Imperial War Museum** stop on the **Trafford Park line** is a short walk over the footbridge.
- **Between Salford campuses:** on open days, the University of Salford runs a free shuttle between MediaCity and Peel Park. Ask the university whether one runs on your dates.
- **Parking:** paid. The Lowry lists **Quayside MediaCityUK** at **£7.50 for up to 6 hours**, pre-booked through YourParkingSpace. Free parking is not available at MediaCity, so a home with its own parking and a tram or short drive in can save money on a long job.

## What a crew house gives you

1. **A room each.** No sharing rooms with colleagues after a long shoot.
2. **A kitchen.** Cook for early calls and late wraps instead of eating out every night.
3. **Washing machine.** Essential on a stay of more than a few days.
4. **Parking for vans and cars.** Several of the homes on Book Homes have free parking. Check space for your vehicles with the host.
5. **Quiet streets.** Homes on Book Homes are in residential areas: strictly no parties or events - bookings found to be for parties are cancelled.
