If you're booking accommodation for locums, agency nurses or other NHS staff on placement in Liverpool, start with the site they'll work at and their shift pattern, then work backwards. A base that's a short walk or drive from the hospital, with parking, blackout-friendly bedrooms and flexible dates, will do more for retention than a city-centre hotel. Check the host's safety basics, get the invoicing arrangements agreed before arrival, and build in flexibility for extensions. ## Know the hospital sites

Liverpool's main acute hospitals now sit within the **NHS University Hospitals of Liverpool Group (UHLG)**, formed on **1 November 2024**. It runs:

- **Aintree University Hospital** (Fazakerley, north Liverpool)
- **The Royal Liverpool University Hospital** (city centre)
- **Broadgreen Hospital** (east Liverpool)
- **Liverpool Women's Hospital** (near the city centre)

The Group employs more than 17,000 people. Other major employers include **Alder Hey Children's Hospital** in West Derby and **The Walton Centre**, which shares the Aintree campus.

### Matching the base to the site

| Placement | Practical base | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Aintree or The Walton Centre | Fazakerley, Aintree, Walton | Walk or a short drive to work |
| The Royal | City centre, or a suburb on the train | Close to Lime Street and Liverpool Central |
| Alder Hey or Broadgreen | West Derby, Old Swan, Broadgreen | Short cross-suburb drive |
| Rotating between sites | A house with parking and a train line | Options for driving and rail |

### Aintree in detail

Aintree University Hospital says the site is reached from **Lower Lane** and **Longmoor Lane**, near **junction 6 of the M57**, and warns that peak-time congestion is common. **Fazakerley station** is across the road from the Longmoor Lane entrance, about a 15-minute walk to the main entrance. The hospital lists visitor parking from free for under 30 minutes up to £5.50 for over 4.5 hours, with a **weekly ticket at £17.50**. Staff parking is arranged separately, so confirm it with the trust before day one.

## What to check before you book

### Location and travel

1. **How long is the journey** at shift times, not in the middle of the day? A 7am handover means leaving before rush hour; a night shift ends in the morning peak.
2. **Is there parking** at the accommodation, and at the hospital?
3. **Is there a train or bus option** if the clinician doesn't drive? Many suburbs are 10 to 20 minutes from Liverpool Central by train, but trains are less frequent late at night and on Sundays.

### Rooms and sleep

4. **Does each person have their own bedroom?** For staff who don't know each other, this is essential.
5. **Are there enough bathrooms** for staggered start times?
6. **Can bedrooms be made dark** for sleeping after night shifts? Ask about blinds or curtains.
7. **Is the street quiet** during the day?

### Living

8. **Is there a full kitchen,** so staff can prepare meals to take to work?
9. **Is there a washing machine and dryer** for uniforms and scrubs?
10. **Is there reliable Wi-Fi** and somewhere to sit and do online training or paperwork?

### Flexibility

11. **What happens if the placement is extended?** Can dates be extended easily?
12. **What happens if it ends early?** Check the cancellation terms.
13. **Is there a maximum stay?** Many short-stay homes cap stays at around 30 nights.

### Paperwork

14. **Can the host invoice your company,** with the clinician's name and dates on the invoice?
15. **Do you need a VAT invoice?** Not all hosts are VAT registered.
16. **What are the payment terms?** Upfront, monthly, or on invoice?

## Safety: what a host should be able to confirm

Short-stay homes in England are covered by fire safety law. The Home Office guide for **small paying guest accommodation**, last updated in January 2025, says it applies to anyone charging guests to stay in a property that isn't occupied as a private home, "even if it is just for one night". The guide says hosts must:

- **Carry out a fire risk assessment** and record the findings, reviewing it regularly

And it advises:

- **Interlinked smoke alarms** in hallways, stairs, living rooms and bedrooms, and **heat alarms** in kitchens
- **Gas appliances serviced every year** by a Gas Safe registered engineer
- **Electrics inspected and tested at least every five years**
- **Clear escape routes,** with exit doors that open from the inside without a key

As the booker, it's reasonable to ask a host to confirm these are in place. A professional host won't mind.

## Tax and expenses

How accommodation is treated for tax depends on the clinician's status (PAYE, umbrella or self-employed) and the placement. The Low Incomes Tax Reform Group (LITRG) explains that:

- A workplace can count as **temporary** if it's attended for a limited period
- It stops being temporary after **more than 24 months** of continuous work there
- **Accommodation and subsistence** can only count as travel expenses where there's an **overnight stay**

This isn't tax advice. Point your clinicians to HMRC guidance or a tax adviser for their own situation.

## Sharing a house between clinicians

A whole house can work well for two to four clinicians on the same placement, or for a team covering a service. Benefits include:

- **Own bedroom each,** with a shared kitchen and living room
- **One booking and one invoice** for several staff
- **Parking** for those who drive
- **Laundry** on site

Ask each clinician whether they're happy to share before you book, and make sure everyone knows the house rules.

## Booking tips for agencies

- **Book early for spring.** The Grand National Festival, 8 to 10 April 2027, fills north Liverpool, especially around Aintree and Fazakerley.
- **Send the clinician the details** before they travel: address, parking, check-in, Wi-Fi and the host's phone number.
- **Agree a contact** at the agency for any problems.
- **Check in after the first night** to make sure everything's fine.
