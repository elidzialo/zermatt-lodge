If you're driving to Liverpool, staying near the M57 and M58 in the north of the city lets you skip city-centre traffic and parking charges while keeping the whole region within reach. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Liverpool, with free parking for two cars.

This guide explains where each motorway goes, what's near the key junctions and why a motorway-side base suits drivers, families and work teams.

## The two motorways explained

### M57: Liverpool's eastern bypass

The M57 runs north to south around the eastern side of Liverpool. It links:

- **The M62** at its southern end, for Warrington, Manchester, Leeds and the M6 south
- **Prescot and Knowsley**: Knowsley Safari is signposted from **junction 2**, and units at Knowsley Business Park are about a mile from **junction 4**
- **Kirkby and Fazakerley** around **junction 6**, where Aintree University Hospital sits on the A506
- **Switch Island** at its northern end, where it meets the M58

### M58: north Liverpool to the M6

The M58 starts at Switch Island and runs east past Maghull and Skelmersdale to the **M6 near Wigan**. It's the quickest route from north Liverpool to:

- **Wigan, Preston and Lancashire**
- **The M6 north** towards the Lake District and Scotland
- **Skelmersdale's industrial estates**
- **Ormskirk and Edge Hill University**, via the nearby A59 and A570

### Switch Island

Switch Island is the big interchange where the M57 and M58 meet several A roads. From here you can reach:

- **The north docks, Bootle and Seaforth**, for the Port of Liverpool
- **Crosby, Formby and Southport** along the Sefton coast
- **Aintree** and the A59 into the city

## Why drivers stay near the motorways

### Avoid city-centre driving

Liverpool city centre has bus lanes, one-way systems and event-day congestion. Parking can be expensive, especially overnight. Staying near the M57 means you arrive, park and leave without going near it.

### Reach more of the region

From north Liverpool it's easy to cover a lot of the region from one base:

- **Football at Anfield or Everton:** train from Fazakerley towards Sandhills
- **Aintree Racecourse:** about a mile from the motorways, according to the Jockey Club
- **Knowsley Safari:** via M57 junction 2
- **Southport and Formby beaches:** via Switch Island
- **Manchester:** via the M57 and M62
- **The Lake District or North Wales** for day trips, depending on how far you're happy to drive

### Keep the city within reach

When you do want the city centre, leave the car at home. Fazakerley station has direct Merseyrail trains to Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes. See our guide to [Fazakerley to Liverpool city centre by train](/guides/fazakerley-to-liverpool-city-centre-by-train).

## Who stays near the M57 and M58?

- **Road trippers** breaking a long journey, for example between Scotland and North Wales or the ferry ports
- **Families on a driving holiday** who want the city one day and the beach the next. See our [Liverpool weekend by car itinerary](/guides/liverpool-weekend-itinerary-by-car).

- **Hospital visitors**, as Aintree University Hospital is by junction 6
- **Racegoers** heading to Aintree

## Driving tips for north Liverpool

- **Rush hour:** the M57 and the routes into Kirkby and Aintree can be slow at peak times. The hospital also warns of congestion around its site.
- **Race and match days:** expect closures and heavy traffic around Aintree on Grand National days, and around the stadiums on match days.
- **Tunnels:** if you're heading to the Wirral, the Mersey tunnels are tolled. See our [Mersey tunnel tolls guide](/guides/mersey-tunnel-tolls-guide).
- **Fuel and supplies:** retail parks around Aintree and the M57 corridor cover the basics.
