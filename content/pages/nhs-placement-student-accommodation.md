Placements are a big part of nursing, midwifery and allied health courses. If yours is in Liverpool and you live too far away to commute, you'll need somewhere to stay for a few weeks. Before you book, it's worth understanding what the NHS might pay back.

We checked the details below on 23 November 2026. This isn't financial advice. Your university placement team and NHS LSF have the final word.

## Travel and Dual Accommodation Expenses (TDAE)

TDAE is part of the **NHS Learning Support Fund** in England. In short:

- **Who can claim:** students who attended a placement as part of their course and are **already receiving the Training Grant**. Eligible Pharmacy and Healthcare Science students can claim some placement travel or accommodation through TDAE, but not the other LSF allowances
- **What counts:** "temporary accommodation you paid for to attend your placement"
- **How to claim:** sign in to your NHS Learning Support Fund account, choose the academic year and open the TDAE tab

### Nightly caps

| Type | Cap |
|---|---|
| **Commercial** (for example, hotel or B&B) | up to **£82.50 a night** |
| **Non-commercial** (for example, a friend or relative, but not parents) | up to **£37.50 a night** |

Travel is paid at **42p a mile** for driving and **30p a mile** for cycling.

### Deadlines and payment

- **Claim within six months** of the last day of the placement
- **Your university submits** the claim, and NHSBSA aims to process it within **20 working days**
- **Advance payments:** you can ask for one, but only after an online 2026/27 TDAE claim has been approved

**Before you book a holiday let,** check how it would be treated. The NHSBSA page only gives hotels and B&Bs as examples of commercial accommodation, so read the TDAE guide or ask NHS LSF. Keep every receipt and booking confirmation.

## Choosing where to stay

1. **Check the placement site first.** Liverpool's hospitals are spread across the city. Our [Aintree hospital accommodation guide](/guides/accommodation-near-aintree-university-hospital) covers one of the biggest sites
2. **Think about shifts.** Long days and nights need a quiet bedroom. See our [night shift sleep tips](/guides/night-shift-sleep-tips-work-stay)
3. **Parking matters.** Early and late shifts don't always line up with trains and buses
4. **Share with coursemates.** If several of you are placed in Liverpool at the same time, a whole house split between you can cost less per person than separate rooms. Check how TDAE treats shared bookings before you book
