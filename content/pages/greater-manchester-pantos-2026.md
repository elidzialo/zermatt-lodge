A panto is the easiest way to give a family Christmas trip a centrepiece. Greater Manchester has one big show in almost every town, plus smaller community productions. We checked these dates on 25 November 2026. Theatres sometimes add or drop performances, so check the exact show you want with the box office before you book travel.

## The big shows

| Show | Venue | Dates |
|---|---|---|
| Dick Whittington | Opera House, Manchester | 5 Dec 2026 to 3 Jan 2027 |
| Jack and the Beanstalk | Stockport Plaza | 4 Dec 2026 to 3 Jan 2027 |
| Cinderella: A Fairy Tale | Octagon Theatre, Bolton | 20 Nov 2026 to 9 Jan 2027 |
| Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs | Albert Halls, Bolton | December 2026 |
| The Jolly Christmas Postman | The Lowry, Salford | 4 Dec 2026 to 3 Jan 2027 |
| A Christmas Carol | The Lowry, Salford | 11 Dec 2026 to 10 Jan 2027 |

### Opera House Manchester

**Dick Whittington** runs from **Saturday 5 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027**, with **Jason Manford** and **Ben Nickless**. This is the city's biggest panto, so weekend and Christmas week seats go first.

### Stockport Plaza

**Jack and the Beanstalk** runs from **Friday 4 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027** at the Art Deco Plaza on Mersey Square. There are no shows on Sunday 6 December, Thursday 17 December, Christmas Day or New Year's Day. A family listing gives tickets from **£21.45 for under-16s** and **£26.95 for adults**, and lists **BSL interpreted shows** on Saturday 5 December at 7.30pm and Thursday 10 December at 10.30am and 2.30pm. Stockport is a short train ride from Manchester Piccadilly: see our [Stockport visitor guide](/guides/stockport-visitor-guide).

### Bolton

Bolton has two big Christmas shows:

- **Cinderella: A Fairy Tale** at the **Octagon Theatre**, opening **Friday 20 November 2026**. It's a modern version of the story, created by Sally Cookson, Adam Peck and the original company, and directed by Sarah Punshon.
- **Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs** at the **Albert Halls** in December 2026, produced by Shone Productions.

More ideas for the town are in our [Bolton visitor guide](/guides/bolton-visitor-guide).

### The Lowry, Salford

- **The Jolly Christmas Postman**, 4 December 2026 to 3 January 2027. It lasts **1 hour with no interval** and is aimed at younger children.
- **A Christmas Carol**, 11 December 2026 to 10 January 2027.

The Lowry has no car park of its own. Our [Salford Quays guide](/guides/salford-quays-visitor-guide) covers trams and parking.

## Tips for a panto trip

- **Matinees** suit small children and leave the evening free. They also avoid driving home in the dark.
- **Book seats first, then the stay.** Christmas week seats sell out before homes do.
- **Combine it** with a light trail. See our [Christmas light trails guide](/guides/christmas-light-trails-manchester-2026).
