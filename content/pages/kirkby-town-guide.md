Kirkby is a town of around 45,000 people in Knowsley, about 6 miles north-east of Liverpool city centre. It's best known for its large industrial estate, a town centre that's been rebuilt in recent years, and Liverpool FC's training ground. Two Merseyrail stations, Kirkby and Headbolt Lane, run frequent trains into Liverpool, and Headbolt Lane also connects towards Wigan and Manchester. ## Kirkby at a glance

- **Borough:** Metropolitan Borough of Knowsley, Merseyside (historically Lancashire)
- **Location:** about 6 miles (10 km) north-east of Liverpool and 5 miles north of Huyton
- **Population:** 45,564 at the 2021 census, down from a peak of 59,917 in 1971
- **Stations:** Kirkby and Headbolt Lane (Merseyrail Northern line)

## A short history

Kirkby appears in the **Domesday Book** of 1086. **St Chad's Church**, built in the 19th century, stands on the site of a church recorded there.

The town changed completely in the 20th century:

- During the Second World War, **ROF Kirkby**, a Royal Ordnance Factory, employed more than 20,000 people at its peak
- Liverpool Corporation bought the land from the Earl of Sefton in **1947**
- Large-scale house building started in **1950**, and the population grew from about 3,000 in 1951 to over 52,000 by 1961
- The factory site became **Kirkby Industrial Estate**, which employed more than 26,000 people at its 1971 peak

That industrial heritage is why Kirkby is still a major employment area today. Our guide to [Kirkby and Knowsley Business Park accommodation](/guides/kirkby-knowsley-business-park-accommodation) covers the work side in detail.

## The town centre today

Kirkby town centre has been through a long regeneration. According to the Local Government Association:

- **Knowsley Council bought the town centre for £43.8m**, with £10m from the Liverpool City Region Combined Authority, to restart regeneration that private developers hadn't delivered
- A **45,000 sq ft Morrisons** superstore with a petrol station has opened
- A **94,000 sq ft retail extension** includes a **Home Bargains**, plus drive-throughs, with PureGym set to open
- A new **Civic Square** sits next to the council's **Kirkby Centre**, where the library moved in 2018
- A **multi-screen cinema** with food and drink outlets has planning permission at **Cherryfield Drive**
- Up to **700 homes** are being explored on land south of Cherryfield Drive
- A multi-storey car park is planned for conversion into a transport hub at **Kirkby Gateway**

Place North West's 2026 development update lists Kirkby, Prescot and Huyton among the Knowsley town centres expected to see significant change through private sector partnerships.

For visitors, the practical upshot is that Kirkby has a big supermarket, everyday shops and fast food close to the station, but it's not a destination for nights out. For that, people head into Liverpool.

## Getting around

### Trains

Kirkby is at the end of the **Kirkby branch** of Merseyrail's Northern line, with **Headbolt Lane** opened in 2023 as an extension beyond Kirkby. The line runs:

**Headbolt Lane - Kirkby - Fazakerley - Rice Lane - Kirkdale - Sandhills - Moorfields - Liverpool Central**

- **To Liverpool city centre:** frequent direct trains. From Fazakerley, one stop towards town, Trainline lists Liverpool Central at about 15 minutes
- **Towards Wigan and Manchester:** Headbolt Lane has an hourly service to Wigan, Manchester and Blackburn via Rainford
- **Paying:** the Combined Authority's Tap and Go scheme, which lets you pay by contactless card with daily and weekly caps, was piloted on the Headbolt Lane line from December 2025

### Roads

Kirkby is close to the **M57** and the **East Lancashire Road (A580)**, with the **M58** a short drive north. For crews driving to sites around Merseyside, that's a useful position.

## Liverpool FC in Kirkby

Kirkby is home to **Liverpool FC's training ground and youth academy**. The academy opened in 1998, and the first team moved from Melwood to Kirkby in **November 2020**. It's a working training ground and isn't open to visitors, so fans should head to [Anfield](/guides/anfield-match-day-accommodation) instead.

## Who visits Kirkby

- **Contractors and engineers** working on the industrial estate, Knowsley Business Park or town centre projects
- **Families** visiting relatives in the town
- **Hospital visitors** for Aintree University Hospital and The Walton Centre, a short train ride away in Fazakerley
- **Football families** whose children are involved with academy football

## Where to stay near Kirkby

Kirkby has limited visitor accommodation of its own. Options include:

- **Hotels** near the motorway junctions and in Liverpool city centre
- **Whole houses** elsewhere in Liverpool, which suit crews and families who need parking and a kitchen

## Tips for working in Kirkby

1. **Drive or train?** If your site is near the stations, the train is easy. For the industrial estate, most people drive.
2. **Shop at the town centre Morrisons** or Aintree Shopping Park for supplies.
3. **Check match days.** Liverpool and Everton home games affect traffic on routes into the city, not usually in Kirkby itself.
4. **Plan for early starts.** A base with self check-in and off-street parking avoids morning stress.
