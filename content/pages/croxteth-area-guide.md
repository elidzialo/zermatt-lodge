Croxteth is a residential suburb in **north Liverpool**, bordered by **Norris Green, Gillmoss, Fazakerley and West Derby**, with the **East Lancashire Road (A580)** to the north and the **M57** close by. Its headline attraction is **Croxteth Hall and Country Park**, where the Victorian walled garden is open **10am to 3pm daily** and parking in the dedicated areas is free. The nearest station is **Fazakerley**, about 3 miles away, with trains to the city centre in about 15 minutes.

## Where Croxteth is

Croxteth sits on Liverpool's north-eastern edge. To the north is the East Lancashire Road, and beyond it Kirkby and the Knowsley business parks. To the west is Fazakerley; to the south, West Derby.

Gillmoss, a neighbouring district, is often searched alongside Croxteth and is part of the same corner of the city.

## A little history

Wikipedia describes how Croxteth's first post-war housing was built for skilled workers who came from Slough and Rugby to work at local factories, and for families from Liverpool's dock areas who had lost homes to bombing or slum clearance. The first residents arrived in **1951** to an estate with no roads, pavements, shops, pubs or buses yet.

Some families were rehoused here from **Scotland Road** when homes were cleared for the second Mersey Tunnel. The old **Dog and Gun** pub, demolished in 2005, was probably named for the hunt from Croxteth Hall.

Wayne and Coleen Rooney grew up in the area.

## Croxteth Hall and Country Park

This is the reason most visitors come.

### Opening times

| Part | Hours |
|---|---|
| Victorian walled garden | 10am to 3pm daily, last admission 2.30pm |
| Inner park (around the hall) | 7am to 5.30pm, November to March; 7am to 6.30pm, April to October |
| Country park | Open all year |
| Hall | Open for "Upstairs Downstairs" tours and events |
| Home Farm | Run separately; check its own website or call 0151 546 4662 |

Croxteth Hall warns that days can see temporary or partial closures for **weddings, filming or special events**. Check its website or social media before you go. When we checked, the café was closed for refurbishment and a concession stand was serving refreshments, with toilets open 9am to 5pm.

### Parking and access

- **Satnav:** **L11 1EH** for the Muirhead Avenue entrance; **L12 0HB** for the Flint Drive entrance
- **Parking:** free in the dedicated areas, but may be charged during special events. Spaces can't be reserved
- **Car park hours:** 7am to 6pm, November to March
- **Walking and cycling:** more than 10 entrances, and bike racks behind the hall
- **Accessibility:** ramped entrance to the hall and a lift upstairs. The walled garden has gently ramped access and fine gravel paths. Croxteth Hall Lane has narrow pavements, so use the Flint Drive gate with pushchairs or wheelchairs. Blue Badge bays are in the main car park

### Christmas at Croxteth Hall

In recent years the hall has hosted festive events such as **Santa Paws** (dogs meet Santa, with a gift, treat and photo), Story Time with Santa and a Christmas makers' market. In December 2025, Santa Paws ran on selected dates from 13 to 21 December. Check Liverpool City Halls' listings for the **2026** dates and prices before you plan around them.

## Getting around

- **Train:** Wikipedia gives **Fazakerley** as the nearest station, about 3 miles away on the Northern line. From Fazakerley it's about **15 minutes to Liverpool Central**
- **Bus:** Merseytravel routes link Croxteth with the city centre, Page Moss, Broadgreen Hospital, Fazakerley hospital and Knowsley Industrial Estate
- **Car:** the East Lancashire Road and M57 are on the doorstep

## Tips for visitors

- Check Croxteth Hall's website or social media on the day, as weddings and filming can close parts of the estate
- Wear boots in winter: some park tracks are grassy and muddy
- Bring cash and card for the concession stand while the café is being refurbished

## A winter afternoon in Croxteth

1. Arrive late morning and park in the main car park
2. Walk the country park while it's light
3. Visit the walled garden before the 2.30pm last entry
4. Check what's on at the hall that day
5. Head back before the car park closes at 6pm in winter
