Two days is plenty for a family weekend in Manchester in winter, as long as you plan around short daylight and the odd downpour. This plan mixes free museums, one outdoor morning and one treat. We put it together on 25 November 2026 from venue details checked in our other guides. Christmas and New Year hours differ, so check each place before you go.

## Before you go

- **Daylight:** in December it's dark by about 4pm. Put outdoor plans in the morning.
- **Mondays:** many museums are closed, including Manchester Museum. Aim for a Saturday and Sunday, or a Friday and Saturday.
- **Book ahead:** shows, light trails and snow sessions sell out at weekends.

## Day one: the city centre

**Morning: Manchester Museum (free)**
On Oxford Road. Dinosaurs including **Stan the T. rex**, and a life-sized moose in Living Worlds. Closed on Mondays. Its Vivarium is closed until May 2027.

**Lunch**
Somewhere central, or a packed lunch from home to keep costs down.

**Afternoon: pick one**
- **National Football Museum** (paid), about two minutes' walk from Manchester Victoria.
- **People's History Museum** (free), closed on Tuesdays.

**Late afternoon or evening**
- **December:** a matinee panto or family show. See our [pantos guide](/guides/greater-manchester-pantos-2026).
- **Late November to early January:** a light trail. See our [light trails guide](/guides/christmas-light-trails-manchester-2026).

## Day two: get outside, then warm up

**Morning outdoors: pick one**
- **Heaton Park**, Prestwich: open from 8am to dusk, with tram stops close by. See our [Heaton Park guide](/guides/heaton-park-prestwich-visitor-guide).
- **Dunham Massey**, Altrincham: deer park walks; parking is included in the ticket. See our [Dunham Massey guide](/guides/dunham-massey-visitor-guide).
- **RHS Garden Bridgewater**, Worsley: closes at 3.30pm in December. See our [Bridgewater guide](/guides/rhs-garden-bridgewater-visitor-guide).

**Afternoon indoors: pick one**
- **The Snow Centre Manchester** (formerly Chill Factore) for real snow, or **LEGOLAND Discovery Centre and SEA LIFE**. See our [Snow Centre and Trafford Centre guide](/guides/snow-centre-and-trafford-centre-family-day).
- **IWM North** at Salford Quays, free. See our [Salford Quays guide](/guides/salford-quays-visitor-guide).

## Wet weather swaps

- Swap the park for **Salford Museum and Art Gallery** and its Victorian street. See our [Salford guide](/guides/salford-ordsall-hall-and-salford-museum-guide).
- More ideas in our [rainy day guide](/guides/rainy-day-manchester-kids).

## Getting around

- **Trams** are the easiest way in with children. The **Bury line** runs from Prestwich and Crumpsall into Manchester Victoria.
- **Bus fares** are **£2 for adults** and **£1 for children** in 2026, as hopper fares. See our [transport guide](/guides/getting-around-greater-manchester-bee-network).
