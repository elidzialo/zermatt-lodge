Here's what to know about the area and how to plan a work stay.

## The Kirkby and Knowsley employment area

Knowsley Council describes **Knowsley Business Park** as one of the largest employment sites in Europe, with **over 800 businesses employing 18,000 people**. Manufacturing, logistics, distribution and engineering firms operate across the area, which means a steady stream of visiting contractors, installers and agency workers.

Two names come up a lot, and they're easy to confuse:

- **Knowsley Industrial Park** is on the eastern side of **Kirkby**, with units on roads such as South Boundary Road (L33 postcodes).
- **Knowsley Business Park** is the name used both for the wider employment area and for units around Unity Grove (L34 postcode, Prescot), which industrial landlord Indurent places about **a mile from junction 4 of the M57**, near the A580 East Lancashire Road.

**Tip:** always get the full unit address and postcode from your client. A wrong turn between the two can cost you 15 minutes at shift change.

## Getting there from Fazakerley

### By car

Fazakerley is next door to Kirkby. Depending on which part of the park you're going to, you'll use:

- **The A506 or M57 junction 6** for Kirkby and Knowsley Industrial Park
- **The M57 to junction 4** and the A580 for the Unity Grove side

Outside rush hour it's a short drive. Shift-change times can be busy at the estate entrances, so allow a few extra minutes for 6am, 2pm and 10pm starts.

### By train

Fazakerley, Kirkby and **Headbolt Lane** are all on the same Merseyrail Northern line branch. Merseyrail lists the stations as Liverpool Central, Moorfields, Sandhills, Kirkdale, Rice Lane, Fazakerley, Kirkby and Headbolt Lane. Headbolt Lane opened in **2023** and was the first extension to the line since 1977. It was built partly to improve access to jobs in the area.

Trains run several times an hour in the daytime, with fewer in the evenings and on Sundays. Check times for your shift on the Merseyrail app. From the stations, some sites are a walk, and others need a short bus or taxi ride. ## Planning a work stay in Kirkby

### For shift workers

- **Sleep matters.** A quiet house, your own room and blackout options are worth more than a lobby bar. See our [night-shift sleep tips](/guides/night-shift-sleep-tips-work-stay).
- **Meal prep.** A big kitchen means proper food before a 12-hour shift, not service station sandwiches.
- **Parking.** Two free spaces mean two cars for staggered shifts.

### For installation and commissioning teams

- **Kit and tools.** Check storage and van parking with your host before you arrive.
- **Early access.** Many sites want contractors on the gate by 7am. Being 10 to 15 minutes away helps.
- **One invoice.** Booking a whole house for a team makes paperwork simpler.

### For auditors, trainers and visiting managers

If you're only visiting for a couple of days, a hotel may be fine. If several colleagues are visiting together, or for a week or more, a shared house is often more comfortable and better value.

## Questions to ask your client before you arrive

1. **What's the full unit address and postcode**, and which gate do contractors use?
2. **What time do we need to be on site** for induction on day one?
3. **Is there parking on site** for vans, or do we need to unload and move?
4. **What PPE is required**, and is anything supplied?
5. **What are the site hours**, and is weekend working likely?
6. **Who's our contact** if we're running late or the gate's closed?

Getting these answered before you travel saves a wasted first morning, and helps you plan who drives and who takes the train.
