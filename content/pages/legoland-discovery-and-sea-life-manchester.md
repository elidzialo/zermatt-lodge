LEGOLAND Discovery Centre and SEA LIFE Manchester are two indoor attractions side by side at the **Trafford Palazzo**, by the Trafford Centre. On a wet Manchester day they're an easy win with younger children, and you can do both in one trip.

We checked the details below on the official websites on 23 November 2026. Prices and times change, so confirm before you go.

## LEGOLAND Discovery Centre Manchester

What's inside, according to its website:

- **LEGO Ninjago City Adventure**
- **MINILAND**, the LEGO model city
- **LEGO 4D Cinema**
- **The Great LEGO Race** VR experience
- **Kingdom Quest** and the **Merlin's Apprentice** ride
- A **DUPLO Farm** for little ones
- A creative workshop, LEGO store and coffee shop

Things to know:

- **Adults need a child with them** to enter, except on special adult nights.
- **Book online in advance** to save money and guarantee entry. Tickets can be rescheduled free up to 24 hours before your arrival time.
- **Children under 2 go free.**
- **Winter closure:** the website says it is **closed to guests from Monday 30 November to Friday 4 December 2026**, reopening on **Saturday 5 December**. The online ticket system is also offline over those dates.

## SEA LIFE Manchester

SEA LIFE says it has **over 2,000 creatures**, including sea turtles, sharks, rays, clownfish and jellyfish. Zones include the **Ocean Tunnel**, **Shark Shipwreck**, **Rocky Hideout**, **Sea Ranger** and **Stingray Bay**, and there's a **Turtle Beach** show.

Things to know:

- **Book online in advance.** SEA LIFE says online booking guarantees entry, and tickets can be rescheduled free up to 24 hours before arrival.
- **Children under 2 go free.**
- **It's cashless**, and so is LEGOLAND Discovery Centre.
- Extra paid experiences, such as a turtle feed or a stingray feed, are available on selected days.

## Doing both in one day

Both attractions sell **multi-attraction tickets**, and LEGOLAND says they are valid for **90 days** from your visit, so you don't have to do both on the same day. Check the booking pages for the current price, as offers change often.

Tips:

- Start with the attraction that has the earlier last entry on your day.
- Eat before or after. The Trafford Centre has plenty of food.
- Avoid the busiest school holiday weekends if you can. Manchester's Christmas school holiday runs from 18 December 2026 to 4 January 2027.
