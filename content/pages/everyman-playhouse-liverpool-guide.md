Liverpool has two producing theatres run by one charity: the **Everyman** on Hope Street and the **Playhouse** on Williamson Square. Between them they stage new plays, classics, musicals and one of the city's best-loved Christmas traditions, the **Rock 'n' Roll Panto**.

We checked the details below on 23 November 2026.

## The Everyman

- **Where:** the north end of **Hope Street**, near the Metropolitan Cathedral
- **History:** the building started as Hope Hall, a chapel built in 1837. The Everyman theatre opened there in **September 1964**, and became known for new writing by Liverpool writers such as Willy Russell and Alan Bleasdale
- **Rebuilt:** closed in 2011 and reopened in **February 2014** in a new building by Haworth Tompkins, which won the **2014 RIBA Stirling Prize**
- **Capacity:** about **400**

## The Playhouse

- **Where:** **Williamson Square**, in the main shopping area near Clayton Square
- **History:** opened in **1866** as the Star Music Hall, became a repertory company in 1911 and was renamed the Liverpool Playhouse in 1916
- **Listed:** Grade II*

Both theatres are run by **Liverpool and Merseyside Theatres Trust**, a registered charity, with a shared programme.

## Christmas 2026: what's on

### Beauty and the Beast, the Rock 'n' Roll Panto (Everyman)

- **Dates:** **14 November 2026 to 16 January 2027**
- **Running time:** about **2 hours 20 minutes**, including the interval
- **BSL performance:** **Friday 18 December, 7pm**
- **Relaxed performance:** **Thursday 14 January 2027, 1.30pm**
- **Note:** contains haze, flashing lights and loud noises

As the name suggests, the Rock 'n' Roll Panto is built around rock and pop songs. It's a good choice for families who want something a bit different from a traditional panto.

### Little Shop of Horrors (Playhouse)

- **Dates:** **3 December 2026 to 9 January 2027**
- **Director and choreographer:** Carrie-Anne Ingrouille
- **About:** a revival of the Alan Menken and Howard Ashman musical, transferring from Hope Mill Theatre in Manchester

## Tips for your visit

1. **Book early for Christmas.** Weekend and school holiday dates sell first
2. **Accessible shows.** If you need a BSL or relaxed performance, book those dates early as they're one-offs
3. **Eat nearby.** Hope Street has restaurants close to the Everyman, and the Playhouse is surrounded by city-centre options
4. **Make a cultural weekend.** The [Philharmonic Hall](/guides/liverpool-philharmonic-hall-guide) is a few minutes' walk from the Everyman, and the [Royal Court](/guides/royal-court-liverpool-guide) is close to the Playhouse
