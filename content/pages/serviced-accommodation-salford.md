Salford covers very different places: the waterfront offices and studios of **Salford Quays and MediaCityUK**, the hospital campus at **Salford Royal**, and the residential streets where many visitors come to see family. Serviced accommodation, a furnished home or apartment with bedding, utilities and wifi included, works well for all three, especially for stays of a week or more. The trick is to choose your base by where you'll be each day.

## MediaCityUK and Salford Quays

According to Salford City Council:

- **The BBC** makes many programmes at MediaCityUK, including Blue Peter, Newsround, Match of the Day and Football Focus
- **ITV** teams for Granada Reports and other programmes work there, and Coronation Street is filmed beside Imperial War Museum North
- **The University of Salford** has a MediaCityUK campus for more than 1,500 students and staff
- **The Lowry**, opened in 2000, has theatres, galleries and conference facilities
- A **regular tram** runs to West Salford, Altrincham and Manchester city centre

Who stays here: production crews, freelancers on a run of shoot days, conference delegates and visiting staff. If you're on long studio days, a kitchen and washer save time and money.

## Salford Royal Hospital

Salford Royal is an **NHS teaching hospital and major trauma centre** with **728 beds**, run by the Northern Care Alliance NHS Foundation Trust, on the site of the former Hope Hospital. Families of trauma patients often need to stay for days or weeks.

What helps:

- A home with **parking**, so you're not paying for hospital parking every day
- A **kitchen**, so you can eat properly between visits
- **Flexible stay lengths**, since discharge dates change

## What to check before booking

1. **Beds and bathrooms:** the real list, not just "sleeps 6"
2. **Parking:** how many cars or vans, and is it off-road?
3. **Invoices:** in your company's name, with VAT if the host is registered. See [invoices and VAT for business stays](/guides/invoice-and-vat-for-business-stays)
4. **Check-in:** self check-in suits late arrivals
5. **Wifi:** ask for a speed if you'll be working from the home
6. **House rules:** many homes don't allow parties
