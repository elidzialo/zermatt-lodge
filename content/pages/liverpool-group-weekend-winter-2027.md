January and February are a great time for a group trip to Liverpool. The city is usually calmer than in December, there's plenty on indoors, and the football is in full swing. The key is picking the right weekend: some are packed, some are quiet.

We checked these dates on 24 November 2026. Football fixtures can move for TV, so check again before you book.

## The weekends at a glance

| Weekend | What's on | How busy |
|---|---|---|
| **Sat 2 to Sun 3 Jan** | Liverpool v Coventry at Anfield (Sat). Skate St George's open until Sun 3 Jan | Busy |
| **Sat 16 to Sun 17 Jan** | Liverpool v Crystal Palace at Anfield (Sat). Back to the Future at the Empire | Medium |
| **Sat 23 to Sun 24 Jan** | Everton v Brentford (Sun, 2pm). Back to the Future | Medium |
| **Sat 30 to Sun 31 Jan** | **Merseyside derby at Anfield, Sat 12.30pm** | **Very busy** |
| **Tue 2 to Wed 3 Feb** | **Strictly Come Dancing Live**, M&S Bank Arena, 7.30pm | Midweek |
| **Sat 6 to Sun 7 Feb** | Lunar New Year (Sat). Everton v Newcastle (Sat) | Busy |
| **Sat 13 to Sun 14 Feb** | Valentine's weekend | Busy for restaurants |
| **15 to 19 Feb** | Half term for Liverpool City Council schools. Liverpool v Hull at Anfield (Sat 20 Feb) | Busy with families |
| **Sat 27 to Sun 28 Feb** | Everton v Nottingham Forest (Sat). **Global Scouse Day** (Sun 28 Feb) | Medium |

## Lunar New Year

Lunar New Year is **Saturday 6 February 2027**. Liverpool's Chinatown is home to the oldest Chinese community in Europe. The city's 2027 celebration dates hadn't been announced when we checked (in 2026 they were on 21 and 22 February). ## Tips for booking a group

- **Fix the date first.** Agree it, then book the house before the tickets if the weekend is a busy one
- **Split the cost up front.** One person books, everyone pays straight away
- **Check fixtures again** in December and January, when TV picks are announced
- **Compare the total.** A whole house can work out cheaper than several hotel rooms. See [four hotel rooms or one house?](/guides/two-hotel-rooms-vs-one-house)
