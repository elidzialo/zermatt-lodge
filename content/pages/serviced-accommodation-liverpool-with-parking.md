If you need serviced accommodation in Liverpool and you're bringing a car, **parking is the cost most people forget**. Most serviced apartments are city-centre flats with no guest parking, so you pay for a public car park every day. A 2022 round-up of city car parks listed **24-hour prices of about £17 to £24** at Q-Park sites, and prices may well be higher now. For drivers, a serviced **house outside the centre with its own parking** is often cheaper and roomier. Zermatt Lodge in Liverpool has **free parking for 2 cars**, and direct trains reach Liverpool Central in **about 15 minutes**.

## What "serviced accommodation" means

Serviced accommodation is a furnished home or apartment let for short or medium stays. It usually includes:

- **Bedding, towels and a fully equipped kitchen**
- **Wifi**, heating and bills
- **Cleaning between stays**, and sometimes a mid-stay clean on longer bookings
- **Self check-in**, rather than a reception desk

It sits between a hotel and a rental. You get more space and a kitchen, which matters if you're staying a week or more, but you won't usually get daily housekeeping or room service.

In Liverpool the term covers everything from studio flats. Ask exactly what's included before you book.

## The parking problem in the city centre

### What parking costs

City-centre apartments often have no spaces for guests. The main options are public car parks:

| Car park | 24-hour price (2022 round-up) |
|---|---|
| Q-Park Queen Square | £17 maximum per 24 hours |
| Albert Dock | £20 |
| Q-Park Liverpool ONE | £22 |
| Q-Park John Lewis | £22 |
| Q-Park Dale Street | £24 maximum per 24 hours |

These figures come from a 2022 local press round-up, so **treat them as a minimum** and check current prices before you travel. Liverpool ONE says its three Q-Park car parks are open 24 hours, have about 3,000 spaces between them, and are cheaper if you **pre-book online**. Q-Park also sells season tickets for its Liverpool car parks, including 24/7 and weekday-only options, which can suit a long project.

At £17 to £24 a day, a two-week stay can add **£240 to £340** in parking alone. With two vans or cars, double it.

### Other city-centre catches

- **Height limits.** Multi-storey car parks may not take a high-top van. Check before you arrive.
- **Walking with kit.** Carrying tools or luggage from a car park to a flat several streets away gets old quickly.
- **Event days.** On match days and concert nights, city car parks fill early.

## Why a serviced house can work better

If you're driving, a house in a well-connected suburb solves most of these problems.

- **Free parking** at the door, so no daily fee and no carrying kit across town
- **A bedroom each.** For a crew or a team, separate beds matter more than a city view
- **A real kitchen and dining table**, so you can cook rather than eat out every night
- **Washer and dryer** for work clothes on longer stays
- **Quieter nights** away from city-centre bars

The trade-off is that you're not walking distance from the city centre. So **check the train time** as well as the distance.

## How to check a listing before you book

1. **Is parking on site, and how many cars?** "Parking nearby" often means a paid car park.
2. **Can it take a van?** If you need van parking, ask directly. Don't assume a driveway fits a long-wheelbase van.
3. **How long is the trip to your site or office?** Check it at rush hour, not at midnight.
4. **Can you get an invoice in your company's name?** Ask whether VAT is included. Not every host is VAT registered.
5. **What's the minimum and maximum stay,** and is there a monthly rate?
6. **What are the house rules?** Quiet hours, smoking and visitors matter for crews.

## Getting into the city centre

- **Train:** from Zermatt Lodge, Liverpool Central is about **15 minutes** by train. Moorfields is the station for the business district.
- **Tap & Go:** since 25 February 2026, Merseyrail accepts contactless bank cards, phones and watches. Tap in and tap out. Merseytravel says fares are frozen until March 2027.
- **Road:** the M57 links to the M58 and M62.

If your work is in Aintree, Kirkby, Knowsley, Bootle or along the M57, you may not need the city centre at all.

## Business bookings

Companies booking for staff usually want three things: a **clear price**, an **invoice** and **flexibility**. Before you book, ask:

- Whether the host can invoice your company and show VAT if it applies. HMRC treats self-catering holiday accommodation as standard-rated for VAT where the supplier is VAT registered.
- What happens if the project is extended or cut short
- Whether named guests can change during the stay
