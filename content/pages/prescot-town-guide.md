Prescot is a small market town in Knowsley, about 8 miles east of Liverpool city centre, with a surprising cultural draw: the Shakespeare North Playhouse, an award-winning theatre built to honour the Elizabethan playhouse that stood in the town in the 1590s. It also has a museum of its watch and clock-making past, a Grade I listed church, and Knowsley Safari on the edge of town. It's an easy half-day or evening out from Liverpool. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Liverpool, sleeping up to 7 with free parking for 2 cars, an easy train ride from Liverpool city centre.

## Prescot at a glance

- **Borough:** Metropolitan Borough of Knowsley, Merseyside
- **Location:** about 8 miles (13 km) east of Liverpool city centre
- **Known for:** Shakespeare North Playhouse, watchmaking history, St Mary's Church, the Knowsley estate
- **Station:** Prescot, on the Liverpool to Wigan line

## Shakespeare North Playhouse

The main reason many people visit Prescot.

### The history behind it

From the **mid-1590s to 1609**, Prescot had a **purpose-built Shakespearean theatre**, the Prescot Playhouse. Playhouses of that era were rare outside London, which makes Prescot's history unusual. The new theatre was built to celebrate it.

### The building

- **Opened:** 15 July 2022
- **Main auditorium:** **420 seats**, laid out to **Inigo Jones's 1629 design** for the Cockpit-in-Court theatre at the Palace of Whitehall
- **Studio:** a modern studio space for smaller shows
- **Outdoor performance garden:** funded with **£700,000** from the widow of comedian **Ken Dodd**
- **Architects:** Helm Architecture and Austin-Smith:Lord
- **Award:** **Theatre Building of the Year 2023** at The Stage awards

### Visiting

The programme mixes Shakespeare, new writing, family shows and events. The auditorium is intimate, so even the back rows feel close to the stage. Check the theatre's own listings for 2027 shows and book ahead for popular runs.

**Tip:** if you're going to an evening show, have dinner first in Prescot or plan to eat afterwards in Liverpool. Prescot is a small town and choices are limited late at night.

## Prescot Museum and the watchmaking story

In the 18th and 19th centuries, Prescot was a centre of **watch and clock-making**. The trade ended with the failure of the **Lancashire Watch Company in 1910**.

**Prescot Museum** has a **permanent exhibition on the town's clock and watch-making history**, plus several temporary exhibitions each year. It's a good short visit before a show.

## St Mary's Church

**Prescot Parish Church (St Mary's)** dates from the **17th century** and is the **only Grade I listed building in the Borough of Knowsley**. It's worth a look as you walk through the town centre.

## Cables Retail Park

Later in the 20th century, the big local employer was **BICC (British Insulated Callender's Cables)**. After it closed in the early 1990s, the site was regenerated in 2000 as **Cables Retail Park**, which is where you'll find big-name shops and supermarkets in Prescot.

## Knowsley Safari

The Earl of Derby's **Knowsley estate** lies on the edge of Prescot and includes **Knowsley Safari Park**. The main safari is a drive-through route, so most visitors come by car. It's one of the most popular family days out in the Liverpool area, so:

- **Book tickets online** in advance, especially in school holidays
- **Arrive early** on busy days
- **Check the safari's rules** for vehicles before you go

For more ideas for children, see our [family days out in north Liverpool](/guides/family-days-out-north-liverpool).

## Regeneration

Place North West's 2026 development update lists **Prescot**, along with Kirkby and Huyton, among the Knowsley town centres expected to see significant change through private sector partnerships. The theatre has already brought new visitors to the town.

## Getting to Prescot

### By train

**Prescot station** is on the **Liverpool to Wigan line**, run by **Northern**. Wikipedia lists:

- **Monday to Saturday:** trains every 30 minutes between **Liverpool Lime Street** and **Wigan North Western**
- **Sunday:** hourly

From most of Liverpool outside the centre, the train route means going into the city first, so for many people driving is quicker.

### By car

The **M57** and **M62** both serve Prescot, so check a live route outside rush hour. Park in a town centre car park and walk to the theatre and museum.
