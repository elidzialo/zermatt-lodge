One day isn't long, but central Liverpool is compact, so you can see a lot on foot. This plan covers the big sights in a sensible order, with one paid attraction and plenty of free ones. It works best if you arrive by mid-morning.

We checked times and prices on 24 November 2026. In winter, daylight is short, so we've put the waterfront in the middle of the day.

## The plan at a glance

| Time | What | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| 10am | Liverpool Cathedral | Free (tower extra) |
| 11am | The Beatles Story **or** RLB360 tour | £20 or £16 per adult |
| 1pm | Lunch near the Albert Dock or Liverpool ONE | |
| 2pm | Pier Head, Three Graces, Museum of Liverpool | Free |
| 3pm | Optional Mersey Ferry River Explorer Cruise | Paid, book online |
| 5pm | Mathew Street and the Cavern Club | £6 adult entry |
| 7pm | Dinner | |

## Morning: Liverpool Cathedral

Start at **Liverpool Cathedral**, the huge cathedral at the top of town. It's **free to visit**, with donations welcome. The **Tower Experience** is extra (£8 standard), and the view over the city and the river is the best way to get your bearings. The cathedral also has the **Welsford Bistro** if you need coffee. On Sundays it's open for worship until midday, so swap the order and visit in the afternoon.

## Late morning: pick one big attraction

Walk down to the waterfront, then choose:

### The Beatles Story

At the Royal Albert Dock. **£20 for adults, £11 for children aged 5 to 15.** Most people spend about **1.5 hours**. Book online in advance. See our [Beatles Story guide](/guides/beatles-story-visitor-guide).

### Royal Liver Building 360

A **70-minute guided tour** up the Royal Liver Building, finished in 1911, with the Liver Birds on its clock towers. **£16 for adults, £11 for children aged 5 to 15**, and **no under-5s**. There are **124 stairs above the 10th floor**. Tours usually start every 15 minutes from 9am, subject to demand.

## Lunch

Around the [Royal Albert Dock](/guides/royal-albert-dock-guide) or in Liverpool ONE. If you want something local, try scouse. Our [scouse guide](/guides/scouse-where-to-eat) lists places in town.

## Afternoon: the Pier Head

- **The Three Graces:** the Royal Liver Building, the Cunard Building and the Port of Liverpool Building
- **Museum of Liverpool:** free, and good for an hour on the city's story
- **Mersey Ferry River Explorer Cruise:** about 50 minutes with commentary. Book timed tickets online. See our [Mersey Ferry guide](/guides/mersey-ferry-cruise-guide)

## Early evening: Mathew Street

Finish at the **Cavern Club**, where live music runs all day from 11.15am. Adult entry is £6. **Under-18s must be with an adult and leave by 8pm**, so go before then with children. See our [Cavern Club guide](/guides/cavern-club-visitor-guide).

## Tips

- **Wear comfortable shoes.** It's a lot of walking, some of it uphill to the cathedral
- **Book ahead** for The Beatles Story and the ferry
- **Short on time?** Skip the ferry and spend longer at the Pier Head
