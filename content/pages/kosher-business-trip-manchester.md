A few days of meetings in Manchester or at MediaCityUK is much easier when you've sorted food before you arrive. This guide covers the practical side. We checked it on 24 November 2026.

## Food: use the official lists

- The **Manchester Beth Din (MBD)** publishes an official **licensee list**, with sections for **butchers, bakeries, caterers, restaurants and takeaways, groceries, milk, confectionery and fishmongers**.
- Other kashrut authorities, including **KLBD** and the **London Beth Din**, publish their own lists and product guides.
- Licences change, so **check the current list** before you buy, and look for the certificate in the shop.
- Our [kosher food in Manchester guide](/guides/kosher-food-manchester-visitors-guide) explains how the different authorities work.

## Meetings, conferences and client meals

1. **Ask early.** Tell the organiser or venue at least a couple of weeks ahead that you need kosher meals.
2. **Ask for sealed meals** from a supervised caterer, with the hechsher shown on the packaging.
3. **Get it in writing.** A confirmation email avoids surprises on the day.
4. **Client dinners.** Suggest a restaurant on the licensee list, or a lunch you can bring in.

## Where to stay

On a trip of more than a night or two, a kitchen of your own makes a big difference.

- On Book Homes, hosts can declare a **kosher kitchen** and **Shabbat-friendly features**, such as an urn, a hotplate, lamp timers, a non-electric key and no motion-sensor lights.
- These are **host declarations, not certification**. Ask the host exactly how the kitchen is set up and decide with your own rabbi.
- Filter for these homes on our [kosher homes page](/kosher-homes). Our [kosher-friendly holiday homes guide](/guides/kosher-friendly-holiday-homes-manchester) explains what to ask.
- If you're staying over Shabbat, see [Shabbat in Manchester](/guides/shabbat-in-manchester-visitor-guide).

**Shul, mikvah and services:** contact the local community or the Manchester Beth Din directly. We don't publish community locations or times.
