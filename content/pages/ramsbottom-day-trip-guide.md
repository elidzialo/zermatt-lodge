Ramsbottom feels like the countryside, but it's still in Greater Manchester. You can ride a heritage train in, climb a hill to a Victorian tower, and be back in Manchester by the evening. We checked these details on 25 November 2026. Winter train days are limited, so check timetables first.

## Getting there

- **Heritage train:** the **East Lancashire Railway** runs from **Bury Bolton Street**, calling at Burrs Country Park and Summerseat, then Ramsbottom, Irwell Vale and Rawtenstall. Reach Bury on the Metrolink Bury line first. Our [Bury guide](/guides/bury-town-visitor-guide) has the details.
- **By car:** from **junction 1 of the M66**, follow signs for Ramsbottom. The station (BL0 9AL) is in the town centre with **limited free parking** nearby and more car parks around town.
- **By bus:** buses run from Bury. Use the Bee Network app for current routes and times.

## Peel Tower and Holcombe Hill

- **Peel Tower** (the Peel Monument) is **128 feet (39 metres)** tall. It was opened on **9 September 1852** in memory of **Sir Robert Peel**.
- It stands on **Holcombe Moor at about 1,100 feet (335 metres)** above sea level, so the views are big on a clear day.
- **The climb is steep.** You can walk up from the town centre, from Holcombe Brook to the south or from Stubbins to the north. Wear walking boots in winter: the paths get muddy.
- **Going inside:** the tower is owned by Bury Council and only opens occasionally, usually when the flag is flying. Don't count on it.

## In the valley

- **Nuttall Park:** woodland, a river walk by the Irwell and a children's playground.
- **Irwell Sculpture Trail:** look out for the "Tilted Vase" near the station.
- **Irwell Works Brewery** offers tours and has a shop.
- The town centre has plenty of cafés, pubs and restaurants for lunch.

## Station access

The railway says platform 2 has flat, street-level access. To reach platform 1 you are escorted across the barrow crossing by staff, and the ramp onto the train is fairly steep. There are disabled parking bays.

## In December

The East Lancashire Railway runs **Santa Specials** in December. See our [Santa Specials 2026 guide](/guides/east-lancashire-railway-santa-specials-2026).

## A simple day plan

1. Tram to Bury, then the heritage train to Ramsbottom.
2. Walk up to Peel Tower in the morning while the light is good.
3. Lunch in town.
4. Nuttall Park in the afternoon, then the train back to Bury.
