Arriving at a holiday house with a group, tired from the drive, and then having to find a supermarket is nobody's idea of a good start. A **supermarket delivery booked for just after check-in** solves it. Here's how to plan one, what it costs, and what to order.

We checked the delivery terms below on 22 November 2026. They change, so check your supermarket's site when you order.

## Step by step

1. **Ask the host** whether deliveries are fine, and where the driver should park and knock
2. **Add the rental as a new address** in your supermarket account
3. **Book a slot after check-in.** If check-in is 3pm, book from 4pm or later to allow for traffic
4. **Add delivery notes:** house name or number, your mobile, and any parking instructions
5. **Say whether substitutions are OK.** Turn them off for allergy or kosher items
6. **Make sure someone is in** for the whole slot

## Minimum spends

| Supermarket | Minimum basket | If you spend less |
|---|---|---|
| Tesco | £50 | £5 minimum basket charge |
| Sainsbury's (Delivery Pass customers) | £50 for slots from 7 April 2026 | £7.50 for home delivery |

Delivery charges on top vary by slot. For a group of six or seven, a first shop will usually be over £50 anyway.

## Timing tips

- **Book early for busy dates.** Christmas week, bank holidays and Grand National week fill up fast
- **Don't book the morning of arrival.** You won't be in yet
- **Last day:** don't over-order. Plan to use everything up by checkout

## What to order for the first night

A simple list for a group of six or seven:

**Breakfast**
- Milk, bread, butter, cereal, eggs, bacon or a veggie alternative, fruit, juice

**First dinner (easy)**
- Two big pasta bakes or curries with rice, a bag of salad, garlic bread

**Basics**
- Tea, coffee, sugar, cooking oil, salt and pepper
- Washing-up liquid and dishwasher tablets, if the host doesn't provide enough
- Kitchen roll and bin bags

**Packed lunches**
- Wraps or rolls, fillings, crisps, fruit, water

## Kosher or special diets

Supermarkets stock some kosher-certified products, but check the hechsher on each item and turn off substitutions. For fresh kosher food in the north west, check the **Manchester Beth Din** (MBD) licensee list on its official website.
