If the weather is grim, TraffordCity has a guaranteed winter day: real snow indoors, then food and shops next door. This guide covers the snow slope that most people still call Chill Factore, plus the Trafford Centre. We checked these details on 25 November 2026. Christmas hours differ, so check both websites before you set off.

## The Snow Centre Manchester (formerly Chill Factore)

- **New name:** Chill Factore has become **The Snow Centre Manchester**, to match its sister site in Hemel Hempstead. A ski news site reported the change on 1 May 2026.
- **Where:** **7 Trafford Way, Trafford Park, Urmston, M41 7JA**, just off **junction 10 of the M60**.
- **The slopes:** a **180-metre** main slope of **real snow**, plus a separate **beginner slope** where lessons start.
- **Snow Park:** tubing, luge and sledging, so non-skiers can join in.
- **Parking:** **free**, over **300 spaces**, with disabled bays.
- **Standard hours:** most days from 10am to 10pm, earlier starts on Wednesday and weekends, and a shorter day on Tuesday. On Tuesdays the main slope closes at 6pm. It is **closed on Christmas Day**, with special hours over the holidays.

### What to wear and bring

- It's **always below freezing** inside. Dress warmly in layers.
- Waterproof jackets and trousers can be **hired**, and **socks and gloves** are on sale.
- **Skis or snowboards, boots and helmets** come with lessons and lift passes. Helmets come with Snow Park passes.
- Bring your booking confirmation and arrive about **30 minutes early** to check in and change.

## The Trafford Centre

- **Where:** **M17 8AA**, about five miles west of Manchester city centre.
- **By tram:** the **Metrolink Trafford Park line** runs direct from the city centre. There is also a bus station on site.
- **Parking:** about **10,000 spaces** across 18 car parks. Time limits apply in some car parks, overnight parking isn't allowed, and match-day parking is enforced. **Premium Parking** costs **£7.50** for the day and can be pre-booked.
- **Hours in late November 2026:** Monday to Friday **10am to 10pm**, Saturday **10am to 9pm**, Sunday **12pm to 6pm**. Hours change over Christmas.
- **Quiet Hour:** every **Wednesday, 10am to 11am**, with less noise and lower lighting.

## Also nearby

**LEGOLAND Discovery Centre** and **SEA LIFE Manchester** are at the Trafford Palazzo by the Trafford Centre. Adults can only go into LEGOLAND with a child. See our [LEGOLAND and SEA LIFE guide](/guides/legoland-discovery-and-sea-life-manchester).

## A family day plan

1. Morning session at The Snow Centre (book a lesson or Snow Park slot ahead).
2. Lunch at the Trafford Centre.
3. Afternoon at SEA LIFE or LEGOLAND, or a film.
4. Home by tram or car before the evening traffic.
