Prestwich sits on the north side of Manchester, in the borough of Bury. For crews working across north Manchester, Bury or the M60 corridor, it makes a practical base: houses with parking, the tram into town and quick access to the motorway.

## Getting around

- **Metrolink.** Prestwich tram stop is on the Bury line, between Heaton Park and Besses o' th' Barn. Trams run into Manchester city centre one way and to Bury the other. Useful for site meetings in town without moving the vans.
- **A56 Bury New Road.** The main road through Prestwich, running south towards Manchester and north to Whitefield and Bury.
- **M60 junction 17.** The A56 meets the M60 at Whitefield, junction 17, for the ring road around Manchester.

Always plan the drive to site at the time you start, not in the middle of the day.

## Work nearby

North Manchester General Hospital in Crumpsall, a short drive from Prestwich, is being rebuilt. Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust says Bovis holds the construction contract for the first phase, a new Outpatient Centre, with building expected to start in 2027, subject to approvals. ## Before you book

1. Ask for the **bed list**, so everyone has their own bed.
2. Tell the host your **van sizes** and how many vehicles you have. See [van parking in Greater Manchester](/guides/van-parking-contractor-stays-greater-manchester).
3. Check **check-in times** if the crew arrives late.
4. Agree the **paperwork**: on most homes you can tick a box at checkout for a company receipt with your company name.
5. Ask about **weekly or monthly** rates for a long job.

## A quiet residential area

These are family homes on residential streets. Keep noise down late at night and early in the morning, park considerately and follow the host's house rules. No parties.
