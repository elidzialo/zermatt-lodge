If your family is flying from Manchester, staying the night before saves a very early drive. If you're flying in, a home with a kitchen and beds for everyone is a calmer first night than a hotel. We checked these facts on 24 November 2026.

## How the airport connects

- **Tram:** the Metrolink stop is **inside Manchester Airport station**. Trams run **direct to Manchester Victoria**, on the Airport line through Wythenshawe. The line opened on 3 November 2014 and has 15 stops.
- **Tram times:** Visit Manchester says trams run **every 12 minutes** through the day, and **every 15 minutes** on Sundays and public holidays outside 9am to 5.30pm. First and last trams depend on where you're going; check tfgm.com.
- **Train:** trains to **Manchester Piccadilly** run **24 hours a day**, about **every 10 minutes**, taking about **20 minutes** on average. Late at night and early in the morning, TransPennine Express runs roughly hourly.
- **To the terminals:** a **5 to 15 minute walk** from the station, depending on your terminal.
- **Works:** Manchester Airport warned that Airport line trams would end at Firswood from 19 October to 1 November 2026 for improvement works. Check the Bee Network travel updates before any trip.

## From homes on Book Homes

- **Prestwich and Crumpsall:** take the **Bury line** tram into the city centre and change for the Airport line or a train from Piccadilly. Use the Bee Network journey planner for the quickest change at your time of day.
- **Salford:** take the tram or a train into the city centre, then the tram or a train to the airport.
- **Driving:** for a very early flight with children and cases, driving or a pre-booked taxi is often easiest. Check airport parking prices in advance.

## Why a home, not an airport hotel

1. **Space.** Families and groups can sleep under one roof instead of booking several rooms.
2. **A kitchen.** Make breakfast before you leave, or a proper meal when you land.
3. **Laundry.** Wash clothes before a long trip, or after one.
4. **Parking.** Several of the homes on Book Homes have free parking, so a friend can drop the car back.
5. **One price.** Every price on Book Homes is the total for the stay, with no guest booking fees.
