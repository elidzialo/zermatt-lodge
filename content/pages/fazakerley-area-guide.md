Fazakerley is a **residential suburb of north Liverpool**, in the **L9 and L10** postcodes, next to **Aintree, Croxteth, Gillmoss and Kirkby**. It isn't a tourist area, and that's the point: it's a quiet, practical base. **Fazakerley station** has direct Merseyrail trains to **Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes**, **junction 6 of the M57** is about **1.5 miles** away, and **Aintree University Hospital** and **Aintree Racecourse** are close by.

## A short history

The name comes from Old English words meaning roughly border, field and wood or clearing. Fazakerley was a township in the parish of Walton-on-the-Hill, became its own civil parish in 1866 and joined the city of Liverpool on **1 April 1922**.

During and after the Second World War, the **Royal Ordnance Factory Fazakerley** made weapons, including Lee-Enfield rifles and Sten and Sterling submachine guns. It closed in 1960, and the site became housing and the **Aintree Industrial Estate**.

The station opened in **1848** as Simonswood, was renamed Aintree, then took the name **Fazakerley in 1860** to avoid confusion with the Aintree station on another line.

## Getting around

### Train

Fazakerley station is on the Merseyrail **Northern line**, on the **Headbolt Lane branch** via Kirkby.

- **To Liverpool Central:** about **15 minutes**, direct. Trainline lists around **69 trains a day**, with the first at about 4.46am and the last at about 11.36pm on a sample day.
- **Frequency:** usually **four trains an hour** off-peak, dropping to **two an hour** in late evenings and on Sundays.
- **Step-free:** a footbridge with **lifts on both sides** links the ticket office to the platforms.
- **Staffed** through the day, with a street-level ticket office.
- **Cycles:** racks for four bikes and secure storage for 20.
- **Paying:** since February 2026 you can **tap in and out with a contactless card**, phone or watch on Merseyrail.

Heading north on the same line takes you to Kirkby and Headbolt Lane. Heading south, you pass through Sandhills, the station for match days at Anfield and Everton's Hill Dickinson Stadium.

### Bus

Stagecoach routes **17, 17A, 17X, 20 and 21** run to the city centre, with Arriva's **62 and 63** and other Merseytravel-supported services. Check current timetables on the Merseytravel website.

### Road

**Junction 6 of the M57** is about 1.5 miles from Fazakerley. A short drive north is **Switch Island**, where the M57 meets the **M58** (to Skelmersdale and Preston), the **A59** (to Ormskirk and Southport), the **A5036** (to Bootle and the docks) and the **A5758** (to Formby and Crosby). The M57 also links to the M62 for Manchester.

## What's nearby

### Aintree University Hospital

The hospital is in Fazakerley itself. If you're visiting a patient, staying nearby saves long daily journeys and hospital parking fees. See our [Aintree hospital accommodation guide](/guides/accommodation-near-aintree-university-hospital).

### Aintree Racecourse

Home of the **Grand National**, which in 2027 runs from **Thursday 8 to Saturday 10 April**, with the big race on the Saturday. The course is on the A59, about a mile from the M57 and M58. On race days, Merseyrail changes its timetables, including on the Kirkby line, so check before you travel. Our [guide to getting to Aintree from Fazakerley](/guides/getting-to-aintree-racecourse-from-fazakerley) has the details.

### Croxteth Country Park

A short drive away, Croxteth Country Park covers **500 acres** of woodland, pasture, ponds and streams. Liverpool City Council lists:

- **Croxteth Park Farm**, a working Victorian farm and one of the UK's leading Conservation Farm Parks
- A **walled garden**, a playground, an outdoor gym and a 5K running route
- A **cafe** in the courtyard
- **Free parking** in designated car parks

Opening times vary by attraction, so check before you go. It's a good half day out with children.

### Into the city

With trains every 15 minutes or so, the **waterfront museums**, **Royal Albert Dock**, shopping at **Liverpool ONE** and the **football grounds** are all easy day trips.

## Who Fazakerley suits

**Good for:**

- **Hospital visitors and staff** at Aintree University Hospital
- **Race goers** for the Grand National and other Aintree meetings
- **Contractors and crews** working in north Liverpool, Kirkby, Knowsley or along the M57 and M58
- **Families and groups** who want a house, a garden and parking rather than hotel rooms
- **Football fans** who'd rather take a short train than pay city-centre prices

**Less good for:**

- Visitors who want to walk to bars and restaurants every night
- Anyone who doesn't want to use a train, bus or car
