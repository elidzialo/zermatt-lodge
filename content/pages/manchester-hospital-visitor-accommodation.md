When someone is in hospital for more than a night or two, the family needs somewhere close to sleep, eat properly and do the washing. Hotels get expensive fast and don't give you a kitchen. A whole home nearby, booked for as long as you need, often works better.

We checked the facts below on the hospitals' own websites on 23 November 2026. For visiting times and ward rules, always check with the ward.

## North Manchester General Hospital, Crumpsall

- **Where:** Delaunays Road, Crumpsall, M8 5RB, about **3.5 miles north** of the city centre, according to Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust (MFT).
- **Tram:** **Abraham Moss** and **Crumpsall** stops are about a **15-minute walk** away.
- **Bus:** four stops are close to the main entrance. Use the TfGM journey planner.
- **Parking:** the Trust says parking is **limited and in high demand**, so allow extra time. Fees apply. There are two EV charging sockets in Car Park 1.
- **Building work:** MFT says construction of a new **Outpatient Centre** is expected to start in **2027**, as the first phase of a full rebuild due to finish in **2035**. Expect site works around the hospital.

Book Homes has a **5-bedroom house in Crumpsall** with free parking, and family houses in nearby **Prestwich**. Staying close and driving or taking the tram in saves paying for hospital parking every day.

## Royal Manchester Children's Hospital, Oxford Road

- MFT describes it as **the largest children's hospital in the UK**, with **371 paediatric beds**, on the Oxford Road campus shared with the Trust's specialist adult hospitals and next to the University of Manchester.
- **Saint Mary's** neonatal unit, with 60 cots, is in the same building.
- **Ronald McDonald House Manchester** offers **65 en-suite bedrooms, free of charge**, for families of children at the Children's Hospital and Saint Mary's neonatal unit. Ask the ward or the charity about a room first. If it's full, or other family members are coming, a nearby home is the next option.

## The Christie, Withington

- The Christie describes itself as **the largest single-site cancer centre in Europe**, treating **more than 64,000 patients a year** from across Greater Manchester and Cheshire.
- Its main site is on **Wilmslow Road, Withington**, in south Manchester.

## What to ask a host

1. **Flexible dates.** Discharge dates move. Ask whether you can extend, and what the cancellation terms are.
2. **Parking.** A space at the house means you can drive in at odd hours.
3. **Kitchen and washer.** You'll want proper meals and clean clothes on a long stay.
4. **Self check-in.** Useful if you arrive late from the ward.
5. **Quiet.** You'll need sleep. Our homes are on residential streets: strictly no parties or events - bookings found to be for parties are cancelled.
