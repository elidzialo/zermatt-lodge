If you're relocating to the Liverpool area with school-age children, the first thing to know is that **the council you're moving into handles your application**, not the one you're leaving. Liverpool, Sefton (Crosby, Formby, Maghull, Southport) and Knowsley (Kirkby, Huyton, Prescot) each run their own process. Mid-year moves are called **in-year admissions**, and councils aim to give you an answer within **15 school days**, though not during school holidays. Councils also want proof of your permanent address, so a short-term rental or holiday let usually won't count. This guide explains the process for each council and the key dates for September 2027.

## Which council?

The Liverpool City Region has several councils, and the boundaries don't always match what people call "Liverpool". A few examples:

| Area | Council |
|---|---|
| City centre, Fazakerley, Walton, West Derby, Allerton, Woolton | **Liverpool City Council** |
| Crosby, Formby, Maghull, Bootle, Southport | **Sefton Council** |
| Kirkby, Huyton, Prescot, Halewood | **Knowsley Council** |

Check the council for any address on the government's "find your local council" service before you apply. If you want a school in a different council area from your home, you still apply through your **home** council, and it coordinates with the other one.

## In-year admissions: moving mid-year

### Liverpool City Council

According to Liverpool City Council:

- If you're **moving into Liverpool** from another council area or from an independent school, apply online through the council's **Citizen Portal**. You may already have an account if you've applied for Reception, Year 7 or a school travel pass before.
- If you're **moving between Liverpool schools,** talk to your child's current school. It liaises with the new school.
- **Home-educated children** use the same online process.
- Applications should be processed within **15 school days**, under the School Admissions Code.
- Applications **can't be fully processed in school holidays,** because schools are closed.
- The council follows up weekly if schools don't respond on time.

### Sefton Council

Sefton coordinates in-year admissions for **all its schools, including academies**. Most Sefton secondary schools, and some primaries, are academies, which set their own oversubscription criteria.

- If you're **moving to Sefton**, use the **In Year Admission form (A1)**. Sefton says it's designed to be printed and filled in by hand. Use one form per child.
- If you're changing school for reasons other than a house move, use the **School Transfer form (T1)**.
- **Apply no earlier than 4 weeks** before you need the place.
- Sefton aims to tell you the outcome **within 10 school days**, and no later than **15 school days**.
- If none of your preferred schools can offer a place, Sefton must offer a place at the nearest school with a vacancy.
- Questions go to the in-year admissions team at iyadmissions@sefton.gov.uk.

### Knowsley Council

Knowsley says:

- You make a formal request by completing an **in-year application**.
- You should expect an outcome **within 15 school days**.
- Applications are **not usually processed in school holidays**.
- There's **no guarantee** of a place at your preferred school, or that brothers and sisters will be offered the same school.
- If your child has an **Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP)**, contact the Special Educational Needs Service before changing schools, as a different process applies.

## What counts as your address?

This is where many relocating families get caught out.

Councils allocate places based on your **permanent home address**. Sefton's in-year guide, for example, asks for:

- **Proof of your new address** if you've already moved, or of your **proposed address** if you're moving in the next 4 weeks
- Usually a **solicitor's letter confirming exchange and completion**, or a **tenancy agreement of at least 6 months**, plus evidence that you live there
- Sometimes proof that you've **disposed of your previous home**
- If you're moving in with family or friends, proof that they live at that address

A **holiday let, serviced apartment or short stay** while you look for a home won't normally count as your permanent address. Many families stay somewhere temporary for a few weeks, secure a rental or complete a purchase, and then apply. If timing is tight, ask the council what evidence it will accept.

## September 2027 entry: Reception and Year 7

Starting school in Reception, or moving up to secondary school in Year 7, isn't an in-year admission. There's a separate, national timetable:

| | Year 7 (secondary) | Reception (primary) |
|---|---|---|
| Applications opened | 1 September 2026 | 1 September 2026 |
| Closing date | **31 October 2026** | **15 January 2027** |
| National offer day | **1 March 2027** | **16 April 2027** |

These dates are from Liverpool City Council. Sefton publishes the same closing dates and offer days, and Knowsley the same Reception dates, with its Reception applications opening on 12 September 2026.

### If you're moving around the deadline

- **Apply from your current address** by the closing date, through your current council.
- **Sefton** lets you update a Year 7 application with a confirmed house move until **8 January 2027**, and a Reception application until **19 February 2027**, if you send proof.
- Late applications are processed **after national offer day**. Liverpool City Council warns they're less likely to get a preferred school.

## Years 10 and 11

Liverpool City Council advises that transfers in **Years 10 and 11** may not be in a child's interest. GCSE courses, option groups and exam boards differ between schools, and option groups may be full. It asks you to discuss the move with your current headteacher first and checks that this has happened.

If you're moving with a child in Year 10 or 11, speak to the new school about their courses before you apply, and think about whether your child could finish at their current school.

## If you don't get a place

- **Join the waiting list.** In Sefton, waiting lists are kept in priority order under each school's admission rules, not by the date you applied, and run until 31 July.
- **Appeal.** You'll receive a letter explaining how to appeal. Appeals go to an independent panel.
- **Fair Access.** Children without a place may be referred to a Fair Access Panel, which recommends a school.

## Timing your move

- **Avoid applying just before a holiday.** Applications aren't fully processed when schools are closed. For Liverpool City Council schools, Christmas runs from 21 December 2026 to 1 January 2027, February half term is 15 to 19 February 2027 and Easter is 26 March to 9 April 2027.
- **Visit schools** while you're in the area house-hunting.
- **Keep copies** of every form and email.
