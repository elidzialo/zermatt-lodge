Liverpool and Manchester are close enough that you don't have to pick just one. The real question is where to sleep. Base yourself where most of your plans are, and take the train or drive to the other city for a day.

## How close are they?

TransPennine Express says **Liverpool Lime Street to Manchester Piccadilly** takes **as little as 51 minutes**, a distance of about **31 miles**, with direct trains through the day. It warns that journeys can take longer at weekends, on public holidays and during engineering work. Check the last train back before you set off, especially on a Sunday. By road, the M62 links the two cities.

## Choose Manchester if...

- **You're going to the football.** Manchester City and Manchester United both play at home through the winter. See our [Manchester football winter guide](/guides/manchester-football-winter-2026-27-stay) for dates and trams.
- **You want a bigger house.** Book Homes has **8 whole homes in Greater Manchester**, sleeping 4 to 12, including 4 and 5-bedroom houses in Prestwich and Crumpsall with free parking. See our [Manchester group accommodation guide](/guides/group-accommodation-manchester).
- **You need kosher-friendly options.** Some Manchester hosts declare a kosher kitchen or Shabbat-friendly features. See the [kosher homes page](/kosher-homes).
- **You're at a show or concert** at the Co-op Live, the AO Arena or one of the city centre theatres.

Browse [homes in Manchester](/stays-in/manchester).

## Choose Liverpool if...

- **You're going to Anfield, the Hill Dickinson Stadium or Aintree.** North Liverpool puts you close to all three.
- **You want the waterfront, the museums and the Beatles.** See [things to do in Liverpool with a group](/guides/things-to-do-in-liverpool-with-a-group).
- **There are up to 7 of you.** Zermatt Lodge is our 4-bedroom house in Liverpool, sleeping 7, with free parking, a garden and a pool table.

See [where to stay in Liverpool for a group](/guides/where-to-stay-in-liverpool-for-a-group).

In either city, a group of 6 or more usually does better in one house: one total price, a kitchen, a living room and parking. Our guide [Four hotel rooms or one house?](/guides/two-hotel-rooms-vs-one-house) runs the numbers.
