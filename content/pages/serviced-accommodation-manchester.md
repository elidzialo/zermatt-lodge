Serviced accommodation is a furnished home or apartment, with bedding, towels, utilities and wifi included, that you book like a holiday let but use like a home. In Manchester it suits people here for work, relocation, a hospital stay in the family or a long project. You get a kitchen, a washing machine and space to spread out, which matters once a trip runs past a few nights.

Book Homes now has **8 whole homes live across Greater Manchester**, in Prestwich, Crumpsall, Salford and elsewhere in the city. They sleep from 4 to 12 guests and most list parking. See every home on the [Manchester hub](/stays-in/manchester).

## Pick your base by where you'll be each day

Manchester traffic is the thing that makes or breaks a work stay. Two simple rules:

- **Stay near a tram stop.** Metrolink routes run out to Bury, Eccles, Manchester Airport, East Didsbury, Altrincham, Ashton-under-Lyne and Rochdale, and all of them meet in the city centre. From a home near the Bury line, for example, you can be in town without driving or paying for city centre parking.
- **Or stay near the job.** If your work is on a site with van access, a house with off-road parking close to the site beats a city centre flat.

Areas we cover:

- **Prestwich and north Manchester.** Larger family houses on quiet streets, most with free parking, near the Metrolink Bury line. See [homes in Prestwich](/stays-in/prestwich).
- **Salford.** Close to the city centre and MediaCityUK, home to BBC and ITV teams according to Salford City Council. See [homes in Salford](/stays-in/salford) and our [Salford serviced accommodation guide](/guides/serviced-accommodation-salford).
- **North-east Manchester.** Manchester City Council says the Victoria North programme will bring 15,000 new homes north-east of the city centre over the next two decades, so expect long-running construction work in this part of the city.

## What to check before you book

1. **The real bed list.** Count beds, not "sleeps". Ask which rooms have doubles, twins or sofa beds.
2. **Parking.** How many cars or vans, on the drive or on the street, and whether permits are needed.
3. **Wifi and a desk.** Ask for the speed if you work online, and whether there's a table you can work at.
4. **Laundry.** A washer and dryer save a lot of money on a stay of a week or more.
5. **Cleaning.** On long stays, ask whether a mid-stay clean or linen change is included or can be added.
6. **Invoices.** If your company pays, ask for an invoice in the company's name before you book. See [invoices and VAT for business stays](/guides/invoice-and-vat-for-business-stays).
7. **Cancellation terms.** Work dates change. Read the cancellation policy on the listing.

## Serviced accommodation vs a hotel

A hotel is simpler for one or two nights. For a week or more, or for three or more people, a whole home usually wins: one booking, one kitchen so you're not eating out every night, and a living room to share.
