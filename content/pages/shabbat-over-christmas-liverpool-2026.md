The Christmas school holidays are a popular time for Jewish families to get away, and in 2026 the calendar adds a twist: **Christmas Day is Friday 25 December** and **New Year's Day is Friday 1 January 2027**. Both are erev Shabbat. With **large shops closed on Christmas Day** and **trains not running on Christmas Day** in past years, the usual Friday routine of shopping and travelling in the morning won't work. Plan to **arrive and shop by Thursday 24 December**.

This guide uses published sources only. Confirm times and arrangements with the shul you plan to use. For homes where hosts have declared kosher kitchens or Shabbat features, see our [kosher homes page](/kosher-homes).

## The two Fridays

| Date | What's happening | What it means for Shabbat |
|---|---|---|
| **Thu 24 Dec** | Christmas Eve | Last chance to shop and travel. Some places may close early |
| **Fri 25 Dec** | Christmas Day, bank holiday | Large shops closed; trains didn't run in past years |
| **Sat 26 Dec** | Boxing Day (Shabbat) | Racing at Aintree and Everton at home: busy roads in north Liverpool |
| **Mon 28 Dec** | Bank holiday (Boxing Day substitute) | Reduced shop hours possible |
| **Thu 31 Dec** | New Year's Eve | Shops open, but busy; taxis scarce late at night |
| **Fri 1 Jan** | New Year's Day, bank holiday | Check shop hours; reduced transport likely |

## Shabbat times

Winter Shabbat in Liverpool starts early. **Sunset is at its earliest on 13 December 2026, at 3.54pm**, so candle lighting on both Fridays is mid afternoon. Princes Road Synagogue publishes weekly Shabbat times. Ask the shul you'll attend for the exact time.

## Food: plan for both weeks at once

Kosher supply in Liverpool is limited: the city has **one kosher deli, in Childwall**. Most visitors bring food or shop in north Manchester, where the **Manchester Beth Din** licenses butchers, bakeries and grocers in Prestwich, Whitefield and Salford.

Over Christmas, that takes extra planning:

1. **Ask shops about Christmas week hours** before you travel. Don't assume normal hours
2. **Order early.** Bakeries and butchers get busy before a long weekend
3. **Bring enough for both Shabbatot**, or plan a second shop on Tuesday 29 or Wednesday 30 December
4. **Freeze what you can** when you arrive
5. **Check the rental kitchen.** See our guide to [keeping kosher in a holiday rental](/guides/kosher-kitchen-holiday-rental-guide)

## Travel

The 2026 festive timetable for Merseyrail and buses hadn't been published when we checked. In past years Merseyrail **hasn't run on Christmas Day**, and the Combined Authority has warned of very limited buses around the holidays.

- **Travel by Wednesday 23 or Thursday 24 December**
- **If you're driving from Manchester**, avoid the M62 on Christmas Eve afternoon if you can
- **For New Year**, arrive on Thursday 31 December or earlier
