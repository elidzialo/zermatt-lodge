If you're searching for a kosher hotel in Liverpool, here's the honest answer: **we could not find one**. No source we checked lists a kosher-supervised hotel in the city, and we couldn't find a kosher restaurant either. Travel directories that promise "kosher hotels in Liverpool" don't name an actual supervised property. So most observant visitors do something else: they book a **self-catering house or apartment**, bring their own food, and stay within walking distance of the shul they'll use.

This guide explains how to do that well. For homes where hosts have declared a kosher kitchen, Shabbat set-up or a nearby shul, see our [kosher homes page](/kosher-homes). These are host declarations, not certification.

## Why self-catering beats a hotel for observant visitors

Even where a hotel calls itself "Shabbat friendly", you usually still face:

- **Electronic key cards** and automatic doors
- **Lifts** you may not want to use on Shabbat
- **Room service and breakfast** that aren't kosher
- **No way to warm food** safely on Shabbat

A house or apartment solves most of this. You control the kitchen, you can set up an urn and hotplate before Shabbat, and you can choose a property with a physical key or a lockbox. For a family or a group coming for a simcha, a whole house is often cheaper than several hotel rooms too.

## Where to stay

If you'll walk to shul on Shabbat, contact the local community directly for shul, mikvah and service details, then search for homes within walking distance and map the walk before you book. It's also the best way to find out about meals. Full details are in our [Shabbat in Liverpool guide](/guides/shabbat-in-liverpool-visitor-guide).

## Food: plan it before you travel

- **In Liverpool:** a small kosher deli. The Liverpool Kashrut Commission is the local authority. Check current supervision before you buy.
- **In Greater Manchester:** the Manchester Beth Din publishes an official list of licensed grocers, butchers, bakeries and caterers. Many Liverpool visitors stock up there or order ahead.
- **Bring sealed food** for Friday night and Shabbat lunch if you're arriving from elsewhere.

Our [kosher food in Liverpool guide](/guides/kosher-food-liverpool) has the detail.

## What to ask a host

Before you book any rental, ask:

1. **How do I get in?** A physical key or lockbox is easier than an app lock on Shabbat.
2. **Lights and heating:** can lights be left on, and is heating on a timer?
3. **Kitchen:** is there an oven, hob and space for your own urn and hotplate?
4. **Motion sensors:** are there sensor lights inside or outside?
5. **Walking route:** how far is your shul on foot, and is the route lit?

## Visiting a patient?

Merseyside Jewish Community Care says **kosher meals are available on request at all Merseyside hospitals**. If there's a problem, ask the ward or contact the local community directly.
