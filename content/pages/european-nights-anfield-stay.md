For a Champions League night at Anfield, stay over in Liverpool rather than driving home. Most European home games kick off at 8pm UK time, so the final whistle comes close to 10pm, and by the time you've queued for the Soccerbus or walked away from the ground it's late. Booking a bed for that night turns a stressful dash into one of the best evenings in football.

Here's how to plan a European night, and what to think about when you book.

## 2026/27 European home fixtures

Liverpool FC published these league-phase home games, all at **8pm UK time**:

- Wednesday 9 September: Liverpool v Atletico Madrid (played)
- Tuesday 20 October: Liverpool v Villarreal (played)
- **Wednesday 9 December: Liverpool v FC Porto**
- **Wednesday 27 January: Liverpool v RC Lens**

Knockout-round dates depend on how the league phase finishes. UEFA also uses a 5.45pm slot for some games, so check the time printed on your ticket.

## Why European nights are different

### They're midweek

Tuesday and Wednesday nights mean you're probably taking time off work, and so is everyone you're travelling with. A one-night stay with a lie-in the next morning makes that worthwhile.

### They finish late

An 8pm kick-off usually ends just before 10pm. In a knockout tie that goes to extra time and penalties, it's later still. Then there's the walk out, the queue for transport and the trip back into town.

### The atmosphere starts early

Many fans arrive hours before kick-off to see the team coach arrive and soak up the atmosphere on Walton Breck Road and around the Shankly Gates. If you're driving in from a distance on the day, you'll miss all of that.

## Getting to Anfield on a European night

The same rules apply as for any match. Liverpool FC says:

- **There's no paid parking at Anfield on match days**, and the area is a residents' parking zone with active enforcement.
- The **Soccerbus** runs from **Sandhills** station from two hours before kick-off until about 15 minutes before.
- The **917** express runs from **Commutation Row** near Lime Street.
- You can walk in from **Kirkdale** or **Sandhills** stations, or from Lime Street if you've got the legs for it.

Our [Anfield match day accommodation guide](/guides/anfield-match-day-accommodation) covers each route in detail, and our [match day parking guide](/guides/liverpool-match-day-parking-guide) explains the residents' zones.

## Getting back afterwards

This is the bit to plan before you leave.

- **Soccerbus back to Sandhills:** Liverpool FC says returns run for about 90 minutes after the game, with the last bus leaving Walton Lane about **75 minutes after the final whistle**.
- **501 to Lime Street:** the club lists the 501 Peoplesbus running from Walton Lane to Lime Street after the game.
- **Last trains:** Merseyrail runs fewer trains late in the evening. Look up the last departure on your line before you go, not at 10.30pm in a queue.
- **Taxis:** there are ranks near the ground, but demand is huge. Liverpool City Council reminds fans that black cabs must use the meter within the city and private hire cars must be pre-booked.

## Tips for European nights

- **Book your room when the fixture is confirmed.** Hotels near the city centre fill quickly for big ties, and away supporters are looking too.
- **Dress for the cold.** December and January evenings at Anfield are bitter, especially in the upper tiers.
- **Eat properly before the game.** Food queues at the ground are long and you won't be back until late.
- **Keep the next morning free.** Plan your drive or train home for late morning.
- **Make a day of it.** Liverpool FC runs a UEFA-themed tour around some European fixtures. See our [Anfield stadium tour guide](/guides/anfield-stadium-tour-guide).

## Sample plan for a Wednesday 8pm kick-off

- **3pm:** check in at your accommodation, drop the bags
- **4.30pm:** early dinner
- **5.45pm:** head into town or straight to Sandhills
- **6pm onwards:** Soccerbus or walk to Anfield, soak up the build-up
- **7.15pm:** through the turnstiles
- **About 9.55pm:** final whistle
- **10.30pm to 11pm:** Soccerbus or walk back, then train
- **Next morning:** slow breakfast, checkout at 11am, head home
