Aintree isn't just the Grand National. The racecourse runs jump racing through the autumn and winter, including Becher Chase Day in December over the famous National fences. Accommodation is much easier to find for these fixtures. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Liverpool, with free parking for two cars. It makes a relaxed base for a racing weekend without festival-week crowds.

This guide covers Aintree's other race days, how to get there and how to turn a race day into a Liverpool weekend.

## Aintree's race days beyond April

The Grand National Festival in April gets the headlines, but Aintree's jumps season also includes autumn and winter fixtures. For 2026, the Jockey Club's announcements list:

- **A November meeting** with the Grade 2 Molyneux Hurdle as its feature race
- **William Hill Becher Chase Day on Saturday 5 December**, which also features the William Hill Grand Sefton Chase

Fixtures and sponsors change from year to year, so check the Aintree events page before booking travel. Aintree has also advertised family-focused race days, such as a Halloween family fun day. These can be a gentler introduction to racing for children.

### Why the Becher Chase is special

The Becher Chase is run over the Grand National fences, including the one it's named after, Becher's Brook. For racing fans who can't face festival crowds, it's one of the best chances to see horses take on those famous fences at a much calmer meeting. See our [Becher Chase weekend guide](/guides/becher-chase-weekend-liverpool).

## Getting to Aintree Racecourse

The racecourse is on **Ormskirk Road, Aintree, L9 5AS**, on the A59, about a mile from the M57 and M58.

### From Fazakerley by train

Fazakerley is on Merseyrail's Northern line, Kirkby branch, and Aintree station is on the Ormskirk branch. Take a train from Fazakerley towards Liverpool, change at **Sandhills**, then catch a train towards Ormskirk to **Aintree**. Aintree station is beside the racecourse.

On normal days the Northern line runs frequently in the daytime, with fewer trains in the evenings and on Sundays. Check the Merseyrail app for your race day, especially for the journey home after the last race.

### By car

Because Fazakerley is so close, driving to a non-festival fixture is often realistic. Check the racecourse's directions and parking page for the day you're going, as arrangements vary by fixture. After the last race, traffic on the A59 and around the course can be slow for a while. Some people have a drink in the racecourse bars or go for a walk while it clears.

### By taxi

For a group of four or more, a taxi between Fazakerley and the racecourse can cost about the same as everyone's train fares. Book your return in advance on busy days.

## Turning a race day into a weekend

### November

An autumn meeting pairs well with a city day: the [Royal Albert Dock](/guides/royal-albert-dock-guide), the Museum of Liverpool or a [Mersey ferry cruise](/guides/mersey-ferry-cruise-guide). If you're bringing children, add an afternoon at Croxteth Hall, close to Fazakerley.

### December

Becher Chase Day falls in Liverpool's Christmas season. The city's festive programme includes ice skating at St George's Hall and a Christmas Wonderland at the Pier Head, alongside pop-up markets. See our [Liverpool Christmas markets guide](/guides/liverpool-christmas-markets-guide). Coming back to a warm house with a big kitchen beats a cold walk back to a hotel.

## New to racing? A few basics

- **Arrive before the first race** so you can look around, find the parade ring and get your bearings.
- **The parade ring** is where you see the horses up close before each race. It's a good way to pick a horse if you're having a small bet.
- **Betting is optional.** The Tote and on-course bookmakers take bets, but many people simply enjoy the spectacle. Set a budget before you go and stick to it.
- **Racecards** list the runners, jockeys and race times. They make the day much easier to follow.
- **Watch at least one race by the rail** and one from the stands. The sound of the horses going past is something else.
- **Leave a little time after the last race** for the crowds to thin before heading for the train or car.

## Race day checklist for winter fixtures

- **Warm layers and a waterproof.** Aintree is open and can be windy in December.
- **Proper shoes** for grass and gravel
- **Tickets on your phone, plus a screenshot** in case signal is poor
- **A plan for getting home**, with train times saved or a taxi booked
- **Cash or card for the Tote** and refreshments
