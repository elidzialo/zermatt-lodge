Getting a big group together in Manchester, for a family visit, a wedding, a reunion or a milestone birthday, is easier when everyone is under one roof. One house means one booking, one kitchen and one living room where everyone can actually sit together.

Book Homes has **8 whole homes in Greater Manchester**. The largest sleep **8, 11 and 12 guests**, and most list free parking. See them all on the [Manchester hub](/stays-in/manchester).

## Where the group houses are

- **Prestwich.** Several of our larger family houses are here, including 4-bedroom homes for 8 and 12 guests. Prestwich and Heaton Park stops are on the Metrolink Bury line, with trams into Manchester Victoria. Heaton Park itself, which Manchester City Council calls "the biggest and best park in the NorthWest", is next door. See [homes in Prestwich](/stays-in/prestwich).
- **Crumpsall.** A 5-bedroom house for up to 11, with a pool table and free parking, also in north Manchester.
- **Salford.** Smaller homes close to the city centre. See [homes in Salford](/stays-in/salford).

## One house or several hotel rooms?

For a group of 8, you'd need four hotel rooms or more. A house gives you:

- **One total price** on Book Homes, with no guest booking fees
- **A kitchen**, so breakfast and at least some dinners cost supermarket prices
- **Space to be together**, which a row of hotel rooms doesn't
- **Parking** at most of our homes, rather than paying a city centre car park

Our guide [Four hotel rooms or one house?](/guides/two-hotel-rooms-vs-one-house) runs through the sums, using Liverpool as the example. The same logic applies in Manchester.

## Planning tips for groups

1. **Get the bed list first.** "Sleeps 12" may include sofa beds. Decide who sleeps where before you book. Our [group booking checklist](/guides/group-booking-checklist) helps.
2. **Agree the money early.** Decide how you'll split the cost and the shopping.
3. **Check parking for every car.** Ask how many cars fit on the drive and what the street rules are.
4. **Mind the school holidays.** Manchester City Council lists the Christmas break as 18 December 2026 to 4 January 2027, and February half term as 12 to 22 February 2027 (school closes and reopens). Book early for those weeks.
5. **Kosher-friendly needs?** Some Manchester hosts declare a kosher kitchen or a Shabbat-friendly set-up. Filter for them on the [kosher homes page](/kosher-homes). These are host declarations, not a certification.

## House rules

These are family homes on quiet residential streets. Strictly no parties or events - bookings found to be for parties are cancelled. Hen and stag groups should look elsewhere.
