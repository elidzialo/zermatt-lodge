## Getting to Co-op Live

- **Address:** 1 Sports City Way, Etihad Campus, Manchester, **M11 3DU**.
- **Tram:** **Etihad Campus** stop is directly next to the venue on the **Ashton to Piccadilly line**. Trams run about **every 7.5 minutes** from the city centre, and until **1am on Fridays and Saturdays**. **Velopark** stop is closed after events.
- **Walk:** about **25 minutes** from Piccadilly station on the step-free CityLink route. Follow the blue lampposts.
- **Parking:** limited official parking, **pre-booked only** on event days. Free **Park and Ride** at **Ashton Moss, Ashton West and Ladywell**. A residents' parking scheme operates around the arena.
- **Taxis:** drop-off and pick-up on **Edwin Road, M11 3ER**. A free pick-up point opens from **10pm**.
- **Traffic:** Co-op Live says congestion usually builds about **2 hours** before a show.

## Getting to the AO Arena

- The AO Arena is **immediately north of the city centre**, partly **above Manchester Victoria station**.
- **From Prestwich and Crumpsall:** Bury line trams run straight into **Victoria**, so you can get to the arena with no change.

## Venue facts

- **Co-op Live** opened on **14 May 2024**. It holds about **20,500 seated** and **23,500** with standing.
- **The AO Arena** opened in **1995** and holds about **23,000**.
