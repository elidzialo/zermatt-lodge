Corporate housing is a **fully furnished home rented for work**, usually for **30 days or longer**, with **utilities, wifi and housewares included** in one rate. Companies book it for relocating employees, project teams, secondments and new starters who need somewhere to live while they find a permanent home. The booker is often HR or a relocation agency, not the person who stays.

This guide explains how it works and what to check when booking in Liverpool.

## What it includes

A typical corporate let comes with:

- **Furniture, kitchenware and linens**
- **Utilities and wifi** in the rate
- **Often cleaning and parking**
- **One monthly price**, so there are no separate bills to set up

That "all-in" set-up is the point: the person can move in with a suitcase and start work the next day.

## How long stays run

Industry guides describe corporate housing stays as running from **about 30 days to a year**, with **relocations and project stays often lasting two to three months**. Extensions are common when a project overruns or a house purchase is delayed.

## Corporate housing vs hotels and serviced apartments

- **Extended-stay hotel:** nightly pricing, daily services, compact rooms
- **Corporate housing:** a residential home, monthly pricing, a full kitchen and room to live and work
- **Serviced accommodation:** a broader term covering stays from a couple of nights up. See [what is serviced accommodation?](/guides/what-is-serviced-accommodation)

For stays of several weeks, a furnished home usually costs less per month than a hotel, and it's far easier for a family.

## Who uses it in Liverpool

- **Relocating staff and families** moving to Merseyside
- **Project teams** on construction, port or industrial work
- **NHS and healthcare staff** on contracts or placements
- **Consultants and secondees** at city-centre offices
- **Families displaced** by insurance claims

## Checklist for HR and relocation teams

1. **Commute:** where is the office or site, and how long is the journey at rush hour?
2. **Beds:** check the actual bed layout for a family or team
3. **Parking:** city-centre blocks often have none
4. **Wifi and a workspace** for remote days
5. **Laundry:** a washer and dryer for longer stays
6. **Cleaning:** included, weekly or on request?
7. **Paperwork:** whose name is on the booking, invoices in the company's name, and VAT. See our [invoice and VAT guide](/guides/invoice-and-vat-for-business-stays)
8. **Flexibility:** can the stay be extended or shortened, and on what terms?

## Choosing an area

Liverpool's city centre suits single professionals working in the business district. Families and teams with cars often prefer a house outside the centre, with gardens, parking and room to spread out.
