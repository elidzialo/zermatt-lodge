The honest answer is that tickets for Liverpool at Anfield and Everton at the Hill Dickinson Stadium go mainly to club members, so the first step is joining the right membership and learning how each club's sales and ballots work. Liverpool sells most tickets through All Red membership sales and ballots; Everton gives its Forever Blue+ members first priority through a ballot before each game. Buy only through official channels, because selling football tickets without the club's authority is a criminal offence in England. Once you have tickets, Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Liverpool, about 15 minutes by train from Liverpool city centre.

## Liverpool FC: how home tickets are sold

Anfield's capacity is **61,276**, according to the club, and demand still far exceeds supply. Liverpool's home ticket information sets out the main routes for men's Premier League games.

### 1. All Red membership

To have a realistic chance of a standard ticket, you need a paid **All Red** membership. For 2026-27 the club lists:

- **All Red Full:** access to ticket sales for cup and Premier League matches at Anfield, plus late availability sales
- **All Red Light:** access to cup and Premier League ticket sales at Anfield
- **Video** and **Junior** options, which aren't mainly about tickets

The club is clear that access is "subject to ticketing criteria and availability". Joining doesn't guarantee a ticket.

### 2. All Red Ticket Sales and credits

Most general admission tickets are sold in two big sales:

- **July** for games up to 31 December
- **November** for games from 1 January onwards

Priority depends on **credits**, built up by buying tickets in previous seasons. For 2026-27:

- Members with **13+ Premier League home credits** are guaranteed a ticket for every game in the sale
- Members with **4+ credits** could register for seven of the 19 home league games in the July sale

If you're new, you won't have credits, which is why most first-time visitors go down the ballot or Ticket Exchange route.

### 3. Ballots

For the biggest games (the club lists Arsenal, Chelsea, Everton, Manchester City, Manchester United, Tottenham and the final home game), All Red members with 4+ credits can enter a ballot. Other All Red members can enter ballots for remaining games. You can apply for up to four people, and you're only charged if you win.

### 4. Additional Members Sales (Ticket Exchange)

Season ticket holders and members who can't go list their tickets on the official **Ticket Exchange**. These are resold in **Additional Members Sales**: registration opens about two weeks before a match and the sale is about one week before. For many visitors this is the most realistic route.

### 5. Local ballots

Liverpool runs a **Local Members Ballot** and a **Local General Ballot**, each with **500 tickets per game**, for people with a registered address in an **L postcode**.

### 6. Young Adult area

From 2026-27 there's a **Young Adult** area in the Lower Anfield Road stand at **£25.75**, for All Red Full or Light members aged 17 to 24.

### 7. Cup games and hospitality

Home cup games (European, FA Cup and League Cup) have their own priority system, including an Auto Cup Scheme. **Official hospitality** is on sale for 2026-27 Premier League and Champions League league phase games and is the main route for people without membership history, at a much higher price.

## Everton: how home tickets are sold

Everton moved to the **Hill Dickinson Stadium** at Bramley-Moore Dock for the 2025-26 season. According to Football Ground Guide's summary of the club's 2026-27 process:

- **Prices for home Premier League games are frozen** for 2026-27 at 2025-26 levels
- A new **18-21 price band** is being trialled
- **Forever Blue+ members** get first priority through a **ballot** that opens about **four weeks before each home game**. It runs from 10am on a Monday to 4pm on the Tuesday, with results on the Thursday
- If the ballot sells out, the **Official Resale Platform** opens to Forever Blue+ and Forever Blue members
- If tickets remain, a **members' sale** opens at 10am on the Friday, first come, first served
- **Season ticket holders** can transfer tickets to other members in their Friends and Family network

Everton says tickets listed for resale more than two weeks before a game are much more likely to sell, so it's worth checking the resale platform early as well as late.

In practice, many Everton games are easier to get into than Liverpool games, but derby matches and the biggest fixtures are very hard without membership history.

## Avoiding scams and touts

This matters for visitors in particular.

- **It's a criminal offence** for an unauthorised person to sell, or offer to sell, a ticket for a designated football match in England, under **section 166 of the Criminal Justice and Public Order Act 1994**. "Unauthorised" means without written permission from the home club or match organiser.
- **Clubs cancel tickets** they find have been passed on outside official channels, so you could be turned away at the turnstile.
- **Websites promising guaranteed tickets** at a large mark-up are a red flag. Many tickets are now digital and tied to a member's account.
- **Never pay by bank transfer** to a stranger for tickets.

## Timing your accommodation

Football fixtures are often moved for TV, sometimes only a few weeks before the game. Our advice:

1. **Get the ticket first** where you can, or at least know which sale or ballot you're entering.
2. **Book flexible accommodation** if you book early.
3. **Allow for evening kick-offs.** If a game moves to a Sunday or a weekday evening, you may need an extra night.
4. **Plan your journey home.** Both stadiums are in north Liverpool, so check how you'll get back before you book.
