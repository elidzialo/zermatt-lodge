Crews working in Manchester usually face the same choice: four or five hotel rooms, or one house. For more than a couple of nights, the house tends to win. You get a kitchen so the team isn't paying for every meal, a washer for work clothes, a living room for the evening and, at most of the homes on Book Homes, parking off the street.

Book Homes has **8 whole homes live in Greater Manchester**, including large houses in **Prestwich** and **Crumpsall** that sleep up to 12. See them on the [Manchester hub](/stays-in/manchester).

## Where the work is in north Manchester

Two long-running projects are worth knowing about if you're quoting or planning a crew:

- **North Manchester General Hospital rebuild.** Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust (MFT) says the hospital in Crumpsall is part of the Government's New Hospital Programme, with **up to £1.5 billion** invested over the next nine years. A new **Outpatient Centre** is the first phase. **Bovis** holds the construction contract, building is expected to start in **2027**, subject to approvals, the Outpatient Centre is due to open in **2029**, and the full hospital in **2035**.
- **Victoria North.** Manchester City Council says this joint venture with the Far East Consortium will create **15,000 new homes** in seven neighbourhoods north-east of the city centre over the next two decades, largely on brownfield land.

The Crumpsall and Prestwich houses on Book Homes are in the north of the city, which suits crews working on that side of Manchester.

## Getting around

- **Metrolink.** The Bury line runs through Prestwich and Crumpsall into Manchester Victoria, and other routes run to Eccles, Manchester Airport, East Didsbury, Altrincham, Ashton-under-Lyne and Rochdale. Useful for site meetings in town without moving the vans.
- **Motorways.** The M60 ring road circles the city, with the M62, M61 and M56 joining it. Plan around peak times.

## What to check before booking

1. **Beds, not "sleeps".** Get the bed list. Ask which rooms are doubles, twins or singles.
2. **Parking.** Number of vehicles, length of vans, drive or street, and any permit zones.
3. **Laundry.** Washer and dryer, and where wet gear can go.
4. **Kitchen.** Enough fridge and freezer space for a crew shopping once a week.
5. **Wifi.** For timesheets, drawings and evening calls.
6. **Check-in.** Self check-in helps if the crew arrives late.
7. **Invoice.** Company name and address on the invoice. See [invoices and VAT](/guides/invoice-and-vat-for-business-stays).
8. **Long-stay terms.** Ask about weekly or monthly rates and a mid-stay clean.

## House rules

These are homes on residential streets, so crews need to keep things neighbourly: quiet late at night, vans parked considerately and bins out on the right day. Strictly no parties or events - bookings found to be for parties are cancelled. Our [house share etiquette guide](/guides/trades-team-house-share-etiquette) is worth sending to the team.
