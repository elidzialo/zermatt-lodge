If you want a night out that feels truly Liverpool, the Royal Court is hard to beat. It's known for loud, warm, very local comedies, and the Christmas show is one of the most popular tickets of the winter. Groups of friends and work teams book it year after year.

We checked the details below on 23 November 2026.

## The Royal Court at a glance

- **Where:** **Roe Street**, in the city centre, a short walk from Lime Street station and Queen Square
- **Building:** an **Art Deco** theatre with a nautical-themed interior, opened on **17 October 1938** after a fire destroyed the old theatre
- **Architect:** James Bushell Hutchins
- **Listed:** Grade II, since 1990
- **Capacity:** **1,186 seats**
- **Known for:** Scouse comedies and musicals, including *The Scouse Nativity*, *The Royal*, *Lennon* and *Girls Don't Play Guitars*

## The cabaret stalls

The Royal Court's stalls aren't rows of seats. They're laid out in **three tiers of tables and chairs**, cabaret style, with a bar at the back. The stalls seat 290 this way. It's a big part of the fun for groups, as you sit together around a table.

**Tips for the stalls:**

- Book together so your group gets the same table
- Arrive early to get drinks in before the show
- If anyone in your group needs step-free access or a fixed seat, ask the box office before you book

## Christmas 2026: The Scouse Nativity

- **Dates:** **6 November 2026 to 16 January 2027**
- **Written by:** Kevin Fearon
- **Tickets:** from **£19**, via the Royal Court's website
- **The story:** a Scouse take on the nativity. It was first staged at the Royal Court in December 2017

The cast hadn't been announced when Arts City Liverpool wrote about it. The listings we found give no age guide, so check before booking for children. For family shows, see our [Liverpool panto guide](/guides/liverpool-panto-2026).

## Planning a group night

1. **Book early.** Friday and Saturday nights in December go first
2. **Eat before.** There are plenty of restaurants around Queen Square and Liverpool ONE
3. **Plan the trip home.** Taxis are in demand after a Christmas show, so pre-book or use the train
4. **Make a weekend of it.** Add the [Christmas markets](/guides/liverpool-christmas-markets-guide) or skating at [St George's Hall](/guides/st-georges-hall-events-guide), just up the road
