December is Manchester's busiest month for visitors. Here's what's on in December 2026, checked on 23 November 2026. Dates do change, so confirm on the official sites before you book tickets or travel.

## Christmas markets

- **Most sites:** open until **Tuesday 22 December 2026**.
- **Albert Square and Cathedral Gardens:** open until **Sunday 3 January 2027**.
- Entry is free. See our [Manchester Christmas Markets 2026 guide](/guides/manchester-christmas-markets-2026-stay) for the sites and tips.

## Theatre

- **Dick Whittington**, Opera House Manchester: **Saturday 5 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027**, starring Jason Manford and Ben Nickless.
- **Billy Elliot the Musical**, Palace Theatre: **Wednesday 2 December 2026 to Saturday 9 January 2027**.

## Light trails

Visit Manchester lists **Northern Lights at Heaton Park** (to 31 December), **Christmas at Dunham Massey** (to 2 January) and **RHS Glow** at RHS Garden Bridgewater in Salford (26 November to 30 December). Dates and prices are in our [Christmas light trails guide](/guides/christmas-light-trails-manchester-2026).

## Aviva Studios

Factory International lists two **Lightroom** productions in the Warehouse from **8 December 2026 to 10 January 2027**: **Prehistoric Planet: Discovering Dinosaurs** and **David Bowie: You're Not Alone**. Check the Factory International website for times.

## Concerts

Big arena listings change often. One example: **Culture Club, with ABC and Haircut 100**, is listed at the AO Arena on **Thursday 10 December 2026**. For everything else, check the AO Arena and Co-op Live websites.

## Football at home in December

These are the home Premier League dates from the fixture lists published by Sky Sports. Dates and kick-off times often move for TV, so check the clubs' websites before you book.

**Manchester City (Etihad Stadium):**
- Wed 2 Dec: v Leeds
- Sat 12 Dec: v Chelsea
- Sat 19 Dec: v Hull

**Manchester United (Old Trafford):**
- Sat 5 Dec: v Coventry
- Sat 26 Dec (Boxing Day): v Nottingham Forest
- Wed 30 Dec: v Sunderland

More on trams and match-day planning in our [Manchester football winter guide](/guides/manchester-football-winter-2026-27-stay).

## Chanukah

Chanukah 2026 begins on the evening of **Friday 4 December** and ends on **Saturday 12 December**. See our [Chanukah family stay guide](/guides/chanukah-manchester-2026-family-stay) for kosher-friendly homes declared by hosts.

## School holidays

Manchester City Council lists schools closing on **Friday 18 December 2026** and reopening on **Monday 4 January 2027**. Family homes go quickly for those weeks.
