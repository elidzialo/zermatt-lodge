Crumpsall is a residential part of north Manchester, between the city centre and Prestwich. Most visitors come for North Manchester General Hospital, for family nearby, or because a big house here costs less than several hotel rooms in town. We checked these facts on 24 November 2026.

## Where it is

- About **3 miles (5 km) north** of Manchester city centre, in the **City of Manchester**.
- **Heaton Park** is just to the north, and **Prestwich** is beyond it.

## Getting around

- **Three Metrolink stops** on the **Bury line**: **Crumpsall** (in the centre of the area), **Bowker Vale** (north-eastern edge) and **Abraham Moss** (the newest).
- Bury line trams run mostly every 12 minutes, with a 6-minute service at peak times, into **Manchester Victoria** and on through the city.
- **Driving:** a car is handy for the hospital at odd hours and for days out. Check parking rules on your street with the host.

## North Manchester General Hospital

- **Where:** Delaunays Road, Crumpsall, M8 5RB.
- **Tram:** Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust says **Abraham Moss** and **Crumpsall** stops are about a **15-minute walk** away.
- **Parking:** the Trust says parking is limited and in high demand, and fees apply.

## Local green space and history

- **Crumpsall Park** has an obelisk and is looked after by the Friends of Crumpsall Park, who run the annual **Crumpsall Carnival**.
- **Sir Humphrey Chetham** was born in Crumpsall in 1580. He founded **Chetham's Library** in the city centre, described as the oldest public library in the English-speaking world.
- **Heaton Park** is close. See our [Heaton Park guide](/guides/heaton-park-prestwich-visitor-guide).
