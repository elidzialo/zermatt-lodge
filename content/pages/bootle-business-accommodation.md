Bootle is a busy place to work, and many business visitors base themselves in a **nearby house or serviced apartment**. **Bootle New Strand** station has trains **every 15 minutes Monday to Saturday**, and **Sandhills is about 6 minutes away** for connections across the Northern line. If you're driving, or bringing a crew for the **Bootle Strand redevelopment**, a house with its own parking saves daily fees. **Zermatt Lodge** in Liverpool sleeps 7 with **free parking for 2 cars**, and trains run into Liverpool Central.

## Who works in Bootle

Bootle, in the borough of Sefton just north of Liverpool, has a mix of public sector offices, port business and regeneration projects.

- **Health and Safety Executive (HSE).** The HSE's **headquarters** is at **Redgrave Court, Merton Road, Bootle, L20 7HS**. The HSE says its offices aren't open to the public, so visitors will be staff, contractors or invited guests.
- **Sefton Council.** The council owns Bootle Strand and is leading its regeneration.
- **The docks**, reached via the A5036, plus Everton's Hill Dickinson Stadium at Bramley-Moore Dock to the south.
- **Bootle Strand.** The town-centre shopping centre, owned by the council since 2017, is being redeveloped (see below).

## The Bootle Strand regeneration

If you're a contractor, this is the big local project.

- **Approved:** Sefton Council's planning committee approved the first of four phases in **January 2025**, in plans reported at about **£20m**.
- **Works:** demolition began in **June 2025**. The council said in August 2025 that the main construction phase was scheduled to start in **early 2026**, with completion expected in **spring 2027**.
- **Phase 1:** refurbishing the former Marks & Spencer building, creating new public spaces including **Mons Square**, and improving links to the canalside.
- **Funding:** the council and the Liverpool City Region committed over **£18m** of extra capital funding.
- **Local jobs:** the council says every vacancy on the project so far has been filled by a local person through Sefton at Work.

Projects like this bring site managers, specialist trades and consultants for weeks or months at a time, often from outside the area.

## Getting to Bootle

### By train

- **Bootle New Strand** is on the Merseyrail **Northern line**, in the town centre. Trains run **every 15 minutes Monday to Saturday** and every 30 minutes on Sundays, with step-free ramps to both platforms.
- Southbound trains run to **Liverpool Central**.
- Sefton Council says Bootle New Strand to **Sandhills** takes about **6 minutes**. Sandhills is where the Southport branch meets the Ormskirk and Kirkby branch, so it's the usual place to change.
- **From the Kirkby or Ormskirk branches:** the journey usually means a change at Sandhills. Contactless Tap & Go also works on Merseyrail.

### By car

The **A5036** runs from **Switch Island**, where the M57 and M58 end, to Bootle and the docks. The **Strand multi-storey** on Vermont Way is the main town-centre car park. On **Everton match days** Sefton Council promotes it as a park and ride for Hill Dickinson Stadium, open until 11pm, so expect it to be busier.

### Match days

When Everton play at home, roads around Regent Road and Derby Road get busy, and the **939 shuttle** runs from Bootle Strand bus station to the stadium. Check the fixture list before booking site deliveries or meetings on a Saturday.

## Choosing where to stay

| Option | Good for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| City-centre hotel | Solo visitors, one or two nights | Daily parking costs, travel back out to Bootle |
| City-centre serviced apartment | Small teams wanting a kitchen | Often no guest parking |
| **House outside the city centre** | **Crews and teams, longer stays, vans and cars** | Check the commute at rush hour |

For a team, a house usually means **separate bedrooms, a kitchen, a washer and parking**, which matter more than a city view after a long shift.

## Questions to ask before you book

- How many cars or vans can park, and is it off-street?
- Are there **separate beds** for colleagues who don't share?
- Is there **wifi** good enough for evening admin?
- Can you get an **invoice** in the company's name? See our [invoices and VAT guide](/guides/invoice-and-vat-for-business-stays).
- What's the **maximum stay**, and is there a **monthly rate**?
