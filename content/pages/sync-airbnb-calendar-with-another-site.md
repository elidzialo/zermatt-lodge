Calendar sync is what lets you take bookings on more than one site without double booking. Every major platform supports iCal links: one link to send your booked dates out, and a place to paste other sites' links in. You need both directions.

## Step 1: export your Airbnb calendar

1. On Airbnb, open **Calendar** and choose the listing.
2. Open the availability settings and find **Connect to another website** (sometimes shown as connected calendars).
3. Copy the **export** link. It ends in .ics.

Airbnb says that once you export, changes on your Airbnb calendar are reflected automatically on the other site's calendar.

## Step 2: paste it into the other site

On Book Homes, open your listing in the host dashboard, go to **Sync your other calendars** and paste the link. We read it every 15 minutes and close those dates. Other sites have a similar box, often called "import calendar".

## Step 3: import the other site's calendar into Airbnb

1. Copy the other site's export link. On Book Homes it is shown as **Export: your Book Homes calendar**.
2. In Airbnb, choose **Import calendar**, paste the link and give it a name.
3. Make sure the link ends in .ics, as Airbnb only accepts those.

## How often each side updates

| Direction | How often |
|---|---|
| Airbnb reading other calendars | About every 3 hours, or when you press Refresh |
| Book Homes reading your Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo or Guesty link | Every 15 minutes |

Airbnb also says imported calendars cover up to 2 years of data and that you cannot pause an imported calendar.

## Tips that prevent double bookings

- **Connect every channel both ways**, not just one direction.
- **Refresh Airbnb by hand** after a booking on another site.
- **Block dates everywhere** when you go away or do repairs, or block them in one calendar that every site reads.
- **Use a channel manager** if you run several homes; most of them give you one iCal link per home.
