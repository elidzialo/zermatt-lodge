North Liverpool is a great base for family days out. Within a short drive or train ride you've got a country park with a farm, a drive-through safari, a beach with 100 iron statues, red squirrel woods and some of the best free museums in the country. Here's our pick, with rough drive times from Fazakerley (they vary with traffic, so always check a live route) and tips for each.

## Close to home (under 15 minutes)

### Croxteth Hall and Country Park

**Rough drive from Fazakerley:** around 10 minutes

A huge country park with woodland walks, a Victorian walled garden and **Home Farm**.

- **Walled garden:** daily, 10am to 3pm, last admission 2:30pm
- **Country park:** open all year. The inner park opens from 7am, closing at 6:30pm from April to October and 5:30pm from November to March.
- **Car park:** 7am to 7pm in summer, 7am to 6pm in winter
- **Home Farm** is run separately, so check its own hours before you go.
- **The hall** opens for tours and events, so contact it first.

**Good for:** toddlers to grandparents, and a first-afternoon outing after check-in. See our [Croxteth Hall visitor guide](/guides/croxteth-hall-visitor-guide).

### Local parks

**Walton Hall Park** and **Stanley Park** (between Anfield and Goodison) are good for a run-around, bike ride or picnic.

## Half-day trips (15 to 30 minutes)

### Knowsley Safari

**Rough drive:** around 20 to 30 minutes via the M57, from junction 2

Drive your own car through the safari, then spend the rest of the day in the park.

- The park lists closing at 4:30pm, with **last entry to the Safari Drive at 3pm**. You'll be refused entry if you arrive after the last entry time, so aim for a morning start.
- Postcode for the sat nav: **L34 4AN**
- Weekday and seasonal hours can differ from weekends, so check the opening times page the day before.

**Good for:** all ages, especially when the weather's mixed, as you're in the car. See our [Knowsley Safari guide](/guides/knowsley-safari-guide).

### Crosby Beach and Another Place

**Rough drive:** around 20 minutes via Switch Island

Antony Gormley's **Another Place** is **100 cast-iron figures**, each weighing 650kg, spread along **more than 3km** of beach.

- On big tides, the statues are **fully submerged for about an hour either side of high tide**, and you can't get onto the beach. Check tide times.
- Sefton Council says walking near the promenade is generally safe, but **don't walk out to the furthest statues**, as the sand there is very soft and muddy.
- The promenade is flat, well surfaced and free of motor traffic, which is good for buggies and scooters.

**Good for:** a fresh-air morning and great photos. See our [Another Place guide](/guides/crosby-beach-another-place-guide).

### Aintree family race days

Aintree Racecourse is the next suburb along. It has advertised family-focused race days, such as a Halloween family fun day. Check the racecourse's events page for dates.

## Full-day trips (30 minutes and more)

### Formby: red squirrels, pinewoods and dunes

**Rough drive:** around 25 to 35 minutes

The National Trust's Formby site combines pinewoods, where you may spot red squirrels, with huge dunes and a sandy beach. Car parks fill up on sunny weekends and holidays, so arrive early. See our [Formby guide](/guides/formby-red-squirrels-guide).

### Southport

**Rough drive:** around 35 to 45 minutes. You can also take the train, changing at Sandhills.

Southport has a long pier, Lord Street's shops and Pleasureland. Combine it with Formby for a coastal day. See our [Southport day trip guide](/guides/southport-day-trip).

## City days by train

Take the Merseyrail Northern line from Fazakerley to **Liverpool Central** (about 15 minutes).

- **World Museum:** an aquarium, planetarium shows, dinosaurs and space, all under one roof
- **Museum of Liverpool:** hands-on galleries by the river
- **Mersey ferry River Explorer cruise:** about 50 minutes from the Pier Head, with commentary. Children love being on the water.
- **Royal Albert Dock:** places to eat and the Beatles Story

The national museums are free, which makes a city day very affordable for families. For more ideas, see our [rainy day activities for kids](/guides/rainy-day-liverpool-kids).

## Tips for family days out

1. **One big outing a day** is plenty for young children.
2. **Go early to the safari and beaches.** They're quieter and you get the best parking.
3. **Pack layers.** The coast is windy even in summer.
4. **Bring a picnic** to save money and time.
5. **Check opening times the day before**, as seasonal hours change.
6. **Have a home plan** for tired afternoons: garden, board games, a film.
