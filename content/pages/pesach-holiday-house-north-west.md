**Pesach 2027 begins at sundown on Wednesday 21 April and ends at nightfall on Thursday 29 April** (diaspora dates). A self-catering holiday house can make a lovely family Pesach, with space for everyone, a big table for the seder and none of the cost of a hotel programme. The hard parts are the kitchen and the shopping. Plan both early, and take your kitchen questions to your rav before you go.

For homes where hosts have declared kosher kitchens or Pesach-friendly set-ups, start with our [kosher stays page](/kosher-homes).

## Pesach 2027 dates

| Date | Day | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Wed 21 April | Erev Pesach | Pesach begins at sundown. First seder that evening |
| Thu 22 April | Pesach I | Yom tov. Second seder that evening |
| Fri 23 April | Pesach II | Yom tov, running into Shabbat |
| Sat 24 to Tue 27 April | Chol Hamoed | Intermediate days, the best days for trips out |
| Wed 28 April | Pesach VII | Yom tov |
| Thu 29 April | Pesach VIII | Yom tov. Pesach ends at nightfall |

These are Hebcal's diaspora dates. The seder nights follow from the schedule; check exact times on a Jewish calendar.

Note that **yom tov runs straight into Shabbat** on Friday 23 April. That's three days in a row where you can't cook from scratch in the usual way, so plan food, warming and lighting accordingly.

## Why families choose a house for Pesach

The big Pesach programmes are almost all catered hotels, and most UK families who go away head abroad. Jewish News' 2026 round-up of Pesach options listed catered programmes in Croatia, Spain, Greece, France and Malta, and no UK ones. A house is the alternative for families who want to stay in Britain, cook their own food and keep costs down.

What a house gives you:

- **One big table** for the seder, with everyone together
- **Bedrooms for three generations**, rather than a corridor of hotel rooms
- **Your own pace.** No set meal times
- **A garden** for children during Chol Hamoed

What a house asks of you: **a kitchen you have to make work for Pesach**, and every bit of food brought in.

## The kitchen: plan with your rav

Most holiday homes are not kosher, let alone kosher for Pesach. STAR-K guidance for rentals, reprinted on Kashrut.com, gives a starting point for ordinary kashrut:

- **Ovens:** kasher at high heat if clean, or double-wrap food in two disposable tins
- **Hobs:** gas grates and electric coils can be kashered; on glass tops, only the area above the element
- **Sinks:** not recommended to kasher. Use a basin and rack
- **Microwave:** double-wrapped food only
- **Grills, dishwashers and can openers:** don't use

For Pesach, the standards are stricter and opinions differ. The simplest approach many families take is to **treat the house kitchen as a room, not a kitchen**:

- Cover worktops and the table
- Bring your own Pesach pots, pans, cutlery and crockery
- Bring hotplates, a portable oven or tabletop grill, and an urn
- Use the fridge after cleaning, lined if your rav advises

Agree with your rav **before** you book which appliances you'll use, so you can ask the host the right questions.

## Food: shop in Manchester

Liverpool's own kosher supply is limited. For Pesach shopping in the North West, Greater Manchester is the place. The Manchester Beth Din lists its licensees, including:

- **Groceries:** Halpern's and Kayes Kosher
- **Butcher:** Glatt Butchers
- **Bakeries and caterers:** several, useful if you'd rather not cook the seder

Use the MBD's official list for current details. KLBD and the London Beth Din also publish Pesach product guides.

Check each one's Pesach supervision, order hours and collection days well ahead. Shops are busiest in the days before Erev Pesach.

## Seders and community

Contact the local community or kashrut authority directly for shul, mikvah, eruv and service details over Pesach.

## Where to stay

Think about what matters most on yom tov:

- **Walking distance to shul.** Ask the local community which shul to use, then check the walk from the door
- **Day trips in Chol Hamoed.** A base with parking and motorway access opens up the coast, the Lake District and North Wales
- **Space.** A seder for 7 to 10 needs a proper dining room

## Pesach house checklist

- Book the house by early in the new year: April is busy
- Agree your kitchen plan with your rav
- Ask the host about worktops, sockets, fridge space and a big table
- Order food and seder items from licensed shops early
- Plan meals for the three-day run of yom tov and Shabbat (Thu 22 to Sat 24 April)
- Save Chol Hamoed (24 to 27 April) for trips out
