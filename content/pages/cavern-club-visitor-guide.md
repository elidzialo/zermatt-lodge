The Cavern Club is the most famous music venue in Liverpool. It's where the Beatles played hundreds of times before they were famous, and it still has live music every day. Here's what to know before your first visit.

We checked prices and times on 24 November 2026.

## Prices

| Ticket | Price |
|---|---|
| Adult (18+), single entry | £6 |
| All day and night pass | £8.50 (£10 during International Beatleweek) |
| Child, 12 to 17 | £3, allowed until 8pm with an adult |
| Under 12 | Free |
| Cloakroom | £2 per item |

The Cavern is **completely cashless**, so bring a card or phone.

## Opening times

| Day | Cavern Club |
|---|---|
| Sunday to Wednesday | 11am to midnight |
| Thursday | 11am to 1am |
| Friday and Saturday | 11am to 2am |

Live music starts at **11.15am every day**.

## Age rules: the 8pm cut-off

- Families are welcome **during the day**
- Adults must stay with children at all times
- **Under-18s aren't allowed in the Cavern after 8pm**
- Evening Live Lounge shows, including the tribute bands, are 18+
- Proof of age may be requested

If you're coming with teenagers, a daytime visit is the way to do it. A late morning or early afternoon slot also tends to be calmer than a Saturday night.

## The Cavern Pub

The **Cavern Pub**, listed alongside the club on the Cavern's website, is **free to enter**. It opens from midday, with live music starting in the evening on weekdays, from 4pm on Fridays, 3pm on Saturdays and all day on Sundays.

## A short history

| Year | What happened |
|---|---|
| 1957 | The Cavern opens as a jazz club on 16 January |
| 1961 | The Beatles play their first Cavern gig on 9 February |
| 1963 | Their last Cavern gig, on 3 August, after **292 appearances** |
| 1973 | The club closes. The warehouses above are demolished and the cellar is filled in |
| 1984 | The Cavern reopens |
| 1991 | It reopens again after a break and has run ever since |

## Tips for first-timers

- **Go in the day for a first look.** Easier with kids, and you can come back at night if you love it
- **Expect stairs.** It's a cellar club
- **Pair it with The Beatles Story.** See our [Beatles Story guide](/guides/beatles-story-visitor-guide) for times and prices
- **Busy weekend?** Friday and Saturday nights get packed, so arrive early
- **Late August** is International Beatleweek, when prices go up and the city fills. See our [Beatleweek guide](/guides/international-beatleweek-accommodation)

## Visiting with a group

The Cavern is a great stop on a group weekend, but it's a busy venue, not a private one. Agree a meeting point outside on Mathew Street in case people get split up, and remember the 8pm rule if anyone in the group is under 18.
