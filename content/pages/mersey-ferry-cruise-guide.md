A ride on the Mersey Ferry is one of the classic things to do in Liverpool. You get the best view of the waterfront and the Three Graces, a commentary on the river's history, and a chance to sit down for an hour. It works for every age, from toddlers to grandparents.

We checked the details below on 23 November 2026.

## The River Explorer Cruise

- **Length:** about **50 minutes**
- **Stops:** **Pier Head** (Liverpool) and **Woodside** (Birkenhead, on the Wirral)
- **Seacombe:** **closed for refurbishment**, so the cruise doesn't call there at the moment
- **Commentary:** throughout the trip

**Note for visitors:** Mersey Ferries says there are **roadworks at Pier Head**, so allow extra time to get to the terminal.

## Times

Mersey Ferries lists these River Explorer Cruise times:

| Day | Times | Departures |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday to Friday** | 10am to 4pm | Pier Head on the hour, Woodside at 20 past |
| **Saturday and Sunday** | 10am to 5pm | Pier Head on the hour, Woodside at 20 past |

Commuter crossings also run on weekday mornings and evenings between Woodside and Liverpool. Holiday timetables can change, so check before visiting over Christmas or New Year.

## Tickets

- **Book online before you travel.** Tickets are timed
- **Ticket types:** adult (16+), child (5 to 15), concession (over 60s and student card holders) and family tickets
- **Under 5s** travel free

We saw different prices on different pages of the Mersey Ferries website, so check the price on the booking page.

## Tips

1. **Sit outside if it's dry.** The open deck has the best views of the waterfront. Wrap up in winter, as it's windy on the river
2. **Book one time slot for the whole group.** Timed tickets mean everyone needs to be on the same sailing
3. **Combine it with the Pier Head.** The [Museum of Liverpool](/guides/liverpool-museums-guide) is right by the terminal and free to enter
4. **Bad weather?** Sailings can be affected by conditions on the river, so check the Mersey Ferries website on the day
