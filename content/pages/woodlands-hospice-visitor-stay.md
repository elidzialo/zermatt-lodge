When someone you love is being cared for at Woodlands Hospice, being close by matters more than almost anything else. Woodlands is on the Aintree University Hospital campus in Fazakerley, north Liverpool, and Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house nearby, where family travelling from further away can stay together, rest between visits and be there quickly if they're needed.

This page is written for families in that situation. It covers the practical things, gently and simply, so you have less to think about.

## About Woodlands Hospice

- **Address:** AUH Campus, Longmoor Lane, Liverpool, L9 7LA
- **Phone:** 0151 529 2299
- **Website:** woodlandshospice.org
- **Who it serves:** the hospice describes its care as being for North Liverpool, South Sefton and Kirkby in Knowsley
- **Services:** an inpatient unit, day services and bereavement support

The hospice team is the best source of information about visiting, overnight stays with your relative and what to bring. Phone them before you set off, especially if you're travelling a long way.

## Getting there

### By car

The hospice shares the Aintree University Hospital campus, which is on the A506 near junction 6 of the M57. For sat nav, the hospice postcode is **L9 7LA**, and the hospital's main postcode is L9 7AL. The roads around the site can be busy at peak times.

### Parking

Aintree University Hospital manages parking across the campus using number plate recognition, and you pay before leaving. Its page lists a weekly ticket and several concessions. Importantly for families, it says **ward or clinic staff can grant discretionary free parking**, for example for relatives called in at short notice. Ask the hospice team on your first visit what applies.

For more detail, see our [Aintree Hospital parking guide](/guides/aintree-hospital-parking-guide).

### By train

Fazakerley station, on Merseyrail's Northern line, is directly across the road from the campus's **Longmoor Lane** entrance. Direct trains run to Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes, so relatives arriving at Lime Street can reach the campus without a car.

## Before you travel

A short list for the journey, so nothing important is left behind:

1. Phone the hospice to let them know when you expect to arrive
2. Save the hospice number (0151 529 2299) in everyone's phone
3. Pack phone chargers, any regular medicines and a few days of clothes, even if you hope to be back sooner
4. Bring photos, music or a familiar blanket if your loved one would find them comforting, and ask the team first
5. Decide who will keep the wider family updated, so the phone isn't ringing constantly

## Being close by: why it helps

For families travelling in, distance is often the hardest part. Being nearby means:

- **You can be there quickly** if the team calls
- **You can take turns.** One person sits with your loved one while others rest, eat or make calls
- **Everyone has somewhere to go** that isn't a car park or a waiting room
- **Children and grandchildren can visit** for short periods and then have space to be themselves

## Looking after yourself

It's easy to forget your own needs at a time like this. A few small things help:

- **Eat proper meals.** Having a kitchen means you're not relying on vending machines or takeaways every night
- **Sleep when you can.** Separate bedrooms mean people on different visiting shifts don't disturb each other
- **Step outside.** A garden gives you a quiet place for fresh air and difficult phone calls
- **Keep a notebook** of names, phone numbers and questions for the care team
- **Share the load.** Let one family member be the main contact for updates, and let others handle meals or school runs

## Flexible dates

Nobody knows exactly how long they'll need to stay. When you book anywhere, ask about:

- Extending if you need to stay longer
- The cancellation terms. Zermatt Lodge uses a Moderate policy: a full refund up to 5 days before check-in, 50% after that
- Arriving at short notice
