This guide explains what crews need, where the work is in the Liverpool City Region, and how a house with parking helps.

## What work crews actually need

Hotels are built for tourists and business travellers. Crews on site need something different:

- **A bed each**, not a sofa bed. Tired workers make mistakes.
- **Off-street parking** for vans or cars, at no extra cost
- **Fast motorway access**, so a 7am start doesn't mean a 5:30am alarm
- **A proper kitchen** to batch cook, make packed lunches and keep food costs down
- **Two bathrooms**, so everyone isn't queueing for the shower before work
- **Space to switch off** after a long shift: a TV, a garden, something to do
- **One booking and one invoice** for the company

## Why a house with parking works for crews

### Motorway access

Liverpool's motorways reach most of the city region:

- **M57:** south to the **M62** for Warrington and Manchester, past Knowsley and Prescot
- **M58:** east to **Skelmersdale** and the **M6** for Wigan, Preston and the north
- **Switch Island**, where the M57 and M58 meet, leads to the **north docks, Bootle and Seaforth**, plus Crosby and Southport

You can reach a lot of the city region without going near the city centre.

### Train into town

If the job is in the city centre, trains from the house run into Liverpool Central. That's handy when city sites have tight parking or delivery restrictions.

## Living well on a long contract

Long contracts are easier when the house works for everyone:

- **Set a shower rota** for early starts.
- **Batch cook on Sundays.** See our [self-catering guide for work crews](/guides/self-catering-for-work-crews).
- **Agree quiet hours** if anyone works nights.
- **Keep work boots and hi-vis by the door.** Don't take muddy kit upstairs.
- **Check out washing arrangements** before you arrive. See our [laundry guide for workers](/guides/laundry-for-workers-liverpool).

## A typical working week

Here's how a small crew might use the house on a multi-site job:

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 05:45 to 06:30 | Two bathrooms and a shower rota get four people out of the door |
| 06:30 | Vans away. Motorways out of the city lead to sites in Knowsley, Kirkby or Skelmersdale |
| 07:00 | On the gate for site induction or the morning briefing |
| 17:30 | Back at the house. Boots off at the door, kit in the vans |
| 18:30 | One person cooks for everyone from the batch-cook plan |
| 19:30 | Downtime: pool, a match on the 55" TV or an early night |
| Friday | Clean-down, then home for the weekend, or stay on for weekend work |

For a city-centre job with tight parking, swap the van for the train to Liverpool Central and leave the vehicles parked at the house. Many city-centre sites have strict delivery and parking windows, so this can save a lot of hassle.
