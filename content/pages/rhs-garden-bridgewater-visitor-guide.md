RHS Garden Bridgewater is the Royal Horticultural Society's garden in Worsley, Salford. It's a good winter walk with children or older relatives, and in December the after-dark Glow trail adds a festive evening option. We checked these details on the RHS website on 25 November 2026.

## Where it is

**RHS Garden Bridgewater, Occupation Road, off Leigh Road, Worsley, Salford, M28 2LJ.** Use the RHS "Getting here" page or the Bee Network app to plan a bus journey.

## Winter opening times

The garden is **open every day except Christmas Day**. Last entry is **3pm** for most of the year.

| Dates | Garden open |
|---|---|
| 26 November to 3 January | 10am to 3.30pm |
| 4 January to 5 February | 10am to 4pm |
| 6 February to 5 March | 10am to 5pm (last entry 4pm) |

The **garden centre** is free to enter. From 2 November to 5 February it opens Monday to Saturday 9am to 4.30pm and Sunday 10.30am to 4.30pm.

## Tickets

| Ticket | Price |
|---|---|
| Adult, booked online in advance | from £13.60 |
| Adult, on the day | £18.40 |
| Adult, car-free (bus, train, bike or walk) | £12.90 |
| Child aged 5 to 16 | £6 |
| Under 5 | free |
| RHS members | free (plus one adult guest or up to two children) |

- **Book ahead:** it's cheaper than paying on the day.
- **Car-free:** show proof of how you travelled on arrival.
- **£1 tickets:** for people receiving Universal Credit, Pension Credit or ESA, for up to 6 in the party, with proof on arrival.
- **Wheelchairs and mobility scooters** can be booked online.
- **Dogs:** the RHS welcomes guide, assistance and service dogs. Check the RHS website for other dogs before you bring one.

## RHS Glow at Christmas

**RHS Glow** runs from **26 November to 30 December 2026**, with standard tickets from **£9.75 for a child** and **£17 for an adult**, as listed by Visit Manchester. It's ticketed separately from daytime entry. Our [Christmas light trails guide](/guides/christmas-light-trails-manchester-2026) compares it with Heaton Park and Dunham Massey.

## Tips for a winter visit

- **Go early.** Daylight is short and the garden closes at 3.30pm in December.
- **Wear boots.** Paths away from the main routes can be wet.
- **Combine it** with [Ordsall Hall and Salford Museum](/guides/salford-ordsall-hall-and-salford-museum-guide) on another day for a Salford weekend.
