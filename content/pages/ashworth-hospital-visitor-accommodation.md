Visiting someone at Ashworth Hospital takes planning. It's a **high-security hospital**, so visits are booked ahead, everyone goes through security, and there are strict rules on what you can bring. If you're travelling a long way, an overnight stay nearby lets you arrive calm and on time instead of rushing up the motorway. This guide sets out the practical steps from Mersey Care's own visitor information.

## About Ashworth

Ashworth is a high-security psychiatric hospital for men aged 18 and over, part of **Mersey Care's Maghull Health Park**. The NHS website lists the address as **Parkbourn, Maghull, Liverpool, L31 1HW**.

## Visiting times

| Day | Time |
|---|---|
| Every day | 1.30pm to 3.30pm |
| Tuesday evenings | 6.15pm to 7.30pm |

## Booking a visit

- **Call 0151 471 2300** at least **72 hours** before the visit.
- You must be an **approved visitor** and answer security questions.
- The patient must **consent** and be **well enough** to take part.
- **Official visitors** need prior approval from the relevant department.

## On the day

### Arrive early

Arrive **at least 30 minutes early** for security. Bring **proof of identity**.

### Security checks

Mersey Care says to expect:

- A metal detector and bag X-ray
- Removing coats, jewellery and sometimes shoes
- A same-sex rub-down search
- Drug detection dogs on occasion

### What not to bring

- **No food or tobacco**
- **Phones, computers and sharp objects** go in the secure lockers at visitors' reception
- Drinks and snacks can be bought in the visitors' area

### Gifts

Only items on the **patient possessions catalogue** can be given, and some are limited to one per patient. Check with visitors' reception **before you buy**. Gifts are left at reception and passed on after the visit.

### Accessibility

Visits take place in the Exchange building inside the secure perimeter. Disabled access is available, and **interpreters can be arranged in advance**.

## Getting there

- **Parking:** free at Maghull Health Park
- **By road:** Maghull sits by Switch Island, where the M57 and M58 begin, so it's reached easily from the motorway network
- **By rail and bus:** Mersey Care's visitors document and the map on its Ashworth page give directions. Maghull has two Merseyrail stations, Maghull and Maghull North

## Looking after yourself

Visits can be emotionally draining, especially after a long journey. A few things help:

- **Don't drive straight home** after a hard visit if you're tired. Stay over and travel the next day.
- **Eat before you arrive,** since you can't bring food in.
- **Plan regular visits** with the booking line rather than one-off trips, if that suits the patient's care.

## Where to stay

Maghull has few large holiday homes. For a family travelling together, look at **Aintree and Fazakerley**, just south of Switch Island on the Liverpool side, which have whole houses with parking.
