**Back to the Future The Musical** comes to the **Liverpool Empire** from **Tuesday 12 January to Saturday 6 February 2027**. The show's official site lists the opening night at **7.30pm**. The Empire is on **Lime Street**, in the heart of the city centre, so it's easy to reach by train and makes a good centrepiece for a January weekend.

Here's how to plan a trip, checked on 21 November 2026.

## The key facts

- **Show:** Back to the Future The Musical
- **Venue:** Liverpool Empire Theatre, Lime Street, Liverpool L1 1JE
- **Run:** Tuesday 12 January to Saturday 6 February 2027
- **Evening start:** 7.30pm on the opening night. Check your ticket for other times and matinees
- **Tickets:** through the Empire's ticketing partner, ATG Tickets, or the show's official site

## Getting there

The Empire faces **Lime Street station**, so it's one of the easiest theatres in the city to reach.

- **By Merseyrail:** from north Liverpool, trains run to **Liverpool Central** in about 15 minutes from Fazakerley. It's a short walk from Central to Lime Street
- **By car:** city-centre parking is limited and charged. Leaving the car at your accommodation and taking the train is usually simpler
- **After the show:** check the time of the last train back before you go, especially on Sundays

## Make a weekend of it

January is quieter in Liverpool, but there's still plenty during the run:

- **Sat 16 Jan:** Liverpool v Crystal Palace at Anfield, 3pm
- **Sat 23 Jan:** Donny Osmond at M&S Bank Arena
- **Sun 24 Jan:** Everton v Brentford at Hill Dickinson Stadium, 2pm
- **Wed 27 Jan:** Liverpool v RC Lens, Champions League, 8pm
- **Sat 30 Jan:** Liverpool v Everton at Anfield, 12.30pm

Kick-off times can change for TV. See our [January 2027 events guide](/guides/liverpool-january-2027-events) for the full month.

### Before the show

The area around Lime Street has the **Walker Art Gallery, World Museum and St George's Hall** close by, which makes a good afternoon if the weather turns. Book a table for an early dinner, as pre-theatre slots fill on Fridays and Saturdays.

## Theatre trips for groups

A group of five to seven going to the show has a choice: several hotel rooms in town, or one house a short train ride out.

- **Hotel rooms:** walk to the theatre, but you pay per room and for parking if you drive
- **A whole house:** one booking, a kitchen for breakfast, a lounge to meet up in after the show, and often free parking

Our [house vs hotel guide](/guides/house-vs-hotel-liverpool-groups) compares the two.
