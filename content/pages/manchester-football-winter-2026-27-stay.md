## Getting to the Etihad

- The ground has its own Metrolink stop, **Etihad Campus**. Manchester City says it's especially useful on match days.
- The club says trams run **every six minutes Monday to Saturday** and **every seven and a half minutes on Sundays**, and suggests touching in and out with a contactless card to skip the ticket machine queue.
- The club also lists park and ride sites with direct trams, including **Ashton Moss** and **Ashton West**.

## Getting to Old Trafford

- **Wharfside**, on the Metrolink **Trafford Park line**, is opposite the stadium.
- **Old Trafford** stop, on the **Altrincham line**, is next to the cricket ground and a short walk from the football ground.
