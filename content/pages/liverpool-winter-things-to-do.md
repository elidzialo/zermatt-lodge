Liverpool doesn't switch off after New Year. In **January 2027** the Everyman's **Beauty and the Beast** panto and the Royal Court's **Scouse Nativity** both run to **16 January**, **Icebox** skating runs to **10 January** and the **Indoor Funfair** to **9 January**. **Jack Whitehall** plays M&S Bank Arena on **10 January**, and the **Merseyside derby at Anfield** is scheduled for **Saturday 30 January**.

For a group of friends, a few couples or a family reunion, January can be a smart time for a winter break. Here's what's on, checked on 19 November 2026.

For adult groups, the Royal Court's Christmas comedy is a Liverpool tradition, and booking a January date can be easier than December. The Everyman's relaxed panto performance is on Thursday 14 January at 1.30pm. See our [Liverpool panto 2026 guide](/guides/liverpool-panto-2026) for the full list.

## Why a house works for a winter group break

Winter breaks are as much about the evenings in as the days out. A whole house gives a group:

- **Somewhere warm to gather.** One living space instead of separate hotel rooms
- **A kitchen.** Breakfasts and a big shared dinner keep costs down. Our [meal plan for a group of 7](/guides/self-catering-meal-plan-group-of-7) helps
- **A games room** for the dark evenings
- **Parking.** Leave the cars and take the train into town

Weighing it up? Read our [house vs hotel guide for groups](/guides/house-vs-hotel-liverpool-groups).

## Tips for a January break

1. **Book shows early** even in January. Weekend panto dates still sell out
2. **Check fixture dates** about a month ahead
3. **Pack for weather.** Wind, rain and the odd frost are normal
4. **Plan one quiet day.** It's winter, and a house day is part of the fun
5. **Agree the house rules** with your group before you arrive

## Getting into the city

From Fazakerley, direct Merseyrail trains reach **Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes**, with fares from £4.85 when booked in advance according to Trainline. Merseyrail also takes contactless. For Hill Dickinson Stadium and the Anfield Soccerbus, Sandhills is a few stops down the same line.
