Liverpool draws visiting researchers for sabbaticals, fellowships, lab collaborations and teaching exchanges. One thing catches many of them out: **the university often won't house you**. Here's what to expect and how to choose a base, especially if you're bringing family or staying for months.

We checked the details below on 22 November 2026. Visa rules change, so always check GOV.UK and your host institution.

## Who visits

Liverpool's research institutions include the **University of Liverpool**, **Liverpool John Moores University**, **Liverpool Hope University** and the **Liverpool School of Tropical Medicine**. LSTM was founded on 12 November 1898 and describes itself as the first institution in the world dedicated to the study of tropical medicine.

## Will the university house me?

Don't assume so. The University of Liverpool's **School of the Arts**, for example, says:

- It **does not provide accommodation** and can't help find it
- Visiting scholars must **arrange their own**, though the university's accommodation service may be able to advise
- You should **apply at least 4 months before arrival**: 12 weeks for approval plus 4 weeks for visas

Visiting scholars there get a named academic contact, library borrowing rights, IT access, a web profile and access to the building and school events, but not housing. Other departments and universities may differ, so ask yours.

## Visa basics

The University of Liverpool explains that the **Academic Visitor** sub-category of the Standard Visitor route:

- Allows stays of **up to 12 months**
- **Can't be extended** from inside the UK
- Covers activities like formal exchanges, sabbatical research and non-profit conferences
- Requires you to be highly qualified, currently working at an overseas academic institution, and planning to leave the UK afterwards

Check your own eligibility on GOV.UK before you book anything non-refundable.

## Choosing a base

| You are | Consider |
|---|---|
| On your own for a few weeks | A city centre flat near your department |
| With a partner or family | A furnished house with a kitchen, laundry and space to work |
| Driving or visiting several sites | Somewhere with free parking near the motorways |
| Staying 1 month or more | Monthly rates and a single invoice |

### What to ask the host

1. **Is there a desk** or a table you can work at, and how fast is the wifi?
2. **Can I get an invoice** in my institution's or funder's name?
3. **Is there a monthly rate?**
4. **Is there a washer and dryer?**
5. **How do I get to my department** by public transport?

## Getting around

Merseyrail is the easiest way in from the suburbs. Since **25 February 2026** you can **tap in and out with a contactless card, phone or watch**, and Merseytravel says fares are frozen until March 2027.
