Aintree University Hospital is on Lower Lane in Fazakerley, north Liverpool (sat nav **L9 7AL**), on the A506 close to junction 6 of the M57. The main entrance is on Lower Lane, and Fazakerley station is opposite the Longmoor Lane side of the site, about a 15-minute walk from the main entrance. This guide is about getting there and finding your way on the day, whether you're a patient, a visitor or a relative arriving from out of town.

The site is large and shared with The Walton Centre and Woodlands Hospice, so a little planning saves a lot of walking.

## The two ways in: Lower Lane and Longmoor Lane

The hospital lists two main walking routes on to the site.

### Lower Lane (main entrance)

- This is the **main entrance**, and where most visitors arrive by car
- **Bike parking** is here, with security patrols and CCTV
- **Freephone taxi lines** are at the main entrances
- Drop-off and pick-up points are outside all main entrances

### Longmoor Lane

- Faces **Fazakerley station**, which is on the opposite side of the road
- Useful if you arrive by train
- The hospital says it's about **15 minutes on foot** from here to the main entrance and outpatient area

If you're not sure which building you need, check your letter. The site includes Aintree University Hospital, The Walton Centre's Main Building (L9 7LJ) and Sid Watkins Building (L9 7BB), and Woodlands Hospice. Our [site map guide](/guides/outpatient-clinics-aintree-site-map) explains which building is which.

## Getting there

### By car

Follow the A506 from M57 junction 6. The hospital warns that roads around the site are often congested at peak times, so allow extra time for morning appointments.

**Parking basics:**

- Car parks use **number plate recognition**. You pay before leaving, so note your registration
- The hospital's page lists a **visitor car park** (free under 30 minutes, then rising in steps) and a **weekly ticket at £17.50**
- A separate **Short Stay Car Park** serves A&E and the Sefton Suite. It's free for 30 minutes and £3 up to 1.5 hours, then jumps to **£10**, so don't use it for long visits
- Park only in marked bays. Breach of contract notices are issued for parking on verges, hatched areas, pavements or loading bays

The Walton Centre's page lists different prices for the same site, so check the signs on arrival. Full details are in our [Aintree Hospital parking guide](/guides/aintree-hospital-parking-guide).

### By train

Fazakerley station is on Merseyrail's Northern line, with direct trains to Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes. It's the hospital's nearest station. See our guide to [walking from Fazakerley station to the hospital](/guides/walking-fazakerley-station-to-aintree-hospital).

### By bus

The hospital says several services stop at the site or within walking distance. It doesn't list route numbers, so use the Merseytravel journey planner or call Traveline on 0871 200 2233.

### By bike or scooter

The hospital lists several nearby routes:

- Bluebell Woods
- The Liverpool Loop Line, part of the Trans Pennine Trail
- Cycle paths on Valley Road
- The segregated path along the East Lancs Road towards Kirkby

There's also a **Voi parking location** nearby for hired e-scooters and e-bikes.

## Paying less: concessions and refunds

Aintree's page lists several concessions. Ask about them on your first visit:

- **Cancer treatment:** free parking
- **Long-term outpatients:** free if you attend at least three times in 30 days, for at least three months
- **Blue Badge holders:** free in marked bays. Give clinic reception your registration and a photo of your badge before leaving
- **Discretionary free parking:** for example, carers of inpatients or relatives called in at short notice. Ask the ward or clinic
- **Clinic delays:** managers can reduce the charge if a clinic runs more than an hour late

If you qualify for the NHS Healthcare Travel Costs Scheme, the hospital's **Cash Office is on the ground floor of Aintree Lodge**. NHS guidance says the scheme covers eligible patients and, in some cases, a medically required escort, but not visitors.

## What to bring

### For an appointment

- Your appointment letter and the building name
- A list of your medicines
- Your phone, a charger and the clinic's phone number
- Your car registration for the pay machines
- A bank card. The Walton Centre says the machines are card only, while Aintree's page mentions cash and card. A card covers both

### For visiting an inpatient

- Check visiting times with the ward first. See [how to check Liverpool hospital visiting times](/guides/liverpool-hospital-visiting-times-how-to-check)
- Phone chargers, toiletries and comfortable clothes for the patient
- Snacks and a water bottle for yourself
