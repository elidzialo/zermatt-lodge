Crew managers and site leads book a lot of accommodation, and the choice between **Airbnb** and **booking direct with the host** comes up every time. Neither is always better. Airbnb is quick for a short, simple stay. Direct is usually easier for **crews, long jobs and anything your accounts team needs to reconcile**. This guide covers the differences that matter for business bookings.

For the general picture on fees and safety, see our [book direct vs Airbnb guide](/guides/book-direct-vs-airbnb). We checked the details below on 22 November 2026.

## Receipts and invoices

### On Airbnb

- **Add business details before you book.** Airbnb's help centre says business details added after a reservation is confirmed won't appear on the receipt. To get them on, you'd have to cancel and rebook, which may be subject to the host's cancellation policy
- **VAT invoices cover Airbnb's fee.** Airbnb says it provides a VAT invoice whenever VAT is charged on its service fees, and that VAT invoices can't be changed once issued
- **The accommodation charge** comes from the host. If you need a VAT invoice for the stay itself, the host must be VAT registered and issue one

### Booking direct

- **Agree the invoice before you pay:** company name and address, guest names, dates, and a purchase order number if you use them
- **Ask whether the host is VAT registered.** Many small hosts aren't, so there may be no VAT to reclaim
- **One invoice for the whole crew** is often possible

Our [invoices and VAT guide](/guides/invoice-and-vat-for-business-stays) explains what a valid VAT invoice must show.

## Price

Airbnb says hosts who set prices through property management software now pay a **single 15.5% service fee**, and guests see the host's price without a separate guest fee on top. Hosts usually build platform fees into their prices, so the same home can cost less booked direct. Compare the **total for the same dates and the same number of guests**.

## Flexibility

| | Airbnb | Direct |
|---|---|---|
| Change dates | Through the platform, subject to the policy | Talk to the host |
| Extend a job | Rebook or request a change | Usually a message or call |
| Monthly rates | Set by host on the listing | Often negotiable |
| Swap crew members | Guest details through the platform | Tell the host |
| Pay on account | Not usually | Sometimes, by agreement |

## Safety and protection

Booking direct means relying on the host's own terms, so check:

- **A real business** with a working phone number and clear terms
- **Card payment on a secure page.** MoneySavingExpert warns about holiday booking scams, and bank transfers give you very little protection
- **Cancellation, deposit and damage terms** in writing

Never pay a stranger by bank transfer for a booking you found on social media.

## Our view

- **One person, two nights, no invoice needed:** use whichever is quicker
- **A crew, a week or more, invoice needed:** book direct and agree terms first
- **Not sure about the host?** Book through a platform you trust
