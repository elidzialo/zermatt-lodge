Arriving at a holiday house after a long drive, especially on a dark winter evening, it's tempting to drop the bags and put the kettle on. Give it 15 minutes first. A quick check on arrival makes the rest of the stay smoother and protects you if anything goes wrong.

## The 15-minute arrival check

### 1. Walk round and take photos

- Look for any **marks, breakages or stains** that were there before you.
- **Photograph them** and send them to the host. It takes two minutes and avoids any confusion at checkout.

### 2. Heat and hot water

- Find the **thermostat** and **heating controls**.
- Check the **hot water** is on before anyone needs a shower.
- In winter, put the heating on as soon as you arrive. Big houses take a while to warm up.

### 3. Wi-Fi and TV

- Connect everyone's phones. Write the password somewhere visible for the group.
- Check the **Smart TV** and log in to your streaming apps.

### 4. Safety

- Spot the **smoke alarms** and **carbon monoxide alarm**.
- Find the **fire exits**, the **fuse box** and the **stopcock** (the main water valve).
- If you have young children, check **windows, stairs and cupboards** with cleaning products.

### 5. Read the house guide

- **House rules:** noise, smoking, pets and visitors
- **Bins:** which bin is which, and what to do with rubbish at the end
- **Checkout instructions:** dishwasher, towels, keys
- **Parking:** where you're allowed to park

### 6. Kitchen basics

- Check the **oven, hob, fridge and dishwasher** work.
- See what's provided: **tea towels, dishwasher tablets, washing-up liquid, salt and pepper**.
- If you've booked a food delivery, put it away first.

## If something's wrong

**Tell the host straight away**, with a photo. Don't wait until checkout. Most problems, like a tripped fuse or a heating timer, can be fixed quickly once the host knows.

## Making it feel like home

- **Pick bedrooms before you unpack** to avoid a group debate later.
- **Agree a shared food kitty** if you're a group cooking together. Our [group booking checklist](/guides/group-booking-checklist) has more on splitting costs.
- **Plan breakfast for the first morning** before you go to bed.
