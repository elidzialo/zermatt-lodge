The Manchester Christmas Markets are one of the biggest festive days out in the north of England. Here are the 2026 dates, the main sites and some practical tips for a family or group trip, plus where to stay if you want a whole home rather than hotel rooms.

## Dates for 2026

- **Opens:** Friday 6 November 2026.
- **Most sites close:** Tuesday 22 December 2026.
- **Albert Square and Cathedral Gardens:** stay open until **Sunday 3 January 2027**.

Entry to the market sites is **free**, and you do not need to book. Opening hours can differ between sites, so check the official [Visit Manchester Christmas page](https://www.visitmanchester.com/christmas) before you set off.

## The market sites

Manchester's Finest lists these city centre locations for 2026:

- **Albert Square**, the centrepiece, with a 50-metre Ferris wheel, a helter-skelter, food, drink and craft stalls.
- **Lincoln Square**, new for 2026, just off Brazennose Street.
- **King Street** and **St Ann's Square**, which has a new layout with more craft, food and drink traders.
- **Exchange Street**, **Exchange Square** and **New Cathedral Street**.
- **Corn Exchange**.
- **Cathedral Gardens**, where Skate Manchester's covered ice rink returns until 3 January.
- **Market Street**.

Visit Manchester describes "over 200 stalls" across the sites. Lists can change, so check before you go.

## Tips for families and groups

- **Go early in the week if you can.** Weekends in December are the busiest times.
- **Use the tram.** Visit Manchester recommends public transport because parking in the centre gets busy.
- **Plan a meeting point.** With sites spread across the centre, it is easy to lose people. Pick one square to meet at.
- **Book skating in advance** if you want a set time at Skate Manchester.
- **The Christmas Parade** was reported for Sunday 6 December 2026, with details to be confirmed.
