Southport is a Victorian seaside town on the **Sefton coast**, north of Liverpool, known for **Lord Street**, its long pier and big summer events. This winter it has **Christmas markets on Market Street from 4 December 2026** and **Cinderella at The Atkinson from 4 to 31 December**. The **pier has been closed for safety works since December 2022**. From Fazakerley in north Liverpool, the train takes **about 1 hour 4 minutes** with one change, which makes Southport an easy day trip from a Liverpool base.

## Southport Pier: what's happening

According to Sefton Council:

- The pier is **more than 3,600 feet long**, the second longest in the UK
- It was **closed in December 2022** for safety reasons and will stay closed during works
- The council submitted a business case for a **£20 million** full restoration, with works hoped to start in early 2026
- **No reopening date** had been given when the council last updated its page in January 2026

You can still walk the promenade and see the pier from the shore.

## The big events of the year

Southport's calendar peaks in spring and summer. In **2026**:

- **Lightport**, a free light festival on Lord Street, ran from 14 to 16 February
- **The 154th Open Championship** was held at **Royal Birkdale** in July
- **Southport Flower Show** ran from **20 to 23 August** in Victoria Park
- **Southport Air Show** was on **29 and 30 August** on the seafront, at £12 with under-16s free

Dates for **2027** had not been confirmed in the sources we checked. Watch Visit Southport if you're planning a summer trip.

## What to do in Southport

- **Lord Street.** Southport's main boulevard, with the bandstand at its heart
- **The Atkinson.** Arts centre, theatre and museum, and this year's panto venue
- **The seafront and promenade.** Big skies and long beaches, even with the pier closed
- **Victoria Park.** Home to the Flower Show in summer
- **Royal Birkdale.** The championship golf course that hosted The Open in July 2026

Further down the coast, **Formby** has pinewoods, dunes and red squirrels, and **Crosby** has Antony Gormley's *Another Place*. See our [Formby guide](/guides/formby-area-guide) and [Crosby guide](/guides/crosby-area-guide).

## Getting to Southport from north Liverpool

**By train.** From Fazakerley, Trip.com lists an average journey of **about 1 hour 4 minutes** with **one change**, run by Merseyrail, with fares from about **£8.74**. The sample timetable showed departures about every 15 minutes in the morning.

**By car.** The coast roads north from Liverpool lead to Southport. Allow extra time on summer weekends and event days, when parking in the town fills up.

## Should you stay in Southport or Liverpool?

**Stay in Southport** if you're here for the Air Show, Flower Show or golf and want to walk to the seafront.

**Stay in Liverpool** if Southport is one day of several and you also want the city, the football or the waterfront. A north Liverpool base keeps you on the right side of the city for the coast, with the M57 and M58 nearby and direct trains into town.

## Tips for a winter visit

- **Dress for the wind.** The seafront is exposed in winter
- **Check light.** Short December days mean the promenade is best before about 3.30pm
- **Book panto early.** Weekend and Christmas Eve performances go first
- **Plan your train home.** One change each way, so check the last connection back

## A December day trip to Southport

- **Morning:** train or drive north
- **Late morning:** walk the promenade and see the pier from the shore
- **Lunch:** Lord Street
- **Afternoon:** the Christmas market on Market Street (from 4 December)
- **Early evening:** Cinderella at The Atkinson, if you've booked
- **Night:** back to Liverpool
