Plenty of work doesn't stop for Christmas: plant shutdowns, rail possessions, emergency repairs and fit-outs timed for the quiet weeks. If your crew is in Liverpool over Christmas 2026, plan around **three bank holidays**, **shops that shut on Christmas Day** and **reduced public transport**. A house with parking and a proper kitchen makes those days much easier than a hotel with a closed restaurant.

We checked the details below on 21 November 2026.

## Key dates

| Date | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Thu 24 Dec** | Christmas Eve. Last full shopping day; some shops may close early |
| **Fri 25 Dec** | Christmas Day, bank holiday. Large shops must close |
| **Sat 26 Dec** | Boxing Day. Busy roads in north Liverpool: Aintree racing and Everton v Sunderland |
| **Mon 28 Dec** | Bank holiday (Boxing Day substitute) |
| **Thu 31 Dec** | New Year's Eve |
| **Fri 1 Jan** | New Year's Day, bank holiday |

Because Boxing Day falls on a Saturday in 2026, the bank holiday moves to **Monday 28 December**. Suppliers, merchants and hire firms may be closed on all three bank holidays, so order materials and plant early.

## Food and supplies

- **Large shops must close on Christmas Day** in England and Wales. Shop on Christmas Eve
- **Holiday hours change.** Supermarkets shorten their hours over Christmas, so check before you go
- **Stock the freezer** with batch-cooked meals. See our [self-catering guide for work crews](/guides/self-catering-for-work-crews)
- **Takeaways** are limited on Christmas Day. Don't rely on one

## Getting to site

The 2026 festive timetable for Merseyrail and buses hadn't been published when we checked. In past years Merseyrail hasn't run on Christmas Day, and the Combined Authority has warned of very limited buses around the holidays.

- **Drive if you can.** A base with **free parking** saves daily fees and works when trains don't
- **Check road closures** around Aintree and Hill Dickinson Stadium on Boxing Day
- **Pre-book taxis** for any early shifts on bank holidays

## Booking tips for holiday-week stays

1. **Book early.** Families want houses over Christmas, so availability goes fast
2. **Confirm check-in on 24 December** if you're arriving then, and ask about access if shifts run late
3. **Ask for an invoice** in your company's name, and check VAT. See our [invoice and VAT guide](/guides/invoice-and-vat-for-business-stays)
4. **Agree house rules with the crew.** Quiet hours and no gatherings apply at Christmas too
5. **Plan for New Year's Eve.** Taxis are scarce and expensive late on 31 December
