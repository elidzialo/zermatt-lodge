The two big weekends left in November 2026 are packed. On **Saturday 21 November** Skate St George's opens on St George's Plateau, and on **Sunday 22 November** Liverpool host Manchester United at Anfield at 4.30pm. The following weekend has **Black Friday on 27 November**, **Gladiators Live** at M&S Bank Arena on 28 and 29 November, and the **Merseyside derby at 12pm on Sunday 29 November** at Hill Dickinson Stadium.

This guide lists what's confirmed for each weekend, with times and where to check. Details were correct when we checked on 19 November 2026, but times can change, so always confirm with the organiser before you travel.

## Weekend 1: Friday 20 to Sunday 22 November 2026

### Skate St George's opens (Saturday 21 November)

Liverpool's Christmas market and outdoor ice rink return to **St George's Plateau**, opposite Lime Street station.

- **Opening day:** Saturday 21 November, 2pm to 11pm
- **Season:** to Sunday 3 January 2027, every day except Christmas Day
- **Normal hours until 18 December:** Monday to Thursday 12pm to 10pm, Friday 12pm to 11pm, Saturday 10am to 11pm, Sunday 10am to 10pm
- **What's there:** an outdoor rink run by Underbelly, a Christmas market with local traders, a rooftop bar and a Big Wheel
- **Prices:** skating prices are on the booking page only. Big Wheel and ride tickets are bought on the day, by card

The opening weekend is likely to be busy, so book a skate slot ahead. Our [Liverpool Christmas market guide](/guides/liverpool-christmas-markets-guide) has more on the market itself.

### Liverpool v Manchester United (Sunday 22 November, 4.30pm)

Liverpool FC moved this Premier League match to **4.30pm on Sunday 22 November**, live on Sky Sports. Tickets go through the club's own sales system. If you're coming for the game, read our [Anfield match day guide](/guides/anfield-match-day-accommodation) for travel and timings.

### Shows and gigs

- **Elf The Musical** at the Liverpool Empire, 19 to 22 November, per Visit Liverpool's Christmas shows guide
- **Fontaines D.C.** at M&S Bank Arena on Friday 20 November
- **Squeeze** at M&S Bank Arena on Saturday 21 November
- **Jonathan Pie** on 21 and 22 November, listed by Visit Liverpool among the arena's events
- **The Rock 'n' Roll Panto: Beauty and the Beast** at the Everyman, with performances from 19 November. See our [Liverpool panto 2026 guide](/guides/liverpool-panto-2026)

### Family evenings out

- **Enchanted at Knowsley Safari** runs on selected evenings from 20 November to 23 December 2026, with tickets from £9.95
- **Icebox at Boxpark Liverpool** has been open since 16 November, with 45-minute sessions from £8 for children and from £10 for adults

## Weekend 2: Friday 27 to Sunday 29 November 2026

### Black Friday (Friday 27 November)

Black Friday falls on **Friday 27 November 2026**. We couldn't find any official Black Friday events announced by Liverpool ONE, so treat it as a normal (busy) shopping day with individual store offers. What is already running at Liverpool ONE:

- A new **30-metre walk-through Christmas tree** at Paradise Place, with decorations live since 6 November
- **Stone's Throw Social** in Chavasse Park, with eight ice-free curling lanes, karaoke huts and shuffleboard, running to 3 January 2027. Walk-ins are accepted, but pre-booking is recommended

Tip: if you're driving in from outside the centre, Black Friday car parks fill early. The train is easier.

### Gladiators Live and the Motown show

- **Gladiators Live Tour** at M&S Bank Arena, Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 November
- **A Night To Remember: Motown Show**, Saturday 28 November

### Speke Hall Christmas opening

The National Trust's Christmas page for Speke Hall lists the house opening on **28 and 29 November**, then Wednesdays to Sundays until 20 December, **12.30pm to 4pm**. Booking isn't required for the house. Entry is included with a property ticket, and free for members and under-5s.

### The Merseyside derby (Sunday 29 November, 12pm)

**Everton v Liverpool** has been moved to **12pm on Sunday 29 November** at Hill Dickinson Stadium, live on TNT Sports. It's the first derby of the season. Expect very busy trains around Sandhills and road closures near the stadium. Our [Merseyside derby weekend guide](/guides/merseyside-derby-weekend) covers planning a trip around it.

## Getting around

### By train from north Liverpool

Direct Merseyrail trains run from Fazakerley to **Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes**. From Central, St George's Plateau, Liverpool ONE and the Empire are all a short walk. Merseyrail takes contactless: tap in and tap out.

### On match days

For Hill Dickinson Stadium, Sandhills station is the usual stop, and it's on the same line as Fazakerley with no change needed. For Anfield, see our [match day parking guide](/guides/liverpool-match-day-parking-guide) if you're driving.

## Planning tips for late November

- **Book skating early** for opening weekend and Saturday afternoons
- **Check kick-off times** the week before. TV changes are common
- **Avoid driving into town** on derby day and Black Friday if you can
- **Pack for wind and rain.** St George's Plateau is exposed
- **Have a plan B indoors**, like the museums or a show, in case of storms
