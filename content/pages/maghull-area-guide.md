Maghull is a canal town on Liverpool's northern edge, in the borough of Sefton. It's mostly residential, with about 20,000 people, two Merseyrail stations and the **Leeds and Liverpool Canal** running through the old centre. For visitors, its big advantage is location: it sits right by **Switch Island**, where the M57 and M58 begin, so Aintree, Ormskirk, the Sefton coast and the motorway network are all close.

## Key facts

- **Where:** a town and civil parish in the Metropolitan Borough of Sefton, Merseyside, north of Liverpool and west of Kirkby
- **Population:** 20,444 at the 2011 Census
- **History:** recorded as "Magele" in the Domesday Survey of 1086
- **Canal:** the Leeds and Liverpool Canal reached Maghull in **1774**
- **Railway:** arrived in **1849**, on the Liverpool, Ormskirk and Preston Railway
- **Main road:** Northway (the A59), built in 1933 and dualled in the early 1960s, runs through the town

## Things to see

### The canal

The Leeds and Liverpool Canal runs through the centre of old Maghull. The towpath is a flat walk, good for a morning stroll or a run, and the Red Lion pub was originally built to serve canal trade.

### St Andrew's Old Church

The small chapel in the churchyard of the Victorian St Andrew's is described as **the oldest church building in Merseyside still in regular use for worship**.

### Frank Hornby's town

Frank Hornby, founder of Meccano, Dinky Toys and Hornby Railways, lived in Maghull, first at "The Hollies" and later at "Quarry Brook". He's buried in St Andrew's churchyard. The Hollies was the **first building outside London to get a blue plaque**.

### Maghull Town Hall

The town hall had an £8.2 million extension in 2009, adding a library and sports facilities.

## Getting around

### By train

Maghull has two stations on Merseyrail's Northern line:

- **Maghull** (opened 1849)
- **Maghull North** (opened **18 June 2018**) at the north end of the town

Trains run south through Aintree towards Liverpool city centre and north to Ormskirk. Check Merseyrail for current times.

### By road

The **M57 and M58 both start at Switch Island**, between Aintree and Maghull. The M58 runs east towards Skelmersdale and the M6, and the M57 runs south around the east of Liverpool to the M62. The A59 Northway runs through Maghull towards Ormskirk to the north and Aintree and Liverpool to the south. See our [M57 and M58 accommodation guide](/guides/accommodation-near-m57-m58-liverpool).
