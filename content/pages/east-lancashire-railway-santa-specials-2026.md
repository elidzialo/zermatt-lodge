A steam train, a brass band and a visit to Santa is hard to beat for young children. The East Lancashire Railway (ELR) runs its Santa Specials from Bury, at the top of the Metrolink Bury line. We took the details below from the ELR's own page on 24 November 2026. Popular dates sell out, so book early.

## 2026 dates

| Date | Adult from | Child from | Adult plus under 3 on knee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sat 28 and Sun 29 Nov | £25 | £24.50 | £39 |
| Sat 5 and Sun 6 Dec | £30 | £29.50 | £44 |
| Sat 12 and Sun 13 Dec | £34 | £33.50 | £48 |
| Sat 19 Dec | £34 | £33.50 | £48 |
| Sun 20 Dec | £41.50 | £41 | £59 |
| Mon 21 Dec | £35 | £34.50 | £51 |
| Tue 22 and Wed 23 Dec | £31 | £30.50 | £45 |
| Thu 24 Dec (Christmas Eve) | £41.50 | £41 | £59 |

A **£2.50 booking fee** applies per order. The ELR offers Pay in 3 through PayPal. **British Sign Language** interpreters are available on 28 and 29 November and 5 and 22 December, with limited places; contact the railway to ask.

## What's included

- A **winter wonderland walkthrough** at Bury Bolton Street station.
- Time with festive characters, including elves, Mrs Claus and the Ice Princess.
- The **steam train ride** from Bury to Rawtenstall and back, about **80 minutes**.
- A visit from **Santa**, with time for photos.
- A **gift for each child**, chosen by age band, and an activity book.
- A **brass band** on board.

Allow about **1 hour 30 minutes** for the whole experience. Passengers stay on the train throughout.

## Getting there

- **Station:** Bury Bolton Street, BL9 0EY, in Bury town centre.
- **Tram:** the ELR recommends the **Metrolink** from Manchester. Bury is the last stop on the Bury line.
- **Parking:** limited and charged at the station.
- **Santa Shuttle:** a diesel railcar from **Heywood**, which has **free parking**, takes about **15 minutes** to Bury. Shuttle tickets are open return and booked separately. It doesn't run on **24 December**.
