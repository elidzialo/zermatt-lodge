An annexe, granny flat or garden studio can make a good short stay: guests get their own front door, and you are close by. Before you list it, a few checks matter more for annexes than for other homes.

## 1. Planning conditions

Many annexes were given planning permission on the basis that they are used together with the main house, for example by family. That can come with a condition that limits letting it separately. Find your planning decision notice (your council's planning portal usually has it) and read the conditions. If you are not sure, ask your council before you take bookings.

## 2. Tax

- **Rent a Room Scheme:** up to £7,500 a year tax-free from furnished accommodation in your only or main home. HMRC's helpsheet HS223 does not settle whether a self-contained annexe counts as part of your home, so check with HMRC or an accountant before you rely on it. More in [the Rent a Room Scheme and short lets, explained](/guides/rent-a-room-scheme-short-lets-2026).
- **Business rates:** in England, a self-catering property available for 140 nights or more a year and actually let for 70 nights or more can be assessed for business rates instead of council tax.

## 3. Mortgage and insurance

Tell your lender and your home insurer. Letting a separate space to paying guests can change both. Get cover that includes paying guests and public liability.

## 4. Safety

- Do a fire risk assessment using GOV.UK's guide for small paying guest accommodation.
- Fit smoke alarms and, where there is a fuel-burning appliance, a carbon monoxide alarm.
- Have gas appliances checked every year by a Gas Safe engineer, and keep an electrical safety report.
- Light the path from the street or parking to the annexe door.

## 5. Privacy for you and your guests

- Explain the entrance, parking and any shared garden on the listing.
- Agree how and when guests can contact you.
- Keep your street address and house number out of the photos and text; booking sites share it after booking.

## 6. Listing it

Describe the space honestly: whether it is one room or has a separate bedroom, the kitchen (full kitchen or kitchenette), and the bathroom. Guests who like annexes are often visiting family nearby, working in the area, or couples on a short break.

## List your annexe on Book Homes

On Book Homes, choose **Studio**, **Flat** or **Cottage** and say it is an annexe. Already on another site? Keep it there and sync the calendars. [See how it works for annexes](/hosts/annexes-and-granny-flats) or [list your home](/become-a-host).
