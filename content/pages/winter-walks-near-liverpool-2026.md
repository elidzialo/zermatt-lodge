The best winter walks near Liverpool are on the **Sefton coast**, just north of the city. **Crosby Beach** has Antony Gormley's 100 cast-iron "Iron Men", and **Formby** has National Trust pine woods, sand dunes and red squirrels. Both are reachable by Merseyrail. Closer to home, **Croxteth Country Park** in north-east Liverpool is a big green space for a gentler stroll.

A walk is the perfect antidote to a big Christmas dinner, a long panto or a day at the market. Here's what to know for winter 2026, checked on 19 November.

## Crosby Beach: Another Place

**Another Place** is Antony Gormley's installation of **100 cast-iron, life-size figures** spread along about three kilometres of foreshore and reaching almost a kilometre out to sea. Each weighs 650 kilos, is cast from the artist's own body, and faces out to the horizon. In winter light, with the wind coming off the Irish Sea, it's one of the most striking walks in the north west.

### Safety first

Sefton Council is clear about the risks:

- Crosby is **not a bathing beach**
- There are **areas of soft sand and mud** and a **risk of changing tides**
- Stay **within 50 metres of the promenade** at all tides
- **Check tide times** before you go, especially if you want to see the statues

In winter, the light goes early, so plan your walk for the middle of the day.

### Getting there

- **Train:** the council names **Blundellsands and Crosby** as the closest station to the Iron Men beach entrance, on Merseyrail's Southport line
- **Car:** there's **no on-beach parking**. Pay-and-display car parks are in Crosby Coastal Park at Crosby Lakeside, Blucher Street and Burbo Bank

Our [Crosby area guide](/guides/crosby-area-guide) has more on the town itself.

## Formby: pine woods, dunes and red squirrels

Formby is run by the National Trust. Its woodlands are habitat for **rare red squirrels**, and the beach is wide and wild in winter.

### Short trails

The National Trust lists three waymarked walks:

| Trail | Distance | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Lost Resort Trail | 1.25 miles (2 km) | The seaside resort that was never built |
| Devil's Hole Trail | 1.5 miles (2.4 km) | Dune grasslands of Ravenmeols Local Nature Reserve |
| Formby Asparagus Trail | 1.75 miles (2.8 km) | The history of asparagus growing at Formby |

All are short enough for children and grandparents.

### Parking and opening

- **Parking:** £9 at either car park. Free for National Trust members and Blue Badge holders
- **Car parks:** Lifeboat Road (postcode L37 2EB) and Victoria Road (L37 1LJ). Both are ticketless with ANPR. Pay at the machine or with JustPark
- **Winter hours:** the page we checked showed car parks open 9am to 4pm, with the beaches and countryside open all day
- **Toilets:** at both car parks, with baby changing, opening 9.30am and closing 4pm in winter
- **Food:** mobile vans run in spring and summer only, so bring a flask and snacks in winter
- **Dogs:** welcome under close control

The page doesn't mention Christmas closures, so check before Christmas Day, Boxing Day or New Year's Day trips.

### Getting there by train

**Formby** station is a short walk from Lifeboat Road, and **Freshfield** station is a short walk from Victoria Road. The National Trust says Merseyrail trains run every 15 minutes.

Note that the National Trust asks all group visits to be booked in advance, and coaches and minibuses can't park at weekends, school holidays or bank holidays. A family in one or two cars is fine. See our [Formby area guide](/guides/formby-area-guide).

## Croxteth Country Park

For something inland, **Croxteth Hall and Country Park** in West Derby has woodland and open parkland around the historic hall. In 2024, Croxteth Hall's website listed **free parking from 7am to 6pm**, using postcode **L11 1EH** for the Muirhead Avenue entrance. Check current arrangements, and look out for Christmas events at the hall. Our [West Derby village guide](/guides/west-derby-village-guide) covers the area.

## Getting to the coast

From the Kirkby or Ormskirk branches of the Northern line, change at **Sandhills** for the Southport line north to Blundellsands and Crosby, Formby and Freshfield. Our [Sandhills station guide](/guides/sandhills-station-guide) explains the station. Over Christmas and New Year, check the festive timetable first: in past years Merseyrail hasn't run on Christmas Day.

By car, both Crosby and Formby are easy to reach, which helps with a family and wet kit.

## Winter walk checklist

- **Tide times** for Crosby
- **Waterproofs and a warm hat.** The coast is exposed
- **Wellies or boots.** Sand, mud and puddles
- **A flask and snacks.** Winter facilities are limited
- **A torch or phone light.** Sunset is early in December
- **A way to pay at the machine, or the JustPark app,** for Formby parking

## A winter walk weekend

Pair a coastal walk with the city: a Saturday at the [Christmas market](/guides/liverpool-christmas-markets-guide), then a Sunday morning at Formby or Crosby before you head home. On Boxing Day, a walk works well before or after racing at Aintree. See our [Boxing Day in Liverpool guide](/guides/boxing-day-liverpool-2026).
