The Tour de France comes to Liverpool on **Saturday 3 July 2027**. Stage 2 of the men's race runs **223km from Keswick to Liverpool**, crossing the Lancashire fells and the boroughs of **Knowsley and St Helens** before a sprint finish on **The Strand**, in front of the Three Graces. It's free to watch. If you want to be there, book accommodation well ahead, plan to use the train, and expect road closures across the city region on the day.

## The 2027 Grand Départ at a glance

The 2027 Tour starts in Britain, with three stages before the race heads back to France.

| Stage | Date | Route | Distance | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fri 2 July 2027 | Edinburgh to Carlisle | 183km | Flat |
| **2** | **Sat 3 July 2027** | **Keswick to Liverpool** | **223km** | **Hilly** |
| 3 | Sun 4 July 2027 | Welshpool to Cardiff | 223km | Medium mountains |

The women's race, the Tour de France Femmes, also starts in Britain in 2027, with stages from Friday 30 July including **Leeds to Manchester**. Check the official site for confirmed details.

## The stage 2 route

According to Cycling Weekly, stage 2 starts in Keswick and passes **Thirlmere, Grasmere, Rydal Water and Windermere**, then follows the Morecambe Bay coast and crosses the Lancashire fells via Lancaster. The categorised climbs include:

- Côte de Jubilee Tower (3.8km at 6.1%)
- Côte de Trough of Bowland
- Côte de Waddington Fell
- Côte de Belmont
- Côte de Parbold

After Parbold the race enters the Liverpool City Region through **Knowsley and St Helens**, then heads for the waterfront. The Liverpool City Region Combined Authority describes the finish as a "super sprint" along the waterfront in the shadow of the Three Graces.

Exact roads through Knowsley and St Helens had not been published in the sources we checked. Watch [letourgb.com](https://www.letourgb.com) for the detailed route and closure maps, which usually appear closer to the event.

## Where to watch

**At the finish on The Strand.** This is where the atmosphere and the big screens will be, and it will be packed. Arrive very early, travel light and expect security checks. The finish is the moment the sprinters arrive at full speed, so the best spots go hours ahead.

**On the approach through Knowsley or St Helens.** Watching on the route outside the city centre usually means more space, an easier day for children and a longer look at the riders as they pass. You won't see the finish, but you can follow it on a phone.

**On the climbs in Lancashire.** Climbs like Parbold spread the riders out, so you see them for longer. This means a drive north and a much earlier start.

Whichever you choose, remember the publicity caravan passes well before the riders and is a highlight for kids.

## Getting around on the day

- **Train is the way in.** From Zermatt Lodge, trains reach Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes. Expect crowded trains and possible changes to services; check Merseyrail before you set off
- **Driving into town is likely to be hard.** Closures around the waterfront and along the route are expected. Leave the car where you're staying if you can
- **Coming from further away?** Liverpool Lime Street has frequent trains from Manchester, with the fastest taking about 31 minutes
- **Cycling?** The Liverpool City Region says it is building more than 600km of new cycling and walking routes. Check secure parking near the finish before you ride in

## Where to stay

A free Saturday event in early July, when Liverpool is already busy with summer visitors, means accommodation will go fast. Things to think about:

- **City centre:** closest to the finish, but highest prices and minimum stays are likely
- **North Liverpool and Aintree:** quick train links into the centre, more space for groups and easier parking
- **Knowsley and St Helens:** handy if you plan to watch on the route rather than at the finish

If you're coming as a group of cycling friends or a family, a whole house is often easier than several hotel rooms. Everyone eats together, bikes and kit have somewhere to go, and you're not paying city-centre rates for four rooms.
