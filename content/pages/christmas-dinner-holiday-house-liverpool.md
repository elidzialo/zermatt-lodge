Cooking Christmas dinner away from home is very doable, and for a big family it's often nicer than a restaurant: no time slots, no taxis, and everyone can stay in their slippers. The trick is planning the shopping and the oven before you arrive.

## Key dates for Christmas 2026

| Date | Day | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 24 December | Thursday | Last full day for a big supermarket shop |
| 25 December | Friday | Large shops closed by law |
| 26 December | Saturday | Boxing Day; many shops reopen, some with shorter hours |
| 28 December | Monday | Substitute bank holiday |

The **Christmas Day (Trading) Act 2004** stops shops over **280 square metres** opening on Christmas Day in England and Wales. Convenience stores, pharmacies and shops at stations can be exempt, but they won't have everything for a full roast.

## Shopping plan

1. **Book a delivery for your arrival day.** Choose a slot after check-in (3pm at Zermatt Lodge). Our [grocery delivery guide](/guides/grocery-delivery-to-holiday-house) has more tips.
2. **Order the meat early.** If you want a particular turkey or joint, order it from your supermarket or a butcher ahead of time.
3. **Top up on Christmas Eve** for bread, milk and anything forgotten.

## What to bring from home

Holiday kitchens are usually well stocked for everyday cooking but not always for a feast. It's worth packing:

- A **large roasting tin** (check the oven size with your host if you can)
- A **meat thermometer**
- **Foil, baking paper and cling film**
- Your favourite **knife** and a **peeler**
- **Gravy, stuffing, cranberry sauce** and any spices you always use
- **Crackers, napkins and a table runner**
- **Containers** for leftovers and Boxing Day sandwiches

## An oven timetable for one oven

For a group of about 7, keep it simple:

- **Morning:** peel and prep all the vegetables. Par-boil the potatoes.
- **Meat first:** roast it, then rest it under foil while the oven does the rest. Resting frees up the oven for 30 minutes or more.
- **Potatoes and stuffing next**, at a high heat.
- **Hob for veg and gravy.**
- **Pudding:** steam it on the hob or warm it in the microwave.

Give each person a job. Peeling, laying the table and washing up are good tasks for children and teenagers.
