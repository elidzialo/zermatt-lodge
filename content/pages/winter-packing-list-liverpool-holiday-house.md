Liverpool in winter is cold, often wet and very windy by the river, but that's half the fun if you're dressed for it. Here's a packing list built for a **self-catering holiday house** stay between December and February, whether you're coming for Christmas, a match, a race day or a family break.

## Clothes

- **Waterproof coat with a hood.** An umbrella is no match for the wind on the waterfront.
- **Warm layers:** jumpers or fleeces you can take off indoors.
- **Hat, gloves and a scarf.**
- **Waterproof shoes or boots.** Parks and beaches can be muddy.
- **Something smarter** if you're booked for a show, a restaurant or a race day.
- **Slippers or thick socks** for the house.
- **A bag for wet clothes and shoes.**

## Match day and race day kit

- Tickets **downloaded to your phone** (signal can be patchy in crowds)
- A **small bag**, as stadiums and racecourses have bag-size rules
- **Portable phone charger**
- **Hand warmers** for long cold afternoons

See our [Anfield match day guide](/guides/anfield-match-day-accommodation) or [Grand National first-timers guide](/guides/grand-national-first-timers-guide) for more.

## For the kitchen

Holiday houses are usually set up for everyday cooking. A few extras make life easier:

- Your favourite **spices, sauces and oils**
- **Tea, coffee and breakfast** for the first morning
- **Snacks** for the drive or train
- **Food storage containers** and freezer bags
- For Christmas, see our [Christmas dinner in a holiday house guide](/guides/christmas-dinner-holiday-house-liverpool)

## For families

- **Medicines** and a small first-aid kit
- **Night lights** for younger children in a new house
- **Favourite toys, books and a tablet with downloads**
- Our [babies and toddlers guide](/guides/babies-and-toddlers-liverpool-stay) has a full list for little ones

## Tech

- **Phone chargers** and a **multi-socket extension**
- A **UK plug adapter** if you're coming from outside the UK and Ireland (the UK uses three-pin Type G plugs)
- **Streaming logins** for the Smart TV
