An open day often means an early start, a long day on your feet and a teenager who wants to see the city too. Coming the night before, and staying somewhere with a kitchen and a bit of space, makes the whole thing easier. We checked these dates on the universities' own pages on 24 November 2026. Always book your place with the university.

## University of Salford

- **Next undergraduate open day:** **Saturday 12 December 2026, 9.30am to 4pm**.
- **Three sites:** **Peel Park**, **Frederick Road** and **MediaCity**. Your check-in point depends on your course.
- **Train:** **Salford Crescent** station is on the Peel Park campus and about 5 minutes' walk from Frederick Road.
- **Tram:** MediaCity is on the tram.
- **Shuttle:** a **free shuttle bus** runs between MediaCity and Peel Park. Allow at least 30 minutes between campuses.
- **Parking:** **free** in university car parks at Peel Park and Frederick Road on the day, except the car park in front of Salford Museum and Art Gallery. There's **no free parking at MediaCity**.

## Manchester Metropolitan University

- **Postgraduate open day:** **Saturday 13 February 2027**. Book on the university's site.
- Manchester Met says its **undergraduate open days are in the autumn**.
- Its campus is on **Oxford Road**, close to central Manchester, with good bus and tram links.

## University of Manchester

- The university runs **in-person and virtual undergraduate open days**. In-person days include subject talks, student life talks and campus tours.
- Its most recent in-person day was on **10 October 2026**. Check the university's open days page for the next date, or ask about individual campus visits.

## Tips for the day

1. **Arrive the night before** if you're coming from far away, so you're not stuck in Saturday traffic.
2. **Book talks early.** Popular subject talks fill up.
3. **Plan a second day** to look at where students live and get a feel for the city.
4. **Bring snacks and water.** Campus cafes get busy.
