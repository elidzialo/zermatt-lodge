When someone is in Fairfield for more than a day or two, the family needs somewhere to sleep, eat and wash clothes between visits. We checked the details below on the Northern Care Alliance's own pages on 24 November 2026. For visiting times and ward rules, always ask the ward.

## Getting there

- **Address:** Fairfield General Hospital, **Rochdale Old Road, Bury, BL9 7TD**.
- **Tram:** the nearest Metrolink stop is **Bury**, about **2 miles** away, at the end of the Bury line.
- **Bus:** from **Bury Interchange**, take the **433, 467 or 468**, about **15 minutes**. The **433** stops inside the grounds, including the main entrance. The 467 and 468 stop on Rochdale Old Road, outside the grounds.
- **Taxi:** taxis can drop off and collect at all hospital entrances.

## Parking

- **Car Park 1:** first left after the traffic lights at the entrance. **Car Parks 2 to 4:** fourth exit at the mini-roundabout.
- The Trust says parking is **very busy on weekday daytimes**, and nearby streets have **permit-controlled parking**.

| Time | Charge |
|---|---|
| Up to 1 hour | £1 |
| 2 hours | £2 |
| 6 hours | £3 |
| 8 hours | £4 |
| 12 hours | £6 |
| 24 hours | £8 |
| Weekly (up to 7 days) | £15 |
| Monthly permit | £25 |

Up to 30 minutes is free only in the marked drop-off and pick-up areas. Pay by the PayByPhone app or online (location 807068), by cash at machines (no change given) or by card at some machines. The Trust warns that PayByPhone doesn't use QR codes, so watch out for scam codes.

**On a long stay, the weekly ticket is far cheaper** than paying daily.

## Free parking

The Trust lists free parking for:

- **Blue Badge** holders, with the badge displayed.
- Visitors to **ICU, neonatal and high dependency** units, with staff authorisation. At Fairfield this covers Ward 5, Ward 10 and Wards 11a and 11b.
- **Parents of children under 18** admitted overnight, **7.30pm to 8am**, for up to two vehicles.
- Some patients in **ongoing cancer or haematology** treatment, **end-of-life care or bereavement**, and **frequent outpatients**. Ask the ward or the Macmillan Information and Support Service.
