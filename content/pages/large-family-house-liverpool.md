If you need a large family house to rent in Liverpool, look for real bedrooms for everyone, at least two bathrooms, a garden and parking, so the holiday doesn't turn into a daily logistics puzzle. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Liverpool, sleeping 7. It has a king master with en-suite, a second king, a queen and a single, plus two bathrooms, a big kitchen, a dining room, a games room and a large private back garden. This guide shows how a family can use the house, which days out are nearby and some tips for a smooth stay.

## Who sleeps where: family layouts that work

Here are some common ways families use the four bedrooms:

**Two families travelling together**
- King master with en-suite: Family A's parents
- Second king: Family B's parents
- Queen: two children sharing
- Single: an older child

**Grandparents, parents and children**
- King master with en-suite: grandparents (handy to have their own bathroom)
- Second king: parents
- Queen: two children
- Single: a teenager who wants their own space

**One big family**
- Parents in the master, the children split across the other rooms, so nobody's sharing with a sibling they're fighting with

## Family days out

### Croxteth Hall and Country Park

Croxteth Hall and Country Park makes an easy first-day outing. The **Victorian Walled Garden** opens daily from 10am to 3pm (last admission 2:30pm). The **country park** is open all year, with inner park hours varying by season. There's also **Home Farm**, which is run separately, so check its own opening times. The hall itself opens for tours and events, so contact it before visiting. See our [Croxteth Hall visitor guide](/guides/croxteth-hall-visitor-guide).

### Knowsley Safari

Knowsley Safari is a drive-through safari reached via the M57, from **junction 2**. The safari drive's **last entry is 3pm**, and you'll be turned away if you arrive later, so set off in good time. See our [Knowsley Safari guide](/guides/knowsley-safari-guide).

### Crosby Beach and Another Place

Antony Gormley's **100 cast-iron figures** stand along more than 3km of Crosby Beach. Sefton Council advises staying close to the promenade and not walking out to the furthest statues, because the sand there is very soft and muddy. Check tide times, as the figures are covered around high tide on big tides. See our [Another Place guide](/guides/crosby-beach-another-place-guide).

### Liverpool's museums

The Museum of Liverpool, World Museum and the Walker Art Gallery are all in or near the city centre (the Merseyside Maritime Museum is closed until 2028), a short train ride from the house. They're great for rainy days.

For a fuller list, see [family days out in north Liverpool](/guides/family-days-out-north-liverpool).

## Tips for a smooth family stay

1. **Arrive after 3pm and do a big shop,** or book a delivery slot for arrival.
2. **Plan one outing per day.** Children (and grandparents) tire quickly.
3. **Mix train days and car days.** Use the train for the city and the car for the safari or beach.
4. **Book popular activities ahead** in school holidays.
5. **Keep a rainy-day plan:** board games, the games room and Netflix at home.
6. **Pack for the coast.** It can be windy on the beaches even in summer.
7. **Remember checkout is 11am** and plan a last-day outing on the way home.

## Mealtimes for a big family

Feeding seven people three times a day is the part of a family holiday that catches people out. A few ideas:

- **Breakfast:** a big pot of porridge or a fry-up at the weekend, with cereal and toast on busy mornings
- **Lunch:** packed lunches on days out are cheaper and quicker than finding a café for seven
- **Dinner:** one-pot meals like chilli, curry or a traybake, cooked once and served at the dining table
- **One night off:** a takeaway or a meal out, so the cooks get a break

Give each adult a night to cook and get the children to set the table. See our [self-catering meal plan for a group of 7](/guides/self-catering-meal-plan-group-of-7).
