Salford Quays packs a theatre, a free national museum and the BBC's northern base into a short walk around the old docks. It works well as a day out from anywhere in Greater Manchester. We checked these facts on 24 November 2026; always check opening times and engineering works before you travel.

## Getting there

- **Tram:** three stops serve the Quays area: **Broadway**, **MediaCityUK** and **Harbour City**. The **Imperial War Museum** stop on the **Trafford Park line** is on the other side of the canal.
- **From north Manchester:** from Prestwich or Crumpsall, take the Bury line into the city and change for an Eccles or MediaCityUK tram. The Bee Network journey planner shows the best change.
- **Engineering works:** The Lowry warned that trams would not run on the Eccles, Trafford Centre or Altrincham lines from 19 October to 1 November 2026 for upgrade works. Check tfgm.com for later works.
- **Bus:** Salford City Council lists Stagecoach's CityConnect 50 between Salford Quays and East Didsbury, via Salford Crescent station.

## The Lowry

- **Address:** Pier 8, The Quays, Salford, **M50 3AZ**.
- **Walk times:** about **5 minutes** from MediaCityUK stop, and about **7 minutes** from Broadway, Harbour City or Imperial War Museum (over the Millennium Bridge).
- **Parking:** no on-site car park. **Quayside MediaCityUK**, opposite, is **£7.50 for up to 6 hours** and must be **pre-booked** through YourParkingSpace. It has accessible spaces and EV chargers. Pitch Car Park (M50 2EQ) is another option.
- **Blue Badge:** two reserved spaces on the service road, first come, first served, not bookable.

## IWM North

- Opened on **5 July 2002**, designed by architect **Daniel Libeskind**.
- On **Trafford Wharf Road**, overlooking the Ship Canal, across from The Lowry and MediaCityUK.
- **General admission is free.**

## MediaCityUK

- A **200-acre** development on the Ship Canal, in Salford and Trafford.
- Home to the **BBC**, **ITV** and the **University of Salford**. The BBC began moving staff in during May 2011.
- The **MediaCityUK tram stop** opened on 20 September 2010, at the end of a spur from the Eccles line.
