Heaton Park sits right next to Prestwich in north Manchester. Manchester City Council calls it "the biggest and best park in the NorthWest". If you are staying in Prestwich for a family visit, a celebration or a longer stay, it is the obvious place for a walk, a run or a day out with children.

## Opening times

The council says the park is **open from 8am to dusk every day**. Some attractions and cafes inside the park keep their own hours, so check the council's Heaton Park pages before you plan a day around one.

## By tram

- **Heaton Park** stop is on the Metrolink **Bury line**, at the corner of Whittaker Lane and Bury Old Road, with an entrance to the park.
- **Bowker Vale** stop is also within a few minutes' walk.
- **Prestwich** stop is the next stop towards Bury.

Trams run between Bury and Manchester city centre, so you can be at the park or in town without the car. For live times use [TfGM](https://tfgm.com/public-transport/tram/stops/heaton-park-tram) or the Metrolink website. Metrolink also runs free Park and Ride car parks for tram users, including Bury Interchange, Radcliffe and Whitefield.

## By bus

The council lists the **x35, 59, 90, 135, 149, 156, 164 and 484** buses. Check routes with the TfGM journey planner.

## By car

Car park access is from the **Sheepfoot Lane, Smithy Lodge or St Margaret's Road** entrances. Postcodes for sat nav, with approximate spaces:

| Car park | Postcode | Spaces |
|---|---|---|
| Lake Car Park | M25 0DL | 263, plus a seasonal overflow |
| Golf Car Park | M25 2SW | 85 |
| Hall Car Park | M25 2GT | 252 |
| St Margaret's Road Car Park | M25 2GT | 126 |
| Garden Centre Car Park | M25 2GT | 9, plus an overflow of 50 |

**Charges:** pay and display every day between 10am and 5pm, with a **maximum of £3.50 a day**. Annual permits are available. **Blue Badge holders** park free in all the car parks, with the badge on display.

On foot or by bike, the entrances are St Margaret's Road, Bury Old Road, Sheepfoot Lane and Middleton Road.
