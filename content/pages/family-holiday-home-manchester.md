Hotels and toddlers don't mix well. One room, one bathroom and a 7pm bedtime means the adults spend the evening whispering in the dark. A whole house fixes that: children in their own rooms, a kitchen for the meals they'll actually eat, a washer for the inevitable spills and, usually, a garden and a drive.

Book Homes has **8 whole homes in Greater Manchester**, sleeping 4 to 12. See them on the [Manchester hub](/stays-in/manchester).

## Where to stay

- **Prestwich.** Quiet streets of family houses next to Heaton Park, with Metrolink trams from Prestwich and Heaton Park stops into the city. Several of our homes here have gardens and free parking. See [homes in Prestwich](/stays-in/prestwich).
- **Crumpsall.** A large 5-bedroom house with a pool table and free parking, good for families travelling together.
- **Salford.** Smaller homes close to the city centre. See [homes in Salford](/stays-in/salford).

## What to check for children

1. **Cot and high chair.** Several of our Manchester listings include them. Confirm with the host, and ask if bedding for the cot is provided.
2. **Stairs and gates.** Ask about stair gates if you have a crawler.
3. **Bath.** Many listings have a bath, which matters for bedtime routines.
4. **Garden.** Ask whether it's enclosed.
5. **Parking.** Kids come with a lot of luggage. A drive at the door helps.
6. **Bed list.** Check exactly who sleeps where before you book.

## Easy days out with children

- **Heaton Park.** Next to Prestwich. Manchester City Council says it is open **from 8am to dusk every day**. Our [Heaton Park visitor guide](/guides/heaton-park-prestwich-visitor-guide) covers the trams, buses and car parks.
- **Manchester Museum.** On Oxford Road, and **free to enter**, with no booking for the general galleries. It's closed on Mondays. Over Christmas it says it is **closed 24 to 28 December and 1 January**.
- **LEGOLAND Discovery Centre and SEA LIFE Manchester.** Next door to each other at the Trafford Palazzo, by the Trafford Centre. Adults can only enter LEGOLAND Discovery Centre with a child. See our [LEGOLAND and SEA LIFE guide](/guides/legoland-discovery-and-sea-life-manchester).
- **Christmas light trails.** Heaton Park, Dunham Massey and RHS Garden Bridgewater all run trails this winter. See [Christmas light trails 2026](/guides/christmas-light-trails-manchester-2026).
- **Christmas markets.** Free to enter in the city centre. See [Manchester Christmas Markets 2026](/guides/manchester-christmas-markets-2026-stay).

## School holiday dates

Manchester City Council lists the **Christmas holiday** from Friday 18 December 2026 (schools close) to Monday 4 January 2027 (schools reopen), and **February half term** from Friday 12 February to Monday 22 February 2027. Homes book up quickly around these dates.

Strictly no parties or events - bookings found to be for parties are cancelled. Family homes on quiet streets work best for families who want a quiet base.
