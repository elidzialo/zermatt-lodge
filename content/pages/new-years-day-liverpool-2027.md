New Year's Day 2027 falls on **Friday 1 January**, a bank holiday, which turns it into the start of a long weekend. In Liverpool you can ease in with the **New Year's Day Proms at 3pm** at the Philharmonic, go skating, catch one of the last Christmas shows, or get a walk in before **Liverpool v Coventry at Anfield on Saturday 2 January**.

We checked the details below on 21 November 2026. Opening hours on bank holidays change, so confirm before you go.

## New Year's Day Proms

Liverpool Philharmonic lists the **New Year's Day Proms** on **Friday 1 January 2027 at 3pm**.

- **Performers:** the Manchester Concert Orchestra, conducted by John Andrews
- **Programme highlights:** the finale of Puccini's La boheme, the finale of Tchaikovsky's Swan Lake, Elgar's Nimrod, Holst's Jupiter and Pomp and Circumstance March No. 1
- **Tickets:** listed at £26, £31, £41.50, £49 and £56, with a 10% fee on online and phone orders

A 3pm start suits families and anyone who stayed up late the night before.

## Skating

- **Icebox at Boxpark Liverpool:** open daily to 10 January 2027 except Christmas Day
- **Skate St George's**, St George's Plateau: runs to 3 January 2027, so New Year's weekend is one of the last chances

Our [ice skating guide](/guides/liverpool-ice-skating-winter) has prices and session times.

## Shows still running

Several Christmas shows run into the new year, including **Mamma Mia** at the Empire and **Snow White** at the Epstein Theatre (both to 3 January) and **Little Shop of Horrors** at the Playhouse (listed to 9 January). Check each theatre for New Year's Day performances. See our [panto guide](/guides/liverpool-panto-2026).

## A New Year's Day walk

A bracing walk is a Liverpool tradition for many. The coast north of the city, at **Crosby** and **Formby**, is close by car. Check car park hours and tide times, and go in the middle of the day while it's light. Our [winter walks guide](/guides/winter-walks-near-liverpool-2026) has routes.

## The long weekend

| Date | What's on |
|---|---|
| **Fri 1 Jan** | Bank holiday. New Year's Day Proms, 3pm |
| **Sat 2 Jan** | Liverpool v Coventry City, Anfield, 3pm. Everton away at Leeds |
| **Sun 3 Jan** | Last day of Skate St George's and several Christmas shows |
| **Tue 5 Jan** | Everton v Aston Villa, Hill Dickinson Stadium, 7.30pm |

For the rest of the month, see our [January 2027 events guide](/guides/liverpool-january-2027-events).

## Getting around

The festive timetable for Merseyrail and buses hadn't been published when we checked. Expect a **reduced service on New Year's Day**, and plan for taxis being in short supply late on New Year's Eve. If you're staying a short train ride out, check the first and last trains before you set off.
