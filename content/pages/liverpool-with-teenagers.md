Keeping teenagers happy on a family trip is mostly about **giving them some choice and a bit of independence**. Liverpool is good for that: it's compact, the trains are cheap for under-19s, and there's plenty to do that isn't a museum (though the museums are good too).

We checked prices and dates on 25 November 2026. Check again before you book.

## On the ice

- **ICEBOX at BOXPARK Liverpool** (Baltic Triangle): open daily **16 November 2026 to 10 January 2027**. Adult and junior tickets (13 to 17) **from £10**, with skate hire and 45 minutes on the ice.
- **Skate St George's** and the **Pier Head rink** are the other big winter options.

Our [ice skating guide](/guides/liverpool-ice-skating-winter) compares all three.

## Up high

The **Liverpool Cathedral Tower** is a good one for teens who like a view. You go up **two lifts and then 108 stairs**. Tickets are **£8 standard** or **£22.50 for a family** (2 adults and up to 3 children). See our [cathedrals guide](/guides/liverpool-cathedrals-visitor-guide) for hours, which are shorter in late November and early December.

## Football

Even non-fans tend to enjoy a stadium tour. See our [Anfield stadium tour guide](/guides/anfield-stadium-tour-guide), or the [Everton stadium guide](/guides/everton-stadium-accommodation) for Hill Dickinson Stadium.

## Games and challenges

- **Escape rooms:** a good rainy-day activity for a family team. See our [escape rooms guide](/guides/escape-rooms-liverpool-groups).
- **Go-karting:** check minimum ages and heights. See our [go-karting guide](/guides/go-karting-liverpool).
- **Competitive socialising:** crazy golf, darts, bowling and more. See our [competitive socialising guide](/guides/competitive-socialising-liverpool).
- **Curling at Liverpool ONE:** Stone's Throw Social at Chavasse Park runs from 3 October 2026 to 3 January 2027, with eight ice-free curling lanes. Check age rules when you book.

## Food and shopping

- **Street food** at Baltic Market, BOXPARK or Duke Street Market. See our [food markets guide](/guides/liverpool-food-markets-guide).
- **Liverpool ONE** for shops. See our [Liverpool ONE Christmas shopping guide](/guides/liverpool-one-christmas-shopping-2026).

## Getting around on their own

A **Merseyrail Day Saver** for 5 to 18 year olds is **£2.95 for all areas**. It isn't valid between 06.31 and 09.29 on weekdays. Our [getting around guide](/guides/getting-around-liverpool-public-transport) explains Tap and Go contactless too.

## Tips

- **Let them plan one day.** Give them a budget and a train map.
- **Agree a meeting point** in the city centre, like a station entrance.
- **Evening venues may be 18+,** so check before you go.
