When a flood, fire or escape of water leaves a Merseyside home unliveable, the insurer or its loss adjuster usually arranges **alternative accommodation** for the household. According to ABI guidance, most buildings and contents policies allow **up to 20% of the total insured value** for alternative accommodation and extra costs, and drying alone can take **weeks to months**. So the right booking is one that fits the family's daily life and can be extended without a fuss.

This guide is written for the people who place families: loss adjusters, claims handlers and alternative accommodation teams. If you are the displaced householder, our [insurance alternative accommodation guide for families](/guides/insurance-temporary-accommodation-liverpool) is written for you.

## The numbers that shape the booking

| Factor | What the sources say | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Typical limit | Up to 20% of the total insured value (ABI). Comparethemarket gives the example of 20% of £300,000 cover, allowing up to £60,000 | Sets the budget for the whole stay, not just the first week |
| Contents-only cover | The limit is based on the contents sum insured (ABI) | Tenants' budgets are often much tighter |
| First contact | The loss adjuster should normally contact the household within 24 hours of the claim (ABI) | The first booking is often urgent |
| Drying | A few weeks to several months; moisture can stay in walls and floors after surfaces look dry (ABI) | Initial bookings are frequently extended |
| Return home | A few weeks to a year or more, depending on damage (ABI) | Choose a provider that can do long stays |
| Extra costs | Many policies pay a disturbance allowance for food, laundry, travel, pet boarding and council tax (Comparethemarket) | Self-catering can reduce the claim's food costs |

## What a displaced household actually needs

Bookers know the policy. The household knows its routine. The placements that work best meet both.

**Stay near normal life.** The single biggest stress for families is the school run. A home within a sensible drive or train ride of the children's school and the adults' work keeps life stable. Our guide to [school places when relocating](/guides/liverpool-school-admissions-relocating) covers the Liverpool, Sefton and Knowsley systems if a move is longer than expected.

**Keep the family together.** Two hotel rooms split a family. A whole house with separate bedrooms, a kitchen and a living room keeps routines going.

**A kitchen.** Self-catering lets the family cook normally, which matters for children, special diets and budgets.

**Parking.** Many families still need the car for work and school. Free private parking avoids daily charges.

**Laundry.** Weeks of hotel laundry is expensive and stressful. A washing machine helps.

**A quiet, residential street.** Families recovering from a loss want a normal home, not a city-centre flat above a bar.

## A booking checklist for claims teams

Before you confirm:

1. **Claim reference and billing details.** Company name, billing address and whether a VAT invoice is needed
2. **Who is staying.** Adults, children, ages, and any mobility needs
3. **Pets.** Many providers, including Zermatt Lodge, don't accept them. Check whether the policy covers kennels or catteries
4. **Start date and likely length.** Book the first block, then agree how extensions will work
5. **Notice to extend or shorten.** Ask the provider how much notice they need
6. **Location.** Map the route to school and work at rush hour
7. **Parking.** How many spaces, and are they free?
8. **Bedding layout.** Who sleeps where? Children may want to share at first
9. **Utilities and Wi-Fi.** Included? Is the connection good enough for home working or schoolwork?
10. **Key handover.** Can the family check in at short notice?

## Where on Merseyside?

Displaced households usually want to stay close to home. A base in Liverpool with motorway access gives reasonable reach to Sefton (Bootle, Maghull, Crosby) and Knowsley (Kirkby). Motorway access matters for longer commutes: the M57 and M58 meet at Switch Island, and the M57 links to the M62 for Manchester and Warrington.

If someone in the household is also in hospital or attending appointments at Aintree University Hospital or the Walton Centre, a temporary home with free parking can take a lot of pressure off.

## When a house is not the right answer

Be honest with the household. A house may not suit:

- A one-night emergency at 2am, when a hotel is quicker
- A family with pets that can't go into boarding
- Someone who needs a ground-floor bedroom and level access throughout. Check layout and steps with the host first
