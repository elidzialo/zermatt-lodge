Greater Manchester is easy to get around without a car if you know the basics. Buses and Metrolink trams run under one brand, the **Bee Network**, with one app for journeys and tickets. This guide is for visitors, families and groups. We checked these fares on 25 November 2026. Prices can change in the new year, so check TfGM before you travel.

## The Bee Network in one minute

- **Buses** across all ten boroughs.
- **Metrolink trams** on lines including Bury, Altrincham, Eccles and MediaCityUK, Trafford Park and the Airport.
- **The Bee Network app** shows journeys, tickets, live times and travel alerts.

## Bus fares in 2026

- **Adult single:** **£2**, and it's a **hopper**: change buses as many times as you like within **an hour** of buying.
- **Child single:** **£1** hopper.
- **Frozen for 2026:** all Bee Network bus fares were frozen for the year. The £2 fare is now in its fourth year.
- **Older and disabled passengers:** free travel on Bee Network buses **at any time of day**. Before 2026 they had to pay before 9.30am.

## Tram fares

- Metrolink fares were **frozen until the end of 2026**.
- Fares depend on **zones**. Prestwich, for example, is in **zone 3** on the Bury line.
- Buy in the app, at the stop, or tap a contactless card. Tap in and out where readers are provided.

## What changes in 2027

- The **Department for Transport** announced on **22 July 2026** that single bus fares on participating services in England outside London will be capped at **£2 from 1 January to 31 December 2027**.
- We haven't seen confirmed **2027 Metrolink prices**. If you're visiting in January or February, check TfGM before you go.

## Handy routes for visitors

- **Prestwich and Crumpsall to the city:** the **Bury line** runs into Manchester Victoria, handy for the AO Arena and the National Football Museum.
- **Bury and Ramsbottom:** the Bury line ends at Bury, where you can pick up the heritage railway. See our [Ramsbottom guide](/guides/ramsbottom-day-trip-guide).
- **Trafford Centre:** the **Trafford Park line** runs direct from the city centre. See our [Snow Centre and Trafford Centre guide](/guides/snow-centre-and-trafford-centre-family-day).
- **Salford Quays:** MediaCityUK and Exchange Quay tram stops. See our [Salford Quays guide](/guides/salford-quays-visitor-guide).
- **Stockport:** quickest by train from Piccadilly, about 8 to 10 minutes. See our [Stockport guide](/guides/stockport-visitor-guide).

## Tips for families and groups

- **Buggies:** trams have level boarding, which makes them easier than buses with a pushchair.
- **Groups:** for six or more adults, compare the tram total with a taxi or minibus, especially at night.
- **Late nights:** check the last tram before an evening show or match. Services are busy after big events.
- **Parking:** if you're driving, a home with free parking near a tram stop saves paying for city centre car parks.
