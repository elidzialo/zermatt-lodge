Prestwich and Whitefield, in the borough of Bury just north of Salford, are where many visiting Jewish families end up: there are plenty of shuls, the biggest choice of kosher restaurants in the region, and two eruvs. As with any Shabbat stay, the key is to **book within walking distance of the shul you'll daven in**. Our [kosher homes page](/kosher-homes) shows homes where hosts have declared kosher kitchens, Shabbat set-ups and walking time to shul.

This guide uses published sources only and was checked on 22 November 2026. Confirm details with the community before you travel.

## The area at a glance

- **Prestwich** runs along **Bury New Road (A56)** and **Bury Old Road**, with Kings Road and Heathlands in between.
- **Whitefield** is just north of Prestwich, around Park Lane and the Besses o' th' Barn tram stop.
- Greater Manchester's Jewish population was **28,072** in the 2021 Census, according to Jewish News.

## Eruvs

### Manchester community eruv

The eruv directory describes it as "the largest Eruv in the UK, covering Broughton Park, Prestwich and surrounding areas", certified by the Manchester Beth Din. Its status hotline is listed as **0161 883 1333**.

### Whitefield eruv

Whitefield Hebrew Congregation says its eruv:

- Was **inaugurated on 12 December 2013**
- Covers **no more than 2 square kilometres** on the southern edge of Bury
- Runs from Leicester Road in the north to the **M60** in the south, and from Old Hall Lane in the west to **Besses o' th' Barn** tram stop in the east
- Is **checked every Thursday**, with "traffic lights" on the front page of the WHC website showing whether it's up

Ask your host which eruv the house is in, if any, and check the boundary yourself.

## Eating out and shopping

The **Manchester Beth Din** licensee list includes, in Prestwich:

- **Restaurants and takeaways:** JS and Sushi World on Bury New Road; Baba's, La Familia and Bono Benito at the Bury New Road end; Glida; Mozzarella and Celia's Kitchen on Kings Road; Benny's Bistro and Pagoda on Bury Old Road; The Central Cafe at Heathlands Village
- **Groceries:** Kayes Kosher on Kings Road
- **Butcher:** Glatt Butchers on Bury Old Road
- **Fish:** Getzels on Kings Road; Singing Fish on Butterstile Lane
- **Bakeries:** Bakers Heaven, Cherry on the Top, The Cake Co, The Cookie Jar

In Whitefield, **State Fayre** on Park Lane has a bakery and coffee shop on the same list.

## Getting around

**Prestwich tram stop** is on the Metrolink **Bury line**, between **Heaton Park** and **Besses o' th' Barn**. Trams run mostly every 12 minutes, with a 6-minute service between Bury and Manchester at peak times. The stop is off Rectory Lane, with a walkway to Bury New Road.

Many homes in the area have driveways or on-street parking. Ask your host, especially for a simcha weekend when the streets are busy.

## What to ask a host

1. **Walking time to my shul**, in minutes
2. **Which eruv,** if any, covers the house
3. **Kitchen:** kosher, or can I kasher, bring my own or use disposables?
4. **Shabbat set-up:** urn, hot plate, light timers, no motion sensors, a key rather than a keypad
5. **Beds and bathrooms** for the whole family
