Chanukah is a popular time for family visits: grandparents, cousins and eight nights of candles, doughnuts and latkes. If you're coming to Manchester to be with family, a whole home nearby gives everyone space, and a kitchen you can cook in.

## Chanukah 2026 dates

According to Hebcal:

- **Begins:** at sundown on **Friday 4 December 2026**, with the first candle lit that evening.
- **Ends:** at nightfall on **Saturday 12 December 2026**.

Because the first night falls on a Friday, the holiday includes **two Shabbatot**. Plan your arrival so you're settled well before Shabbat on the first night, and ask your own rabbi about lighting times on Friday and on Motzei Shabbat.

## Choosing a home

Look for:

- **A host-declared kosher kitchen**, or a kitchen where you can bring your own pots and use disposables. See our [kosher kitchen guide for holiday rentals](/guides/kosher-kitchen-holiday-rental-guide).
- **Shabbat-friendly features** declared by the host, such as an urn, a hotplate, lamp timers, a non-electric key or keypad override, and no motion-sensor lights.
- **Enough bedrooms** for the whole family, so nobody's on a sofa for eight nights.

Filter for these homes on our [kosher homes page](/kosher-homes). These are **host declarations, not a certification**. Our guide to [kosher-friendly holiday homes in Manchester](/guides/kosher-friendly-holiday-homes-manchester) lists the questions to ask.

## Lighting candles safely in a rental

- **Tell the host** you'll be lighting, and agree where the menorah goes.
- **Keep it clear** of curtains, blinds, paper decorations and anything else that can burn.
- **Use a stable, heat-proof surface** and a tray under the menorah.
- **Never leave it unattended**, and keep children and pets away.
- **Know where the smoke alarm and fire blanket are** when you arrive.
