Orrell Park is one of north Liverpool's smaller neighbourhoods: a pocket of **Victorian and Edwardian streets** between Aintree and Walton, in the L9 postcode. It doesn't have big attractions, but it has a station, a famous music venue, a tiny award-winning park and quick routes to Aintree, Anfield and the city centre. If you're visiting family, moving to north Liverpool or looking for a base for the races, it's worth knowing.

## Key facts

- **Where:** north Liverpool, between Aintree and Walton-on-the-Hill
- **Postcode:** L9
- **Constituency:** Liverpool Walton
- **Built:** mostly from **1850**, for workers and managers at the Bootle docks, with some Edwardian and post-war housing
- **Before that:** part of the Earl of Sefton's estate, used for pasture and orchards
- **Main roads:** Orrell Lane and Moss Lane

Several streets are named after historical figures from when they were built, such as Victoria Drive, Albert Drive, Alexandra Drive and Kitchener Drive.

## Things to know about

### Orrell Park Ballroom

Built as a ballroom in **1927**, the "OPB" is now a nightclub and concert venue. Rory Storm and the Hurricanes, Ringo Starr and Jerry Lee Lewis have all played there, so it has a place in Liverpool's music story.

### Devonfield Gardens

A Green Flag Award winner, and said to be **the smallest Green Flag park in England**. It hosts community events through the year.

### Local amenities

Wikipedia lists an ASDA on Orrell Lane, the Orrell Park Community Centre opposite the station, pubs including the Cuckoo and the Windsor, and the Orrell Park Hotel on Orrell Lane, which has 12 bedrooms. Check opening times before you visit.

## Getting around

### By train

**Orrell Park station** opened in **1906** and is on the Merseyrail network, the area's main link to Liverpool city centre. Check Merseyrail for current times.

### To the big venues

- **Aintree Racecourse:** in neighbouring Aintree
- **Anfield and Goodison Park:** to the south, beyond Walton
- **Hill Dickinson Stadium:** on the north docks; Sandhills is the nearest station

Use a map app for walking and driving times from the street you're staying on.

## Who stays near Orrell Park

- **Race-goers** for Aintree, especially the Grand National Festival (8 to 10 April 2027)
- **Football fans** for Anfield and Everton
- **Families visiting patients** at Aintree University Hospital and the Walton Centre in neighbouring Fazakerley
