**Knowsley Safari** is one of the best-known family days out near Liverpool, and in winter it adds an evening light trail, **Enchanted**. You drive your own car through the Safari Drive, then walk the Foot Safari.

We checked the details below on 25 November 2026. Winter hours change, so check the official site for your date.

## Enchanted 2026

- **Dates:** selected dates from **20 November to 23 December 2026**
- **Tickets:** from **£9.95**
- **Where:** the trail runs through the **Foot Safari**
- **Not included:** refreshments, rides and Santa's Grotto
- **Booking:** through the Knowsley Safari ticket portal. Not every night runs, so pick from the dates shown there.

## Daytime visits and the Safari Drive

When we checked, the opening times page listed:

| | Time |
|---|---|
| Knowsley Safari | 10am to 3.30pm |
| Safari Drive | 10am to 3pm |

**Last entry to the Safari Drive is 3pm,** and the park says cars arriving after that are refused entry. The page showed hours for **Friday to Sunday** only, so check whether it's open on your day before you set off.

## Tips for families

- **Arrive early** in winter. The Safari Drive closes mid-afternoon and it gets dark early.
- **Fill up with fuel and snacks** before the drive. You stay in your car for that part.
- **Wrap up** for the Foot Safari and Enchanted. Wellies or waterproof boots help after rain.
- **Pair it with Prescot.** Our [Prescot town guide](/guides/prescot-town-guide) covers the town and Shakespeare North Playhouse.
