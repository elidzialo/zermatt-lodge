Three days is the sweet spot for Liverpool. You get the city's big sights without rushing, an evening out, and a day on the coast or at the safari park. This plan suits a family or a group of friends, and each day has an easy option if the weather turns.

We checked opening times and closures on 24 November 2026.

## Day 1: The waterfront and the Beatles

- **Morning:** the [Royal Albert Dock](/guides/royal-albert-dock-guide) and **The Beatles Story** (£20 adults, £11 children 5 to 15, about 1.5 hours). See our [Beatles Story guide](/guides/beatles-story-visitor-guide)
- **Lunch:** at the dock or in Liverpool ONE
- **Afternoon:** walk to the **Pier Head** and the Three Graces. Pop into the free **Museum of Liverpool**
- **Late afternoon:** **Mathew Street and the Cavern Club** (£6 adults, £3 for ages 12 to 17, under-12s free). Under-18s must leave by 8pm. See our [Cavern Club guide](/guides/cavern-club-visitor-guide)
- **Evening:** a relaxed dinner

## Day 2: Cathedrals, museums and a night out

- **Morning:** **Liverpool Cathedral**, free to visit with a paid tower (£8 standard). On Sundays, go after midday
- **Late morning:** the free **World Museum** or **Walker Art Gallery** on William Brown Street. Our [museums guide](/guides/liverpool-museums-guide) explains what's open
- **Afternoon:** the **Mersey Ferry River Explorer Cruise** (about 50 minutes, timed tickets online). See our [Mersey Ferry guide](/guides/mersey-ferry-cruise-guide)
- **Evening:** one big thing: a match, a show or a concert. Ideas:
  - [Anfield or Everton on a match day](/guides/lfc-and-everton-ticket-guide)
  - [The Royal Court](/guides/royal-court-liverpool-guide) or [the Philharmonic](/guides/liverpool-philharmonic-hall-guide)
  - [Crazy golf, darts or bowling](/guides/competitive-socialising-liverpool)

## Day 3: Out of the city

Pick one:

| Option | Why go | Getting there |
|---|---|---|
| **Another Place, Crosby Beach** | 100 Antony Gormley iron figures over 3km of beach. Free. Check the tide | Merseyrail to Hall Road or Blundellsands and Crosby, or drive. See our [Crosby Beach guide](/guides/crosby-beach-another-place-guide) |
| **Formby** | National Trust pinewoods, dunes and red squirrels | Merseyrail or car. See our [Formby guide](/guides/formby-area-guide) |
| **Knowsley Safari** | A 5-mile drive-through with lions, rhinos, giraffes and more, open since 1971 | Car |

Crosby and Formby are both on the Merseyrail Northern Line, so you could combine them on a long day.

## Tips for a family or group

- **Book ahead:** The Beatles Story, the ferry and any match or show
- **Mix paid and free:** the national museums, the cathedral and the waterfront cost nothing
- **Eat in some nights.** With a kitchen, a group saves a lot. See our [meal plan for a group of 7](/guides/self-catering-meal-plan-group-of-7)
