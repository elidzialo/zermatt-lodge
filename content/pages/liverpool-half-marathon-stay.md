The **2027 Liverpool Half Marathon is on Sunday 14 March**. The half starts at **9.30am at Hill Dickinson Stadium** on Regent Road and finishes at the **Pier Head**, in front of the Three Graces. A new **Liverpool 10K** starts at **8.45am** from the Pier Head the same morning. The nearest station to the start is **Sandhills**. With the race selling out at 15,000 places in 2025, beds for the Saturday night go early, so book now.

### Course

BTR says the first four miles of the half were updated for 2025 and pass **Liverpool Town Hall, the Chinese Arch and Liverpool Cathedral**. The 10K heads along The Strand and follows parts of the half course, including Upper Parliament Street, Princes Road and Sefton Park Road, turning on Croxteth Drive and passing through Princes Park before returning to the Pier Head.

### How big is it?

- First held in **1994**; 2027 is the **34th** edition
- **12,000** places sold out in 2023 and 2024
- Capacity rose to **15,000** in 2025, when the start moved to Hill Dickinson Stadium, and it sold out
- **2026** was the biggest race yet, and BTR expects a fourth sell-out in 2027
- The new 10K has **3,000** places; entries opened on 14 October 2026

## Getting to the start

**By train.** Sandhills, on the Merseyrail Northern line, is the nearest station. It's a Sunday, so services are less frequent than on weekdays. Check the timetable the night before and aim for a train that gets you there well before the 9.10am bag drop deadline.

**By shuttle.** BTR lists an **Arriva shuttle bus from Liverpool ONE** to the start line at **£2 per person**, one way.

**On foot.** The start is about **1.4 miles** (around 30 minutes) from the city centre.

**By car.** BTR lists several city-centre car parks between 0.3 and 2.2 miles from the stadium. Roads around the event close from **8.30am**, so arrive early or leave the car where you're staying.

### Bag drop

Bag drop is on **Regent Road, north of the stadium**, and **closes at 9.10am**. After the start it moves to the **Cruise Liverpool car park** near the finish, so your bag is waiting at the end.

## For supporters

- **At the start:** Regent Road by Hill Dickinson Stadium is the place to see everyone set off at 9.30am
- **On the course:** the early miles pass Liverpool Town Hall, the Chinese Arch and the Cathedral, all walkable from Liverpool Central
- **At the finish:** the Pier Head, in front of the Three Graces. Agree a meeting point away from the finish funnel, as it gets very busy
- **Children:** a games room or garden back at your accommodation helps if they've had an early start

## After the race

Walk it off along the waterfront, refuel, and give yourself time before travelling home. If you're driving a long way on Sunday afternoon, a late checkout or an extra night on the Sunday makes the day far less rushed.

## Where to stay

Think about the morning, not the night before.

- **City centre:** walkable to the finish and the 10K start, but you'll still need to get to Regent Road for the half
- **North Liverpool:** handy for the start. The Northern line runs through Sandhills, so stations on all three of its branches have a direct train to the start
- **A whole house for a running club:** if several of you are running, a house means a shared kitchen for pasta on Saturday, porridge on Sunday and space to stretch

Supporters can watch at the start, then take the train or walk into town to see runners finish at the Pier Head.
