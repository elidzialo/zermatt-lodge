Event work runs on long days: early load-ins, a show, a late get-out and sometimes an overnight build. For a crew in Liverpool, the best base is usually **one house with enough beds, free parking for vans and a kitchen**, rather than scattered hotel rooms and daily car park fees. Here's what to plan for.

We checked the facts below on 22 November 2026.

## Liverpool's main venues

| Venue | What it hosts |
|---|---|
| **Exhibition Centre Liverpool**, Kings Dock | Opened September 2015, 8,100 square metres, linked by a sky bridge to the convention centre |
| **M&S Bank Arena**, Kings Dock | About 11,000 capacity including standing: concerts, Premier League Darts, the Liverpool International Horse Show |
| **Aintree Racecourse** | Race meetings, the Grand National Festival and non-racing events |
| **Anfield** and **Hill Dickinson Stadium** | Football, plus concerts and other large events |

## Parking: the hidden cost

The Exhibition Centre lists **1,450 spaces** in the Kings Dock Car Park, with these tariffs when we checked:

| Stay | Price |
|---|---|
| Up to 1 hour | £4 |
| Up to 2 hours | £9 |
| Up to 5 hours | £13 |
| Up to 9 hours | £17 |
| Up to 24 hours | £27 |

On a four-day build with two vehicles, that adds up quickly. A house with **free parking** at night means you only pay at the venue while you're working, or you can travel in by train.

## Plan for fatigue

Crews often work long days back to back. The HSE's good practice guidelines for shift work include:

- **Cap shifts at 12 hours** including overtime, or **8 hours** for night work or safety-critical tasks
- Keep long, night and early shifts to **2 or 3 in a row**
- Allow **two nights of uninterrupted sleep** when switching between day and night shifts

The HSE also notes that fatigue has been implicated in **20% of accidents on major roads**. After a late get-out, a short trip to a bed is safer than a long drive home. Our [night shift sleep tips](/guides/night-shift-sleep-tips-work-stay) cover how to rest in a shared house.

## What to look for in a crew house

1. **Real beds**, not sofa beds, and enough bathrooms for staggered starts
2. **Off-road parking** for vans or splitter vans. See our [van parking guide](/guides/van-parking-accommodation-liverpool)
3. **A washer and dryer** for blacks and hi-vis
4. **Self check-in** for late arrivals
5. **A kitchen** for early breakfasts and late meals
6. **An invoice** in the company's name. See [invoices and VAT for business stays](/guides/invoice-and-vat-for-business-stays)
7. **Clear house rules**, especially on noise after a late finish
