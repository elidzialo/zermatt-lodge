If you're visiting Manchester and keep kosher, head to **north Manchester**. Almost all the supervised shops and restaurants are in **Prestwich**, **Salford (Broughton Park and Leicester Road)** and **Whitefield**. The most useful single reference is the **Manchester Beth Din's licensee list**, and two other local authorities, **Machzikei Hadass** and **Badatz Igud Rabbonim (KIR)**, also certify. Need somewhere to stay? Our [kosher homes page](/kosher-homes) shows homes where hosts have declared kosher kitchens and walking time to shul.

We read the lists below on 22 November 2026. Shops open, close and change supervision, so check before you go.

## Licensed by the Manchester Beth Din

### Groceries, meat and fish

| Business | Type | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Halpern's | Grocers | Leicester Road, Salford |
| Kayes Kosher | Grocers | Kings Road, Prestwich |
| Glatt Butchers | Butcher | Bury Old Road, Prestwich |
| Getzels | Fishmonger | Kings Road, Prestwich |
| Singing Fish | Fishmonger | Butterstile Lane, Prestwich |

### Bakeries

- **Salford:** Brackmans (Leicester Road), Royal Bake, The Granary, Pittanor
- **Prestwich:** Bakers Heaven, Cherry on the Top, The Cake Co, The Cookie Jar, Gluten Free-dom
- **Whitefield, Crumpsall and Hale Barns:** State Fayre

### Restaurants, cafes and takeaways

- **Bury New Road, Prestwich:** JS, Sushi World, Baba's, La Familia, Bono Benito, Glida
- **Kings Road, Prestwich:** Mozzarella, Celia's Kitchen
- **Bury Old Road, Prestwich:** Benny's Bistro, Pagoda
- **Heathlands Village, Prestwich:** The Central Cafe
- **Mountheath Trading Estate, Prestwich:** Ruby's Pizza
- **Whitefield:** State Fayre Coffee Shop, Park Lane
- **Salford:** Brackmans Coffee Shop, Leicester Road
- **City centre:** Mozzarella, John Dalton Street

### Caterers

Celia's Kitchen, Niru and Orange Catering in Prestwich, Craft Catering in Salford and Corporate Caterers in Crumpsall are among the listed caterers. Useful for a simcha or a family Shabbat in a holiday home.

## Other local hechsherim

An older community directory lists:

- **Machzikei Hadass Manchester**, Northumberland Street, Salford, which offers Mehadrin certification
- **Badatz Igud Rabbonim (KIR)**, Broom Lane, Salford, which also offers Mehadrin certification

Many shops in Broughton Park carry one of these rather than the Beth Din's. If you're cooking for family, ask which they keep.

The **London Shechita Board** also publishes a licensee list that includes some north west shops.

## Planning food for a holiday home stay

- **Order Shabbat food by Thursday.** Winter Fridays are short.
- **Ask the host about the kitchen.** A standard rental isn't kosher. Bring your own pots, or use disposables and foil. See our [kosher kitchen guide for holiday rentals](/guides/kosher-kitchen-holiday-rental-guide).
- **Supermarkets** may stock kosher-certified products. Check the hechsher on each item.
- **Staying in Liverpool?** Liverpool has one kosher deli, in Childwall. Many visitors stock up in Prestwich. See our [kosher food in Liverpool guide](/guides/kosher-food-liverpool).
