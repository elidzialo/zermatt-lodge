If you're visiting Manchester and keep kosher, the most useful single reference is the **Manchester Beth Din's (MBD) official licensee list**. Two other local authorities, **Machzikei Hadass** and **Badatz Igud Rabbonim (KIR)**, also certify. Need somewhere to stay? Our [kosher homes page](/kosher-homes) shows homes where hosts have declared kosher kitchens.

Shops open, close and change supervision, so always check the official list before you go.

## Licensed by the Manchester Beth Din

When we checked, the MBD list included, among others:

- **Grocers:** Halpern's, Kayes Kosher
- **Butcher:** Glatt Butchers
- **Fishmongers:** Getzels, Singing Fish
- **Bakeries:** Brackmans, Royal Bake, The Granary, Bakers Heaven, The Cake Co, The Cookie Jar, State Fayre
- **Restaurants and takeaways:** JS, Sushi World, Mozzarella, Celia's Kitchen, Benny's Bistro, Pagoda and others
- **Caterers:** Celia's Kitchen, Niru, Orange Catering and others. Useful for a simcha or a family Shabbat in a holiday home

For addresses and opening hours, use the MBD's official list or the business's own website.

## Other hechsherim

- **Machzikei Hadass Manchester** offers Mehadrin certification
- **Badatz Igud Rabbonim (KIR)** also offers Mehadrin certification
- The **London Shechita Board** and **KLBD** publish their own licensee and product lists

If you're cooking for family, ask which hechsher they keep.

## Planning food for a holiday home stay

- **Order Shabbat food by Thursday.** Winter Fridays are short.
- **Ask the host about the kitchen.** A standard rental isn't kosher. Bring your own pots, or use disposables and foil. See our [kosher kitchen guide for holiday rentals](/guides/kosher-kitchen-holiday-rental-guide).
- **Supermarkets** may stock kosher-certified products. Check the hechsher on each item.
- **Staying in Liverpool?** Kosher supply there is limited. See our [kosher food in Liverpool guide](/guides/kosher-food-liverpool).
