Food markets are one of the easiest ways to feed a group in Liverpool. Everyone picks their own stall, you share long tables, and nobody has to agree on one menu. In winter there are also plenty of daytime markets for local produce and gifts.

We checked the dates below on 25 November 2026. Hours change often over Christmas, so check before you go.

## Street food halls

| Market | Where | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Baltic Market** | Baltic Triangle | Liverpool's first street food market; lots of traders and events |
| **BOXPARK Liverpool** | Cains Brewery Village, Baltic Triangle | Street food, sport on big screens, and the ICEBOX ice rink until 10 January 2027 |
| **Duke Street Market** | Duke Street, near the Ropewalks and Chinatown | A big hall of independent traders with long shared benches and a mezzanine |

All three are within reach of the city centre. Our [Baltic Triangle guide](/guides/baltic-triangle-area-guide) covers that area in more detail.

## Council-run markets this winter

Liverpool City Council lists these markets:

| Market | When | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Cunard Building Market | Wednesdays | 10am to 4pm |
| Basnett Street Artisan Market | Saturdays | 10am to 6pm |
| Great Homer Street Market | Saturdays | 7am to 3pm |
| Stanley Park Market | Wednesdays, except LFC match days | 9am to 3pm |
| Lark Lane Market | **Sat 28 November** and **Sat 19 December 2026** | 9am to 2pm |
| Woolton Village Market | **Sat 12 December 2026** | 9am to 2pm |

The council page lists specific dates for some of these, so check it before you travel.

For the big Christmas markets in the city centre, see our [Liverpool Christmas markets guide](/guides/liverpool-christmas-markets-guide).

## Tips for groups

- **Go early in the evening** at weekends. Halls fill up later on.
- **Some venues become 18+ at night.** If you have children or teenagers with you, check first.
- **Card payment is normal**, but a little cash helps at smaller farmers' markets.
- **Combine a market with a walk.** Lark Lane is next to Sefton Park. See our [Sefton Park and Palm House guide](/guides/sefton-park-palm-house-guide).
