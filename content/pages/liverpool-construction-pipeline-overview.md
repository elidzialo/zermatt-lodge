Liverpool City Region has a busy construction pipeline for 2026-27. The biggest areas of activity are the north docks, where a 174-hectare development zone has been proposed; the city centre, with the £5bn Liverpool Central masterplan and new Grade A offices; Paddington Village in the Knowledge Quarter; new rail stations led by Liverpool Baltic; and town centre schemes in Kirkby, St Helens, Bootle and Southport. Crews working on north Liverpool, Knowsley and motorway-side sites often base themselves outside the city centre for easier parking and shorter commutes. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in north Liverpool, sleeping up to 7, with free parking for 2 cars, minutes from the M57 and M58 and about 15 minutes by train from the city centre.

This is a summary from public reports. Projects change, so check the latest status before planning a crew around any of them.

## North Liverpool and the docks

### The North Docks development zone

In December 2025, the Metro Mayor and the leader of Liverpool City Council announced a proposed **North Docks Mayoral Development Corporation**, according to Liverpool Express:

- **About 174 hectares**, mostly brownfield, from the River Mersey to the Pumpfields and the business district
- Potential for **more than 17,500 homes** and **5 million sq ft of commercial space over 15 years**
- Powers to bring land forward, deliver infrastructure and use flexible funding

Named schemes include:

- **Central Docks:** a new urban park backed by a £55m government grant and £26m from Peel Waters, due for completion in **2028**
- **King Edward Triangle:** an emerging £1bn development
- **Pall Mall:** described as Liverpool's first major Grade A office scheme in a decade

The area builds on the momentum of Everton's **Hill Dickinson Stadium** at Liverpool Waters.

### Why it matters for crews

The north docks are on the north side of the city. From north Liverpool suburbs like Fazakerley, Walton and Aintree, the dock road and the A5036 are close by, and Sandhills station is on the same Merseyrail line.

## The city centre

### Liverpool Central masterplan

Place North West's 2026 development update describes a **£5bn masterplan** on an **86-acre site** around Liverpool Central, linking Lime Street, the business district and the waterfront. It includes the planned relocation of the **Beatles Experience** to a 100,000 sq ft space.

### Offices and refurbishments

The same update mentions:

- **Kings** skyscraper cluster, reported at £2bn
- Refurbishment of **Martins Bank** into Grade A offices
- **Pall Mall** offices
- **Festival Gardens** regeneration in south Liverpool
- A **film and TV campus** at the former Littlewoods building

## The Knowledge Quarter: Paddington Village

**Paddington Village** is a 30-acre, £1 billion site at the eastern gateway to the Knowledge Quarter, earmarked for up to **two million sq ft** of science, technology, education, health and residential development, according to Invest Liverpool. Completed buildings include The Spine, a Novotel and a 1,200-space car park. **HEMISPHERE**, a life sciences office and laboratory building, is planned.

## Rail and transport

### Liverpool Baltic station

The most advanced of the new station projects. Place North West reports:

- Cost now **£160m**, up from £96.4m approved in 2024
- On the Northern line between Liverpool Central and Brunswick, on the former St James station site
- Planning permission granted in **April 2025**
- Construction hoped to start in **2027**, subject to funding approval
- Project team includes WSP, Mott MacDonald and Skanska, among others

### Other new stations

Railway News reports that the Mayor has committed to begin work on four new stations by 2030:

- **Liverpool Baltic** (above)
- **Carr Mill** in St Helens, with initial designs complete
- **Daresbury** in Halton, with findings expected in early 2027
- **Woodchurch** on the Wirral, with findings expected in early 2027

From Place North West's 2026 update and other public sources:

| Area | What's happening |

| **Kirkby** | Knowsley Council bought the town centre for £43.8m. A Morrisons superstore and a retail extension have opened, with a cinema scheme planned at Cherryfield Drive |
| **Bootle** | Redevelopment of the Strand shopping centre |

| **Maghull** | Plans for a mental health and life sciences innovation centre |
| **St Helens** | Contractors on site across town centre and Earlestown schemes, reported at £300m combined, plus the Project Halo life sciences development |

## Science and innovation

The Combined Authority lists **SciTech Daresbury's Project Violet Phase 2**, a £17m scheme for a laboratory building and an office and innovation building, with construction starting in summer 2026.

## Housing

Housing is a big part of the pipeline:

- The north docks' potential 17,500 homes
- **Onward Homes'** affordable housing programme: 3,000 homes, around 800 in the city region, backed by £345m of Homes England grant, according to Place North West
- Up to **700 homes** being explored on land south of Cherryfield Drive in Kirkby, according to the Local Government Association
