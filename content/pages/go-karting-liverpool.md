Go-karting is a great group activity: it's competitive, it's over in a couple of hours and everyone has a story afterwards. Here's what's on offer in Liverpool and how to pick the right format for your group.

We checked the details below on 24 November 2026.

## TeamSport Liverpool

TeamSport is the dedicated indoor track in Liverpool.

| | Main track | Starter track |
|---|---|---|
| **Length** | 475m, multi-level | 275m |
| **Corners** | 10 | 10 |
| **Top speed** | 45mph | 30mph |
| **Karts** | Electric | Electric |

Junior karts are limited to 25mph.

### Age and size rules

- **Juniors:** from **age 8**, with an inside leg of at least **25 inches**
- **Adult karts:** from **age 13**, with an inside leg of at least **29 inches**
- **Combat Karts:** 13+

Measure inside legs before you book if you have younger teens in the group.

### Group formats

| Format | Track | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Grand Prix** | Main | Qualifying, then a grid-start race and a podium presentation |
| **Ultimate Race Experience** | Main | Two sessions with a pit lane start. Fastest lap wins |
| **Combat Karts** | Starter | Two 12-minute sessions, medals for the top three. 13+ |
| **Rookie Race** | Starter | Two sessions, good for younger drivers. Parties need 6 drivers or more |

For a group of adults who all want to win, go for the Grand Prix. For a mixed group, the Ultimate Race is more relaxed.

### Prices and booking

TeamSport says prices **vary by day, time, experience and group size**. It recommends **pre-booking large groups**, especially at weekends, and offers 10% off online pre-bookings with a code shown on its site.

## Gravity MAX at Liverpool ONE

If you'd rather stay in town, **Gravity MAX** in Liverpool ONE has an **e-karting track** with downhill twists and fast corners, across a two-floor venue that also has:

- AR darts and AR bowling
- Immersive GameBox and Hologate VR
- Urban Street Golf
- Shuffleboard and batting cages
- Food and bars, including a rooftop terrace

It's a good option for a group with mixed interests, because not everyone has to kart. Check age rules and opening times on its booking site.
