An evening kick-off is a great atmosphere and a hard trip home. A 7.30pm or 8pm game finishes after 9.30pm, and then you've got crowds, queues for buses and trains, and a long drive if you've come from outside Merseyside. If you live more than an hour or so away, **staying over turns a stressful midweek dash into an easy night out**, and you can travel home the next morning.

## Getting home late

### From Anfield

The matchday Soccerbus links Anfield with Sandhills station. It runs back to Sandhills for about 90 minutes after the game, with the last bus from Walton Lane about 75 minutes after the final whistle. From Sandhills, Merseyrail's Northern line runs north towards Kirkby and Fazakerley.

### From Hill Dickinson Stadium

Everton's stadium is at Bramley-Moore Dock on the north docks. Sandhills is the nearest Merseyrail station for most fans and is walkable from the ground. Trainline lists the Fazakerley to Sandhills trip at around 8 minutes.

### Check the last train

Late-evening Merseyrail services run less often than daytime trains. Check the last train from Sandhills to your station **before** the game, not after it. If you miss it, taxis near the grounds are in high demand straight after the match.

## Why stay over

- **No late drive.** You won't be on the motorway at midnight after a long day.
- **Beat the crowds.** Have a drink, wait for the rush to clear, then head back.
- **Make a trip of it.** Spend the day in the city, see the stadium tour or the waterfront, then go to the match.
- **Work the next day?** If you're travelling for work anyway, a midweek match fits around meetings.
