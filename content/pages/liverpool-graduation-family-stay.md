For a Liverpool graduation, book accommodation as soon as the ceremony dates are announced, because the city's universities hold most of their ceremonies in the same few weeks of July and central hotels fill fast. If the whole family is coming, a house a short train ride from the city centre is often easier than several hotel rooms, especially when you need parking.

This guide covers where each university holds its ceremonies, parking and transport on the day, and how to plan a stay that works for everyone.

## Ceremony venues and dates

| University | Venue | Recent dates |
|---|---|---|
| University of Liverpool | Philharmonic Hall (2026) | 13 to 17 July 2026 |
| Liverpool John Moores (LJMU) | Liverpool Cathedral, St James' Mount, L1 7AZ | 20 to 24 July 2026 |
| Liverpool Hope (winter) | Liverpool Metropolitan Cathedral | Thursday 28 January 2027, 2.30pm |
| Edge Hill | Ormskirk (see our separate guide) | Check the university |

The University of Liverpool ran sessions at 10am, 1pm and 4pm each day, grouped by faculty. Ceremony venues and patterns can change from year to year, so always check the official page before you book anything.

Edge Hill is in Ormskirk, not Liverpool city centre, so it has its own [Edge Hill graduation guide](/guides/edge-hill-university-graduation-stay).

## Guest tickets: check before you invite everyone

Families are often surprised by how few ceremony seats there are. Liverpool Hope, for example, gives each graduand **one ticket for themselves plus three guest tickets**, and says children over two need a ticket from that allocation. Entry without a ticket isn't possible.

If more relatives want to come, they can usually join for photos and a meal afterwards. Some universities also livestream ceremonies. Liverpool Hope streams its winter ceremony on YouTube.

## Getting there and parking

### LJMU at Liverpool Cathedral

LJMU's graduation travel page gives clear advice:

- **Lime Street and Liverpool Central** stations are both nearby. It's about 15 minutes' walk from either station to the Cathedral.
- Students collect tickets, gowns and photos from the **LJMU Sport Building, 85 Brownlow Hill**, before the ceremony.
- **Car parks:** Mount Pleasant (multi-storey, no lifts), Brownlow Hill (open-air) and Liverpool Central on Heathfield Street (multi-storey with lifts). LJMU says each is about 15 minutes' walk from the Cathedral. Prices change, so check on the day.
- **Cathedral parking:** during the 2026 ceremonies, the Cathedral said there was no public parking from Sunday evening to Friday evening. Blue Badge parking was for graduation guests only.

### Liverpool Hope at the Metropolitan Cathedral

Hope says there's **no general parking** at the Metropolitan Cathedral for the ceremony, only a small number of disabled spaces. Use nearby public car parks, many of which can be booked online. The last entry to the Cathedral is 20 minutes before the start, and the ceremony lasts about an hour and a half.

### University of Liverpool at the Philharmonic Hall

The Philharmonic Hall is on Hope Street, between the two cathedrals. Check the university's travel information for your ceremony. Our [city centre parking guide](/guides/parking-liverpool-city-centre) covers the main car parks.

## Planning the day

Graduation days are long. A typical day looks like this:

1. **Morning:** collect gown and tickets, first photos
2. **Ceremony:** roughly 1.5 to 2 hours including seating
3. **Afternoon:** official photos, a drink at the university's reception if there is one
4. **Evening:** family meal

Tips that help:

- **Book the evening meal early.** Restaurants near the universities get busy in graduation week.
- **Bring flat shoes.** Hope Street and the area around the cathedrals is hilly.
- **Plan for grandparents.** Long walks between car park, venue and restaurant add up. Drop them off first.
- **Have a wet-weather plan** for photos. Liverpool summers are changeable.

## Where should the family stay?

### City centre

Best if there are only two or three of you and you want to walk everywhere. Expect higher prices in graduation week, and paid parking if you drive.

### A house outside the centre

Better when the group is bigger: parents, grandparents, siblings, partners. You get one place for everyone, a kitchen for breakfast and space to relax after a long day. The trade-off is a short train or taxi ride into town.

### Questions to ask before you book

- How long is the journey to the ceremony venue, and is there a train?
- Is there free parking, and for how many cars?
- Can everyone have a proper bed, not a sofa bed?
- Is there a downstairs space for the family to gather?
