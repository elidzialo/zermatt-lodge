New Year's Eve 2026 is on a **Thursday**, with New Year's Day on Friday 1 January 2027. When we checked on 19 November 2026, **we couldn't find an official city fireworks display announced** for Liverpool. What there is: plenty of ticketed NYE parties, family-friendly skating, Christmas shows still running, and **Liverpool v Coventry at Anfield on Saturday 2 January**.

This guide is for families and mixed groups who want to see in the New Year in Liverpool without a hotel party, and for adults heading out who want a calm base to come back to.

## Is there an official New Year's Eve event in Liverpool?

Liverpool's big council-backed events tend to cluster before Christmas. For 2026 we found no announcement of a council fireworks display or a free waterfront event for 31 December. Some travel guides say fireworks can be seen over the Mersey from the Pier Head and Royal Albert Dock at midnight, but they don't name an organiser, so don't plan a trip around it unless details are confirmed.

We'll update this page if anything official is announced. In the meantime, check the Visit Liverpool and Liverpool City Council websites in late December.

## New Year's Eve with children

If you've got kids with you, the evening is more about a nice day out and a cosy night in.

### Skating on 31 December

- **Skate St George's** is open on New Year's Eve. From 21 December it opens at 10am daily, closing at 10pm Sunday to Thursday, which includes Thursday 31 December. On New Year's Day it's open 12pm to 10pm
- **Icebox at Boxpark** runs to 10 January 2027 with 45-minute sessions, from £8 for children (3 to 12) and from £10 for adults

See our [ice skating guide](/guides/liverpool-ice-skating-winter) for both rinks.

### A New Year show

Several Christmas shows run through New Year, including Mamma Mia at the Empire (to 3 January), Snow White at the Epstein (to 3 January) and the Everyman's Beauty and the Beast panto (to 16 January). The [Liverpool panto 2026 guide](/guides/liverpool-panto-2026) has dates and prices.

### The Indoor Funfair

**Liverpool Indoor Funfair Winter** runs from 19 December 2026 to 9 January 2027, listed by Liverpool Experience Campus. Times and prices weren't on the listing, so check Ticket Quarter.

### Midnight at home

For many families, the best New Year's Eve is a big dinner, games and a countdown on the TV. A whole house makes that easy, and everyone is asleep in the same place when the fireworks stop.

## New Year's Day and the first weekend

- **Fri 1 Jan:** Everton are away at Leeds (8pm). Skate St George's opens 12pm to 10pm
- **Sat 2 Jan:** **Liverpool v Coventry City** at Anfield. Sources differed on kick-off when we checked (3pm and 12.30pm both appear), so confirm with Liverpool FC
- **Sun 3 Jan:** last day of Skate St George's, Mamma Mia and Snow White

## Getting home on New Year's Eve

This is where planning matters most.

- **Trains:** the 2026 festive timetable hadn't been published when we checked. In past years, the Liverpool City Region Combined Authority has said some Merseyrail services finish earlier than usual on New Year's Eve. Check merseyrail.org before you go out
- **Taxis:** demand peaks just after midnight. Pre-book if you can, or agree to leave at 12.30am rather than 12.05am
- **Driving:** don't. Leave the car at your accommodation

From Fazakerley, direct trains normally take **about 15 minutes to Liverpool Central**. If you're out late, a taxi back to north Liverpool is a shorter ride than to towns further out.

## Tips for a New Year trip

1. **Book NYE tickets early.** Popular nights sell out before Christmas
2. **Shop before 31 December.** Some shops close early on New Year's Eve, and opening is limited on New Year's Day
3. **Check opening hours on 1 January** for anywhere you plan to visit
4. **Pack layers and waterproofs.** Waterfront wind at midnight is cold
5. **Agree a meeting point** if your group splits up
