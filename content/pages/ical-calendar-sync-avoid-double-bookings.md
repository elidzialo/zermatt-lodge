iCal is the plain, free way to keep calendars in step across booking sites. It is not instant, so it helps to know how it works and where the gaps are.

## How iCal works

Each listing on each site has an **export link**: a calendar address that usually ends in .ics. Other sites can **import** that link and block the dates it shows. It is one-way, so for two sites you need two connections: A into B, and B into A.

iCal shares **booked and blocked dates only**. Prices, minimum stays, guest names and messages stay on each site.

## How often each site refreshes

| Site | Imported calendars refresh |
|---|---|
| Airbnb | About every 3 hours, or when you press Refresh |
| Vrbo | Up to 20 minutes to show on the dashboard; the app syncs every 30 minutes |
| Booking.com | On its own schedule, often every few hours (third-party guides say 1 to 4 hours) |
| Book Homes | Every 15 minutes |

The gap between a booking and the next refresh is when a double booking can happen.

## 8 habits that stop double bookings

1. **Connect every pair both ways.** One missing direction is the most common cause.
2. **Refresh by hand** on Airbnb and Vrbo straight after a booking on another site.
3. **Name each imported calendar** clearly (for example "Book Homes" or "Booking.com"), so you can see where a block came from.
4. **Block personal dates in one place** that every site reads, rather than on each site separately.
5. **Check your links monthly.** A link can stop working without warning if a listing is changed or recreated.
6. **Keep a short buffer** around high-demand dates if you cannot refresh quickly.
7. **Do not mix methods on one channel.** If a site is connected through a channel manager, do not also connect it by iCal.
8. **Know the limits.** Vrbo allows up to five imported calendars per property, and properties managed through third-party software cannot import calendars there.

## If a double booking happens

Contact both guests quickly and honestly, follow each site's cancellation rules, and help the guest you cannot host find somewhere else. On Book Homes, if a clash happens on our side, the Book Homes guest is refunded in full and told straight away.
