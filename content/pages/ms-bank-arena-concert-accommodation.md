If you're going to a concert at M&S Bank Arena with a group, the best place to stay is one that gets you to the waterfront easily and home again safely afterwards, without paying city-centre hotel prices for several rooms. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Fazakerley, north Liverpool, sleeping up to 7. It's near Fazakerley station, about 15 minutes by train from Liverpool Central, which is roughly a 20-minute walk from the arena. There's free parking for two cars too.

This guide covers getting to the arena, parking, the journey home and how to make a weekend of it.

## About the arena

M&S Bank Arena (formerly the Echo Arena) sits on the waterfront at **King's Dock, Liverpool, L3 4BX**, between the Royal Albert Dock and the Baltic Triangle. It holds up to around **11,000 people** for standing shows. It's part of the same waterfront campus as the Exhibition Centre Liverpool and the convention centre. If you're coming for a conference rather than a concert, see our [ACC Liverpool conference accommodation guide](/guides/acc-liverpool-conference-accommodation).

## Getting to the arena

### From Fazakerley by train

1. Take a Merseyrail Northern line train from **Fazakerley** to **Liverpool Central**. Direct trains take about 15 minutes.
2. Walk from Central to the arena through **Liverpool ONE**, down towards the waterfront and King's Dock. It's about a mile, around 20 minutes.

If someone in your group has mobility needs, a taxi from Central or a drop-off near the arena is easier.

### Other stations

- **James Street** (Merseyrail) is a few minutes from the arena and Liverpool ONE.
- **Lime Street** is about 20 minutes' walk, or a short taxi or bus ride.
- **Liverpool ONE Bus Station** is a few minutes' walk from the arena.

### Driving and parking

If you'd rather drive in, there are several options near the waterfront:

- **Kings Dock Street:** a 24-hour car park a short walk from the arena entrance
- **Royal Albert Dock:** parking about three minutes' walk from the dock, then a short stroll
- **James Street area:** car parks a few minutes' walk away
- **Private driveways** near the river, bookable through parking apps

On-street bays fall within the city's controlled parking zone, so you risk a penalty if you overstay or park without a valid permit. Pre-booking a space through an app saves circling, and some apps let you extend if the show runs over.

## The journey home: plan it before the show

Getting home is where concert nights go wrong. A few tips:

- **Check the last train.** Northern line trains back towards Kirkby and Headbolt Lane, via Fazakerley, run less often in the evening. Check the exact last train for your date on the Merseyrail app before you go.
- **Leave a few minutes before the encore** if catching a train really matters, or accept that you'll get a taxi.
- **Pre-book a taxi** for big shows. Ranks get long queues at the end. See our [guide to taxis in Liverpool](/guides/taxis-in-liverpool).
- **Agree a meeting point** outside, away from the main doors, in case anyone gets separated.
- **Keep your phone charged.** Your ticket, taxi app and train times are all on it.

For more on late journeys, see [arriving late in Liverpool](/guides/arriving-late-liverpool).

## Why groups stay outside the centre on gig nights

On big concert nights, city-centre hotels fill up and prices rise. For a group of five to seven, that can mean three or four rooms on separate corridors, plus parking. A whole house a short train ride away offers:

- **Everyone under one roof**, so you can get ready together and debrief afterwards
- **Free parking**, so the drivers aren't paying waterfront rates overnight
- **A kitchen**, so a pre-gig dinner or next-morning fry-up doesn't cost restaurant prices
- **Space to relax the next day** before heading home

## Making a weekend of it

The arena is right next to some of Liverpool's best sights:

- **Royal Albert Dock:** museums, the Beatles Story and places to eat. See our [Royal Albert Dock guide](/guides/royal-albert-dock-guide).
- **Pier Head and the Three Graces**, a short walk north along the river
- **Baltic Triangle**, just south, for food halls and bars
- **A Mersey ferry cruise**, for a different view of the skyline the next morning

## Sample gig day

Arrive at the house from 3pm, cook an early dinner, then catch a train to Liverpool Central around 6pm. Walk down to King's Dock, with time for a drink near the Albert Dock. After the show, walk back to Central for a train, or take a pre-booked taxi back to Fazakerley.
