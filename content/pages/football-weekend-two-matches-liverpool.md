Liverpool is one of the few cities where you can see two Premier League clubs at home on the same trip. With Everton now at **Hill Dickinson Stadium** on the waterfront and Liverpool at **Anfield**, a double-header is a great few days for fans, groundhoppers and overseas visitors.

Fixtures below are from the Sky Sports fixture lists, with TV changes checked on 23 November 2026. Kick-off times can still move.

## The best double-headers, December 2026 to February 2027

### 1. Midweek and weekend: 2 to 5 December

| Date | Match | Ground |
|---|---|---|
| **Wed 2 Dec, 8pm** | Liverpool v Sunderland | Anfield |
| **Sat 5 Dec, 3pm** | Everton v Fulham | Hill Dickinson Stadium |

**Bonus:** Saturday 5 December is also **Becher Chase Day** at Aintree.

### 2. Over New Year: 30 December to 2 January

| Date | Match | Ground |
|---|---|---|
| **Wed 30 Dec, 8pm** | Everton v Manchester City | Hill Dickinson Stadium |
| **Sat 2 Jan, 3pm** | Liverpool v Coventry | Anfield |

Four nights takes in New Year's Eve in the city too. Check the festive train timetable, as services are reduced around the bank holidays.

### 3. Boxing Day: football and racing

| Date | Event | Venue |
|---|---|---|
| **Sat 26 Dec** | Boxing Day racing | Aintree Racecourse |
| **Sat 26 Dec, 3pm** | Everton v Sunderland | Hill Dickinson Stadium |

You can't easily do both on the same afternoon, so pick one for Boxing Day and see Liverpool or Everton on another day. Our [Christmas fixtures guide](/guides/liverpool-everton-christmas-fixtures-2026) has the full list.

### 4. February: two Everton games in five days

| Date | Match | Ground |
|---|---|---|
| **Sat 6 Feb, 3pm** | Everton v Newcastle | Hill Dickinson Stadium |
| **Wed 10 Feb, 8pm** | Everton v Leeds | Hill Dickinson Stadium |

Then **Liverpool v Hull** at Anfield on **Sat 20 Feb, 3pm**.

### The big one: the derby

**Liverpool v Everton**, Anfield, **Saturday 30 January 2027, 12.30pm** (moved for TNT Sports). It's the hardest ticket of the winter. See our [Merseyside derby weekend guide](/guides/merseyside-derby-weekend).

## Tips for a double-header

1. **Tickets first, travel second.** Only book non-refundable travel once you have tickets from the official clubs. See our [ticket guide](/guides/lfc-and-everton-ticket-guide)
2. **Watch for TV moves.** Games are often moved a few weeks ahead
3. **Midweek nights finish late.** An 8pm kick-off means getting home close to midnight. See our [midweek match night guide](/guides/midweek-match-night-stay-liverpool)
4. **Don't plan to park at the ground.** There's no match-day parking at Anfield, and Everton's stadium has a wide match-day parking zone. Use trains, shuttle buses or taxis. Our [match-day parking guide](/guides/liverpool-match-day-parking-guide) explains the options
5. **Fill the day in between.** A [stadium tour](/guides/anfield-stadium-tour-guide), the [museums](/guides/liverpool-museums-guide) or a [ferry cruise](/guides/mersey-ferry-cruise-guide)
