Moving to Liverpool is more affordable than most big UK cities, but it pays to know the numbers. ONS figures put the **average private rent at £897 a month in April 2026**, from about **£675 for a one-bed to £1,275 for four or more bedrooms**. **Band D council tax is £2,674 for 2026/27**. School places are applied for **online through the council**, which aims to process in-year applications **within 15 school days**. And getting around is easier since Merseyrail started taking **contactless cards** in February 2026. Many families rent a **furnished short-stay house** for the first few weeks while they find a home.

## Cost of living: the big numbers

### Rent

The Office for National Statistics' Price Index of Private Rents gives these averages for the Liverpool council area in **April 2026**:

| Size | Average monthly rent |
|---|---|
| 1 bedroom | £675 |
| 2 bedrooms | £823 |
| 3 bedrooms | £946 |
| 4 or more bedrooms | £1,275 |
| **All properties** | **£897** |

Rents rose **6.3%** in the year to April 2026. These are city-wide averages. City-centre flats and popular suburbs cost more, and some northern and eastern areas cost less.

Buying? The ONS reported that house prices in the **North West rose 4.7%** in the 12 months to June 2026, the highest rate of any English region.

### Council tax

Liverpool City Council's **2026/27 Band D** bill is **£2,673.59**, made up of:

| Part of the bill | Band D | Increase |
|---|---|---|
| Liverpool City Council | £2,254.37 | 4.99% |
| Police | £293.97 | 11% |
| Fire | £101.25 | 5.19% |
| Liverpool City Region Combined Authority | £24 | |

Other bands are fixed fractions of Band D. Before discounts, that works out at about:

| Band | A | B | C | D | E | F | G | H |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Annual bill | £1,783 | £2,080 | £2,377 | £2,674 | £3,268 | £3,862 | £4,457 | £5,348 |

Ask your letting agent or seller which band a property is in before you commit. It makes a real difference to your monthly budget.

## Schools

If you're moving with children during the school year, you'll need an **in-year application**.

- **How:** apply online through the council's **EMS Citizen Portal**. You'll need to register for an account first.
- **Who:** families moving from another council area or an independent school, and home-educated children who now want a place.
- **How long:** the council says that under the School Admissions Code, processing "should be no longer than 15 school days". Applications can't be fully processed during school holidays, because schools are closed.
- **Years 10 and 11:** the council advises against moving at this stage unless necessary, because course options and exam boards may differ. Talk to your current head teacher first.

## Choosing an area

Liverpool's areas feel very different. A broad guide:

- **City centre:** flats, nightlife and short commutes. Parking is expensive and space is tight.
- **Baltic Triangle and the docks:** creative and lively, with mostly apartments.
- **South Liverpool** (Aigburth, Allerton, Woolton, Childwall): leafy, close to Sefton Park and popular with families.
- **North Liverpool** (Fazakerley, Aintree, Walton, Orrell Park): more houses for the money, good rail links into town and close to the M57 and M58.
- **Crosby and the Sefton coast:** seaside, beaches and a direct train to the city.
- **The Wirral:** across the river, with coastline and countryside, linked by Merseyrail and the tunnels.

The best way to choose is to **spend a few weeks living in the city** before you sign a long lease. Try the commute at rush hour. Walk the school run. Visit on a match day if you're near Anfield or the docks.

## Getting around

### Merseyrail

The Merseyrail network covers much of the city region. Since **25 February 2026**, you can **tap in and tap out** with a contactless bank card, phone or watch, and the system aims to charge the cheapest fare. **Fares are frozen until March 2027**.

### Driving

The M62 runs east to Manchester, and the M57 and M58 serve the north and east of the city. North Liverpool suits drivers well. City-centre parking is costly.

### Example commute

From **Fazakerley** in north Liverpool, direct trains reach **Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes**, with around 69 trains a day. The first train is around 4.46am, and the last around 11.36pm on a sample day.

## Your first weeks: a checklist

- **Register with a GP.** You can register as a temporary resident at first. See our [temporary resident GP guide](/guides/temporary-resident-gp-liverpool).
- **Apply for school places** as soon as you have a date.
- **Set up council tax** once you have a permanent address.
- **Open utilities and broadband** early, as installs can take weeks.
- **Learn your local station** and the train times.
