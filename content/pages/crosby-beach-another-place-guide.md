Another Place is one of the best free things to see near Liverpool. Antony Gormley's iron men stand along Crosby Beach looking out to sea, some up to their knees in sand, some half covered by the tide. It's a short trip from the city and works in any season, as long as you check the tide and dress for the wind.

We checked the details below on 24 November 2026.

## What it is

- **100 cast-iron figures** by Antony Gormley
- Spread across **more than 3km (about 2 miles)** of sandy beach
- Arrived in **2005** and is now **permanent**
- **Free**, on a public beach

## Check the tide first

This is the one thing to plan. At high tides of around **9 to 10 metres**, the figures are **submerged and you can't get onto the beach**. Sefton Council links to tide times on its website. Aim to arrive around low tide for the most figures and the most beach.

## Stay safe on the sand

Sefton Council's advice:

- **Walking near the promenade is generally safe**
- **Don't walk out to the furthest statues.** There's soft sand and mud
- **Obey warning signs** and follow lifeguard, coastguard and ranger instructions
- **Don't feed the birds,** as it can disturb them

With children, keep to the figures nearest the prom. The photos are just as good.

## Parking

Sefton Council lists:

| Car park | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Hall Road car park**, Blundellsands, L23 8SY | Closest to Another Place |
| **Crosby Leisure Centre car park**, Mariners Road, L23 6SX | Also listed by the council |
| **Two car parks by Crosby Lakeside Adventure Centre**, off Cambridge Road, Waterloo, L22 1RR | Also listed by the council |

## By train

Merseyrail's **Northern Line** runs about **every 15 minutes**:

- **Hall Road:** about 5 minutes' walk
- **Blundellsands and Crosby:** about 5 to 10 minutes
- **Waterloo:** about 10 to 15 minutes

A nice walk is to get off at Waterloo, walk along the prom past the figures and catch the train back from Hall Road or Blundellsands and Crosby.

## Tips for a winter visit

- **Sunset** is early in winter, so plan an afternoon visit around the light as well as the tide
- **It's exposed.** Bring a windproof coat, hat and gloves
- **Wellies or old boots** if you want to walk on the sand
