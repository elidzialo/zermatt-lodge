St George's Hall is the huge classical building you see as you walk out of Lime Street station. It's a concert hall, a former court building and one of Liverpool's best-loved landmarks. Over Christmas 2026 it's also home to the city's new outdoor ice rink.

We checked the details below on 23 November 2026.

## The building

- **Opened:** **1854**, with the small Concert Room following in 1856
- **Architect:** Harvey Lonsdale Elmes, who won the design competition aged 25. After his death, Charles Cockerell completed much of the interior
- **Listed:** Grade I
- **Inside:** the **Great Hall**, the elliptical **Small Concert Room** (seating 480) and the old courts
- **Heritage Centre:** created during the restoration that reopened the hall in 2007, with tours, exhibitions and talks
- **The Minton floor:** more than **30,000 Minton tiles**, usually covered by a removable floor and only revealed on special occasions

## Skate St George's: winter 2026

Underbelly runs **Skate St George's**, an outdoor rink at the hall. The operator lists:

- **Dates:** every day from **Saturday 21 November 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027**, except Christmas Day
- **Until Friday 18 December:** Monday to Thursday 12pm to 10pm, Friday 12pm to 11pm, Saturday 10am to 11pm, Sunday 10am to 10pm
- **From Monday 21 December:** opens 10am daily, closing 10pm Sunday to Thursday and 11pm on Friday and Saturday
- **Exceptions:** opening day 2pm to 11pm, Christmas Eve 10am to 6pm, Boxing Day and New Year's Day 12pm to 10pm
- **Sessions:** the first starts 15 minutes after opening, the last 45 minutes before closing
- **Children** must be with someone aged **16 or over** at all times
- **Tickets:** book online for busy times. The box office on site is card only

Prices weren't shown on the main page when we checked, so look at the booking page. For other rinks, see our [ice skating guide](/guides/liverpool-ice-skating-winter).

## Christmas events in the hall

- **Carols by Lantern Light**, Great Hall, **Saturday 5 December 2026**. City Organist Ian Tracey hosts, with the St George's Singers, organist Ian Wells on the Willis organ and mezzo-soprano Danielle Louise Thomas. Entry is via the North Hall entrance on the east side
- **Liverpool City Halls** also lists *A Christmas Carol* and *Story Time with Santa* in its festive programme. Visit Liverpool lists Story Time with Santa at Liverpool Town Hall on selected dates from 5 to 24 December

Check times and tickets on the Liverpool City Halls website.
