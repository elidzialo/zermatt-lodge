If you're staying in Liverpool for a few days this winter, **Chester** makes a good change of scene. It's a compact walled city with Roman roots, the famous two-level shopping **Rows**, a cathedral, and in December a Christmas market. **Chester Zoo** is just outside the city too.

We checked the dates below on 25 November 2026. Check again before you book.

## Getting there

- **Train:** Merseyrail runs to Chester. Its own guide says destinations like Chester are **within about 45 minutes** of Liverpool city centre stations, and that the **last train from Chester to Liverpool leaves at 23:00**.
- **Car:** handy if you're going to the zoo, which is outside the city centre.

Our [getting around Liverpool guide](/guides/getting-around-liverpool-public-transport) explains Day Savers and contactless.

## Chester Christmas Market 2026

- **Dates:** **Wednesday 18 November to Tuesday 22 December 2026** (as listed on Chester.com)
- In 2025 the council said the market was open **seven days a week**. Check the 2026 hours before you go.

For markets in Liverpool itself, see our [Liverpool Christmas markets guide](/guides/liverpool-christmas-markets-guide).

## Chester Zoo: Lanterns and Light

- **Dates:** **Friday 13 November 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027**
- **Main listed time:** 4.15pm to 8pm
- **Price note:** £12 for slots after 6.30pm in November, excluding Saturdays. Other prices are on the zoo's ticket page.
- **Ticket types:** timed tickets, Anytime tickets (any slot on your date), Anytime Any Date tickets, and some **adult-only evenings** for over-18s

The zoo also lists **Our Story with David Attenborough** running until **Sunday 31 January 2027**. On the day we checked, daytime opening was 10am to 3.30pm, with last entry at 2.30pm. Winter hours can vary, so check the zoo website.

## In the city

- **The Rows:** two-level medieval shopping galleries in the centre
- **The city walls:** you can walk around the old city on top of them
- **Chester Cathedral:** worth a look if you're already on the walls
- **The Eastgate Clock:** the ornate clock on the walls above Eastgate, a classic photo stop

## A simple plan

1. Morning train to Chester.
2. Walk the walls and the Rows, then lunch.
3. Christmas market in the afternoon (until 22 December).
4. If you're going to the zoo lanterns, head there for an evening slot, then back to Liverpool.
