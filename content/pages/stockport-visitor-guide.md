Stockport is one of the easiest day trips from Manchester: one short train ride and you're in a town with sandstone tunnels under the streets, a hat museum and a huge red-brick viaduct. We checked these details on 25 November 2026. Opening times change over Christmas, so check each venue before you go.

## Getting there

- **Train:** Visit Manchester says Stockport is about **8 to 10 minutes** from **Manchester Piccadilly**, with frequent trains.
- **Plan it:** the Bee Network app shows journeys, tickets and live times. See our [Greater Manchester transport guide](/guides/getting-around-greater-manchester-bee-network).

## What to see

| Place | Open (as listed by Visit Manchester) | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Hat Works, Wellington Mill | Thursday to Saturday, 10am to 4pm | Museum of hatting, with restored machinery |
| Air Raid Shelters, 65 Chestergate | Tue to Fri 1pm to 5pm, Sat 10am to 5pm, Sun 11am to 4pm | Wartime tunnels cut into the sandstone |
| Staircase House, Market Place | Wednesday to Sunday (hours vary by day) | Medieval townhouse with a Jacobean staircase |
| Bramall Hall, Bramhall | Thursday to Sunday, 11am to 4.30pm | Tudor manor house in about 70 acres of parkland |
| Market Hall | Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday, 9am to 4.30pm | Independent traders and fresh food |

A few notes:

- **Hat Works:** in the 19th century Stockport was known as the **"Hatting Capital of the World"**. Visit Manchester calls Hat Works the only museum of its kind in the UK.
- **Air Raid Shelters:** closed on **Mondays**. Good on a wet day, but wear sensible shoes.
- **Staircase House:** closed Monday and Tuesday. Visit Manchester's listing has two different Friday times, so check before you go.
- **Bramall Hall** is just outside the town centre, so allow extra travel time.

## Around the town centre

- **Stockport Viaduct:** a **27-arch** brick viaduct built in **1840**.
- **The Underbanks:** old streets with independent shops, bars and street art.
- **Foodie Friday:** a street food event at Stockport Market on the **last Friday of each month**.
- **Stockport Town Hall**, a landmark since 1908, offers regular tours.

## The Plaza

The **Plaza** on Mersey Square is a restored 1930s Art Deco theatre and cinema. Its 2026 panto is **Jack and the Beanstalk**, from **Friday 4 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027**. See our [Greater Manchester pantos guide](/guides/greater-manchester-pantos-2026) for other shows.

## A one-day plan

1. Morning train from Piccadilly. Coffee in the Underbanks.
2. Air Raid Shelters (from 10am on Saturdays, 1pm on weekdays).
3. Lunch at the Market Hall.
4. Hat Works in the afternoon (Thursday to Saturday).
5. An evening show at the Plaza, then back to Manchester.
