If you're sailing to the Isle of Man from Liverpool, staying in the city the night before means you won't be racing up the motorway to make check-in. Zermatt Lodge is a whole 4-bedroom house in Liverpool, an easy train ride from Liverpool city centre, with free parking for two cars and room for up to 7. It suits families, groups of friends and work crews heading over, or coming back late and not wanting to drive home the same night.

This guide covers the Liverpool terminal, check-in, parking and the extra planning needed around TT.

## The Liverpool ferry terminal

The Isle of Man Steam Packet moved into a new purpose-built terminal on Liverpool's waterfront in June 2024.

- **Address:** Isle of Man Ferry Terminal, 1 Triskelion Way, Liverpool, L3 0BN
- **Sat nav:** the Steam Packet says some don't recognise L3 0BN. Use **L3 0AY** (Jesse Hartley Way) or the what3words address on its website
- **Terminal opening hours:** 09:00 to 19:15 from March to October and 09:00 to 16:00 from November to February, varying with sailings

### Crossing times

| Vessel | When | Crossing to Douglas |
|---|---|---|
| Manannan (fast craft) | Seasonal service, daily | About 2 hours 45 minutes |
| Manxman | Covers the route in winter, with weekend sailings | About 4 hours 15 minutes |

Check the Steam Packet timetable for your date. Weather and tides can change sailings, so sign up for service updates.

### Check-in times

From the Steam Packet's port page:

- **Vehicles:** check in at the booths at the port entrance. **Last check-in is 45 minutes before departure**
- **Foot passengers:** check in inside the passenger terminal. **Last check-in is 25 minutes before departure**
- Example: check-in opens at 08:45 for the 11:15 sailing and 16:45 for the 19:15 sailing

## Getting to the terminal

### Driving with a vehicle on the ferry

If you're taking your car, van or bike across, staying in Liverpool the night before makes the morning easy, avoiding the motorway rush from Manchester or the M6. Check a live route the night before and leave a margin for traffic.

### Foot passengers

If you're travelling on foot, don't plan to leave a car at the port. The Steam Packet says **long-term parking isn't available** there. It suggests:

- **Princes Dock multi-storey**, about a 5-minute walk, open 24/7 with CCTV and EV charging, bookable through JustPark
- **Q-Park Liverpool ONE**, a short walk away, with a discount code listed on the Steam Packet site

Alternatively, leave the car at your accommodation and take the train. From Zermatt Lodge, trains run into Liverpool Central. The Steam Packet says a council bus (£2, pay on board) runs to the terminal from Skelhorne Street via Lime Street and Liverpool ONE bus station, starting two hours before departure. It also names local taxi firms and Uber.

### Left luggage

The terminal offers left luggage at £2.50 per bag, for use on the day of travel only.

## Planning around TT

TT is the busiest fortnight of the year on the route. **TT 2027 runs from Monday 31 May to Saturday 12 June 2027**, with the Senior TT on Saturday 12 June.

- **Book the ferry first.** The Steam Packet opens TT sailings well in advance and vehicle space goes fast
- **Then book your Liverpool stay** around your sailing, especially for early departures or late returns
- **Bikes and vans:** the house has free parking for two vehicles. If you're travelling with trailers or several bikes, call the host before booking to check what will fit
- **Coming back:** a late arrival into Liverpool after a long day at the races is a good reason to stay one more night rather than drive home tired

## A simple plan for a morning sailing

1. **Arrive the afternoon before.** Check in from 3pm, do a food shop and load the car properly so nothing is in the way at the vehicle booths.
2. **Check the Steam Packet's service updates** in the evening for any weather or tide changes.
3. **Eat breakfast at the house**, then leave with plenty of margin before last check-in.
4. **Keep tickets and ID handy** in the front of the car, not packed in the boot.
