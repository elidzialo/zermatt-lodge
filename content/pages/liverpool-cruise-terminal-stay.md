If you're sailing from Liverpool Cruise Terminal and travelling more than a couple of hours to get there, stay the night before. The terminal is at Prince's Dock on Princes Parade (L3 1DL), and cruise lines give each passenger a set embarkation time, so arriving the evening before means a missed train or a motorway jam can't spoil the start of your holiday.

This guide covers where the terminal is, how drop-off and parking work, and how to choose somewhere to stay, especially if you're a family or group with a car full of luggage.

## About Liverpool Cruise Terminal

- **Address:** Princes Parade, Liverpool **L3 1DL**
- **Location:** Prince's Dock, on the waterfront just north of the Pier Head
- **What it is:** a floating structure on the River Mersey, around 350 metres long

The terminal opened in 2007, and full turnaround cruises (where passengers start and finish their holiday in Liverpool) began in 2012, according to Wikipedia. In 2024 control passed to Peel Ports Group, with Global Ports Holding as operator, and a £25m upgrade has been promised. Facilities may change as that work goes ahead, so read your cruise line's latest port information.

## Drop-off and pick-up

Ambassador Cruise Line's port guide says:

- Guests can be **dropped off and picked up outside the main terminal building**.
- **The drop-off area is small.** Arrive at your designated embarkation time, not hours early.
- On the way back, arrange pick-up **for your disembarkation time** so drivers aren't circling.

That makes a nearby overnight stay especially useful: you can time a short drive or taxi to arrive right on your slot.

## Cruise parking

Parking arrangements depend on your cruise line. As an example, Ambassador says:

- Parking is run by **CPS at Q-Park Liverpool ONE**, 35 Strand Street, L1 8LT.
- There's a **dedicated luggage drop** at the car park, and luggage is taken to the ship.
- A **free minibus** takes guests from the car park to the ship.
- You book it through the cruise line, or your travel agent if you booked that way.

Other lines may use different arrangements. Check your booking documents. Our [city centre parking guide](/guides/parking-liverpool-city-centre) covers general city car parks.

## Getting there by train

Liverpool Lime Street is the city's mainline station. Ambassador says the terminal is about **20 minutes' walk** from Lime Street, or about **10 minutes by taxi**. With cases, take the taxi. Liverpool City Council says black cabs must use the meter for journeys within Liverpool. Ambassador's guide also lists several local taxi firms.

## Pre-cruise night: a simple plan

**The day before**

- Arrive mid-afternoon and check in.
- Do a final luggage check: passports, boarding documents, medicines, chargers.
- Weigh cases if your line has limits.
- Have an early dinner and a good night's sleep.

**Embarkation day**

- Pack the car or book your taxi for a set time that gets you to the terminal for your slot.
- Keep essentials (documents, medicines, a change of clothes) in hand luggage, as checked bags may take time to reach your cabin.
- If you're using cruise parking, follow the line's instructions for the luggage drop.

## Post-cruise night

Plenty of people also stay a night after they get off the ship, especially families with young children or anyone with a long drive home. Disembarkation mornings can be early, and a few hours' rest before a three-hour drive is sensible.

## Where to stay before a cruise

### City-centre hotel

Close to the terminal, which is handy if you're arriving by train with no car. Expect paid parking if you're driving.

### A house outside the centre

Better for bigger families and groups travelling together, particularly with a car. You get space to spread out luggage, a kitchen for an early breakfast and free parking. The trade-off is a slightly longer journey to the terminal on the morning.

### What to check before booking

- How long does it take to reach the terminal at the time you'll be travelling? Use a live route planner for that time of day.
- Can you check in early enough the day before?
- Is the checkout time compatible with your embarkation slot?
- Is there secure parking for the night?
