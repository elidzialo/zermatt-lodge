If you're working in Skelmersdale and need somewhere for a crew to stay, look on the north side of Liverpool as well as in the town itself. Skelmersdale sits right on the M58, about 13 miles north-east of Liverpool, and the motorway's western end is at Switch Island on the edge of north Liverpool, so a house near there gives you a short commute plus far more shops, food and accommodation choice.

This guide is for site managers, contractors and agency teams working at Skelmersdale's estates and distribution centres.

## Skelmersdale at a glance

Skelmersdale was built up as a new town, and today it's home to a lot of industry and logistics. According to Wikipedia:

- It's in **West Lancashire**, about **13 miles (21 km) north-east of Liverpool**.
- The **M58** runs along the south of the town, between the M6 near Wigan and the Switch Island interchange in north Liverpool. The A570 and A577 also serve the town.
- Employers include the **Co-operative Bank** (around 650 staff), distribution centres for **Asda**, **P&G** and **Victorian Plumbing**, a **Walkers** snack food factory and **Chemist 4 U**.
- **Pimbo Industrial Estate** is one of the main estates. Gillibrands is another industrial area in the town.

Always confirm your exact site address and postcode before you book anywhere. Estates in Skelmersdale are spread out, and the right motorway junction depends on where you're going.

## No railway station: what that means for crews

Skelmersdale doesn't have a railway station. Wikipedia notes the original station closed in 1956 and that Skelmersdale is the second most populous town in the North West without one. Plans for a new rail link were studied, but the Department for Transport rejected the business case in 2022.

The practical upshot:

- **Most crews drive.** Plan for at least one vehicle per two or three people.
- **Public transport is bus-based.** The nearest station is Upholland. Wikipedia mentions a Stagecoach bus linking Skelmersdale with **Kirkby station**, which is on the Merseyrail Northern line. Check current timetables before relying on it.
- **Days off are easier elsewhere.** Without a station, getting into Liverpool or Southport for an evening takes planning.

## Why stay on the Liverpool side?

### More accommodation, especially for groups

Skelmersdale's own short-stay options are limited, particularly whole houses for crews of five to seven. Widening the search to north Liverpool gives you more choice.

### Short motorway commute

From the north Liverpool end of the M58, it's a straight motorway run to Skelmersdale. Traffic varies, so check live journey times for your shift start.

### Better downtime

After a long shift, a crew wants food, a supermarket and something to do. North Liverpool has supermarkets, takeaways and delivery apps, and a train to Liverpool city centre for nights off.

### Access to other sites

Plenty of contractors work on more than one job. North Liverpool is also close to Knowsley's industrial areas in Kirkby, the Aintree area and the M57 for south and east Liverpool. ## What to check before booking a crew house

- **Bedrooms:** how many proper beds, and who's sharing?
- **Bathrooms:** two is the realistic minimum for a crew on the same shift pattern.
- **Parking:** how many vehicles fit off-street? Will a van fit? Our [van parking guide](/guides/van-parking-accommodation-liverpool) covers what to ask.
- **Kitchen:** essential for early breakfasts and packed lunches.
- **Laundry:** ask what's available rather than assuming.
- **Invoicing:** check whether the host can provide an invoice for your company's accounts.
- **Flexibility:** can you extend if the job overruns?

## Shift work and logistics sites

Many Skelmersdale jobs are at distribution centres and factories that run around the clock. If your crew is on nights or rotating shifts:

- **Ask for separate bedrooms** so day sleepers aren't woken by people getting up for an early start.
- **Think about blackout.** Ask the host about curtains or blinds if anyone will be sleeping in the day.
- **Plan meals around shifts.** A big kitchen lets people cook at odd hours rather than relying on takeaways.
- **Stagger bathroom use.** Two bathrooms make a split-shift crew far easier to manage.

## A typical working week

- **Sunday afternoon:** arrive, check in from 3pm, food shop
- **Monday to Thursday:** early breakfast, M58 to site, back for dinner
- **Friday:** last shift, then home, or stay on into the weekend if the job runs on
- **Weekly:** keep the house tidy between cleans; agree it as a crew
