There's no single "best Airbnb in Liverpool". The best one is the one that **fits your trip**: where you need to be, how many beds you need, whether you're driving, and what time you'll arrive. A gorgeous city-centre flat is a poor choice for a family of seven with two cars, and a big suburban house is the wrong pick for a weekend of bars on foot. Decide the area first, then use the checks below to compare listings.

## Step 1: pick the area

| Your trip | Area to look at | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Nightlife, shops, waterfront on foot | City centre | Walk everywhere, but parking is limited and costly |
| Liverpool or Everton match | North Liverpool | Close to Anfield and Hill Dickinson Stadium by train or bus |
| Aintree races or Aintree Hospital | Fazakerley, Aintree, Maghull | Near the racecourse and the hospital site |
| Jewish community, shul on Shabbat | South Liverpool (Childwall, Allerton) | Where the shuls and community are |
| Beach and countryside | Crosby, Formby, Southport | Coast with rail links into the city |

Our [where to stay in Liverpool guide](/guides/where-to-stay-in-liverpool) covers each area in more detail.

## Step 2: the 10 checks

1. **The real bed list.** "Sleeps 8" can mean sofa beds and shared doubles. Check bed by bed, especially for adult groups and work crews.
2. **Bathrooms.** One bathroom for seven people is hard on a busy morning.
3. **Total price.** Compare the full total, including cleaning and any service fee, not the nightly rate.
4. **Parking.** Is it free, off-street, and how many cars? City-centre parking adds up fast.
5. **Check-in method and time.** Self check-in with a lockbox is easiest for late arrivals.
6. **House rules.** Parties, quiet hours, smoking, pets and maximum guests.
7. **Reviews.** Read the recent ones, and look for comments on cleanliness, noise and accuracy.
8. **Location on the map.** Check the walk to the station or venue yourself, at night if you'll be out late.
9. **Cancellation policy.** Know when you lose your money if plans change.
10. **Host contact.** How fast does the host reply before booking? That's a good sign of how they'll respond during your stay.

## Party rules

Airbnb brought in a worldwide party ban in 2020 and made it official policy in June 2022. Airbnb says the rate of party reports dropped by 44% year on year after the August 2020 policy. Hosts in residential streets take this seriously, because neighbours do too. If your trip is a birthday or a stag or hen weekend, be honest with the host about the size and purpose of the group. Most are happy with a quiet dinner and drinks; few are happy with a party.

## Stay safe when you pay

Airbnb advises guests to keep messages and payment on its platform, and to be wary of anyone who asks you to pay elsewhere or sends you to a different payment site. If you'd rather book direct, use the host's genuine business website with secure card payment, never a bank transfer to someone you found on social media.

## Airbnb or book direct?

Many Liverpool homes are listed on Airbnb and also bookable direct. Since 27 October 2025, Airbnb has moved hosts who use property management software to a single fee of **15.5%**, deducted from the host's price, according to Rentals United's summary. Hosts usually build that into the nightly rate. Booking direct can be cheaper for the same home and dates. Our [book direct vs Airbnb guide](/guides/book-direct-vs-airbnb) explains the trade-offs.
