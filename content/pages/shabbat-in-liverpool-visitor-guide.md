If you're spending Shabbat in Liverpool, three decisions matter most: **where you sleep, which shul you'll use, and where your food comes from**. Stay in south Liverpool (Childwall or Allerton) if you want to walk to an Orthodox synagogue, email or phone the shul before you travel, and bring or order kosher food, because supply in the city is limited. In winter, Shabbat starts mid afternoon. On 20 November 2026 it began at 3.50pm.

This guide uses published sources only. Community details change, so always confirm with the shul. For homes where hosts have declared kosher kitchens, Shabbat set-ups or a nearby shul, see our [kosher stays page](/kosher-homes).

## Shabbat times this winter

Liverpool is a northern city, so winter Shabbat comes in early.

- **20 to 21 November 2026:** Princes Road Synagogue listed Shabbat as beginning at **3.50pm** and ending at **5.05pm** (Parashat Vayeitzei).
- **December 2026:** sunset in Liverpool is at its earliest on **13 December, at 3.54pm**, so candle lighting through December falls well before 4pm.
- **Shortest day:** Monday 21 December 2026, with about 7 hours 32 minutes of daylight.

What this means in practice: if you arrive on a Friday in December, aim to be in your accommodation by early afternoon. A 2pm check-in is not a luxury in winter; it can be the difference between a calm Shabbat and a rush. Ask your host about early check-in before you book, and get the exact candle lighting time from the shul you'll attend.

### Chanukah falls across two Shabbatot

Chanukah 2026 begins on the evening of **Friday 4 December** and ends at nightfall on **Saturday 12 December**. The first candle and the eighth candle (Friday 11 December) are both lit on a Friday, before Shabbat candles. If you're staying in a rental, check where you can safely place a chanukiah, and ask the host about smoke alarms and windowsills before you arrive.

**Ring ahead.** Every UK community takes security seriously. Introduce yourself, say how many are coming and ask about a minyan and service times. It's also the best way to be invited for a meal.

### Is there an eruv?

We could not find a published eruv for Liverpool in any source we checked. The nearest large one is the **Manchester Community Eruv**, which covers Broughton Park, Prestwich and the surrounding areas and is certified by the Manchester Beth Din (status hotline 0161 883 1333). If you need to carry, plan as though there is no eruv in Liverpool and ask the local rabbi.

## Kosher food: plan before you travel

Kosher food is the hardest part of a Liverpool Shabbat. There is no long list of local shops to rely on, and the city's kosher supply has had well-reported problems in recent years. Please check current supervision with the local rabbi before you buy anything locally.

The practical route most visitors take:

- **Bring it with you.** Pack sealed, hechshered food for Friday night and Shabbat lunch
- **Use Manchester.** The Manchester Beth Din lists licensed butchers, bakeries, groceries and caterers, all in Greater Manchester. Examples include Halpern's in Salford and Kayes Kosher in Prestwich (groceries), several Salford and Prestwich bakeries, and caterers in Prestwich
- **In hospital?** Merseyside Jewish Community Care says kosher meals are available on request at all Merseyside hospitals. Call 0151 733 2292 if there is a problem

If you're cooking in a rental, read our [Shabbat-friendly holiday home checklist](/guides/shabbat-friendly-holiday-home-checklist) first. It covers urns, hotplates, keys and how to handle a kitchen that isn't kosher.

## Where to stay for Shabbat

Walking distance is everything. Before you book any rental:

1. **Map the walk** from the door to the shul you'll use. Check pavements and lighting, as it will be dark on Friday night from late afternoon
2. **Ask about the door.** Electronic keypads and app locks are a problem on Shabbat. A physical key or a lockbox you can leave open is easier
3. **Ask about lights and heating.** Can you leave lights on, and is the heating on a timer?
4. **Check the kitchen.** A hotplate and urn make a big difference. Ask the host what is there

South Liverpool, around Childwall and Allerton, is where the community lives and where the shuls are. That's the area to search.

## A simple Friday plan for December

- **Thursday:** confirm shul contact and food delivery or collection
- **Friday morning:** travel, arriving by early afternoon
- **Friday, about 2pm:** check in, set up urn, hotplate and lights
- **Before about 3.30pm:** candle lighting (check the exact time)
- **Shabbat morning:** walk to shul. Allow extra time in the dark and wet
- **Motzei Shabbat:** Shabbat ends around 5pm in winter, leaving a long evening
