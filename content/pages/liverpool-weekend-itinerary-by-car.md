A car makes a Liverpool weekend more flexible: you can reach the beaches, the pinewoods and the safari park without juggling trains. But it's a pain in the city centre, where parking is busy and adds up fast. The trick is to use the car for the right days.

We checked the details below on 24 November 2026.

## The rule of thumb

| Going to | Best way |
|---|---|
| City centre, Albert Dock, Pier Head, Cavern | **Train** (Merseyrail) |
| Anfield or Hill Dickinson Stadium on a match day | **Train or bus**, not the car |
| Crosby Beach | **Either.** Car parks by the beach, or Merseyrail |
| Formby | **Either**, but car parks fill fast on sunny days |
| Knowsley Safari | **Car.** It's a drive-through |

## Friday: arrive and park up

Aim to arrive mid-afternoon, before the Friday rush. Do a food shop on the way, park at your accommodation and leave the car there until Sunday. Staying somewhere with **free parking** saves a lot over a weekend. See our [group accommodation with parking guide](/guides/group-accommodation-liverpool-with-parking).

## Saturday: the city, by train

- **Morning:** train into the city centre. Start at the [Royal Albert Dock](/guides/royal-albert-dock-guide) and The Beatles Story (open from 9am, last winter Saturday entry 5.15pm)
- **Lunch:** Liverpool ONE or the dock
- **Afternoon:** the Pier Head, the free Museum of Liverpool, or the [Mersey Ferry](/guides/mersey-ferry-cruise-guide)
- **Evening:** the [Cavern Club](/guides/cavern-club-visitor-guide) or a show, then the train back

No parking charges, no driving after a drink, no circling for a space.

### If you do drive into town

Q-Park lists **Liverpool ONE** (about a 5-minute walk from the Albert Dock), **Hanover Street** and the **John Lewis car park**. The Beatles Story also points to the car park opposite the dock entrance. Check prices before you go.

## Sunday: drive out

Pick one:

### Crosby Beach and Another Place

Antony Gormley's **100 iron figures** along more than 3km of beach. Free. **Check the tide**: at high tide you can't get onto the beach. Sefton Council lists parking at **Hall Road** (closest to the statues), **Crosby Leisure Centre** and **Crosby Lakeside**. See our [Crosby Beach guide](/guides/crosby-beach-another-place-guide).

### Formby

National Trust pinewoods, dunes and red squirrels. Car parks fill quickly on fine days, so go early. See our [Formby guide](/guides/formby-area-guide).

### Knowsley Safari

A **drive-through route of about 5 miles** with lions, rhinos, giraffes, zebras and more. It's been open since 1971. Allow most of the day.

## Match days: leave the car behind

If Liverpool or Everton are at home, roads near the grounds get very busy before and after the game, and there are parking restrictions on the streets around both grounds. Plan around it, or take the train or bus. Our [match-day parking guide](/guides/liverpool-match-day-parking-guide) has the details.
