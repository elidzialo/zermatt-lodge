On **Saturday 10 April 2027**, Liverpool hosts two of its biggest crowds on the same afternoon. The **Randox Grand National** is run at Aintree at **4pm**, and **Liverpool v Newcastle** is listed at Anfield for **3pm**. That kick-off is provisional and could move for TV. Either way, the city will be at its busiest, so book your stay early, leave the car and use Merseyrail. And accept that you can't watch both live.

This guide covers what is confirmed, how the transport works on the day and how to plan a weekend around the clash.

## What's confirmed so far

| Event | Date | Time | Venue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening Day, Grand National Festival | Thu 8 April 2027 | See event schedule | Aintree Racecourse |
| Ladies Day | Fri 9 April 2027 | See event schedule | Aintree Racecourse |
| **Grand National** | **Sat 10 April 2027** | **4pm** | Aintree Racecourse |
| **Liverpool v Newcastle** | **Sat 10 April 2027** | **3pm (provisional)** | Anfield |

The Jockey Club confirms the festival dates and says the Grand National "will be 10 April 2027 at 4pm". Gate times and the full race card are published on its event schedule nearer the time.

The Liverpool fixture comes from the published 2026-27 Premier League list. As with every fixture, **dates and kick-off times are subject to change for TV**. Saturday games often move to 12.30pm or 5.30pm. Check the club's site about six weeks before you travel.

## Can you do both?

Not live. A 3pm kick-off ends at about 4.50pm, after the race has been run. If the game moves to 12.30pm, it ends at about 2.20pm. In theory you could then cross north Liverpool to Aintree, but you would need a racing ticket, the trains will be packed, and roads near both venues will be closed or gridlocked. Most people pick one and watch the other on TV.

A group of friends can split up, though. Some go racing and some go to the match, and you all meet back at the house in the evening. Fazakerley sits between the two venues, so that works well from north Liverpool.

## Getting to Aintree on Grand National Day

**By train.** This is the best option. The Jockey Club's travel page says Merseyrail runs:

- **Liverpool Central and Moorfields to Aintree every 7.5 minutes** from about 11am to 1pm, before racing
- **Aintree back to Central and Moorfields every 7.5 minutes** from about 4.30pm to 8.30pm, after racing
- A **15-minute service on the Ormskirk line**, with a reduced service at Aughton Park and Town Green

**An important point if you're staying near Fazakerley.** At busy times on race days, **Kirkby line trains start and end at Sandhills**. The Jockey Club says that between about 11am and 2pm and between 5pm and 9pm, people heading to or from Rice Lane, Fazakerley, Kirkby or Headbolt Lane must change at Sandhills. In 2026 Merseytravel also ran a reduced 30-minute service on the Headbolt Lane branch on all three race days. So allow extra time, or walk or take a taxi to the course if you're close enough.

**By bus.** Merseytravel says Stagecoach ran a racecourse shuttle from Elliot Street in the city centre in 2026, and routes 300, 310 and 345 serve the Aintree area on diversion.

**By car.** The course is on the A59, about a mile from the M57 and M58. Racecourse parking **must be booked in advance**. Car parks open at 6am, but you can't enter the course until the gates open. The Melling Road crossing closes 30 minutes before the big race and reopens 30 minutes after.

## Getting to Anfield on the same day

The Grand National crowd doesn't go away for the match, so plan for a slower trip than usual.

- **Soccerbus from Sandhills.** Merseyrail's Soccerbus runs between Sandhills station and Anfield. For Liverpool games it runs for up to 3 hours before kick-off, and returns run for about 90 minutes after the final whistle. Merseyrail lists an adult return at £3.80 bought with a rail ticket, or £4 on the bus. Fares can change.
- **917 express bus.** LFC says the 917 runs from Commutation Row, near Lime Street, to Anfield in about 15 minutes.
- **Matchday buses 926 and 927** run from Liverpool ONE before the game and from St Domingo Road after it.
- **Don't drive.** Anfield is surrounded by a residents' parking scheme, and LFC says enforcement is active. Taxis from the city centre usually cost £8 to £10, according to the club, but expect long waits on a day like this.

Remember the Sandhills point above. If Kirkby line trains are terminating there, you'll change at Sandhills anyway, and that's also where the Soccerbus leaves from.

## A suggested weekend plan

**Friday 9 April (Ladies Day).** Arrive, check in from 3pm and do a big food shop. If you're racing on Friday too, go straight to Aintree.

**Saturday 10 April.**

- **Racing group:** leave early. Aim to be at the course well before the first race and well before the 4pm National.
- **Match group:** leave earlier than normal. The Soccerbus queue at Sandhills will be long.
- **Evening:** don't try to eat in town without a booking. Many groups cook at home or order in and swap stories.

**Sunday 11 April.** Late breakfast, then checkout by 11am. Expect busy trains as visitors head home.

## Booking tips for 10 April

- **Book now.** Grand National weekend is the busiest weekend of the year in north Liverpool, and this year the football adds to it.
- **Check the minimum stay.** Many hosts set a two-night minimum or longer for festival weekends.
- **Read the cancellation policy.** If the match moves, your racing plans won't change, but check what happens if your own plans do.
- **Count beds, not just guests.** Groups of friends usually want separate beds rather than shared doubles.
