Going from one home to several changes the job: the risk is no longer one double booking, it is missing something across many homes. This checklist keeps each home in order.

## Per owner

- A written management agreement: your fee, who pays for what, how payouts and statements work, and how either side can end it.
- The owner's written consent from their lender, freeholder and insurer for short lets.
- Agreement on which sites the home is listed on.

## Per home: compliance file

- Gas safety record (yearly) and electrical safety report.
- Fire risk assessment. Larger or more complex homes should follow GOV.UK's sleeping accommodation guidance.
- Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, tested at every turnover.
- Insurance covering paying guests, with public liability.
- Council answer on planning and licensing. In London, the 90-night limit for whole homes.
- A log of nights let, ready for the national register expected in England from March 2027.

## Calendars and channels

- One source of truth per home: a channel manager, or one master calendar that every site reads.
- Every site connected both ways, with clear names for each imported calendar.
- A monthly check that every iCal link still works.
- Prices and minimum stays set per site, as iCal does not share them.

## Turnovers

- A cleaning checklist per home, with photos of how each room should look.
- Linen and consumables stock, and who restocks.
- A damage and lost property process, with photos and dates.

## Guests

- Saved replies for arrival, wifi, parking and checkout, written per home.
- One place you see messages from every site.
- A local emergency contact for each home.

## Money and tax

- Payouts reconciled per home, per site, each month.
- Owner statements on a fixed date.
- Your accountant's view on VAT and tax now the furnished holiday lettings rules have ended (April 2025).
