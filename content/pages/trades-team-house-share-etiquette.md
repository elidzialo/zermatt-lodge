A shared house is often the best way to put a crew up: more space than hotel rooms, a proper kitchen and parking for the vans. But four to seven people who work together all day, then live together all evening, need a few ground rules. Agree these on day one and the job goes better.

## Before you arrive

The **site lead or booker** should:

- **Share the house rules** with everyone, especially on parties, smoking, guests and parking
- **Agree bedrooms** in advance, so nobody arrives at 9pm to an argument
- **Send the check-in details** to one or two people, not the whole crew
- **Tell the host** who the lead contact is

## Bedrooms

- **Master to the lead** or the longest stayer
- **Night shift workers get the quietest room,** away from the kitchen and front door
- **No sharing beds** unless everyone is genuinely fine with it. Book enough beds instead
- **Keep your room tidy.** The host will see it at changeover

## Bathrooms

- **Make a rota for early starts.** With two bathrooms and seven people, 6am gets busy
- **Ten minutes in the shower** on workdays
- **Wipe the basin and hang towels up.** Nobody wants to clean up after a colleague

## Kitchen and food

Pick one system and stick to it:

- **Food kitty:** everyone puts in, one person shops, you cook together. Cheaper and sociable. Our [meal plan for a group of 7](/guides/self-catering-meal-plan-group-of-7) helps
- **Own shelf each:** labelled food, your own cooking, your own washing up

Either way:

- **Wash up after each meal**
- **Bins out daily**, and follow the local recycling rules the host gives you
- **Clean the hob and microwave** after use

## Noise and quiet hours

- **Agree quiet hours,** for example 10pm to 6am, and longer if someone is sleeping in the day
- **Headphones** for music and videos in shared rooms
- **Close doors gently** on early starts

The HSE's advice for shift workers includes asking others in the house to keep noise down while you sleep, and using earplugs or white noise. If anyone is on nights, read our [night shift sleep tips](/guides/night-shift-sleep-tips-work-stay).

## Workwear, boots and tools

- **Boots off at the door.** Bring a boot tray if the host doesn't provide one
- **No tools or materials inside** unless the host has said it's fine
- **Laundry rota** for the washer and dryer, and empty them when done
- **Don't dry hi-vis on radiators** if the host asks you not to

## Vans and parking

- **Park only where the host says.** Don't block neighbours' drives
- **No engine idling** early in the morning
- **Load and unload quietly** before 8am

See our [van parking guide](/guides/van-parking-accommodation-liverpool).

## Guests, parties and smoking

- **Only the people on the booking** stay overnight
- **No parties.** Most homes ban them, and bookings found to be for parties can be cancelled
- **No smoking indoors,** and follow the host's rules on smoking outside

## Checkout day

- **Strip beds** if the host asks
- **Run the dishwasher, empty the fridge, take the bins out**
- **Report any damage** before you leave
- **Leave keys** exactly as instructed
