Away fans at Everton's Hill Dickinson Stadium sit in the north-east corner and get in through turnstile block 24 on the North Stand. The easiest way there is by train to Sandhills, about a 15-minute walk, or on a matchday shuttle bus from the city centre. There's no general parking at the ground, bags bigger than A4 aren't allowed, and after an evening kick-off it's worth staying over rather than driving home late.

The details below come from the away supporter information Everton issued to visiting clubs during the 2025/26 season and from the bus operator. Always check your own club's travel notes for your fixture.

## The stadium at a glance

- **Where:** Bramley-Moore Dock, on Liverpool's north docks, between the city centre and Bootle
- **Capacity:** 52,769, according to Wikipedia
- **First competitive match:** August 2025
- **Away section:** north-east corner, rail seating throughout, accessible seating available

## Getting in

From recent away supporter information:

- **Away concourse:** Level 1, through **turnstile block 24** on the North Stand
- **Gate E** is the closest gate to the supporters' coaches and the away turnstiles
- **Turnstiles open two hours before kick-off**
- **Budweiser Plaza**, the route to all turnstiles, opens **three hours before** kick-off
- **Away ticket office** is next to turnstile 24 and opens three hours before kick-off. Bring **photo ID** to collect duplicate tickets

## Getting there

### By train

**Sandhills** is the closest station, about a **15-minute walk**. It's on every Merseyrail Northern line route (Southport, Ormskirk, Hunts Cross and Headbolt Lane). Recent guidance says trains run every 15 minutes on those lines from roughly three hours before to two hours after the match, with extra services and longer trains planned.

After the game Sandhills gets very busy. Alternatives, each about **25 to 30 minutes' walk**, are **Bank Hall**, **James Street** and **Moorfields** (the Old Hall Street entrance opens before and after matches).

### By shuttle bus

Stagecoach runs matchday shuttles for the 2026/27 season:

| Route | From | Outbound |
|---|---|---|
| 919 | Commutation Row, city centre (near Lime Street) | From 3 hours before kick-off until 30 minutes before |
| 939 | Bootle Bus Station (Washington Parade) | From 3 hours before kick-off until 30 minutes before; returns from 10 minutes after the final whistle |
| 918 | Kirkby Bus Station | Scheduled timetable |

Fares: **£2 adult single, £4 adult return**, or **£12 for a group of four adults return**. Pay with the Stagecoach app, contactless or cash.

### By coach

Away coaches park on **Regent Road**, north of the stadium, from the Boundary Street junction towards Bootle. It's free, and it's about a 10-minute walk to the turnstiles. Approach from the north along Derby Road, turn right onto Boundary Street, then right onto Regent Road. Home coaches park first. If you need an accessible coach space, your club must tell Everton in advance.

### By car

There's no general parking at the stadium. Recent guidance says:

- Use car parks in the **city centre** or at **Bootle Strand**, then take the train, a shuttle or walk.
- **Don't park on residential or industrial streets.** Enforcement is in operation and cars are fined.
- **Road closures** apply on matchdays.
- There are **six free accessible spaces** for away supporters, booked through your club's disability access officer. Arrive at least two hours early.
- Merseyrail station car parks offer **over 4,400 free spaces** at more than 40 stations as park and ride.

Sefton Council says the **Strand Shopping Centre multi-storey** in Bootle stays open until 11pm for match fans. Our [Liverpool match day parking guide](/guides/liverpool-match-day-parking-guide) has more.

### By bike

Sefton Council lists **152 cycle stands** along Regent Road and the Dock Wall within the stadium footprint, at the owner's risk.

## Bag policy

- Small personal bags only, **no bigger than A4**
- **No rucksacks, briefcases, suitcases or suit bags**
- **No storage** at the stadium; left luggage is available in the city centre
- Medical exemptions need a certificate requested at least 24 hours before kick-off

If you're travelling for the weekend, drop your bags at your accommodation before the game.

## Evening kick-offs: plan the journey home

Midweek and evening games at Hill Dickinson finish late. Queues at Sandhills can be long, trains run less often late in the evening, and a long drive home after 10pm isn't much fun. Check the last trains and coach times before you set off, or book somewhere to stay so you can travel home the next morning.
