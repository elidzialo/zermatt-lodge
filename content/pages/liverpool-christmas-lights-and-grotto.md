The best-documented Santa visits for 2026 are at **Knowsley Safari**, with Santa's Grotto evenings from **20 November to 23 December** (£21.50 per child) and daytime visits from **21 November to 24 December**. In the city, **Story Time with Santa** runs at Liverpool Town Hall on selected dates from 5 to 24 December. For lights, the new **30-metre walk-through Christmas tree** at Liverpool ONE and the **Enchanted** trail at Knowsley Safari are the standouts.

Here's what we could confirm, checked on 19 November 2026, and what's still to be announced.

## Santa grottos

### Knowsley Safari

Knowsley Safari's Santa page lists three options. The page doesn't print a year, but the dates match its 2026 Enchanted season.

| Option | Dates | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Santa's Grotto Evening | 20 Nov to 23 Dec | £21.50 per child | Book alone or with Enchanted. Standalone grotto tickets don't include the trail |
| Santa's Grotto Daytime | 21 Nov to 24 Dec | Check booking page | Needs Safari admission or membership |
| Santa's Grotto SEN Evening | 9 Dec only | Check booking page | Relaxed, welcoming setting |

Every child gets a large plush toy. If you combine the grotto with Enchanted, the Safari advises leaving at least 30 minutes before or 1 hour after your trail slot.

### Story Time with Santa (Liverpool Town Hall)

Visit Liverpool's 2026 Christmas guide lists **Story Time with Santa** at Liverpool Town Hall on selected dates from **5 to 24 December**, with a sensory-friendly session. Prices and times are on the Liverpool City Halls website. It's a gentler option for little ones than a busy grotto queue.

### Croxteth Hall

Croxteth Hall in West Derby has run Christmas events in past years, including Story Time with Santa and a "Santa Paws" grotto for dogs in 2024. We couldn't find 2026 dates when we checked, so look at the Croxteth Hall and Liverpool City Halls websites nearer the time.

### Speke Hall

The National Trust's Christmas page for Speke Hall lists a Merry Mice family indoor trail, festive music evenings on 2, 4 and 10 December, and the house open 28 and 29 November then Wednesdays to Sundays until 20 December, 12.30pm to 4pm. It's more festive house than grotto, but a calm afternoon with children.

## Christmas lights

### City-centre lights

The **Liverpool BID Company** says it funds about **80% of the city's festive lights** in 2026. We couldn't find a 2026 switch-on event or a published lights trail. The lights are up, though, so a walk from Lime Street through Church Street and Liverpool ONE works well after dark.

### Liverpool ONE

Liverpool ONE's decorations went live on **Friday 6 November 2026**, including:

- A new **30-metre Christmas tree** at Paradise Place that you can walk through
- A light canopy at Peter's Lane and Keys Court
- A light curtain at the Church Street and Keys Court entrance
- A light drape and oversized bauble at Peter's Square
- The returning bauble arch, reindeer and angel wings

**Festive Thursdays** bring free live entertainment through December, and **Wishmas**, a free all-day event, is on Saturday 5 December.

### Enchanted at Knowsley Safari

Knowsley Safari's Christmas light trail through the Foot Safari, described as "an all-new extended trail" for 2026.

- **Dates:** selected evenings, 20 November to 23 December 2026
- **Tickets:** from £9.95
- **Not included:** refreshments, rides and Santa
- **New for 2026:** an Enchanted SEN Night for families of children with additional needs

The Safari says a number of dates sold out in advance last year, so book soon.

### St George's Plateau after dark

**Skate St George's**, with its Big Wheel and Christmas market, lights up the plateau in front of St George's Hall every evening to 3 January (closed Christmas Day). It's one of the best spots in the city for Christmas photos.

## A simple Santa and lights day plan

1. **Morning:** drive to Knowsley Safari for a daytime grotto visit with your admission
2. **Afternoon:** back to base for lunch and a rest
3. **Evening:** train into town, walk through Liverpool ONE to see the tree, then to St George's Plateau for the market

Or reverse it: city lights on one night, Enchanted on another.

## Getting around

- **City centre:** trains from the house reach Liverpool Central in about 15 minutes
- **Knowsley Safari:** near Prescot, east of Liverpool. Easiest by car via the M57
- **Speke Hall:** south Liverpool, near the airport. Easiest by car
